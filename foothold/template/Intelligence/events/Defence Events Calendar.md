# Defence Events Calendar

The recurring UK and international defence events worth tracking. Each series with substantive content has its own page in `Intelligence/events/<Series>/`. Use this page as the navigation surface; create dated event notes inside the series folder as you attend.

## UK exhibitions and trade shows

| Event | Cadence | Venue | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Intelligence/events/DSEI/About DSEI\|DSEI]] | Biennial (odd years) | ExCeL London | The UK's largest defence and security trade show. End-user demonstrations, supplier exhibition, conference programme. |
| [[Intelligence/events/Farnborough International Airshow/About Farnborough International Airshow\|Farnborough International Airshow]] | Biennial (even years) | Farnborough, Hampshire | Largest UK aerospace and defence airshow. Trade days plus public days; major defence procurement announcements typical. |
| [[Intelligence/events/DPRTE/About DPRTE\|DPRTE]] | Annual | Cardiff (and online) | Defence Procurement, Research, Technology, Exportability. MOD-engaged conference focused on SMEs and supply-chain entry. |
| [[Intelligence/events/Security and Policing/About Security and Policing\|Security and Policing]] | Annual | Farnborough, Hampshire | The Home Office's annual security-and-policing-only trade event. Closed door (Home Office vetting); the homeland-security counterpart to DSEI. |

## UK conferences and senior-engagement events

| Event | Cadence | Run by | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|

| **RUSI Air and Space Power Conference** | Annual | RUSI | Air and space equivalent. |

| **Defence Investors' Advisory Group (DIAG)** | Recurring | MOD Defence Investment Unit | Senior banking, venture capital, and strategic finance gathering on defence investment priorities. |
| **Strategic Command Conference** | Annual | [[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command\|CSOC]] | Senior-leadership conference covering joint, cyber, intelligence, and specialist operations. |

## Regional cluster events

The [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/Regional Defence and Security Clusters|Regional Defence and Security Clusters]] each run a calendar of regional events through their RFCA-convened steering groups. The closest event programme to a defence-tech founder is typically their home cluster (SCRDSC, SWRDSC, London RDSC, etc.).

## How to use this calendar

- Pre-event: create a dated event note inside the relevant series folder (`Intelligence/events/<Series>/YYYY-MM-DD <Series> — <Event Name>.md`) per the [[Intelligence/events/Events Guide|Events Guide]].
- During the event: capture observations, quotes (with Chatham House Rule applied where stated), and contacts.
- Post-event: link attendees to their CRM contact pages, link organisations to their CRM org pages, and surface any follow-up actions.
