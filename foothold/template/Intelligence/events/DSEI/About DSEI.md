# DSEI — Defence and Security Equipment International

DSEI is the UK's largest defence and security trade event. Held **biennially in odd years** at **ExCeL London**, it brings together international defence ministries, supply-chain primes, SMEs, military end-users, and policy figures over four trade days.

DSEI is the event the UK MOD uses as its principal industry-engagement platform: senior MOD speakers, named programme announcements, demonstration zones across land/air/sea/joint domains, and an active SME presence under trade-association banners (ADS, techUK).

Website: [dsei.co.uk](https://www.dsei.co.uk).

## What's typically on

- **Exhibitor zones** across land, air, sea, joint, and security domains.
- **MOD-engaged demonstration areas** including live demos of unmanned and counter-uncrewed systems.
- **Conference programme** covering policy, capability, and emerging-technology themes. Senior MOD figures often deliver keynotes.
- **Buyer / supplier meetings** brokered via the wider trade-association ecosystem.
- **International delegation programmes** through UKDSE (now [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments International (NA-Intl)|NA-Intl]]).
- **Themed days** typically including a UK Capability and Innovation focus.
- **DSEI Gateway** — the year-round content and intelligence platform run alongside the live event ([dsei-gateway.com](https://dsei-gateway.com/)).

## Why it matters for defence-sector SMEs

DSEI is one of the few moments when the UK defence customer ecosystem is physically co-located. Most major UK defence procurement narratives have a visible thread through DSEI: programme announcements, supplier appointments, framework launches, and senior policy speeches. The DSEI Gateway commentary is a useful intelligence source between events.

SMEs that cannot afford a dedicated stand typically attend under a trade-association banner (ADS UK SME Zone, techUK pavilion) or as visitors. Meet-the-buyer sessions are pre-bookable.
