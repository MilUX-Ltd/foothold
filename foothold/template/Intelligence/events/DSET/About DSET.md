# DSET — Defence Simulation, Education and Training

DSET is the UK's leading annual conference for military simulation, education and training. Organised and delivered by **Ruddy Nice**, the event sits at the centre of the UK Modelling, Simulation and Training (MS&T) community and is the principal meeting point for military training users, simulation and training suppliers, academia, and policy figures across the synthetic-training stack.

The 2026 edition is scheduled for **23 to 25 June 2026**. DSET runs in a hybrid format with a physical conference and exhibition plus a virtual hub providing year-round access to recordings, downloads, and DSET virtual events for attendees, who become DSET members on registration.

Website: [dset.co.uk](https://dset.co.uk).

## What's typically on

- **Speaker programme** with a military-driven agenda. UK Defence Academy, MOD training community, NATO partners, and primes in the simulation and training space are typical contributors.
- **Exhibitor zone** covering modelling and simulation, learning technology, immersive systems, AI-enabled training, serious games, and the wider synthetic-environment stack.
- **Serious Games Showcase & Challenge Europe (SGSCE)** — an associated competition platforming serious-games innovation, with its own finalists and sponsors stream.
- **Awards dinner** typically hosted around the event.
- **Intern programme** linking the next generation of MS&T practitioners into the conference.
- **Virtual hub** carrying live streaming, networking, and post-event recordings throughout the year.

## Why it matters for defence-sector SMEs

DSET is the right room for any SME working in simulation, modelling, synthetic training, immersive systems, serious games, learning technology, or AI-enabled training. The community sits at the user-supplier boundary in a way that doesn't really replicate at the larger trade shows: military training users are deliberately present and the agenda is shaped to reflect their problems rather than running supplier-led. The hybrid format and year-round virtual access lower the barrier for SMEs that cannot commit to in-person attendance every year.

**Lead media partner** is MS&T (Halldale), the trade press of record for the simulation and training sector. **Digital partner** is Digivents.
