# DVD (Defence Vehicle Dynamics)

DVD is the UK's biennial land-equipment exhibition, held in even years at the UTAC Millbrook proving ground in Bedfordshire. It is the land domain's counterpart to the all-domain DSEI: DE&S and British Army-facing, focused on military vehicles, mobility, and land systems. The proving-ground setting is the point of difference, with equipment demonstrated live on the test tracks rather than shown only on static stands.

Sponsored by the National Armaments Director Group and Army Headquarters. The 2026 edition runs on **16 and 17 September 2026** under the theme "Connected for Combat: Aligning people, systems and decisions".

Website: [theevent.co.uk](https://www.theevent.co.uk).

## What's typically on

- **Live mobility demonstrations** on the Millbrook tracks, alongside a static exhibition.
- **DE&S and Army engagement**, with the land-equipment programme community present.
- **Supplier exhibition**, weighted to land platforms, mobility, protection, power, and vehicle systems and sub-systems.
- **Free entry** for defence and industry, with registration required.

## Defence and security overlap

DVD sits squarely in the land domain. It is the natural show for companies working on vehicles, mobility, protection, autonomy for land platforms, soldier systems, and the sub-systems and software that go into them. Buyers and integrators from the DE&S land portfolio and the major land primes attend.

## Why it matters for defence-sector SMEs

For a founder in or adjacent to land systems, DVD is the most direct UK route to the DE&S and Army land community in one place, and the live-demonstration format rewards anything that can be shown working rather than only described. It is less relevant to founders with no land-domain angle, where DSEI, DASA, or a home cluster will be a better use of time. Entry is free, so the main cost is travel to Bedfordshire and two days.
