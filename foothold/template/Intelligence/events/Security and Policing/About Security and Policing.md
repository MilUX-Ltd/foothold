# Security and Policing

The official **Home Office event** showcasing the very best of British policing, security, and law enforcement. Held annually at **Farnborough, Hampshire**, Security and Policing is a **closed event**: attendance is vetted by the Home Office, with access restricted to the law enforcement, security, and government community plus their suppliers.

Operated by the **Home Office** with delivery support from ADS Group.

Website: [securityandpolicing.co.uk](https://www.securityandpolicing.co.uk).

## What's typically on

- **Exhibitor zones** covering policing technology, counter-terrorism, cyber, surveillance, intelligence, forensic, identity, public-safety communications, and protective security.
- **Conference programme** featuring senior Home Office figures, police chiefs, intelligence-community speakers, and international counterparts.
- **Live demonstration areas** for capability that needs to be shown rather than described.
- **International delegation programme** including UK Defence and Security Exports (now [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments International (NA-Intl)|NA-Intl]]).
- **NPSA presence** alongside the [[CRM/organisations/government/National Protective Security Authority|National Protective Security Authority]] for protective-security industry engagement.

## Why it matters for defence-sector SMEs with dual-use capability

Security and Policing is the closest thing the Home Office has to its own [[Intelligence/events/DSEI/About DSEI|DSEI]]: a curated, closed gathering of the homeland security customer base. Relevant for any defence-tech SME with dual-use capability that touches policing, counter-terrorism, surveillance, intelligence, identity, or protective security. The vetted-access model means the audience is qualified.

The [[CRM/organisations/government/Accelerated Capability Environment (ACE)|Accelerated Capability Environment (ACE)]] (Home Office innovation accelerator) and the wider Homeland Security Group are typically present.
