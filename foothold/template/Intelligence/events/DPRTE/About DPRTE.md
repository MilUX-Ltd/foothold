# DPRTE — Defence Procurement, Research, Technology and Exportability

DPRTE is the UK's principal **MOD-engaged annual procurement conference**, focused on opening up MOD supply-chain opportunities to SMEs. The main expo is at **Farnborough**, and regional summits run through the year.

| **DPRTE Expo 2027** (main event) | **23 to 24 March 2027** | **Farnborough** |
| Scottish Defence Procurement & Supply Chain Summit 2027 | 26 May 2027 | Glasgow |
| Defence Estates, Infrastructure & Procurement Summit 2027 | 8 June 2027 | Birmingham |

Operated by **BiP Solutions** with strong MOD and prime-contractor engagement. DPRTE positions itself as the place where MOD procurement officers, prime-contractor supply-chain teams, and SME suppliers physically meet.

Website: [dprte.co.uk](https://www.dprte.co.uk).

## What's typically on

- **MOD plenaries** with senior MOD commercial, [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|NA-M / DE&S]], [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|UK Defence Innovation (UKDI, formerly DASA)]], and front-line command speakers.
- **Meet-the-buyer programme** brokering structured meetings between SMEs and primes' supply-chain teams.
- **Streams** by procurement category: digital and ICT, equipment support, complex weapons, naval, air, land, infrastructure.
- **Innovation pitch sessions** for SMEs to demo capability against MOD-set problem areas.
- **Conference and exhibition** running side-by-side with content tracks.
- **Regional summits** (Manchester, Glasgow, Birmingham in 2026 to 2027) to broaden reach beyond the main event.

## Why it matters for defence-sector SMEs

DPRTE is arguably the single most efficient annual event for an SME looking to engage MOD procurement at scale. The meet-the-buyer format is structured around pre-arranged appointments rather than ad-hoc networking, and the content programme reflects the current MOD commercial agenda (Defence Industrial Strategy, the MOD SME Action Plan, [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Frameworks Index|frameworks]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|DSP]] registration, Procurement Act implications).

[[CRM/organisations/trade bodies/techUK|techUK]] runs panels at DPRTE and uses the event as a key touchpoint for its Defence and Security SME Forum membership.
