# DPRTE — Defence Procurement, Research, Technology and Exportability

DPRTE is the UK's principal **MOD-engaged annual procurement conference**, focused on opening up MOD supply-chain opportunities to SMEs. Held annually, usually in **Cardiff**, with parallel online and regional satellite events.

Operated by **BiP Solutions** with strong MOD and prime-contractor engagement. DPRTE positions itself as the place where MOD procurement officers, prime-contractor supply-chain teams, and SME suppliers physically meet.

Website: [dprte.co.uk](https://www.dprte.co.uk).

## What's typically on

- **MOD plenaries** with senior MOD commercial, [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|NA-M / DE&S]], [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|UKDI / DASA]], and front-line command speakers.
- **Meet-the-buyer programme** brokering structured meetings between SMEs and primes' supply-chain teams.
- **Streams** by procurement category: digital and ICT, equipment support, complex weapons, naval, air, land, infrastructure.
- **Innovation pitch sessions** for SMEs to demo capability against MOD-set problem areas.
- **Conference and exhibition** running side-by-side with content tracks.
- **Regional editions** in some years to broaden reach beyond the Cardiff venue.

## Why it matters for defence-sector SMEs

DPRTE is arguably the single most efficient annual event for an SME looking to engage MOD procurement at scale. The meet-the-buyer format is structured around pre-arranged appointments rather than ad-hoc networking, and the content programme reflects the current MOD commercial agenda (Defence Industrial Strategy, [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Frameworks Index|frameworks]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|DSP]] registration, Procurement Act implications).

[[CRM/organisations/trade bodies/techUK|techUK]] runs panels at DPRTE and uses the event as a key touchpoint for its Defence and Security SME Forum membership.
