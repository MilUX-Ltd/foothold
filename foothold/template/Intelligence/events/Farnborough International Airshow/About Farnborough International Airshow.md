# Farnborough International Airshow

The Farnborough International Airshow is one of the world's two flagship aerospace and defence trade shows (alongside the Paris Air Show). Held **biennially in even years** at **Farnborough, Hampshire**, the airshow combines a major industry trade exhibition, a flying display, a procurement-and-investment programme, and a public weekend.

Run by **Farnborough International Limited (FIL)**, a wholly-owned subsidiary of [[CRM/organisations/trade bodies/ADS Group|ADS Group]].

Website: [farnboroughairshow.com](https://www.farnboroughairshow.com).

## What's typically on

- **Trade days** (Monday to Friday): senior industry, defence, and government attendance. Major procurement deals and supplier appointments are often announced at the show.
- **Public weekend**: live flying display, public exhibitor access.
- **Space Zone**: dedicated UK Space Agency-led space technology zone, increasingly prominent.
- **Innovation and Investment Programme**: meet-the-buyer sessions, SME-focused engagement, an investor track.
- **Major government engagement**: UK Prime Minister, Defence Secretary, and senior ministers from partner nations routinely attend.

## Defence and security overlap

Although marketed as an aerospace show, the defence dimension is substantial: military aircraft procurement (Tempest / GCAP, F-35, MQ-9B, Apache, Tornado / Typhoon successors), defence-aerospace primes (BAE Systems, Rolls-Royce, Leonardo UK, MBDA), and the wider UK aerospace defence supply chain. The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/TEMPEST (GCAP)|TEMPEST / GCAP]] partnership announcements (UK / Italy / Japan) and similar international defence agreements typically surface at Farnborough.

## Why it matters for defence-sector SMEs

Farnborough is the principal aerospace-defence networking moment of the UK calendar. ADS members (including SMEs) can access supplier pavilion options, meet-the-buyer sessions, and the wider trade-association programme. The South Central regional defence cluster ([[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/clusters/South Central Regional Defence and Security Cluster|SCRDSC]]) typically runs side events around the show given the Farnborough location.
