# Defence Landscape Index

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/<bucket>/`. This index, and the topical pages it links to, are navigation surfaces over those entities. Don't put entity-specific content here; put it on the entity's CRM page and link.

A model of the UK defence innovation and procurement landscape in 2026, in the wake of Defence Reform and the Strategic Defence Review. Surfaces the entities and routes a defence-technology SME needs to navigate.

The original framing of this landscape as the "Valley of Death" came from inink Ltd's [Hitchhiker's Guide to the Valley of Death](https://inink.io/hitchhikers-guide-to-the-valley-of-death/) by James Pryor and Gabrielle Brooks. Each Foothold page that draws on that framing is sourced from public material and decomposed into individual entity pages under CRM plus topical maps here.

## Key Concepts

**The Valley of Death.** The gap between successful innovation or demonstration and actual procurement or deployment in defence. SMEs often struggle to cross this gap due to the complexity of MOD procurement, long timelines, and the challenge of navigating from a DASA competition win to a funded programme of record.

**Defence Reform and the Strategic Defence Review (SDR).** Reshaping the landscape significantly in 2025-2026. Many organisations are being restructured, merged, or created. The major structural change is the establishment of the **National Armaments Director Group (NAD Group)** in March 2025, consolidating DE&S, Dstl, DIO, Defence Digital, Defence Support, and four new functional areas under a single enterprise. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/MOD Structure|MOD Structure]].

### Programme-to-SME engagement

SMEs engage with named programmes through:

1. **Subcontracting** to prime contractors.
2. **DASA competitions** themed around programme capability gaps.
3. **BattleLab experiments** against programme requirements.
4. **Framework call-offs** — see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Frameworks Index|Frameworks Index]].

## Where entities live

The defence landscape includes a lot of entities (organisations, networks, clusters) as well as mechanisms (frameworks, portals, programmes). Entities have relationships, contacts, and engagement history; they belong in `CRM/`, not here.

- **Regional Defence and Security Clusters** live at [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/Regional Defence and Security Clusters|CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/]] because each cluster is an entity with members, events, and (optionally) your own engagement history.
- **MOD bodies** (Dstl, DASA, the NA-* National Armaments structure, etc.) live at `CRM/organisations/mod/` for the same reason.
- **Defence companies** live at `CRM/organisations/defence companies/`.

## Discipline

Anything on these pages must be sourceable from a public place: gov.uk, MOD website, company website, LinkedIn public profile, published report. If you've learned something through a private relationship, it belongs in `CRM/`, not here.

When MOD bodies restructure (which happens), use the parent-folder pattern in `CRM/organisations/mod/`: a folder named after the parent organisation, with renamed children inside carrying both their new acronymic title and a `former_name:` frontmatter entry. Keeps search continuity intact.
