# Defence Landscape Index

The defence landscape, mapped from public sources. This page is a map, not an entity home. Each entity that matters to your work should also have a CRM entry under `CRM/organisations/`, with engagement context. The pages here carry only what's publicly known.

## Sections

- **[[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Clusters/Clusters Index|Clusters]].** Regional defence and security clusters across the UK. Member-organisation networks supported by DASA and the MoD. Useful entry point for SMEs looking to find peers, sponsors, and routes to market in their region.
- **MOD Bodies** (to come). The National Armaments structure (NA-Intl, NA-C&I, NA-P&P, NA-Innov, NA-M, NA-C, NA-L&S, NA-D&D, DIO, NADE) and the visible child organisations.
- **Frameworks** (to come). UK gov procurement frameworks relevant to defence work: DOS, G-Cloud, EDP, NGAP, and others.
- **Programmes** (to come). Publicly visible defence programmes: TEMPEST/GCAP, SKYNET, ZODIAC, MORPHEUS, and others.
- **Portals** (to come). MOD-facing acquisition portals.

## Discipline

Anything on these pages must be sourceable from a public place: gov.uk, MOD website, company website, LinkedIn public profile, published report. If you've learned something through a private relationship, it belongs in `CRM/`, not here.

When MOD bodies restructure (which happens), use the parent-folder pattern: a folder named after the parent organisation, with renamed children inside carrying both their new acronymic title and a `former_name:` frontmatter entry. Keeps search continuity intact.
