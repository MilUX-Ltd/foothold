# Founder's Procurement Playbook

The landscape pages tell you what exists. This page tells you which route fits your situation. It is a decision layer over the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Frameworks Index|Frameworks]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Portals Index|Portals]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Programmes Index|Programmes]] catalogues; the detail stays on those pages.

Everything here is sourceable from public material. Where the landscape shifts (and in 2026 it is shifting), gov.uk wins over this page; update this page when it does.

## The 2026 situation, in brief

Defence Reform has consolidated who buys and who funds. The **NAD Group** owns capability delivery; see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/MOD Structure|MOD Structure]] for how it is organised.

- **Innovation has one front door: UK Defence Innovation (UKDI).** Launched 1 July 2025, it absorbed DASA (formally part of UKDI from February 2026) and has a ringfenced budget of at least £400m a year (£1.6bn to 2030). The **Defence Open Call has not reopened**: UKDI is building a new "Innovating for Defence" route, with no date yet. The **Security Open Call is open**. **Defence Innovation Loans are paused.** See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI: How to Engage]].
- **SMEs have their own office.** The **Defence Office for Small Business Growth (DOSBG)** has been open since January 2026 and leads the **MOD SME Action Plan** (21 July 2026): an additional **£2.5bn of MOD spend with SMEs by summer 2028**, a 50% increase, refreshed SME Champions with the primes, and a promised public guide to MOD entry points and portals.
- **Faster contracts for new entrants.** **Commercial X** can place contracts up to £50m and under two years; **Defence Unicorns** offers up to £100m over four years for firms with little or no MOD business. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Commercial X and Defence Unicorns|Commercial X and Defence Unicorns]].
- **The money is set out.** The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] (30 June 2026) is the four-year demand signal: over £5bn for uncrewed and autonomous systems, £1.8bn for the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Digital Targeting Web|Digital Targeting Web]], and at least 10% of the equipment budget on novel technology. It also cancelled or replaced programmes (see the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Programmes Index|Programmes Index]]).

Practical consequence: the names on last year's guidance are stale. Start from the UKDI [live funding page](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/live-funding-opportunities) and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Innovation Organisations|Innovation Organisations]] before you plan around a route.

## Three questions before any route

1. **What are you actually selling?** R&D effort, a product, or a service? The routes are different and mixing them wastes bids.
2. **How mature is it?** Be honest about TRL. Assessors will be.
3. **Who is the customer with money?** "The MOD" is not an answer. A programme, a Command, a framework buyer, or a prime is.

## Route by maturity

### TRL 1 to 4: you need funded R&D

Your buyer is an innovation funder, not a procurer.

- **UKDI themed competitions and Open Calls** (formerly DASA). Typically 100% funded, themed around capability gaps. The Security Open Call is open (up to £350k, TRL 6 to 7); the Defence Open Call has not reopened. Ask for a UKDI Innovation Partner, who will contact you within 15 working days. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI: How to Engage]].
- **[[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Taskforce RAID|Taskforce RAID]]** if you have AI with near-term operational effect.
- **[[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Innovate UK Contracts for Innovation|Innovate UK Contracts for Innovation]]** for dual-use and civil-adjacent work.
- **[[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Serapis|Serapis]]** and **[[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/R-Cloud|R-Cloud]]** if you can deliver S&T research for Dstl, often via a lot-holding prime.
- **NATO DIANA** challenge calls for international reach. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/NATO and International|NATO and International]].

What this route does not do: buy your product. A competition win funds a phase, nothing more. Plan the exploitation route (below) from day one.

### TRL 5 to 7: you need proof in a defence setting

Your problem is evidence and access, not (only) money.

- **[[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence BattleLab|Defence BattleLab]]** for rapid experimentation against real requirements.
- **Programme-adjacent experimentation.** Programmes like [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/ASGARD|ASGARD]] have shown the MOD will pull capability through in months, not years, when the operational need is sharp. Watch programme pages for industry engagement days; see the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Programmes Index|Programmes Index]].
- **[[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Defence Technology Exploitation Programme (DTEP)|DTEP]]** grants for collaborative projects with a higher-tier supplier, which buys you a prime relationship as well as funding.
- **Defence Innovation Loans** (£100k to £1m, below-market rate) bridged development between grant phases without giving up equity. They are **paused** while UKDI builds a new service; do not plan on one until they reopen.
- **The [[CRM/organisations/mod/T&E Marketplace|T&E Marketplace]] and the Uncrewed Systems Centre** in Swindon if you need test and evaluation evidence; see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Centres of Excellence|Centres of Excellence]].

### TRL 8 plus: you are ready to sell

Now you are in procurement proper. There are three doors, plus the rules that apply if a contract comes to you without competition.

- **Frameworks and dynamic markets.** The fastest legitimate route if what you sell fits a lot. Digital and cloud: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/G-Cloud|G-Cloud]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Digital Outcomes and Specialists (DOS)|DOS]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Technology Services (TCS)|Technology Services]]. Engineering: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Engineering Delivery Partner (EDP)|EDP]]. Dynamic markets stay open for joiners; see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Dynamic Markets|Dynamic Markets]]. If you are new to the MOD, the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Commercial X Dynamic Market (DMP)|Commercial X Dynamic Market]] is the lowest-friction way to see innovation competitions.
- **Open tenders.** Everything over £10k is advertised on the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal]] (free to register). Sweep [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Contracts Finder|Contracts Finder]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Find a Tender Service|Find a Tender]] for the wider public sector, and the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/MOD Acquisition Pipeline|MOD Acquisition Pipeline]] to see what is coming before it lands.
- **Sole-source contracts.** If you win one above the threshold, the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Single-Source Contract Rules|Single-Source Contract Rules]] set your price and profit; the incentive cap rose from 2% to 10% in June 2026.
- **Subcontracting to primes.** For most SMEs this is where the first real revenue comes from. Register on [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/JOSCAR|JOSCAR]] and the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Helios SME Portal|Helios SME Portal]] so prime supply-chain teams can find you, then pursue the primes attached to the programmes you fit (each programme page lists its industrial partners).

### Selling services rather than product

Skip the innovation funnel entirely. [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Digital Outcomes and Specialists (DOS)|DOS]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Digital and IT Professional Services (DIPS)|DIPS]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Engineering Delivery Partner (EDP)|EDP]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Aurora Provider Network|Aurora]] are where defence buys people and outcomes. Associate work through established framework holders is the fastest first pound.

## Route by timeline

- **Revenue inside 6 months.** Subcontract or associate to a prime or framework holder; respond to live DSP tenders you already fit. Nothing else pays this fast.
- **6 to 18 months.** Themed competitions, BattleLab, DTEP. Budget for the bid-to-award gap.
- **18 months plus.** Programmes of record and framework positions you build towards. This is where the durable business is, and where the Valley of Death lives: a competition win is not a programme slot.

## Hygiene: do these regardless of route

1. **Register on the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal]]** and set alerts. Free.
2. **Get discoverable**: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/JOSCAR|JOSCAR]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Helios SME Portal|Helios]].
3. **Cyber Essentials, early.** Since November 2025 all MOD contracts carrying DEFCON 658 require compliance with Def Stan 05-138 Issue 4; Cyber Essentials is the floor and Cyber Essentials Plus is required at moderate risk levels and above, with the Defence Cyber Certification scheme phasing in on top. The `cyber-essentials-ready` skill in this pack gets you moving.
4. **Know your export-control position** before you pitch internationally or hire non-UK nationals onto controlled work. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Export Controls Orientation|Export Controls Orientation]].
5. **Understand clearance lead times** before you promise delivery: SC realistically runs one to three months or more, DV six to nine. Contracts needing cleared people are won by companies that already have them, so sequence hiring accordingly. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Security Clearance Primer|Security Clearance Primer]].
6. **Track everything in the vault.** Opportunities are engagements in `Customer Engagements/scoping/`; the organisations behind them belong in `CRM/`.

## Common failure modes

- **Treating a UKDI (formerly DASA) win as a sale.** It funds a phase. Exploitation needs a named programme, framework or prime pull, planned from the start.
- **Single-threading.** One route, one buyer, one champion. Run at least two routes in parallel; champions post out every two years.
- **Bidding everything.** A tender you cannot evidence is a donation of your week to the eventual winner. Qualify hard: incumbent? budget? do we meet the cyber and clearance floor? The `eligibility-check` skill in this pack runs that gate.
- **Ignoring the pipeline.** The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/MOD Acquisition Pipeline|Acquisition Pipeline]] tells you what is coming. Positioning starts twelve months before the tender drops.
