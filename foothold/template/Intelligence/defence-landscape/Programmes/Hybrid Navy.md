# Hybrid Navy

## Summary

The Hybrid Navy is the Royal Navy's move to a fleet of crewed ships networked with uncrewed surface and undersea vessels. The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] (30 June 2026) made it the centre of the maritime plan. It **replaces both the Type 83 destroyer and Type 32 frigate concepts** with a hybrid fleet of uncrewed and crewed vessels, driven by the Royal Navy with the NAD Group.

For a founder, this is a large new maritime opening for SMEs: autonomy, sensors, payloads, communications, AI and the data links that join uncrewed vessels to crewed ones.

## What the fleet will contain

| Element | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Type 91** | Uncrewed missile platforms |
| **Type 92** | Uncrewed underwater sense platforms (anti-submarine work in the North Atlantic) |
| **Type 93** | Extra-large uncrewed underwater vessels (XLUUV), working with crewed submarines |
| **Type 94** | Uncrewed radar platforms (scanning for air threats) |
| **Common Combat Vessel (CCV)** | A future crewed class at the centre of the Hybrid Navy; at least six planned in 2030 to 2035, acting as the "central brain" that controls the uncrewed elements |
| **Maritime Fighting Web** | The network joining the Hybrid Navy; the maritime "edge" of the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Digital Targeting Web|Digital Targeting Web]] |
| **Project PANTHEON** | Autonomous systems to start a Hybrid Carrier Air Wing, with trials of jet-powered drones alongside F-35B under Project VANQUISH |

From the early 2030s, Type 91 and Type 94 platforms, controlled from a Common Combat Vessel, will form a Hybrid Navy Maritime Air Defence system that will eventually replace the Type 45 destroyers, due to retire from 2035.

## Money and milestones

- **At least £1.3bn** in the hybrid fleet over FY26/27 to FY29/30 (the plan's maritime table shows **£1.5bn** for the Hybrid Navy line in total).
- **Almost £240m** in Project PANTHEON. The plan gives this figure as "almost £250m" in one place and "almost £240m" in another.
- **By 2030**: the aim is to bring the first large autonomous vessels into service, including a prototype uncrewed missile platform and XLUUVs, with payloads developed through [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/AUKUS Pillar 2|AUKUS Pillar 2]].
- **2030 to 2035**: scale the Hybrid Navy to its full vision and build at least six Common Combat Vessels.

Related lines in the same plan: £1.1bn for the next phase of the autonomous Mine Hunting Capability (jointly procured with France), additional seabed warfare and critical undersea infrastructure protection, and Project BEEHIVE uncrewed surface vessels for the Royal Navy and Royal Marines (see the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Programmes Index|Programmes Index]]).

## How to engage

There is no single Hybrid Navy front door. The practical routes are:

- **The Royal Navy's innovation office**, the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office|Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office (DCTO)]], which absorbed NavyX.
- **UK Defence Innovation** competitions on autonomy and maritime themes. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI: How to Engage]].
- **Primes and yards** bidding the uncrewed vessel and Common Combat Vessel work.
