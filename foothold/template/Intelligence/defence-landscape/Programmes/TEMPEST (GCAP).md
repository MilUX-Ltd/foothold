# TEMPEST (GCAP)

## Summary

**Tempest** is the UK's future combat air programme — a next-generation fighter aircraft. Subsumed into the **Global Combat Air Programme (GCAP)** as a joint UK, Italy, and Japan effort. A major UK defence investment involving the principal UK industrial partners (BAE Systems, Rolls-Royce, Leonardo, MBDA) alongside Italian and Japanese industrial partners under GCAP.

## Industrial partners (UK)

- BAE Systems
- Rolls-Royce
- Leonardo UK
- MBDA

## International partners

- Italy
- Japan (under the GCAP intergovernmental agreement)
- Canada joined as the first **Observer Nation** on 21 July 2026. It has no decision-making role, but the status gives it privileged access and opens talks on future participation.

## 2026 position

- **£4.6bn international contract.** Signed on 3 July 2026 with the industry joint venture **Edgewing**, through the GCAP Agency, and funded jointly by the three nations. It advances the next stage of the aircraft's design.
- **In service target: 2035.**
- **UK funding.** The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] commits **£8.6bn** to GCAP over FY26/27 to FY29/30, plus £300m to start a UK Collaborative Combat Aircraft (autonomous jet) programme that draws on GCAP partner technology.
- **Supply chain.** About 600 organisations are in the supply chain, and the wider future combat air system supports about 4,500 jobs.

## Where a founder fits

The supply chain of about 600 organisations is the realistic entry: subcontract to the UK industrial partners above. The new £300m Collaborative Combat Aircraft line is aimed at autonomous jets and is a likelier fit for autonomy and AI firms than the core aircraft.
