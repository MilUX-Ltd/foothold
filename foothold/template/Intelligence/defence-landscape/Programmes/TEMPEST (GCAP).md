# TEMPEST (GCAP)

## Summary

**Tempest** is the UK's future combat air programme — a next-generation fighter aircraft. Subsumed into the **Global Combat Air Programme (GCAP)** as a joint UK, Italy, and Japan effort. A major UK defence investment involving the principal UK industrial partners (BAE Systems, Rolls-Royce, Leonardo, MBDA) alongside Italian and Japanese industrial partners under GCAP.

## Industrial partners (UK)

- BAE Systems
- Rolls-Royce
- Leonardo UK
- MBDA

## International partners

- Italy
- Japan (under the GCAP intergovernmental agreement)
