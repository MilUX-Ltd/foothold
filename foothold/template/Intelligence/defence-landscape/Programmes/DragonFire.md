# DragonFire

## Summary

DragonFire is the UK's Laser Directed Energy Weapon (LDEW) capability demonstrator, developed under a Dstl-led consortium and on track for first Royal Navy deployment from 2027. The system operates in the 50 kW class and uses a spectral beam-combining architecture to merge multiple glass-fibre laser sources into a single beam with near diffraction-limited quality. Use cases include very short-range air defence, close-in protection for naval vessels, counter-UAV defence, and protection against mortar and artillery attack.

## Consortium and funding

The **UK DragonFire** consortium consists of Dstl (lead), MBDA UK, Leonardo UK, and QinetiQ. The original Dstl contract was awarded in January 2017 (£30 million to UK industry). Total commitment to the programme is around £100 million as of 2026.

## Timeline

- **2017**: Dstl contract awarded; first public unveiling at DSEI as a technology demonstrator.
- **2024**: Royal Navy fielding plan announced for 2027.
- **12 April 2026**: Accelerated rollout plan announced. First installation planned on a Type 45 destroyer, with at least two Type 45s expected to receive the system in the initial phase.
