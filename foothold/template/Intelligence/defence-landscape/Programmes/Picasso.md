# Picasso

## Summary

Develops and sustains the UK's national Geospatial Intelligence (GEOINT) ICT capabilities. Scope includes access to allied imagery, platform-agnostic exploitation systems for sovereign GEOINT collection, and production of maps, air charts, and foundation geospatial information.
