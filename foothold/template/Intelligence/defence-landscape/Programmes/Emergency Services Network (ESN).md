# Emergency Services Network (ESN)

## Summary

The Emergency Services Network (ESN) is an LTE radio communications network under development by the Home Office to provide unified communications for British emergency services. ESN is intended to replace the existing TETRA-based **Airwave** network. It includes a first-of-a-kind 'push-to-talk' capability layered onto a commercial LTE bearer with priority access and extended coverage.

## Status and supplier history

- **2015**: Home Office contracts with EE (now part of BT) for priority access on its mobile network, and with Motorola Solutions UK for software and systems including push-to-talk.
- **February 2016**: Motorola acquires the incumbent Airwave network, creating a dual-role conflict.
- **October 2021**: Competition and Markets Authority opens investigation into Motorola's dual role.
- **December 2022**: Home Office and Motorola agree to end Motorola's ESN involvement early.
- **January 2025**: Home Office announces IBM will take over delivery of user services.
- **2026**: ESN remains under development; the Home Office forecasts £2 billion spent since 2015 and Airwave remains operational.
