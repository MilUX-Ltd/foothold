# Future Flight Challenge

## Summary

The Future Flight Challenge supports the introduction of drones, advanced air mobility (AAM) vehicles, and zero-carbon regional aircraft into the UK. The main run (Phases 1 to 3) operated from 2019 to 2024 under the UKRI Industrial Strategy Challenge Fund, with **£300 million total funding** (£125m public investment, £175m industry co-funding). A further one-year set of activities is running in 2025/26 funded by Innovate UK and the Department for Transport, with £4.4 million supporting 14 innovative aviation projects.

The programme is building the aviation ecosystem (regulators, infrastructure, airspace integration) needed for electric sub-regional aircraft, AAM vehicles, and drones at scale.
