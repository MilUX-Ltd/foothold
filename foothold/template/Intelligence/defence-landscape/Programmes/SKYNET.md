# SKYNET

## Summary

The UK military satellite communications programme. Provides strategic and tactical satellite communications to UK and allied forces worldwide. A long-running cross-service capability programme.

## Current position (Defence Investment Plan 2026)

- **SKYNET 5** is the in-service constellation. The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] adds **£50m** to extend its life.
- **SKYNET 6A**, the next satellite, is still due to launch in **2027**. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/SKYNET 6A|SKYNET 6A]].
- **SKYNET 6 Narrowband Satellite System: not proceeding.** The plan offsets this by extending SKYNET 5 and using different procurement routes, including "space as a service" options alongside allies.

For a founder, "space as a service" is the line to watch: it points to buying capacity rather than owning every satellite, which widens the door for commercial providers.
