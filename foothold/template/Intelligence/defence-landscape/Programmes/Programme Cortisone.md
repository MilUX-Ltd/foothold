# Programme Cortisone

## Summary

Programme Cortisone is the MOD programme to digitise Defence Medical Services (DMS) healthcare information. Addresses the need to record, manage, and exploit all healthcare information generated by DMS when delivering patient-focused healthcare.

**GOV.UK:** [Programme Cortisone](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/programme-cortisone)

## Programme Goals

Provide a sustainable, integrated, cohesive, and enduring Medical Information Services (MedIS) capability that fully and effectively supports the delivery of evidence-based medical and dental health and healthcare outputs across DMS.

## Current Status (2026)

In the **Delivery Phase**:

- **TPP** — awarded the main digital medical records contract. Introducing a modern, interoperable digital medical record system for recruitment and ongoing care of service personnel.
- **Avenue3** — awarded £2.5M for **Project Mercury**, a Deployed Clinical Record system enabling clinicians to access military medical records anywhere in the world, including offline via NFC technology. First live release Spring 2026.
- Currently procuring a **Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS)** for DMS deployments and operations.
