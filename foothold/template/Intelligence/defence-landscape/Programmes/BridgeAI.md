# BridgeAI

## Summary

Innovate UK BridgeAI is a £100 million programme launched in April 2023 to accelerate the adoption of artificial intelligence and machine learning across UK sectors with high growth potential but low AI maturity. Priority sectors include agriculture and food processing, construction, creative industries, and transport (logistics and warehousing). The programme is delivered by a consortium: **Innovate UK, Digital Catapult, The Alan Turing Institute, STFC Hartree Centre, and BSI**.

## Offer

- Funded competitions through the Innovation Funding Service.
- Connection to AI experts via Innovate UK Business Connect.
- AI leadership training and upskilling for priority sectors.
- Standards work through BSI (responsible and ethical AI practices).
