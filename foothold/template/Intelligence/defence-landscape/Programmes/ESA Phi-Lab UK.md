# ESA Phi-Lab UK

## Summary

ESA's Φ-lab (Phi-lab) is the European Space Agency's transformative-innovation initiative within its Directorate of Earth Observation Programmes, focused on disruptive technologies and frontier development for future Earth-observation missions. The Phi-Lab network has a national branch in the UK (Phi-Lab UK), operating with UKSA support to channel UK-led innovative ideas into the wider ESA Phi-Lab and into Earth-observation mission planning.
