# AUKUS Pillar 2

## Summary

AUKUS Pillar 2 is the advanced-capabilities limb of the trilateral AUKUS security partnership between the **United States, United Kingdom, and Australia**. Pillar 1 covers nuclear-powered submarines; Pillar 2 covers a suite of cooperative activities to develop and field "advanced capabilities" and to integrate the three nations' defence science, technology, and industrial bases.

## Technology areas

- **Undersea capabilities** (including the AUKUS Undersea Robotics Autonomous Systems / AURAS project).
- **Quantum technologies**.
- **Artificial intelligence and autonomy**.
- **Advanced cyber**.
- **Hypersonic and counter-hypersonic capabilities**.
- **Electronic warfare**.

## Named initiatives

- **Deep Space Advanced Radar Capability (DARC)**: announced December 2023. Three radar sites planned, providing 24-hour continuous all-weather global deep-space coverage. First site in Western Australia targeted to be operational in 2026; further sites in the US and UK to be in service by the end of the decade.
- **AURAS (AUKUS Undersea Robotics Autonomous Systems)**: autonomous underwater vehicles work.

## 2026 developments

- **First signature project (30 May 2026).** AUKUS defence ministers announced the inaugural Pillar 2 signature project: payloads (sensors and weapons) and enabling systems for **uncrewed undersea vehicles**, usable across all three nations' fleets. The first capabilities are expected in service by 2027, starting with detecting threats to critical undersea infrastructure. Alongside it, four winners of the 2025 AUKUS Maritime Innovation Challenge (three UK firms, including an SME and a micro-consultancy) share £3 million.
- **Project Bowline (21 July 2026).** A £20 million hypersonic target, run under Pillar 2 and contracted to Lockheed Martin UK, to test air defence systems against threats at around Mach 5. Fifteen UK SMEs benefit through the supply chain.
- **Defence Investment Plan.** The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] funds weapons and sensor payloads for underwater drones as the signature project, and says the first Hybrid Navy extra-large uncrewed underwater vessels will carry payloads developed through AUKUS. No separate Pillar 2 figure is published.

For a founder, Pillar 2 work reaches SMEs mainly through innovation challenges and prime supply chains, as both 2026 examples show.
