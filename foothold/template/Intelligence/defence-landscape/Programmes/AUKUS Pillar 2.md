# AUKUS Pillar 2

## Summary

AUKUS Pillar 2 is the advanced-capabilities limb of the trilateral AUKUS security partnership between the **United States, United Kingdom, and Australia**. Pillar 1 covers nuclear-powered submarines; Pillar 2 covers a suite of cooperative activities to develop and field "advanced capabilities" and to integrate the three nations' defence science, technology, and industrial bases.

## Technology areas

- **Undersea capabilities** (including the AUKUS Undersea Robotics Autonomous Systems / AURAS project).
- **Quantum technologies**.
- **Artificial intelligence and autonomy**.
- **Advanced cyber**.
- **Hypersonic and counter-hypersonic capabilities**.
- **Electronic warfare**.

## Named initiatives

- **Deep Space Advanced Radar Capability (DARC)**: announced December 2023. Three radar sites planned, providing 24-hour continuous all-weather global deep-space coverage. First site in Western Australia targeted to be operational in 2026; further sites in the US and UK to be in service by the end of the decade.
- **AURAS (AUKUS Undersea Robotics Autonomous Systems)**: autonomous underwater vehicles work.
