# Sustainable Aviation Fuel Innovation Programme

## Summary

The Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) Innovation Programme is the UK Department for Transport's funding route to build and accelerate a domestic SAF industry. It supports the DfT ambition to deliver **10% SAF in the UK jet fuel mix by 2030**, accelerating the testing, certification, and commercialisation of next-generation sustainable aviation fuels.

The Programme operates alongside related DfT funding instruments:

- **Advanced Fuels Fund (AFF)**: £198 million committed to date for scale-up of cleaner aviation fuels. Latest tranche awarded £63 million across 17 projects.
- **UK SAF Clearing House** (DfT-funded): provides cross-industry support for development, testing, qualification, and production of SAF, including guidance on funding access and connections to recognised testing facilities.
