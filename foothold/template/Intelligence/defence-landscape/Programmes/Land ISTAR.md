# Land ISTAR

## Summary

Land Intelligence, Surveillance, Target Acquisition and Reconnaissance programme. Delivers a suite of new sensor systems and enables a digitised Army by fusing data sources and providing capacity for effective and faster decision-making. At its core is [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/ZODIAC|ZODIAC]], the digital infrastructure backbone.
