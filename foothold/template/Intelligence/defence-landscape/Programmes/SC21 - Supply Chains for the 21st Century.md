# SC21 — Supply Chains for the 21st Century

## Summary

SC21 is a long-running continuous-improvement programme designed to raise the competitiveness of UK aerospace and defence supply chains. Launched in 2006, **managed by ADS** and endorsed by major UK aerospace and defence companies (BAE Systems, Ultra Electronics, Smiths Aerospace, Thales, and others). Over a thousand companies have participated.

## Operating model

- Scalable framework, applicable from SME to large OEM.
- Free diagnostic tools including the Baseline Assessment Tool (BAT).
- Structured improvement journey culminating in nationally recognised **Bronze, Silver, or Gold Performance Standard Awards**.
- Themes: competitiveness, sustainability, innovation, digitalisation.
- National strategy executed regionally (delivered via regional aerospace alliances).

Members benefit from improved On-Time-In-Full (OTIF) delivery performance, lead-time reduction, and better cash flow.
