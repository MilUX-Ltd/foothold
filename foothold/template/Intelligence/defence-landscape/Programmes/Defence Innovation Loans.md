# Defence Innovation Loans

## Summary

Defence Innovation Loans are a joint DASA / Innovate UK Loans Ltd funding instrument for **single SMEs** with solutions to defence-themed problems. Loans of **£100,000 to £1 million** are available at a below-market interest rate of **7.4% per annum**, covering up to 100% of eligible project costs to aid commercialisation. Individuals, academic institutions, research organisations and large companies are not eligible.

## 2025-26 cycle schedule (illustrative)

The FY 2025-26 programme runs in rolling cycles approximately every 6 to 8 weeks. Cycles 1 through 7 span March 2025 to May 2026, with decisions typically issued within 8 to 11 weeks of cycle close.

Applicants must have engaged DASA pre-application to confirm suitability for the Innovation Loans route.
