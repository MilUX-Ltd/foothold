# Defence Innovation Loans

> **Paused (October 2026).** UK Defence Innovation's live funding page says: "The Defence Innovation Loans cycles are currently paused while we work on a new, improved service." No relaunch date has been given. Check the [live funding page](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/live-funding-opportunities) before planning around a loan. The detail below describes the scheme as it ran in 2025-26.

## Summary

Defence Innovation Loans are a joint UK Defence Innovation (UKDI, formerly DASA) / Innovate UK Loans Ltd funding instrument for **single SMEs** with solutions to defence-themed problems. Loans of **£100,000 to £1 million** are available at a below-market interest rate of **7.4% per annum**, covering up to 100% of eligible project costs to aid commercialisation. Individuals, academic institutions, research organisations and large companies are not eligible.

## 2025-26 cycle schedule (historical)

The FY 2025-26 programme runs in rolling cycles approximately every 6 to 8 weeks. Cycles 1 through 7 span March 2025 to May 2026, with decisions typically issued within 8 to 11 weeks of cycle close.

Applicants had to engage DASA (now UKDI) before applying to confirm suitability for the Innovation Loans route. For advice now, use a [UKDI Innovation Partner](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-work-with-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi). See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI: How to Engage]].
