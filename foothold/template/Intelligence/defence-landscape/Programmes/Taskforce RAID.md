# Taskforce RAID

## Summary

The **Rapid AI Delivery Taskforce (TF RAID)** was announced on 10 June 2026 to get AI-enabled tools to the Armed Forces faster. It reports directly to the **Chief of the Defence Staff** and has exemptions from standard financial and procedural controls so it can move at the pace of the technology. Its stated test of success is operational advantage delivered, not technology demonstrated.

The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] gives it a ringfenced budget of **£100m over four years**. Work runs as sprints of six to nine months.

## What it is looking for

The four initial challenge areas on the TF RAID guidance page:

1. **Understanding and decision advantage**: AI for processing and analysing intelligence and supporting decisions.
2. **Electromagnetic and information advantage**: building situational understanding from many information sources.
3. **Planning and automation**: AI tools that speed up and improve military planning.
4. **Autonomous systems**: AI-enabled uncrewed capability at greater scale and pace, including swarms.

Related AI lines in the same plan: £100m for Project FRONTIER and other enablers (decision-support AI at all classifications) and £80m for Land AI C2.

## How to engage

- **Industry Front Door.** Submit an idea, concept or technology through the TF RAID Industry Front Door survey, linked from the [TF RAID guidance page](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rapid-ai-delivery-taskforce-tf-raid). It is for engagement and horizon scanning only: it is not a procurement or a competition, and TF RAID warns it may not reply to every submission. Where an idea fits current priorities, it will try to make contact.
- **Competitions.** Future opportunities are announced and run through normal commercial channels.
- **Live now: Avengers AI swarming competition.** The first competition under the UK–Ukraine AI partnership gives selected UK firms access to Ukraine's **Avengers Labs** platform, which holds imagery and footage of ground and aerial targets collected in Ukraine, to improve autonomous drone swarming and collaborative drone operations. Proposals should benefit both UK and Ukrainian forces. **Deadline: 11:59pm BST on 22 October 2026.** See the [competition page](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/tf-raid-avengers-ai-swarming-competition).

For a founder: TF RAID wants fielded effect within months. Lead with an operational problem from one of the four areas and evidence your product works, not a research roadmap.
