# Fleet Solid Support (FSS) Ships

## Summary

The Fleet Solid Support (FSS) programme delivers three ships to the Royal Fleet Auxiliary to provide underway replenishment of dry stores (ammunition, spare parts, supplies) to Royal Navy vessels. Each ship is 216m long, 34.5m wide, displaces 39,000 tonnes, has a maximum speed of 19 knots and a range of 11,000 nautical miles.

## Contract and consortium

DE&S awarded a £1.6 billion contract in January 2023 to **Team Resolute**, comprising Harland & Wolff (UK shipyards), BMT (UK ship design), and Navantia UK. Ship blocks and modules are built across Harland & Wolff facilities in Belfast and Appledore (Devon) and Navantia's Cadiz shipyard in Spain. Final assembly is exclusively at Harland & Wolff Belfast.

## Status (May 2026)

- **RFA Resurgent**, lead ship: steel cut 3 December 2025 at Appledore.
- First ship operational date: 2031 per MoD.

The programme is expected to generate 1,200 jobs in UK shipyards and a further 800 across the UK supply chain.
