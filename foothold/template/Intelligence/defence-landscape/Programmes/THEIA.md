# THEIA

## Summary

THEIA (pronounced "THAY-A") drives digital transformation in the British Army. Focuses on how the Army uses digitised information and digital technologies to improve operational and business decision-making.

## Objectives

- Change digital culture and behaviours.
- Integrate information from C2, intelligence, sensors, effectors, and platforms across domains.
- Deliver better data and enable operational competitiveness and efficiency.
