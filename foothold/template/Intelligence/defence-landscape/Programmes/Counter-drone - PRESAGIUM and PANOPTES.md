# Counter-drone: PRESAGIUM and PANOPTES

## Summary

Counter-drone is a large spending line in the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]]. The plan puts **over £750m** into short-range drone protection for the homeland and deployed forces and, in its land section, describes **£200m** of further investment in deployable counter-drone technology. This page gathers the named lines a founder can act on.

## The lines

| Line | What it is | Money and status |
|---|---|---|
| **Project PRESAGIUM** | An enhanced counter-drone system to detect and defeat emerging drone threats, operated by the RAF Regiment and wider Defence users. Builds on the success of Rapid Sentry | **£240m** in the plan |
| **Low-cost ground-based effectors** | A suite of cheap effectors such as **Skyhammer** | Part of the £750m+ |
| **Project PANOPTES** | A UK Defence Innovation themed competition for an integrated, autonomous counter-UAS system for force protection. Phase one of a planned multi-phase path towards a possible future programme of record; the main deliverable is an evidence-based programme plan | A share of **up to £5m**. Stage 1 closed at midday on 30 September 2026; Stage 2 is by invitation. On 7 October 2026 UKDI added revised dates because of the number of submissions, so check the competition page |
| **Low-Cost Air Defence Effectors (LCADE)** | Affordable interceptors against drones and other airborne threats, under the NAD Group's Low-Cost Effectors and Autonomous Platforms (LEAP) effort with Poland, France, Italy and Germany | **£3.16m** to three UK SMEs (Frankenburg Technologies, Greenjets, Cambridge Aerospace), 13 July 2026. The next phase looks for designs that can be mass-produced across the five nations |
| **Project VANAHEIM** | UK–US counter-drone trials run by the Army's RAPSTONE Task Force | See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Project VANAHEIM\|Project VANAHEIM]] |

## Where a founder fits

Counter-drone is unusually open to SMEs: the 2026 interceptor contracts went to three small firms, and PANOPTES drew enough submissions that UKDI revised its timetable. Sensing, classification, electronic warfare, low-cost effectors and the command-and-control that joins them are all live. Watch the UKDI live funding page: PANOPTES is planned as the first of several phases.
