# Eurofighter Typhoon

## Summary

The Eurofighter Typhoon is the multi-role combat aircraft jointly developed and operated by the UK, Germany, Italy, and Spain. The Royal Air Force is the lead customer for the UK fleet. The current focus of the UK programme is on capability upgrades (radar, weapons, mission systems) to keep the Tranche 3 fleet at the leading edge of contested-airspace combat through the late 2020s, as a bridge to [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/TEMPEST (GCAP)|TEMPEST (GCAP)]] entry into service.

## Active upgrade activity (2026)

- **ECRS Mk2 AESA radar**: £453.5 million contract awarded 22 January 2026 to BAE Systems, Leonardo UK, and Parker Meggitt for full-rate production of 40 ECRS Mk2 radars for Tranche 3 Typhoons. Provides step-change detection range, electronic-attack capability, and resilience in contested airspace.
- **QinetiQ engineering support**: £205 million five-year contract extension for mission-critical engineering and technical support, including ECRS Mk2 integration.
- **APKWS** (Advanced Precision Kill Weapon System) integration, announced 17 May 2026, developed by BAE Systems and QinetiQ. Already deployed operationally with 9 Squadron RAF Typhoons in the Middle East as a low-cost counter-drone capability.
- **Phase 4 Enhancement (P4E)**: four-nation upgrade (UK, Germany, Italy, Spain) in System Definition de-risking phase.
