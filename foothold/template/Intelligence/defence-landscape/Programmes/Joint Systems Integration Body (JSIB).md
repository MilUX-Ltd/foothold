# Joint Systems Integration Body (JSIB)

## Summary

JSIB is a partnering arrangement between **General Dynamics UK** and the MOD Tactical Systems (TacSys) Service Executive. Established in **2003**. Integrates BISAs (Battlefield Information System Applications) onto the Bowman ComBAT Infrastructure and Platform BISA (BCIP) infrastructure, assuring Land Environment Tactical Communications Information System (LE-TacCIS) integrity.

## Roles

- Integrates third-party Applications onto the BCIP infrastructure.
- In-service support, including bug resolution.
- System-of-System testing.
- Supports BCIP-to-non-BCIP interfaces.

## Benefits (as stated)

- One-stop shop for System Integration of Battlefield Applications.
- Works closely with BISA developers, MOD project teams, and BISA users — efficiencies of time, cost and performance.
- **JSP 604** Joining Rules compliance.
