# EUREKA SMART

## Summary

EUREKA SMART is a flexible industry-driven EUREKA Cluster on Advanced Manufacturing. Its objective is to promote collaborative, international, close-to-market R&D&I projects in advanced manufacturing and production, funded via decentralised national funding mechanisms. SMART is industry-led, with participation from large companies, SMEs, universities, and research organisations across multiple countries.

## UK funding support

Innovate UK announced in September 2022 that the UK would support the 6th SMART Cluster Call with **£5 million** of funding. Registered UK businesses are eligible to apply. Participation gives UK entities access to an international network of partners and additional funding opportunities through their respective national programmes.
