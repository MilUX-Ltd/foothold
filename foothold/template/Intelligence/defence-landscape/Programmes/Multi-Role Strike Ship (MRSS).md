# Multi-Role Strike Ship (MRSS)

## Summary

The Multi-Role Strike Ship (MRSS) is a planned class of up to six multi-mission amphibious warfare ships for the Royal Navy. The class replaces the Albion-class (HMS Albion, HMS Bulwark) and other ageing amphibious assets, and provides the seagoing platform for the Royal Navy's Future Commando Force doctrine. Each ship is to feature well docks for landing craft, hangars capable of accommodating Chinook helicopters, and a flight deck for helicopters and uncrewed systems.

The class was first officially mentioned in the 2021 UK defence white paper "Defence in a Competitive Age" (then under the "Multi-Role Support Ship" designation). In March 2025, Defence Minister Maria Eagle renamed the class **Multi-Role Strike Ship** to reflect its functionality as a combat ship with offensive and defensive weaponry.

## Timeline

- **March 2021**: First officially mentioned in the Defence Command Paper.
- **September 2023**: UK-Netherlands MoU signed exploring joint procurement under Project CATHERINA.
- **March 2024**: Project CATHERINA's main joint-design effort abandoned.
- **14 May 2024**: First phase approval announced at the Sea Power Conference, London.
- **March 2025**: Renamed from Multi-Role Support Ship to Multi-Role Strike Ship.
- **2033/34**: Planned in-service date (three ships initially, up to three more).
