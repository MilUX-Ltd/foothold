# Multi-Role Strike Ship (MRSS)

> **Cancelled (June 2026).** The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] says the MRSS programme "was too complex and did not reflect the UK Commando Force we are now pursuing", and pivots to the **Netherlands-led Amphibious Transport Ship Programme**. In July 2026 the UK and the Netherlands agreed a **£2.4bn** amphibious ship partnership: each nation will operate four ships, built in UK shipyards working alongside Dutch industry, replacing the capability of the Albion class. HMS Bulwark is transferring to the Brazilian Navy. The rest of this page is kept as history.

## What replaces it

- **Amphibious Transport Ship** (UK and Netherlands): about 160 metres and 15,000 tonnes, carrying troops, vehicles and equipment, including drones. No in-service date has been published.
- **Funding.** The plan allocates **£240m** to amphibious shipping over FY26/27 to FY29/30, as an initial investment during this Parliament, alongside Joint Commando Craft developed with Norway.

For a founder, the opening is in the equipment and uncrewed systems these ships will carry and in UK yard supply chains, rather than in the ship design itself.

## Summary (as planned before cancellation)

The Multi-Role Strike Ship (MRSS) was a planned class of up to six multi-mission amphibious warfare ships for the Royal Navy. The class was to replace the Albion-class (HMS Albion, HMS Bulwark) and other ageing amphibious assets, and to provide the seagoing platform for the Royal Navy's Future Commando Force doctrine. Each ship was to feature well docks for landing craft, hangars capable of accommodating Chinook helicopters, and a flight deck for helicopters and uncrewed systems.

The class was first officially mentioned in the 2021 UK defence white paper "Defence in a Competitive Age" (then under the "Multi-Role Support Ship" designation). In March 2025 the MOD renamed the class **Multi-Role Strike Ship** to reflect its functionality as a combat ship with offensive and defensive weaponry.

## Timeline

- **March 2021**: First officially mentioned in the Defence Command Paper.
- **September 2023**: UK-Netherlands MoU signed exploring joint procurement under Project CATHERINA.
- **March 2024**: Project CATHERINA's main joint-design effort abandoned.
- **14 May 2024**: First phase approval announced at the Sea Power Conference, London.
- **March 2025**: Renamed from Multi-Role Support Ship to Multi-Role Strike Ship.
- **2033/34**: Planned in-service date at the time (three ships initially, up to three more).
- **30 June 2026**: Cancelled in the Defence Investment Plan (paragraph 56); UK pivots to the Netherlands-led Amphibious Transport Ship Programme.
- **July 2026**: £2.4bn UK–Netherlands amphibious ship agreement.
