# NavyX

> **Consolidated (April 2025).** On 29 April 2025 the Royal Navy merged NavyX, the Office of the Chief Technology Officer and the Navy AI Cell into the **[[CRM/organisations/mod/Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office|Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office (DCTO)]]**, which sits in [[CRM/organisations/mod/Navy Develop|Navy Develop]]. The NavyX name is retired; approach DCTO instead. XV Patrick Blackett remains in use as the testbed. For where Royal Navy autonomy money now goes, see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Hybrid Navy|Hybrid Navy]]. The rest of this page is kept as history.

## Summary (historical)

NavyX was the Royal Navy's **Autonomy and Lethality Accelerator**, focused on introducing uncrewed and autonomous capabilities into the fleet. It ran experimentation, trials, and rapid integration of new technology aboard the testbed ship **XV Patrick Blackett** (a Damen 4008 Fast Crew Supply ship). It was an early delivery arm for what is now the **Hybrid Navy** approach of crewed, uncrewed and autonomous platforms.

## Work before consolidation

- **MADFOX** (Maritime Demonstrator For Operational eXperimentation), a 13m Uncrewed Surface Vessel. NavyX conducted the Navy's first USV trials with MADFOX, including a Switchblade 300 Tactical Missile System launch under remote control from ashore during the REPRUS exercise in September 2021.
- **APAC24** (Autonomous Pacific 24 RIB) development.
- **XV Patrick Blackett** entered service as the experimentation testbed.
- Anduril partnership announced for Royal Marines modernisation.
- £75m MoD boost to NavyX-aligned naval technology (2025).
