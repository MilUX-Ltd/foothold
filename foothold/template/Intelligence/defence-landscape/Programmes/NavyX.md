# NavyX

## Summary

NavyX is the Royal Navy's **Autonomy and Lethality Accelerator**, focused on introducing uncrewed and autonomous capabilities into the fleet. It runs experimentation, trials, and rapid integration of new technology aboard the testbed ship **XV Patrick Blackett** (a Damen 4008 Fast Crew Supply ship). NavyX is the Navy delivery arm of the wider **Hybrid Navy** doctrine combining crewed, uncrewed, and autonomous platforms.

## Active and recent work

- **MADFOX** (Maritime Demonstrator For Operational eXperimentation), a 13m Uncrewed Surface Vessel. NavyX conducted the Navy's first USV trials with MADFOX, including a Switchblade 300 Tactical Missile System launch under remote control from ashore during the REPRUS exercise in September 2021.
- **APAC24** (Autonomous Pacific 24 RIB) development.
- **XV Patrick Blackett** entered service as the experimentation testbed.
- Anduril partnership announced for Royal Marines modernisation.
- £75m MoD boost to NavyX-aligned naval technology (2025).
