# Digital Targeting Web

## Summary

The Digital Targeting Web (DTW) connects **sensors, deciders and effectors** across all domains so that the Armed Forces can find, decide on and strike targets faster than an adversary, including in contested cyber and electromagnetic conditions. The Strategic Defence Review recommended it; the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] funds it. It runs on the **Digital Backbone**, Defence's underlying connectivity and infrastructure.

## Money

| Line (FY26/27 to FY29/30) | Amount |
|---|---|
| Digital Targeting Web | **£1.8bn** |
| Digital Backbone | £5.5bn |

Among the plan's most significant DTW investments: **£740m** for connecting sensors, deciders and effectors (targeting, imagery exploitation, geospatial analysis, a **Defence-wide Secret Cloud** and resilient position, navigation and timing) and **£420m** for the Digital Backbone that supports a sovereign DTW. A minimum viable product of the Secret Cloud is due **later in 2026**. Between 2030 and 2035 the MOD plans to invest at least **£17bn** in the Digital Backbone and Targeting Web, taking DTW to full operating capability.

## The Challenge Book: the way in

Cyber & Specialist Operations Command, with UK Defence Innovation, published the **DTW Challenge Book 2026** on 20 March 2026. It turns user problems into challenges for industry and is the basis of engagement for 12 months, with quarterly iterations. The MOD's routes-to-market page lists it as open to new entrants. The six challenges:

1. Securely sourcing and sharing data
2. Combining data sources to produce information and generate insight
3. Building knowledge from information and making decisions faster
4. Providing the Digital Targeting Web at the edge
5. Upgrading, integrating and interoperating at pace
6. Providing skills, training and tools

Its guiding principles include workflow-driven change, building on existing assets, "assume automation; prove trust", data-centric design, open and modular architecture, and security by design.

For a founder: read the challenge that matches your product, then frame your offer in its language. Data integration, fusion, AI decision support, edge computing in degraded networks and training tools are all named. The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/ASGARD|ASGARD]] (Army) and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Hybrid Navy|Hybrid Navy]] Maritime Fighting Web lines connect to it.
