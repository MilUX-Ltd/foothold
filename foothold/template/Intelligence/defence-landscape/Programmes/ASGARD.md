# ASGARD

## Summary

Army digitisation and data programme. ASGARD is the British Army's flagship Transformative Capability Initiative (TCI) inside the Chief of the General Staff's Growth Through Transformation strategy. The main delivery mechanism for ASGARD is the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/DDAD|DDAD]] Open Framework.

ASGARD sits alongside other Army digital programmes (THEIA, Land ISTAR, ZODIAC) in the broader Army digital transformation effort.

## Funding (Defence Investment Plan 2026)

The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] (30 June 2026) puts **£370m** into ASGARD over FY26/27 to FY29/30 to "develop and expand the Army's new 'Recce-Strike' approach", which the plan says is already in use by the Forward Land Force in Estonia. ASGARD links existing capabilities such as armoured vehicles with drones and other new technology. Among its planned investments for 2030 to 2035, the plan lists new radios and data systems to extend the ASGARD network to all crewed and uncrewed systems.

For a founder, the signal is steady money for sensors, data, AI-enabled targeting and the networks that join them, with [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Digital Targeting Web|Digital Targeting Web]] as the wider frame.
