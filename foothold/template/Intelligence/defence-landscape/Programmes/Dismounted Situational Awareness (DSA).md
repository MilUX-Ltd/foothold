# Dismounted Situational Awareness (DSA)

## Summary

DSA is a requirement for a **lightweight Command and Battlespace Management capability** that improves Situational Awareness (SA) and mission effectiveness, supporting Commanders in Dismounted Close Combat (DCC). Filed under HQ & WAN in the MOD TacSys portal but conceptually serves DCC users.

## Benefits (as stated)

- Increased tempo.
- Better-informed and quicker decisions.
- Increased cooperation.
- Reduction in risk and own-force casualties.
- Decrease in physical and cognitive burden on the operator.
