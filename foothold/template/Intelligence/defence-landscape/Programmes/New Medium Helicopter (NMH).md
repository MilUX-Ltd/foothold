# New Medium Helicopter (NMH)

## Summary

The New Medium Helicopter (NMH) programme procures 23 medium-lift support helicopters for the Royal Air Force to replace the retired Westland Puma HC2 fleet. The selected platform is the **Leonardo AW149**, contracted under a £1 billion agreement awarded on 2 March 2026. The new fleet consolidates roles previously performed by three separate aircraft types into a single platform.

## Timeline

- **2021**: Programme launched.
- **August 2024**: Submission deadline. Airbus (H175M) and Sikorsky (S-70M Blackhawk) had withdrawn, leaving Leonardo as the sole bidder.
- **February 2026**: Leonardo AW149 confirmed selected.
- **2 March 2026**: £1 billion contract awarded.
- **Summer 2030**: First platform delivery expected.
- **January 2031**: Induction into service.
- **Autumn 2033**: Final rotorcraft delivery.

## Industrial impact

The aircraft are built at Leonardo's **Yeovil facility in Somerset**, the UK's only end-to-end military helicopter manufacturing site. The contract sustains 3,300 jobs at Yeovil and a further 12,000 jobs across a UK supply chain of around 70 companies. Leonardo has committed to moving its AW149 export production line from Vergiate in Italy to Yeovil, expanding UK workshare above 40 percent and positioning the site to service both British and international orders.
