# Type 31 Frigate

## Summary

The Type 31 Inspiration-class is a programme to build five general-purpose frigates for the Royal Navy, prime contracted to Babcock at its Rosyth shipyard in Scotland. The class is designed to deploy across the globe supporting tasks including surface-to-surface missile and air-defence capability, interception, intelligence-gathering, maritime security, counter-smuggling, and disaster relief support.

## Status (May 2026)

- **HMS Venturer**, lead ship, launched June 2025 and undergoing outfitting.
- **HMS Active**, ship two, rolled out at Rosyth February 2026; float-off completed March 2026.
- **HMS Formidable**, ship three, keel laid December 2024.
- **HMS Bulldog**, ship four, steel cut February 2026.
- All five frigates expected to be commissioned by the early 2030s.

The programme sustains 2,500 highly skilled jobs (1,250 direct at Babcock Rosyth, 1,250 across the UK supply chain).

Babcock has taken a £140 million charge against the programme in Q1 2026 owing to design changes and rework affecting the first two ships, bringing cumulative losses on the order to over £300 million.
