# Home Office Biometrics (HOB)

## Summary

The Home Office Biometrics (HOB) programme consolidates the UK's fingerprint, DNA, and facial-image biometric capabilities into a single platform supporting law enforcement, immigration, and border security. The platform is designed to be scalable and adaptable, accommodating both the optimisation of existing products and the future addition of biometric modalities such as voice and iris.

## Status and capability

- **Business Case v10** approved at InvestCo on 18 July 2024.
- **Programme end date**: extended to December 2026 (from previous March 2026).
- **Total cost**: £1.55 billion across FY 2014-15 to FY 2034-35.
- **Capabilities delivered to date**: crime-mark searching across immigration datasets, mobile biometric search for on-street fingerprint identification, greater automation of National DNA Database processes, Prüm fingerprint collaboration with 22 EU countries.

## Supplier activity

- **Leidos UK** appointed to deliver transformation of the biometrics platform.
- **May 2026**: UK seeking fresh fingerprints on a £300 million biometrics platform.
