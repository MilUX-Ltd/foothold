# Connectivity in Low Earth Orbit (C-LEO)

## Summary

C-LEO is the UK Space Agency's flagship satellite communications R&D programme, providing up to **£160 million of funding over 6 years** to UK companies and researchers to develop satcom technology for low-Earth-orbit constellations. C-LEO supports UK priorities including integration of satellite systems with terrestrial networks (5G and beyond), resilient connectivity for remote communities, and partnerships that attract inward investment.

## Delivery routes

Earlier calls were delivered in partnership with the **European Space Agency** through the **ARTES (Advanced Research in Telecommunications Systems)** programme: applicants submitted an ESA Outline Proposal alongside a UKSA Annex, with £10 million to £25 million per project funded jointly by UKSA and ESA ARTES. Call 3 is **national only** and does not involve ESA.

## Status

- **April 2024**: Programme launched with £60 million for satellite constellation ideas.
- **Call 2 (ARTES Only)**: closed 12 June 2026.
- **Call 3 (National Only)**: up to **£42 million**, around £4 million to £25 million per project for a small number of projects, matched funding required, UK-registered lead. Opened 20 July 2026; Stage One closed at midday on 7 September 2026. Stage Two (invited applicants) closes 11 November 2026, with final outcomes due by 16 December 2026.

If you missed Call 3, watch the UK Space Agency's C-LEO pages for the next call.
