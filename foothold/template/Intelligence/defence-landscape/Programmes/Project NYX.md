# Project NYX

## Summary

Project NYX is the Army's concept demonstrator programme for **armed autonomous drones that fly alongside AH-64E Apache helicopters** as "loyal wingmen", supporting reconnaissance, precision strike, target acquisition and electronic warfare. The MOD says all decisions that result in the use of weapons will continue to be made by a human.

**UK Defence Innovation (UKDI)** is the delivery agent, providing delivery, commercial, engineering and safety expertise.

## Status

- **15 May 2026**: £10m announced; four firms offered contracts: **Anduril UK, BAE Systems, Tekever and Thales UK**.
- **Autumn 2026**: MOD aims to take **up to two** designs forward to build prototypes.
- **By 2030**: if the prototypes succeed, the aim is an operational variant. The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] says NYX will deliver up to 24 armed autonomous drones by 2030.

## Funding (Defence Investment Plan 2026)

**£220m** over FY26/27 to FY29/30 to build advanced armed autonomous drones to operate alongside Apache.

## Where a founder fits

The four bidders are primes or established drone makers, so SME openings are in their supply chains: autonomy software, sensors, electronic warfare payloads, communications and safety assurance. Watch for the down-select in autumn 2026, which will show which two teams to approach.

## Related air-domain work

Project NYX is the Army's. The RAF's equivalent effort is **Collaborative Combat Aircraft**: £300m in the same plan to start a UK autonomous jets programme, with a demonstrator flying by at least 2030. It is listed in the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Programmes Index|Programmes Index]].
