# Aerospace Technology Institute (ATI) Programme

## Summary

The Aerospace Technology Institute (ATI) Programme co-funds civil aerospace research and technology development in the UK. Funding is aligned to the UK aerospace technology strategy and aims to maintain and grow the UK's competitive position in civil aerospace, including the clean-growth transition (sustainable propulsion, lightweight structures, advanced systems).

The Programme is delivered in partnership by three organisations: the **Aerospace Technology Institute** (industry-led not-for-profit), the **Department for Business and Trade (DBT)**, and **Innovate UK**.

## Funding and impact

- **10-year R&D budget extended to 2035**, up to **£2.3 billion** available.
- **£975 million** allocated by HM Government for FY 2025-26 through FY 2029-30.
- **Over £4.2 billion** of total R&D investment facilitated to date.
- **467 projects** awarded funding to date.

## Funding streams

- **Strategic Programme**: flagship funding stream for technology research and development aligned to clean growth.
- **SME-tailored stream**: encourages innovation within the supply chain.
- **Non-CO2 programme**: distinct funding route for non-CO2 aviation effects.

Open competitions are listed at the [Innovation Funding Service](https://apply-for-innovation-funding.service.gov.uk/).
