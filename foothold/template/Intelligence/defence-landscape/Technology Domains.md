# Technology Domains

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/`. This page is one functional view over the landscape: the technology domains where MOD is actively seeking innovation.

Key technology areas driving UK defence innovation and procurement. Domains earn dedicated pages as they accumulate substantive content; the catalogue lives here for now.

UK Defence Innovation (UKDI) frames its demand around five themes: **Autonomy, Decision Advantage, Logistics and Support, Effects, and Protection** ([GOV.UK, 15 July 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/ukdi-unifies-innovation-to-accelerate-frontline-capabilities-and-drive-national-growth)). The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] commits at least 10% of the annual equipment budget to novel technology such as AI, autonomy, quantum and directed energy. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI: How to Engage]].

## C4ISR — Command, Control, Communications, Computers, Intelligence, Surveillance and Reconnaissance

The backbone of military operations. Networks, sensors, data fusion, and decision support. Major UK programmes include the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Digital Targeting Web|Digital Targeting Web]] (£1.8bn in the Defence Investment Plan), [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/ASGARD|ASGARD]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/THEIA|THEIA]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Land ISTAR|Land ISTAR]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/ZODIAC|ZODIAC]], WAVELL, [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Strike Net|Strike Net]].

## Artificial intelligence

AI and machine learning for defence applications: autonomous decision-making, predictive maintenance, intelligence analysis, targeting. The Defence AI Centre (DAIC) coordinates AI policy and governance, and the Defence Investment Plan says it is being developed as Defence's enduring centre for AI delivery. [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Taskforce RAID|Taskforce RAID]] (£100m) runs fast AI delivery sprints with industry.

## Uncrewed vehicles (UxVs)

Uncrewed air, ground, surface, and underwater vehicles. The Defence Investment Plan commits **over £5bn** to uncrewed and autonomous systems by 2030, its largest drone investment. Programmes include the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Hybrid Navy|Hybrid Navy]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Project CORVUS|Project CORVUS]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Project NYX|Project NYX]], uncrewed ground vehicles, one-way attack drones and SURFLOT (Royal Navy fleet of uncrewed surface vessels for training, tactic development, and capability development). Connected to the Uncrewed Systems Centre in Swindon (see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Centres of Excellence|Centres of Excellence]]), the National Drone Hub and the Joint Counter-UAS Office. Counter-drone is its own growing line: see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Counter-drone - PRESAGIUM and PANOPTES|Counter-drone]].

## Robotics

Autonomous and semi-autonomous robotic systems for logistics, EOD (explosive ordnance disposal), and combat support. Overlaps significantly with UxVs.

## Cybersecurity

Defensive and offensive cyber capabilities. Connected to GCHQ, NCSC, the National Cyber Force, and the Defence Cyber Marvel annual exercise. A priority area post-SDR; the Defence Investment Plan puts £2.5bn into cyber and the electromagnetic domain.

## Strategic semiconductors

Sovereign semiconductor capability for defence applications. A national priority given global supply-chain risks.

## Weapons and defence systems

Munitions, directed-energy weapons, integrated weapons systems. Covered by the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Weapons Sector Research Framework|Weapons Sector Research Framework]] and the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/HTCD|HTCDF (Hypersonic Technologies and Capability Development Framework)]].

## Training and simulation

Synthetic training environments, live-virtual-constructive training, simulation. Connected to the Defence Modelling and Simulation Office.

## Space and satellite

Space-based capabilities: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/SKYNET|SKYNET]] (military satcomms; the SKYNET 6 narrowband satellite is not proceeding and the plan points to "space as a service" options), Earth observation, space situational awareness, launch. The Defence Investment Plan puts £3.2bn into space capabilities. The UK Space Agency oversees the civilian space sector; the National Space Operations Centre handles military space.

## Information integrity

Countering disinformation, information operations, maintaining trust in data and communications. Connected to the National Security Online Information Team.

## Quantum and cryptography

Quantum computing, quantum sensing, quantum communications, post-quantum cryptography. A strategic priority for defence and intelligence.

## Naval and maritime

Maritime technologies — autonomous vessels, underwater systems, naval communications, shipbuilding innovation.

## Materials and manufacturing

Advanced materials, additive manufacturing, novel manufacturing processes for defence applications.

## Sensing and sensors

Advanced sensor technologies — radar, EO/IR (electro-optical/infrared), acoustic, seismic, multi-spectral sensing.

## Service providers and infrastructure

IT infrastructure, cloud computing, managed services, communications infrastructure. Accessed primarily through Government Commercial Agency (GCA, formerly Crown Commercial Service) frameworks ([[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/G-Cloud|G-Cloud]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Technology Services (TCS)|Technology Services]], Network Services).

## Cross-cutting themes

- **Interoperability.** NATO standards, data exchange, coalition operations.
- **Sustainability.** Energy efficiency, green defence, climate adaptation.
- **Supply-chain resilience.** Sovereign capability, diversification.
- **Ethics and governance.** Responsible AI, autonomous systems governance.
