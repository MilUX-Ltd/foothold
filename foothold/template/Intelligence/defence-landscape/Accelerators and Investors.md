# Accelerators and Investors

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/`. When you start engaging with one of the organisations below, add it to your CRM via `/add-organisation` and link from there.

Organisations supporting the growth, funding, and scaling of defence technology start-ups and SMEs in the UK.

## Government-backed accelerators and funds

- **DASA — Defence and Security Accelerator.** Primary MOD innovation funder. Runs themed competitions, an Open Call for Innovation, and an Ideas Marketplace. See also [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Innovation Organisations|Innovation Organisations]].
- **ARIA — Advanced Research and Invention Agency.** High-risk, high-reward R&D funding for breakthrough technologies. Independent of MOD; UK-government-funded.
- **NSSIF — National Security Strategic Investment Fund.** Government investment fund for national-security-relevant technology. Part of the British Business Bank.
- **UK Innovation and Science Seed Fund.** Early-stage investment in commercialising publicly-funded research.
- **UKRI Innovation Accelerator.** UK Research and Innovation programme accelerating research-based innovation in target regions.
- **Defence BattleLab.** Co-collaboration and rapid experimentation. Based at Dorset Innovation Park.
- **jHub Defence Innovation Accelerator.** Accelerator programmes connecting start-ups with military problems.
- **NATO DIANA — Defence Innovation Accelerator for the North Atlantic.** Multilateral NATO-backed accelerator network for dual-use deep-tech.
- **Defence Tech Scaler.** Scaling support from demonstration to deployment for defence-tech companies.
- **Ploughshare Accelerator Fund.** Investment arm of Ploughshare Innovations (Dstl's commercialisation partner).

## Private-sector accelerators

- **Digital Catapult.** UK's leading advanced digital-technology innovation centre. Runs defence-relevant programmes.
- **Airbus UK Space Accelerator.** Airbus-backed accelerator for space-technology start-ups.
- **Entrepreneur First.** Talent investor and company-builder; backs founding teams pre-product.
- **Seedcamp.** European seed-stage venture capital.
- **Founders Factory.** London-based accelerator and venture studio.
- **Techstars.** Global start-up accelerator network with defence- and government-focused programmes.

## Investors with a defence or security focus

The UK defence-tech investment ecosystem includes specialist VC funds, family offices, and government-backed funds. Considerations for SMEs seeking investment:

- **ITAR implications.** If your product touches US-origin technology or you take US-based investment, ITAR may apply.
- **Security-clearance requirements.** Some defence customers require board members and senior staff to hold UK security clearance.
- **Export-control compliance.** UK export-licensing regime (and equivalents) affects what you can sell, to whom.
- **Dual-use positioning.** Many defence-tech start-ups raise more efficiently when they position as dual-use (defence plus civil) rather than defence-only.

## Help and advice

- **Defence Office for Small Business Growth (DOSBG).** MOD's dedicated SME engagement function. Single front door for SMEs looking to engage with MOD.
- **Regional Innovation Partners.** Local support networks aligned with the Regional Defence and Security Clusters.
- **Trade associations.** ADS Group (Aerospace, Defence, Security and Space), techUK Defence and Security, others. See your sector trade body for the latest.
- **Think tanks.** RUSI (Royal United Services Institute), IISS (International Institute for Strategic Studies), Chatham House. Useful for sector intelligence and stakeholder access.
