# Frameworks Index

Commercial routes to market for defence technology companies. Each framework lives on its own page; this index is the navigation surface. Portals (supplier-facing platforms rather than frameworks per se) live under [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Portals Index|Portals]]. End dates below are from the MOD's [routes-to-market page](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-contracts-in-technology-and-research-and-development-with-the-ministry-of-defence) (updated 18 September 2026) or the operator's own agreement page; check before you plan around one.
