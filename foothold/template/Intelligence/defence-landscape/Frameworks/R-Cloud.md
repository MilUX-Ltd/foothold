# R-Cloud (Research Cloud)

> **Naming note.** R-Cloud is sometimes mistaken for a Defence Digital cloud-hosting framework. It is in fact **Dstl's Research Cloud**, a Dynamic Purchasing System for science and technology research, not a hosting service.

## Summary

**R-Cloud (Research Cloud)** is the Defence Science and Technology Laboratory (Dstl)'s marketplace for science and technology (S&T) research. A **dynamic agreement** ("dynamic gateway") allowing academic and industry suppliers to apply quickly and easily using an online application process, **join at any time during the R-Cloud term**, and receive targeted invitations to bid for opportunities tailored to their key capabilities and skills.

R-Cloud is the principal supplier-side contracting mechanism through which Dstl pulls external S&T research into the MOD pipeline. Sits alongside [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/ASTRID|ASTRID]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/PROGENY|PROGENY]], the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Weapons Sector Research Framework|Weapons Sector Research Framework]], and the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Defence Technology Exploitation Programme (DTEP)|DTEP]] in the Dstl research-contracting family.

## Operating Model

- **Dynamic agreement.** R-Cloud is a Dynamic Purchasing System rather than a fixed-membership framework. Suppliers can apply and onboard at any time during the R-Cloud term, instead of being locked in or out by a single competition window.
- **Targeted invitations.** Suppliers receive invitations to bid for opportunities tailored to their declared capabilities and skills, rather than having to scan a generic opportunity index.
- **IP retention.** Ownership of any intellectual property generated under R-Cloud research remains with the suppliers, which is a deliberate signal to industry partners who would otherwise be reluctant to engage on IP-heavy research.
- **Complementary to other Dstl mechanisms.** R-Cloud sits alongside other Dstl contracting mechanisms; academic and industry suppliers of S&T research are invited to pre-qualify into Dstl's research supplier community.
- **Refresh.** A framework renewal (visible on Contracts Finder) is being run periodically to refresh the supplier community.

## R-Cloud is distinct from MODCloud

R-Cloud is sometimes confused with **MODCloud**, the MOD's public-cloud hosting platform. They are different things:

| | R-Cloud | MODCloud |
|---|---|---|
| **Function** | S&T research contracting marketplace (Dstl) | Public cloud hosting platform for MOD applications |
| **Owner** | Dstl | Defence Digital |
| **What it does** | Lets Dstl find and contract S&T research suppliers | Hosts MOD applications on Azure and AWS up to OFFICIAL |
| **Classification** | Operates at OFFICIAL | Up to OFFICIAL via ACE (Azure) and ICE (AWS) variants |

See [Digital MOD: Use the Cloud First](https://www.digital.mod.uk/policy-rules-standards-and-guidance/service-manual/architecture/defence-technology-principles/use-the-cloud-first) and [MOD Cloud Service](https://mod-cloud.service.mod.gov.uk/) for the hosting platform.

## Membership / Suppliers

Open to academic and industry suppliers of S&T research. Suppliers apply directly via [rcloud.dstl.gov.uk](https://rcloud.dstl.gov.uk/).
