# HTCDF (Hypersonic Technologies & Capability Development Framework)

## Summary

A £1 billion, 7-year **Hypersonic Technologies & Capability Development Framework (HTCDF)** set up by DE&S (now NA-M) to rapidly develop advanced hypersonic missile capabilities for the UK. **Ninety organisations from across industry and academia** have secured a place on the framework. The framework is structured into **eight lots**, with maturity of services and supplies covering the full **TRL 1 to 9** range. **Nearly half of suppliers are SMEs**, the vast majority UK-based.

## Operating model

- **Eight-lot framework.** Suppliers accepted onto specific lots are eligible to bid for Call Off contracts.
- **Maximum value.** Up to £1 billion across the next seven years.
- **TRL spread.** Maturity of services and supplies covers TRL 1 to 9.
- **Pace by design.** Project delivery has been designed with pace in mind: the framework adopts mechanisms which align procurement and development objectives, intended to prevent the traditional challenges that impede capability acquisition.
- **Re-opening cadence.** The framework re-opens to new suppliers every 6 to 12 months, so MOD can continue to draw upon new technologies and emerging market capabilities. This is unusual for a defence framework and is a deliberate procurement-policy move.

## SME access

Nearly half of accepted suppliers are SMEs, the vast majority based in the UK. The framework is one of MOD's headline post-SDR procurement initiatives demonstrating commitment to SME access to large-scale defence work.

## Strategic context

HTCDF underpins UK indigenous hypersonic capability development at a moment when hypersonics are a peer-competitor priority area globally. Sits inside the wider NAD Group structure under the post-April-2026 reorganisation, alongside the wider Defence Industrial Strategy 2025.
