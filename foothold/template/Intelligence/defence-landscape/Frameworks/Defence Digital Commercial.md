# Defence Digital Commercial

## Summary

The **Defence Digital Commercial** function is the commercial-routes portfolio inside **NA-D&D (formerly Defence Digital)**: the **umbrella term** for the framework agreements and direct-award vehicles that NA-D&D uses to procure digital and technology services for UK Defence. Not itself a single framework or agreement; the umbrella covers a portfolio of named frameworks plus the cross-cutting commercial-and-policy direction inside NA-D&D.

Per industry analysts (notably TechMarketView), NA-D&D (as Defence Digital) accounts for an **estimated c.62% of total UK Defence SITS spend**, making it the single largest customer in the UK Defence systems-integration market. The Defence Digital Commercial function is the contracting muscle behind that spend.

NA-D&D corporate: [Digital MOD](https://www.digital.mod.uk/); [About Foundry](https://data-ai.service.mod.gov.uk/about-foundry/); [D2S service](https://d2s.dev.service.mod.gov.uk/).

## Named constituent frameworks and vehicles

The Defence Digital Commercial portfolio includes (non-exhaustive):

- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/DDAD|DDAD (Digital Decision Accelerators for Defence)]]: £180m, ASGARD delivery, Open Framework under the Procurement Act 2023.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/R-Cloud|R-Cloud]]: Dstl-operated S&T research marketplace (cited here because the wider Defence Digital cloud strategy positions it alongside MODCloud and Defence Tech Scaler).
- **TacCIS**: Tactical Communications and Information Systems framework.
- **Defence Tech Scaler**: a Defence Digital programme intended to give industry, particularly SMEs, a scaling route into MOD digital.
- **MODCloud** (ACE/ICE): MOD's public cloud hosting platform on Azure and AWS up to OFFICIAL. See [GOV.UK: Use the Cloud First](https://www.digital.mod.uk/policy-rules-standards-and-guidance/service-manual/architecture/defence-technology-principles/use-the-cloud-first); [MOD Cloud Strategic Roadmap](https://www.techuk.org/resource/ministry-of-defence-publishes-cloud-strategic-roadmap.html).
- **Foundry**: NA-D&D's digital delivery and experimentation function. Now hosts much of the AI build muscle inside Defence Digital. See [About Foundry](https://data-ai.service.mod.gov.uk/about-foundry/).
- **D2S (Defence Digital Service)**: software and applications acceleration capability inside the Foundry. See [d2s.dev.service.mod.gov.uk](https://d2s.dev.service.mod.gov.uk/).

## Operating Model

- **NA-D&D Commercial** acts as the contracting authority across the constituent frameworks.
- **Crown Commercial Service frameworks** ([[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/G-Cloud|G-Cloud]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Digital Outcomes and Specialists (DOS)|DOS]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Technology Services (TCS)|TCS]]) are used in parallel where the requirement is generic enough to suit them.
- **Defence Digital-specific frameworks** ([[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/DDAD|DDAD]], TacCIS, the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Digital and IT Professional Services (DIPS)|TCS3 DIPS sub-lot]]) carry the bulk of Defence-specific spend.

## Strategic context

Across 2024 to 2026, NA-D&D has been moving from a portfolio of long-running large contracts toward a **portfolio-and-services operating model**, with implications for how suppliers contract and how UCD, product, and service-design disciplines plug into MOD delivery.

The post-March-2025 DE&S Operating Model transition and the post-April-2026 NAD Group reorganisation place NA-D&D as one of the nine NAD Group pillars, with the DG Digital & Data also retaining the cross-MOD **Chief Digital Information Officer (CDIO)** role.
