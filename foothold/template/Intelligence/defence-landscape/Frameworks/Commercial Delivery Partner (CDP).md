# Commercial Delivery Partner (CDP / CommDP)

## Summary

**Commercial Delivery Partner (CDP, also ComDP / CommDP).** MOD framework providing professional short-term commercial resources and commercial project teams to MOD Commercial functions. Addresses surge demand, specialist non-core skillsets, hard-to-fill vacancies, and ad-hoc capability requirements. Also runs an innovation-and-improvement lane to drive efficiency gains within existing commercial processes.

CDP is the commercial sister to the EDP (engineering) and PDP-S (programme management), completing the trio of "Delivery Partner" frameworks under MOD.

## Operating model

Typical capability areas:

- Commercial professional services (short-term resource, project teams)
- Major-project surge resource
- Specialist (non-core) commercial skillsets
- Innovation and improvement in MOD Commercial processes

Lead award and supply-chain detail per the 2020 MOD Commercial Delivery Partner (CommDP) project award.
