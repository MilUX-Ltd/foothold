# SME Searchlight

## Summary

A Dstl initiative aimed at attracting **non-traditional Defence suppliers and SMEs** into UK defence and security research. The initial launch was framed as a 12-month programme designed to deliver a projected **£40 million to £45 million increase in Dstl research spending** with external companies, in support of the government's target of **33% of central government procurement spend going to SMEs**. The programme has continued in follow-on form.

GOV.UK launch: [Defence Science and Technology Laboratory launches SME Searchlight to harness innovation](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory-launches-sme-searchlight-to-harness-innovation). Coverage: [Open Access Government: Dstl signals bright future for SME research in defence and security](https://www.openaccessgovernment.org/sme-research/99176/). Tag archive: [Defence Online: SME Searchlight](https://www.defenceonline.co.uk/tag/sme-searchlight/).

## Operating Model

- **Capability-visibility programme.** Not a competitive procurement route. SME Searchlight exists to help Dstl discover SMEs that can deliver research and to help SMEs find their way into defence research engagements.
- **Event-driven engagement.** Series of events, workshops, and consultations focused on specific Dstl research themes.
- **Stated event-attendance target.** 60% of attendees at SME Searchlight events should have never worked with Defence before: the programme explicitly biases toward bringing genuinely new companies into the supplier base.
- **Onward routes.** Engaged SMEs are routed into Dstl's supply chain via three mechanisms:
  1. Direct supplier engagement with Dstl.
  2. Membership of Dstl framework contracts (including [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/R-Cloud|R-Cloud]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/ASTRID|ASTRID]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/PROGENY|PROGENY]]).
  3. Through the Defence and Security Accelerator (DASA) competition route.

### Initial focus research areas

The initial 12-month programme picked four research areas where Dstl wanted to broaden the supplier base:

1. **Radio frequency engineering.**
2. **Artificial intelligence.**
3. **Synthetic biology.**
4. **Wargaming.**

### Delivery partners

Dstl runs SME Searchlight in partnership with [[CRM/organisations/trade bodies/ADS Group|ADS]], the **Federation of Small Businesses (FSB)**, **Team Defence Information (TDI)**, and [[CRM/organisations/trade bodies/techUK|techUK]]. The partner set is deliberate: between them, the four bodies cover the bulk of the UK SME defence and tech trade-association membership.

## How SME Searchlight differs from Helios SME Portal

Both surface UK SME capabilities to defence buyers, but the mechanism is different:

| | SME Searchlight | Helios SME Portal |
|---|---|---|
| **Operator** | Dstl + trade associations | Hellios + JOSCAR Governance Group |
| **Mechanism** | Themed events and direct Dstl supplier engagement | Self-registration on an online capability-visibility portal |
| **Focus** | Dstl S&T research themes | MOD and prime contractor capability discovery, all sectors |
| **Cost** | Free | Free |
| **Best for** | SMEs with research-grade capability in a focus theme | All UK SMEs wanting MOD and prime visibility |

Both are worth registering for. They are complementary rather than substitutable.
