# Innovate UK Contracts for Innovation (formerly SBRI)

The UK's flagship pre-commercial procurement route. Lets public-sector bodies (including MOD, DASA, Dstl, Home Office, NHS) run **fully-funded R&D contracts** with SMEs and other innovators where existing market solutions are insufficient. Rebranded from **Small Business Research Initiative (SBRI)** in the 2024-25 Innovate UK refresh.

UKRI: [Innovate UK Contracts for Innovation](https://www.ukri.org/what-we-do/browse-our-areas-of-investment-and-support/innovate-uk-contracts-for-innovation/). Innovate UK Business Connect: [Programme overview](https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/programme/contracts-for-innovation/).

## Role in the landscape

Contracts for Innovation is the UK's primary pre-commercial procurement (PCP) mechanism. A public-sector challenge owner defines a problem where off-the-shelf solutions don't exist; SMEs and innovators compete for **fully-funded R&D contracts** to develop new solutions. The route is widely used for DASA themed competitions, Defence Innovation Loans, Dstl and Defence Digital challenges, Home Office competitions, and NHS England medtech challenges.

### Why it matters for defence-tech SMEs

- **Fully funded.** No matched funding required.
- **No IP transfer.** SMEs retain commercial IP rights to whatever they develop.
- **Two-phase structure typical.** Feasibility phase (smaller award) followed by prototype or demonstrator phase (larger award) for the most-promising Phase 1 contractors.
- **66%+ SME share.** The mechanism is built for SMEs by design.
- **At least £2.40 per £1 economic return** per UKRI's own evaluation.

### Defence usage

The SBRI / Contracts for Innovation route is the underlying mechanism for **DASA's Themed Competitions** and **Open Call for Innovation**. Most published DASA competitions are Contracts for Innovation procurements in form, even where the DASA branding is used outwardly.
