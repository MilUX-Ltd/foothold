# Commercial X and Defence Unicorns

## Summary

**Commercial X** is a specialist function within the MOD that buys innovation fast. The MOD's [SME Action Plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mod-small-and-medium-sized-enterprise-sme-action-plan) (21 July 2026) describes it as delivering contracts **up to £50m and under two years**, and says it runs three routes:

- the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Commercial X Dynamic Market (DMP)|Commercial X Dynamic Market]], open to suppliers of any size at any development stage;
- the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Neutral Vendor Framework for Innovation|Neutral Vendor Framework for Innovation (NVFi)]];
- the **Defence Unicorns** search.

For a founder new to defence, this is the route built for you: contracts larger than most innovation competitions, approval freedoms that shorten the timeline, and, in Defence Unicorns, a search aimed at firms the MOD has not worked with before.

## Defence Unicorns

- **What it is.** Accelerated contracts for small, innovative British firms with **limited or no previous MOD business**, aiming to grow the next billion-pound British defence companies.
- **First round (19 May 2026).** Thirteen British companies across England, Wales and Scotland won contracts of up to £4m each. More than half were new to defence, and all were founded after 2011. Themes included AI, robotics and autonomy, and precision capabilities.
- **What comes next.** The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] expands the search, with **up to £100m over four years** in accelerated contracts for British tech firms with limited or no MOD business.
- **How to take part.** No standing application process has been published. Join the Commercial X Dynamic Market through the Defence Sourcing Portal so you see competitions as they are published, and talk to the [[CRM/organisations/government/Defence Office for Small Business Growth|Defence Office for Small Business Growth]].

## The wider SME commitment

The SME Action Plan, led by the Defence Office for Small Business Growth, commits the MOD to spend **an additional £2.5bn with SMEs by summer 2028**, a 50% increase on about £5bn a year today. Targets: £1.48bn direct and £4.5bn indirect in 2026/27, rising to £2bn direct and £5.5bn indirect in 2027/28. It also promises a simple public guide to MOD entry points, portals and marketplaces, and will consider consolidating portals or offering cross-platform sign-on.
