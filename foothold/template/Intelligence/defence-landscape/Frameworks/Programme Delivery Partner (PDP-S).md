# Programme Delivery Partner (PDP-S)

## Summary

**Programme Delivery Partner — Successor (PDP-S).** MOD professional-services framework supporting a wide range of defence programmes with project management, project controls, and integrated logistics resources. The "Successor" iteration replaces the original Programme Delivery Partner framework, which concluded 31 March 2023.

PDP-S sits alongside the EDP (engineering capability) and the CDP/CommDP (commercial functions) as one of the three principal MOD "Delivery Partner" frameworks. Where EDP procures engineering, PDP procures programme management.

## Operating model

Lead delivery partner: **Turner & Townsend** under contract PDP/001 (Successor). The framework operates a tiered supply chain — the lead and supporting partners draw on specialist suppliers for specific assignments.

Typical capability areas:

- Project and programme management (PPM)
- Project controls (PC)
- Integrated logistics
- Operational delivery support

PDP-S is not a Dynamic Purchasing System; entry is via the lead's supply chain or via direct prime appointments.
