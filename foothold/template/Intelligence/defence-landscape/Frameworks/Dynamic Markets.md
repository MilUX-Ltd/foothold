# Dynamic Markets

## Summary

**Dynamic Markets** are the **Procurement Act 2023** replacement for the previous Dynamic Purchasing Systems (DPS) and qualification systems. A list of qualified suppliers who have met specific "conditions for membership" and are eligible to participate in future procurements. Suppliers can apply and join at any time during the life of a Dynamic Market, rather than being locked in or out by a single application window. Standard Dynamic Markets carry **no membership fees**.

Dynamic Markets came into force on **24 February 2025** when the Procurement Act 2023 came fully into effect. They are used across UK central government, the MOD, the wider public sector, and the devolved administrations.

## Operating Model

- **Open admission.** Suppliers can apply to join at any time during the life of a Dynamic Market.
- **Conditions for membership.** Each Dynamic Market sets its own conditions (technical capability, financial standing, accreditations such as [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/JOSCAR|JOSCAR]]). Suppliers must meet and continue to meet these to remain on the market.
- **Mini-competitions.** Buyers run structured competitions among current Dynamic Market members per procurement, rather than a single PQQ-based award at framework setup.
- **Broader scope than DPS.** The previous DPS regime was limited to "specific goods and services commonly available on the market". Dynamic Markets can be used for any procurement type, including bespoke or complex requirements.
- **No membership fees** on standard Dynamic Markets, which is a deliberate change to reduce SME barriers to entry.

### Key advantages over the legacy DPS

- **No application windows to miss.** Suppliers can join at any time.
- **Broader applicability.** Suitable for bespoke and complex requirements, not just commodity buys.
- **No membership fees** on standard markets.
- **Integration with the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Central Digital Platform|Central Digital Platform]].** Supplier core information feeds directly into Dynamic Market applications.

### MOD-specific Dynamic Markets

The MOD operates Dynamic Markets through the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal]] using the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/eSourcing Tool|eSourcing Tool]] (Jaggaer / AWARD®). Specific MOD Dynamic Markets vary over time; a current list is maintained inside DSP.

## Relationship to the Central Digital Platform

Under the Procurement Act 2023, supplier core information sits in the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Central Digital Platform|Central Digital Platform (CDP)]] and is reused across covered procurements, including Dynamic Markets. Suppliers complete the CDP profile once, then apply to relevant Dynamic Markets without duplicating the bulk of the supplier information.
