# Digital Outcomes and Specialists (DOS)

## Summary

**Digital Outcomes and Specialists (DOS).** Crown Commercial Service framework for procuring digital outcomes (e.g. a beta application), individual specialists (e.g. a developer or user researcher), and user research services. Default CCS route for service-design and digital-delivery engagements in UK public sector.

## Operating model

Three primary lots:

- **Digital outcomes.** Buyers describe an outcome; suppliers propose multi-disciplinary teams.
- **Digital specialists.** Buyers describe a role (designer, researcher, product manager); suppliers propose individuals.
- **User research participants and studios.** Sourcing for user research activities.

Buyer competitions run on the Digital Marketplace with structured shortlisting and assessment.
