# Digital and IT Professional Services (DIPS)

## Summary

**Digital and IT Professional Services (DIPS).** Crown Commercial Service sub-lot under [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Technology Services (TCS)|Technology Services 3]] for digital and IT professional services. Four-year framework, estimated ~£1.2bn spend, run by CCS principally for Defence Digital. Major defence appointments include QinetiQ (announced March 2024).

## Operating model

Buyer side: Defence Digital and other public-sector buyers run CCS-compliant competitions against the DIPS supplier list. Supplier side: prequalified IT and digital professional-services suppliers compete for individual lots and task orders.
