# Digital and IT Professional Services (DIPS)

## Summary

**Digital and IT Professional Services (DIPS), RM6249.** A Government Commercial Agency (GCA, formerly Crown Commercial Service) framework for digital and IT professional services, **available to MOD customers only**. It runs from 17 November 2023 to **16 November 2027** with no extensions, and is not open to new suppliers. Reported spend estimate about £1.2bn. Six lots: architecture, data and innovation; development, apps, UX and DevOps; cyber security and secure operations; infrastructure and telecoms; project, programme and portfolio management; intelligence solutions. Seventeen suppliers hold places, including QinetiQ (announced March 2024).

For a founder not on it, the route is associate or subcontract work through a lot holder.

## Operating model

Buyer side: Defence Digital and other public-sector buyers run GCA-compliant competitions against the DIPS supplier list. Supplier side: prequalified IT and digital professional-services suppliers compete for individual lots and task orders.
