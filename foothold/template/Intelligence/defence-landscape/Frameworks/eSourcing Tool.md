# eSourcing Tool

## Summary

The MOD's **electronic sourcing platform** that underpins the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal (DSP)]]. The platform through which MOD competitions are issued, suppliers respond, and the audit trail is maintained. Delivered to the MOD by **Jaggaer**, with **AWARD® from Commerce Decisions** providing the specialist solution for complex strategic procurements.

The eSourcing Tool and the Defence Sourcing Portal are tightly coupled: DSP is the supplier-facing front door; the eSourcing Tool is the underlying competition-management capability.

User guide: [contracts.mod.uk: DSP](https://contracts.mod.uk/web/user-guide.html). DE&S: [New Defence Sourcing Portal and Supplier Registration](https://des.mod.uk/new-defence-sourcing-portal/). Commerce Decisions: [MOD Defence Sourcing Portal and AWARD®](https://help.commercedecisions.com/support/solutions/articles/80001028784-mod-defence-sourcing-portal-and-award-).

## Operating Model

- **Single workflow.** Buyer-side and supplier-side activity for MOD competitions runs through one e-sourcing workflow.
- **Secure document exchange.** Replaces inefficient email and paper-based practices with secure online document exchange and a clear audit trail.
- **Supports competitions of all sizes.** From small MOD competitions through to complex, strategic, multi-lot frameworks handled via AWARD®.
- **Cost.** Free for suppliers to access and bid.
- **Helpdesk.** 24-hour supplier support.

### Two-tier technical stack

- **Jaggaer.** Delivers the underlying e-sourcing platform that MOD uses end-to-end.
- **AWARD® (Commerce Decisions).** A specialist tooling layer used specifically for complex, strategic procurements where evaluator-side workflow and scoring needs are higher than the standard Jaggaer flow provides.
