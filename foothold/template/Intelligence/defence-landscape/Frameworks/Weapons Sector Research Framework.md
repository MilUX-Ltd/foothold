# Weapons Sector Research Framework (WSRF)

## Summary

The MOD's principal R&D framework for **weapons science and technology**, designed to accelerate the development of emerging and disruptive weapons technologies and to support the **Future Kinetic Effects and Weapons (FKEW) programme**. Awarded as a **5-year framework contract worth £300 million** to **QinetiQ**, with **MBDA** and **Thales** as key partners.

WSRF replaces the previous **Weapons Science and Technology Centre (WSTC)** contract with a broader scope including **directed energy weapons** alongside complex, conventional, and novel weapons technologies.

## Operating Model

- **5-year framework.** Awarded 2020. Total value £300 million.
- **Prime contractor.** QinetiQ. Key partners MBDA and Thales.
- **Wider community.** Over **150 partners** across academia, SMEs, and the broader weapons industry.
- **Owned by Dstl.** Can also be used by other parts of MOD: Front Line Commands, **Weapons Evaluation and Capability Assurance (WECA)**, and DE&S (now NA-M).

### Scope

The framework covers the overall scope of the **Future Kinetic Effects and Weapons (FKEW) programme**: complex, conventional, and novel weapons technologies, plus directed-energy weapons. The expansion from WSTC reflects the increased priority of directed-energy and other novel weapon technology and effects.

### Place in the Dstl framework family

WSRF is the weapons-domain equivalent of the maritime [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/PROGENY|PROGENY]] and the more general [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/ASTRID|ASTRID]] Dstl S&T research framework. Together with [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/R-Cloud|R-Cloud]] (dynamic supplier marketplace) and the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Defence Technology Exploitation Programme (DTEP)|DTEP]] transition route, they constitute the major Dstl-operated research-contracting estate.

The wider [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Storm|STORM]] missile-defence R&D framework and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/HTCD|HTCDF]] hypersonic capability framework cover different parts of the broader UK weapons-and-effects landscape.

## Membership / Suppliers

- **Prime.** QinetiQ.
- **Key partners.** MBDA, Thales.
- **Wider community.** 150+ partners across academia, SMEs, weapons industry.
