# Space Technology Solutions (CCS RM6370)

## Summary

The Crown Commercial Service's **dynamic-market framework** for procuring space-related technologies, developed in collaboration with the **Ministry of Defence** and other organisations. Covers **upstream and downstream space technology**, available to all UK public sector buyers including defence and security. **Six-year agreement** with an option to extend for a further two years. Compliant with the Procurement Act 2023; uses a **Dynamic Market** buying approach with a list of pre-approved suppliers.

CCS reference: **RM6370**.

CCS: [RM6370 Space Technology Solutions agreement](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6370); [New space technology agreement launched](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/news/new-space-technology-agreement-launched). Coverage: [UKAuthority: CCS sets up Space Technology Solutions procurement framework](https://www.ukauthority.com/articles/ccs-sets-up-space-technology-solutions-procurement-framework); [Open Access Government: UK public sector now has better access to space innovation](https://www.openaccessgovernment.org/the-uk-public-sector-now-has-better-access-to-space-innovation-through-ccs-agreement/196368/). ## Operating Model

- **Dynamic Market under Procurement Act 2023.** New suppliers can join at any time, rather than waiting for a fixed framework window.
- **6+2 year duration.** Six-year base agreement, two-year extension option.
- **CCS as operator.** Available to all UK public sector buyers, including defence and security.
- **Broad supplier base.** Supports SMEs and primes across upstream and downstream space technology.

### Scope of goods and services

Per CCS: covers goods and services related to space and geospatial technologies, including:

- **Satellite communications.**
- **Geospatial and remote sensing.**
- **Space domain awareness.**
- **Space command and control.**
- **Advisory, consultancy and training.**

### Relationship to predecessor

Replaces and expands on the previous CCS [Space-Enabled and Geospatial Services (RM6235)](https://www.crowncommercial.gov.uk/agreements/RM6235) dynamic purchasing system. The new agreement has an enhanced scope suitable for complex procurements that the previous DPS could not handle.

### Sector context

The UK space sector is currently worth over **£18.9 billion annually** and employs more than 52,000 people. Space Technology Solutions is positioned as the central UK public-sector procurement vehicle for tapping that sector at scale, including for resilient communications (low-Earth-orbit satellite networks) and emergency / remote-community connectivity.

### MOD and defence-space angle

The agreement was developed with the MOD in collaboration with CCS. UK Space Command and the defence-space programmes (notably SKYNET) are natural buyers under the agreement when commercial goods and services are appropriate, alongside the [Defence Space Strategy](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defence-space-strategy) direction.
