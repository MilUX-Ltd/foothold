# DDAD (Digital Decision Accelerators for Defence Open Framework)

## Summary

The MOD's **Digital Decision Accelerators for Defence (DDAD) Open Framework** is a multi-supplier procurement designed to encourage the development and acceleration of advanced **digital "Decision" capabilities** for UK Defence. The framework is the **main delivery mechanism for ASGARD**, the British Army's flagship Transformative Capability Initiative (TCI) inside the Chief of the General Staff's Growth Through Transformation strategy. Estimated value c. £180 million over four core years, with options to extend.

DDAD focuses on the **"Decide" element** of the Sense-Decide-Effect target acquisition cycle.

## Operating model

- **Open Framework under the Procurement Act 2023.** Defined as a scheme of successive frameworks that allows for the periodic re-opening of supplier lists under substantially the same terms. Contrasts with traditional "closed" frameworks where suppliers are locked in or out for the full duration after a single competition.
- **Estimated value.** £180 million over four core years (with options to extend).
- **Delivery purpose.** Acts as the main delivery mechanism for ASGARD, the British Army's Transformative Capability Initiative delivering a central part of the Chief of the General Staff's Growth Through Transformation strategy.
- **Focus area.** The "Decide" element of the Sense-Decide-Effect target acquisition cycle.

### Lot structure

The framework is structured into multiple lots that together address the full digital-decision capability stack:

- **Data-driven decision-making solutions.** Model development, machine learning operations, synthetic data usage, real-time inference. Focus on reducing time-to-insight and improving operational efficiency.
- **Applications.** Rapid development and management of enterprise-grade applications: microservices, CI/CD, observability, secure deployments. Focus on fast delivery, scalability, and continuous innovation.
- **Expert services.** Technology adoption and integration support: technical training, architecture consulting, synthetic data support, proof-of-concept development.
