# Neutral Vendor Framework for Innovation

## Summary

Crown Commercial Service framework providing access to innovation services through a neutral vendor model. The neutral vendor sits between buyer and end-supplier, brokering capacity and managing the supply chain on the buyer's behalf.

## Operating model

Lead vendor, refresh cycle, and scope detail to expand from CCS public documentation.
