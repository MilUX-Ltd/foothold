# PROGENY (Progeny Maritime Research Framework)

## Summary

The **Progeny Maritime Research Framework (PMRF)** is the contracting mechanism that underpins delivery of the UK's **maritime science and technology capability** from within Dstl and the wider MOD. Awarded by Dstl to **QinetiQ**, which builds and manages a community of innovators contributing ideas to maximise the impact of S&T for UK maritime defence.

QinetiQ: [Progeny Framework](https://www.qinetiq.com/en/our-company/suppliers-and-smes/frameworks/progeny); [Collaborative enterprise opens MOD contracts to new suppliers](https://www.qinetiq.com/en/news/progeny).

## Operating Model

- **Purpose.** Provide a flexible, enduring, and responsive contracting mechanism to deliver maritime-related S&T, enabled by the breadth of relevant capabilities across industry and academia.
- **Prime.** Awarded by Dstl to **QinetiQ**, which manages the supplier community.
- **Estimated value.** Up to **£152 million** of work to be placed for Dstl and wider MOD.
- **TRL spread.** Covers everything from low-TRL "exploratory" activities through to high-TRL activities that de-risk solutions for potential future procurement programmes.
- **Mission.** De-risk technologies, concepts, and processes that enable the UK to deliver tasks and develop understanding and models from and within the maritime environment.

### Open community model

The Progeny community is explicitly **open to any company, academic institution, or SME** whose knowledge, products, or services could have applications in maritime defence, including organisations that do not usually work in the defence industry. This is one of the more open MOD frameworks for crossover engagement.
