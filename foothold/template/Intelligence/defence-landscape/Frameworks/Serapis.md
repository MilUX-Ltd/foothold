# Serapis

## Summary

Serapis is the Defence Science and Technology Laboratory's (Dstl) framework agreement for placing contracts for scientific and technical research and development on behalf of Dstl, the wider MoD, and the front-line commands. The framework is structured to provide a fast, simple route for placing S&T contracts and is **explicitly aimed at broadening Dstl's supplier base** to include suppliers who have not traditionally worked in defence, SMEs, and academia.

The framework covers Dstl's science and technology programmes, and its scope contributes positively to MOD's SME spend targets.

## Scope

Serapis covers six capability lots spanning:

- C4ISR (command, control, computers, communication, intelligence, surveillance and reconnaissance).
- Space systems.
- Synthetic environments and simulation technology.
- ISTAR technology.
- Command and control systems.
- Data analytics with AI and machine learning capabilities.
