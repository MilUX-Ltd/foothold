# Technology Services (TCS)

## Summary

**Technology Services** is the Government Commercial Agency's (GCA, formerly Crown Commercial Service) main framework for IT strategy, service desk, infrastructure and application support. It sits in a family of separate GCA technology frameworks; earlier versions of this page described those as sub-lots of Technology Services 3, which was not accurate.

**Technology Services 4 (RM6190)** is the current iteration: an **open framework** that started on 12 December 2025 and reopens before the end of years 3 and 5. The MOD's routes-to-market page says it runs until **11 December 2028**; GCA's agreement page shows the current lots expiring on 11 June 2028. **Technology Services 3 (RM6100) ended on 14 March 2026.**

## Technology Services 4 lots

1. Technology and Digital Consultancy Services
2. Transition and Service Integration and Management (SIAM)
3. End User Services (minimum £3m), and 3a for lower value and complexity (up to £3m)
4. Infrastructure Management (minimum £3m), and 4a (up to £3m)
5. Application and Data Management (minimum £3m), and 5a (up to £3m)
6. Major Technology Services Transformation Programmes

The "a" lots are the realistic entry for smaller suppliers.
