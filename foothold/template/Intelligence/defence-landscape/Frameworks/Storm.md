# STORM (Science and Technology Oriented Research and Development in Missile Defence)

## Summary

**STORM** is the UK MOD's framework for **missile defence R&D**, sustaining, exploiting, and developing UK industrial and academic expertise to deliver next-generation and generation-after-next technologies applicable to Integrated Air and Missile Defence. Approved by the Missile Defence R&D Category Strategy in May 2023. Co-located with the Missile Defence Centre in Farnborough.

## Operating model

- **Single-contract framework.** STORM brings core research and technology maturation projects together into a single contract. The unifying aim is to enhance research outcomes and supply-chain efficiency.
- **Duration.** Core three-year period plus a three-year optional extension period.
- **Estimated value.** £110 million to £251 million across the framework.
- **Coordination location.** Co-located with the Missile Defence Centre, Farnborough.

### Threat scope

The contract covers research across the full missile threat landscape:

- Simple non-separating threats.
- Complex separating threats.
- Manoeuvring Re-Entry Vehicles (MaRVs) and Multiple Independently Targetable Re-Entry Vehicles (MIRVs).
- Hypersonic Glide Vehicles (HGVs).
- Hypersonic Cruise Missiles (HCMs).
- Hybrid threats.

### Five research pillars

The research required covers analysis, experimentation, trials, and technology or system development across all five pillars of missile defence:

1. Counter-proliferation.
2. Deterrence.
3. Counterforce.
4. Active defence.
5. Passive defence.

## Recent award

Chemring secured a major contract under STORM: see [UK Defence Journal: Huge £251m UK missile defence contract awarded to Chemring](https://ukdefencejournal.org.uk/huge-251m-uk-missile-defence-contract-awarded-to-chemring/). This appears to be the prime contractor for the consolidated framework.
