# JOSCAR

## Summary

**Joint Supply Chain Accreditation Register (JOSCAR).** A collaborative pre-qualification and compliance register used by the UK defence, security, and aerospace industries as a single repository for supplier risk-assessment, governance, and compliance information. Not itself a procurement framework; a supplier accreditation that primes and buyers require before placing work.

## Operating model

JOSCAR is managed by Hellios Information Limited (a supplier information and risk-management organisation). The accreditation mark is recognised by major defence and aerospace buyers including BAE Systems, Leonardo, NATS, QinetiQ, and Lockheed Martin, and is required or preferred by the Ministry of Defence and many primes for supply-chain entry.

Two-stage registration:

1. **Stage 1.** General supplier information collected, validated, and monitored by Hellios on the buyer community's behalf.
2. **Stage 2.** Deeper questions on compliance, governance, and risk management; required by certain buyers for higher-tier supplier categories.

JOSCAR appears as a near-universal pre-qualification line item in defence framework PQQs. The JOSCAR Governance Group worked with MOD on the Helios SME Portal in response to the MOD's SME Action Plan.
