# Aurora Provider Network

## Summary

The Aurora Provider Network is the supplier framework that sits underneath the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Engineering Delivery Partner (EDP)|Engineering Delivery Partner (EDP)]] arrangement between the UK Ministry of Defence and the Aurora Engineering Partnership. The Partnership comprises QinetiQ (lead), AtkinsRéalis and BMT. EDP itself is a ten-year partnership; the Provider Network is the wider group of specialist companies that the three primes draw on to deliver engineering and support outcomes to DE&S, the wider Ministry of Defence and other UK government departments and agencies.

Aurora is administered by QinetiQ on behalf of the partnership. For defence-sector SMEs the practical importance is that Aurora is one of the larger routes to recurring engineering and support work for DE&S, accessible through a closed supplier network rather than through open competition.

## Operating model

Two supplier streams sit under the Aurora Provider Network umbrella, both administered through the same eSourcing platform:

- **Provider Network — Evolve (PN-Evolve).** The refreshed EDP supplier cohort, replacing the legacy EDP supplier list as part of the 2025 to 2026 refresh exercise.
- **Futures Lab Provider Network (FLPN).** The supplier stream for the UK MOD Futures Lab, a service powered by EDP (in place since 2021, replacing the former Niteworks service) that gives the Front Line Commands and DE&S impartial decision support and constructive challenge. QinetiQ publishes that the Futures Lab provider network spans 300+ organisations of which roughly 74% are micro, small or medium-sized enterprises and ten are UK universities.

Opportunities run through the Aurora Curtis Fitch eSourcing portal at `aurora-evolve.esourcingportal.com`, which became the live system following the 2025 to 2026 refresh. The legacy Aurora portal at `auroraengineeringpartnership.esourcingportal.com` was retained in read-only form for legacy reference. The two portals are separate systems; legacy accounts do not transfer.

## How to get on

Membership is gained through a Pre-Qualification Questionnaire (PQQ) process administered on the Curtis Fitch portal. The partnership controls when the network is open to new applications; intake windows are publicly announced (the 2025 refresh window opened in late June 2025 and ran for around six weeks per QinetiQ's blog).

The PQQ is a standard defence-supply-chain qualification exercise: commercial and assurance items (terms, rate card, insurances, [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/JOSCAR|JOSCAR]] registration, ISO 9001 or equivalent, Cyber Essentials), a technical and governance assessment, and capability-by-capability evidence. The specific question set is published to applicants on the Curtis Fitch portal at registration.

## Refresh 2025 to 2026

The Provider Network was opened to a refresh in 2025 to realign the supplier base to current Aurora and MOD requirements after roughly six years of EDP delivery. QinetiQ published the headline characteristics it was looking for in the refreshed network in a public blog: collaboration across the partners and the Provider Network, SME inclusivity, Social Value delivery, closer strategic supplier relationships, greater pace and flexibility, and support for Defence digital and data transformation. The refresh outcome was the refreshed cohort transacting through `aurora-evolve.esourcingportal.com` from May 2026 onwards, with the legacy supplier list retired.
