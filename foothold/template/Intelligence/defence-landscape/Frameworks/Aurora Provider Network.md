# Aurora Provider Network

## Summary

The Aurora Provider Network is the supplier framework that sits underneath the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Engineering Delivery Partner (EDP)|Engineering Delivery Partner (EDP)]] arrangement between the UK Ministry of Defence and the Aurora Engineering Partnership. The Partnership comprises QinetiQ (lead), AtkinsRéalis and BMT. EDP itself is a ten-year partnership; the Provider Network is the wider group of specialist companies that the three primes draw on to deliver engineering and support outcomes to DE&S, the wider Ministry of Defence and other UK government departments and agencies.

Aurora is administered by QinetiQ on behalf of the partnership. For defence-sector SMEs the practical importance is that Aurora is one of the larger routes to recurring engineering and support work for DE&S, accessible through a closed supplier network rather than through open competition.

## Operating model

Two supplier streams sit under the Aurora Provider Network umbrella, both administered through the same systems:

- **Provider Network — Evolve (PN-Evolve).** The refreshed EDP supplier cohort, replacing the legacy EDP supplier list as part of the 2025 to 2026 refresh exercise.
- **Futures Lab Provider Network (FLPN).** The supplier stream for the UK MoD Futures Lab, a responsive service providing advice and expertise to the Front Line Commands and DE&S. FLPN tasking flows through QuickLooks and longer-running studies.

From 5 May 2026 all opportunities run through the Aurora Curtis Fitch eSourcing portal at `aurora-evolve.esourcingportal.com`. The legacy Aurora portal at `auroraengineeringpartnership.esourcingportal.com` was moved to read-only at the end of May 2026 and retained for legacy reference only. Existing events, contracts and projects on the legacy portal are not migrated to the new portal.

A Kahootz workspace runs alongside the Curtis Fitch portal as the communications and document-sharing platform for the network.

Provider voice into the partnership is channelled through the **Provider Network Advisory Group (PNAG)**, with one representative per supplier-size segment: Micro, Small, Medium and Large. The structure is intended to keep the commercial, technical and governance evolution of the network informed by suppliers across the size range, not just the larger primes.

## How to get on

Membership is gained through a Pre-Qualification Questionnaire (PQQ) process administered via the Curtis Fitch portal. The PQQ has three assessment streams:

- **Commercial Assessment** (pass/fail). Includes signed accession agreement and Aurora terms and conditions, maximum rate card, employer / public / professional indemnity insurance, Cyber Essentials, [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/JOSCAR|JOSCAR]] accreditation, and an ISO 9001 (or equivalent) quality management system.
- **General Technical Assessment** (weighted). Covers governance and assurance, technical delivery, continuous improvement and collaboration, resource management, and staff competency management.
- **Technical Capability Assessment** (weighted). Capability-by-capability questions with case studies per capability category applied for.

Suppliers applying only for the contingent labour category complete a reduced set of questions.

## Refresh 2025 to 2026

The Provider Network was opened to a refresh in 2025 to realign the supplier base to current Aurora and MOD requirements after roughly six years of EDP delivery. QinetiQ published the headline refresh characteristics in a public blog: collaboration across the partners and the Provider Network, SME inclusivity, Social Value delivery, closer strategic supplier relationships, greater pace and flexibility, and support for Defence digital and data transformation.

Outcomes were notified to applicants in late 2025 and early 2026. The new portal at `aurora-evolve.esourcingportal.com` became operational on 5 May 2026, and the refreshed cohort began transacting through it from that date.

## What is different on the refreshed portal

- The Suppliers Catalogue and Provider Network Capability Code Structure are aligned directly to the refreshed cohort.
- Data fields are simplified and the legacy supplier list does not appear; only suppliers successful through the refresh and FLPN members are present.
- Registration on the new portal is fresh, not migrated; suppliers receive an invitation email to the address that took part in the refresh exercise.
- The Curtis Fitch in-portal Knowledge Hub (`?` icon, top right) carries the user-facing help.
