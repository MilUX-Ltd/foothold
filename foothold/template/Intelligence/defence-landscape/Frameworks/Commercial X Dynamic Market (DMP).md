# Commercial X Dynamic Market (DMP)

## Summary

The **Commercial X Dynamic Market Place** is a Procurement Act 2023 dynamic market run by **Commercial X**, a Ministry of Defence commercial unit set up to get innovation into defence faster. Commercial X holds its own approval freedoms for contracts up to £50m and two years, and drives fast-paced acquisition of innovative and digital capability across the MOD.

Unlike a traditional framework, the Dynamic Market is not divided into lots or categories. It is an open, MOD-wide ecosystem of suppliers, organised by maturity rather than by service type. Suppliers join once and can then bid on competitions as they are published. It is built for above-threshold values, with call-offs run through the Competitive Flexible Procedure. There is no fee or levy, membership does not constitute a contract, and it carries no end date. It is deliberately SME-rich and open to consortia.

It exists to close the "valley of death": to give early-stage and scaling suppliers a clear route into the acquisition pipeline, increase competition in R&D and innovation procurement, and improve MOD engagement with the market across the whole development cycle.

## Operating model: Dynamic Readiness Levels

The market asks two questions: can you innovate, and can you scale? Suppliers are placed on **Dynamic Readiness Levels (DRLs)**, which combine the established Technology Readiness Levels (TRL), Commercial Readiness Levels (CRL) and Integration Readiness Levels (IRL). There are two parts and four levels:

**Innovation**

- **DRL 1** (TRL 1 to 3, CRL 1 to 2, IRL 1 to 2): early-stage research and conceptual innovation. Ideation or lab validation, little market traction, immature business model. Suited to feasibility studies and co-creation.
- **DRL 2** (TRL 4 to 6, CRL 3 to 4, IRL 3 to 4): validated prototypes, early market viability through pilots or user testing, interfaces under development.

**Scalability**

- **DRL 3** (TRL 7 to 8, CRL 5 to 7, IRL 5 to 6): mature prototypes or pre-production systems ready for operational demonstration or limited deployment, proven market fit.
- **DRL 4** (TRL 9, CRL 8 to 9, IRL 7 to 9): fully proven, commercially viable, operationally integrated capability with robust support and interoperability with defence systems.

Buyers shape their problem statements against the DRLs, so competitions will indicate which DRL or DRLs are the best fit. Sitting in any DRL neither precludes nor favours a supplier in a competition; it is an indicator of maturity, not a gate.

## How to join

There is no minimum DRL required to join, and the barrier is deliberately low:

1. **Register a company profile on the Central Digital Platform (CDP)** via GOV.UK One Login. The CDP profile is used in the membership application and is the central register for government procurement opportunities.
2. **Complete the Conditions of Membership on the Defence Sourcing Portal (DSP)**, which includes the **DRL self-assessment** (a short yes/no questionnaire per DRL; the summary indicates which DRL or DRLs you sit in). It can be re-taken as you mature.
3. **Disclose security clearances held and/or a willingness to obtain them.** There are no minimum clearance requirements to join, but individual tenders may set their own.
4. **Commercial X assesses the application for compliance and admits the member.** You can request membership at any time, including after a competition has been advertised.

Once admitted: immediate access to competitions, early engagement with MOD stakeholders, regular communication on upcoming opportunities, and invitations to collaborate. Tender windows run to a Procurement Act 2023 minimum of 10 days, extended where complexity warrants.

## Why it matters for a defence-sector founder

This is one of the lowest-friction routes into MOD competition for a small or early-stage company. It is organised by readiness rather than category, has no fee and no obligation, welcomes consortia, and explicitly places no limits on the categories or types of innovation, including services and the information and cognitive domain rather than only physical or technology products. Membership buys visibility of competitions and early engagement; winning work still requires responding to specific competitions under the Competitive Flexible Procedure.

It complements other innovation routes: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/R-Cloud|R-Cloud]] is capped at TRL 6 and the Dynamic Market provides a path to scale beyond it, while NVFi (a single-supplier framework for micro-organisations) sits alongside it as a direct-award option.
