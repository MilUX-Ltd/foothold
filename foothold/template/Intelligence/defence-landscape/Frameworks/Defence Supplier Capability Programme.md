# Defence Supplier Capability Development Programme (DSCDP)

> **Note on naming.** Older sources sometimes call this the "Defence Supplier Capability Programme" (DSCP). The substantive MOD programme with this scope is **Defence Supplier Capability Development Programme (DSCDP)**.

## Summary

The MOD's flagship business-development support programme for **UK-based SMEs and mid-tier suppliers** in defence sector supply chains. Provides up to **£200,000 worth of customised, MOD-funded support per supplier** over a 12-month period, tailored to each supplier's specific developmental requirements through a diagnostic assessment. Managed by **Grant Thornton UK LLP** on the MOD's behalf.

DSCDP is **support-in-kind, not cash funding**: it pays for expert advice, mentoring, and business-development services rather than R&D or capital. Companies are expected to demonstrate matched resource commitment through the time they spend on improvement activities.

GOV.UK: [Defence Supplier Capability Development Programme](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/defence-supplier-capability-development-programme). Coverage: [techUK: MOD launches Defence Supplier Capability Development Programme](https://www.techuk.org/resource/ministry-of-defence-launches.html); [Business Wales: DSCDP](https://businesswales.gov.wales/news-and-blog/defence-supplier-capability-development-programme); [WEAF: DSCDP](https://www.weaf.co.uk/defence-supplier-capability-development-programme/); [FAC: Defence Supplier Capability Development](https://www.fac.org.uk/defence-supplier-capability-development).

## Operating Model

- **Eligibility.** UK-based SMEs and mid-tier suppliers active in defence sector supply chains.
- **Diagnostic-first.** Each supplier accepted onto the programme undergoes an initial diagnostic assessment evaluating its capabilities and needs. Support is then tailored to address the specific gaps identified.
- **Up to £200,000 per supplier.** Customised business-development support over a 12-month engagement.
- **Matched resource.** Companies must demonstrate matched resource commitment through the time they spend on improvement activities, ensuring real commitment to the development plan.
- **Delivered by Grant Thornton UK LLP.** The MOD contracted Grant Thornton to run the programme on its behalf.

### Stated objectives

Per gov.uk and techUK:

- Reduce costs and risk for MOD programmes.
- Support increased domestic and export opportunities.
- Reduce defence supply chain disruption.
- Support defence exports and growth across the UK.
- Develop stronger SME and mid-tier defence companies.

### What support looks like in practice

Typical interventions reported in coverage include strategic planning, financial planning, operational improvement, productivity and efficiency improvement, governance and risk management, export readiness, and supplier accreditation readiness (including [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/JOSCAR|JOSCAR]] preparation). DSCDP is the support-side complement to the procurement-route frameworks elsewhere in this folder.

## How DSCDP differs from DTEP

Both are MOD-funded SME-facing programmes, but the mechanism is different:

| | DSCDP | DTEP |
|---|---|---|
| **Type** | Business-development support (in-kind, advisory) | Grant funding for collaborative R&D projects |
| **Maximum per supplier** | £200,000 of support over 12 months | £500,000 grant (50% of £1m projects) |
| **Delivery partner** | Grant Thornton UK LLP | DASA-managed process, ADS Group panel review from August 2024 |
| **Best for** | Strengthening the supplier's business, not a specific tech project | Maturing a specific innovation toward MOD acquisition |
| **Eligibility** | UK SMEs and mid-tier suppliers | UK-registered Lower Tier Suppliers (typically SMEs) with Higher Tier collaboration |

Both programmes sit inside the wider MOD SME Action Plan stack alongside [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/SME Searchlight|SME Searchlight]] (Dstl research visibility) and the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Helios SME Portal|Helios SME Portal]] (capability discovery).
