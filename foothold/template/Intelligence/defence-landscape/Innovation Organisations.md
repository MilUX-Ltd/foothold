# Innovation Organisations

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/`. When you start engaging with one of the organisations below, add it to your CRM via `/add-organisation` and link from there.

Organisations within the UK defence ecosystem that drive innovation, experimentation, and technology adoption. Useful entry points for a defence-sector SME founder looking to find route-to-market through MOD's innovation channels.

## MOD innovation bodies

- **UKDI — UK Defence Innovation.** Central innovation function within the NAD Group structure. Houses DASA and FCI.
- **DASA — Defence and Security Accelerator.** Finds and funds exploitable innovation. Runs themed competitions, an Ideas Marketplace, and an Open Call for Innovation. Currently a discrete part of UKDI; full integration into UKDI is in progress.
- **FCI — Future Capability Innovation.** Longer-term capability development alongside DASA within UKDI.
- **Defence BattleLab.** Co-collaboration space for rapid experimentation. Based at Dorset Innovation Park.
- **jHub Defence Innovation Accelerator.** Accelerator programmes connecting start-ups with military problems.
- **Dstl — Defence Science and Technology Laboratory.** MOD's science and technology organisation; one of the NAD Group enabling organisations.
- **Rapid Capabilities Office.** Accelerates delivery of urgent operational capabilities.
- **Rapid Innovation Team.** Sits within UKDI.
- **Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office.** Navy-focused innovation office.
- **Centre for Intelligence Innovation.** Innovation hub for the intelligence community.
- **Defence Modelling and Simulation Office.** Central coordination for modelling and simulation across MOD.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/HMGCC Co-Creation|HMGCC Co-Creation]]. Open-innovation arm of His Majesty's Government Communications Centre. Runs at OFFICIAL classification so no clearance is needed to participate.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Integration Design Authority|Integration Design Authority (IDA)]]. CSOC's integration-architecture body; owns the Integrated Procurement Model and Multi-Domain Integration doctrine.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Innovation and Research InSight Unit|Innovation and Research InSight Unit (IRIS)]]. MOD horizon-scanning function inside the Defence Science and Technology group.

## Service-specific innovation

- **Army Innovation / Army Futures / Army Warfighting Experiment (AWE).** British Army innovation channels.
- **Navy Develop / MarWorks.** Royal Navy innovation channels.
- **RAF Innovation / RAF X.** Royal Air Force innovation channels.

## External and commercial innovation connectors

- **London Tech Bridge.** US Navy / Royal Navy collaboration in the London tech ecosystem.
- **Ploughshare Innovations.** Commercialises Dstl intellectual property. Operates the Ploughshare Accelerator Fund.
- **Defence Tech Scaler.** Scaling support for defence technology companies.
- **Regional Innovation Partners.** Regional hubs connecting local innovation ecosystems with defence needs.

## How to engage

- **DASA.** Watch the themed competitions and the Open Call for Innovation. Apply through the DASA submission service when a competition aligns with your capability.
- **BattleLab.** Apply for experimentation slots, or attend public events (BattleLab runs hackathons and demonstrations several times a year).
- **Dstl.** Engage via ASTRID, the Weapons Sector Research Framework, or through DASA-routed competitions. Direct contracting with Dstl typically requires framework membership.
- **jHub.** Apply through their published cohort calls.
- **Service-specific innovation channels.** Each branch publishes points of contact; engagement is typically via DASA-themed competitions or service-specific calls.
