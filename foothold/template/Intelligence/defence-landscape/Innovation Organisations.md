# Innovation Organisations

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/`. When you start engaging with one of the organisations below, add it to your CRM via `/add-organisation` and link from there.

Organisations within the UK defence ecosystem that drive innovation, experimentation, and technology adoption. Useful entry points for a defence-sector SME founder looking to find route-to-market through MOD's innovation channels.

## MOD innovation bodies

- [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|UKDI (UK Defence Innovation)]]. The MOD's single innovation front door, in NA-Innov within the NAD Group. **DASA (the Defence and Security Accelerator) became part of UKDI in February 2026**; the DASA name has gone, though some DASA web addresses remain. UKDI has a ringfenced budget of at least £400m a year (£1.6bn over the four years of the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan]]) and works to five themes: Autonomy, Decision Advantage, Logistics and Support, Effects, and Protection. Routes: Open Call for Innovation, Themed Competitions, Defence Innovation Loans, the Ideas Marketplace (collaboration, not funding) and the Futures Lab. Sources: gov.uk [DASA record](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-and-security-accelerator); [UKDI unifies innovation](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/ukdi-unifies-innovation-to-accelerate-frontline-capabilities-and-drive-national-growth) (15 July 2026); [How to work with UKDI](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-work-with-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi) (22 July 2026, updated 6 October 2026).
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Future Capability Innovation|FCI (Future Capability Innovation)]]. Former DE&S innovation function, now part of UKDI.
- **Defence BattleLab.** Co-collaboration space for rapid experimentation. Based at Dorset Innovation Park.
- **jHub Defence Innovation Accelerator.** Accelerator programmes connecting start-ups with military problems.
- **Dstl (Defence Science and Technology Laboratory).** MOD's science and technology organisation, inside NA-Innov in the NAD Group. See [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|NA-Innov]].
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Royal Air Force/Rapid Capability Office|Rapid Capability Office]]. RAF office that accelerates delivery of urgent operational capabilities.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Rapid Innovation Team|Rapid Innovation Team]]. Sits within UKDI; uses commercially available dual-use technology on urgent operational problems.
- **Taskforce RAID (Rapid AI Delivery).** Set up in June 2026 to get AI to the front line, reporting to the Chief of the Defence Staff, with £100m over four years in the Defence Investment Plan. Source: gov.uk [Rapid AI Delivery Taskforce (TF RAID)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/rapid-ai-delivery-taskforce-tf-raid).
- **Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office.** Navy-focused innovation office.
- **Centre for Intelligence Innovation.** Innovation hub for the intelligence community.
- **Defence Modelling and Simulation Office.** Central coordination for modelling and simulation across MOD.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/HMGCC Co-Creation|HMGCC Co-Creation]]. Open-innovation arm of His Majesty's Government Communications Centre. Runs at OFFICIAL classification so no clearance is needed to participate.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Integration Design Authority|Integration Design Authority (IDA)]]. CSOC's integration-architecture body; owns the Integrated Procurement Model and Multi-Domain Integration doctrine.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Innovation and Research InSight Unit|Innovation and Research InSight Unit (IRIS)]]. MOD horizon-scanning function inside the Defence Science and Technology group.

## Service-specific innovation

- **Army Innovation / Army Futures / Army Warfighting Experiment (AWE).** British Army innovation channels.
- **Navy Develop / MarWorks.** Royal Navy innovation channels.
- **RAF Innovation / RAF X.** Royal Air Force innovation channels.

## External and commercial innovation connectors

- **London Tech Bridge.** US Navy / Royal Navy collaboration in the London tech ecosystem.
- **Ploughshare Innovations.** Commercialises Dstl intellectual property. Operates the Ploughshare Accelerator Fund.
- **Defence Tech Scaler.** Scaling support for defence technology companies.
- **UKDI Innovation Partners.** Civil servants who advise on UKDI services and competitions. Ask through your UKDI submission service account; a partner contacts you within 15 working days. UKDI also administers the [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/Regional Defence and Security Clusters|Regional Defence and Security Clusters]].

## How to engage

- **UKDI (formerly DASA).** Check the [live funding opportunities](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/live-funding-opportunities) page, not old competition lists. At its 1 October 2026 update: no themed competitions open; the Defence Open Call not yet reopened (a new "Innovating for Defence" route is in development, no date); Defence Innovation Loans paused; the **Security Open Call** open, Cycle 2 closing 29 October 2026. Apply through the UKDI submission service.
- **BattleLab.** Apply for experimentation slots, or attend public events (BattleLab runs hackathons and demonstrations several times a year).
- **Dstl.** Engage via ASTRID, the Weapons Sector Research Framework, or through UKDI competitions. Direct contracting with Dstl typically requires framework membership.
- **jHub.** Apply through their published cohort calls.
- **Service-specific innovation channels.** Each branch publishes points of contact; engagement is typically via UKDI themed competitions or service-specific calls.
