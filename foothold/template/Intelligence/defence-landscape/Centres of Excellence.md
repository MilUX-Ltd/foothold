# Centres of Excellence

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/<bucket>/`. This page is one functional view over the landscape. Don't put entity-specific content here; put it on the entity's CRM page and link.

Specialist centres within the UK defence ecosystem providing domain expertise, testing, and standards. Substantial centres typically warrant their own organisation page under `CRM/organisations/`.

## UK Defence CoEs

- **[[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence AI Centre (DAIC)|Defence AI Centre (DAIC)]]**: AI policy and governance. The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] says the MOD is developing DAIC as Defence's enduring centre for AI delivery. Fast operational AI work also runs through [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Taskforce RAID|Taskforce RAID]].
- **Defence Wargaming Centre** — wargaming and analytical support for operational planning, concept development, capability assessment.
- **Defence Availability Centre** — equipment availability and supportability.
- **Defence Materials CoE** — materials science and engineering.
- **CoE in Defence, Risk & Resilience**.
- **Defence Standardisation** — sets and maintains defence standards (DEFSTANs, STANAGs).
- **Digital Engineering Centre** — model-based systems engineering, digital twins, digital threads.
- **Uncrewed Systems Centre (USC)**: Defence's central authority for uncrewed systems, opened on 12 June 2026 at the **DroneTEX** facility in **Swindon** (545,000 sq ft, described by the MOD as Europe's largest drone testing centre). The Defence Investment Plan puts **£310m** into it. It sets common standards, open architectures, data frameworks and assurance models, leads on a regulatory pathway for defence autonomy, and works with industry, allies and partners. An Uncrewed Systems Taskforce sits alongside it to field capability with industry "in weeks not years". For a founder building drones, autonomy or counter-drone kit, this is the centre to know. Sources: [GOV.UK, 12 June 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/europes-largest-drone-testing-centre-opens-in-swindon-to-boost-defence-innovation); [GOV.UK: The Defence Investment Plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-defence-investment-plan).
- **National Drone Hub** — testing and evaluation for uncrewed aerial systems.
- **Test & Evaluation Innovation Gateway** — entry point for innovative T&E services.
- **T&E Marketplace** — platform for accessing T&E capabilities across the defence estate.
- **Defence Modelling & Simulation Office** — central coordination for M&S capability, standards, tools.

## NATO Centres of Excellence

NATO-accredited centres specialising in specific domains (e.g., Cyber Defence CoE in Tallinn (CCDCOE); Energy Security CoE; Strategic Communications CoE). Provide expertise, training, and doctrine development. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/NATO and International|NATO and International]].
