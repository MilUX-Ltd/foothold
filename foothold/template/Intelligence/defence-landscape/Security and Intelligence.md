# Security and Intelligence

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/<bucket>/`. This page is one functional view over the landscape. Don't put entity-specific content here; put it on the entity's CRM page and link.

UK security and intelligence organisations relevant to defence technology and innovation. These agencies have their own innovation pipelines and procurement routes. Each substantive entity warrants its own page under `CRM/organisations/`.

## Intelligence Agencies

- **MI6 — Secret Intelligence Service** — foreign intelligence.
- **MI5 — The Security Service** — domestic counter-intelligence.
- **GCHQ — Government Communications Headquarters** — SIGINT and information assurance.
   - [[CRM/organisations/mod/HMGCC Co-Creation|HMGCC Co-Creation]] — His Majesty's Government Communications Centre's open-innovation arm; runs at OFFICIAL classification.
   - [[CRM/organisations/government/NCSC|NCSC — National Cyber Security Centre]] — cyber guidance, incident response, Active Cyber Defence.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Joint Terrorism Analysis Centre|Joint Terrorism Analysis Centre (JTAC)]] — analyses intelligence on international terrorism, sets UK threat levels.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/National Protective Security Authority|National Protective Security Authority (NPSA)]] — protective security advice; security clearance policy.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/UK NACE|UK NACE — National Authority for Counter-Eavesdropping]] — third UK National Technical Authority alongside NCSC and NPSA; technical security.
- **Joint State Terrorism Threats** — state-based threats.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Intelligence|Defence Intelligence]] — MOD's intelligence assessment function.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/National Cyber Force|National Cyber Force]] — offensive cyber operations alongside GCHQ, MOD, MI6, Dstl.

## Cross-government coordination

- [[CRM/organisations/government/National Security Secretariat|National Security Secretariat (NSS)]] — Cabinet Office secretariat supporting the National Security Council and National Security Adviser.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Resilience Directorate|Resilience Directorate]] — Cabinet Office; owns the UK Government Resilience Framework.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/UK Integrated Security Fund|UK Integrated Security Fund (UKISF)]] — Cabinet Office cross-government security funding pot; CSSF successor.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/National Security Online Information Team|National Security Online Information Team (NSOIT)]] — DSIT-housed lead on mis- and disinformation that touches national security.

## Counter-terrorism delivery

- [[CRM/organisations/government/Homeland Security Group|Homeland Security Group (HSG)]] — Home Office directorate; CT policy and CNI protection.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Counter Terrorism Policing|Counter Terrorism Policing (CTP)]] — national policing network for counter-terrorism.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/ProtectUK|ProtectUK]] — public-facing protective-security platform operated by NaCTSO.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/National Crime Agency|National Crime Agency (NCA)]] — serious and organised crime; adjacent operational partner to NCSC on cyber.

## Home Office Innovation

- [[CRM/organisations/government/Accelerated Capability Environment (ACE)|Accelerated Capability Environment (ACE)]] — Home Office capability within Homeland Security Group. 260+ organisations and experts. Key entry point for SMEs with cyber, data, AI capabilities relevant to homeland security.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Joint Security and Resilience Centre|Joint Security and Resilience Centre (JSaRC)]] — Home Office industry-engagement hub for security and resilience tech. Jointly funded by the UK security industry and the Home Office.

## Clearances, Regulations & Compliance

For SMEs working in the security space:

- **Security Clearance (SC)** — required for OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE and above.
- **Developed Vetting (DV)** — required for SECRET and above.
- **List X** — facility vetting for handling classified information on company premises.
- **Cyber Essentials / Cyber Essentials Plus** — baseline cyber security certification.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/JOSCAR|JOSCAR]] — Joint Supply Chain Accreditation Register.
