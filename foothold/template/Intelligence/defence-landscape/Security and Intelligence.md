# Security and Intelligence

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/<bucket>/`. This page is one functional view over the landscape. Don't put entity-specific content here; put it on the entity's CRM page and link.

UK security and intelligence organisations relevant to defence technology and innovation. These agencies have their own innovation pipelines and procurement routes. Each substantive entity warrants its own page under `CRM/organisations/`.

## Intelligence Agencies

- **MI6 — Secret Intelligence Service** — foreign intelligence.
- **MI5 — The Security Service** — domestic counter-intelligence.
- **GCHQ — Government Communications Headquarters** — SIGINT and information assurance.
   - **HMGCC Co-Creation** — GCHQ's technology arm; runs co-creation programmes.
   - [[CRM/organisations/government/NCSC|NCSC — National Cyber Security Centre]] — cyber guidance, incident response, Active Cyber Defence.
- **Joint Terrorism Analysis Centre (JTAC)** — analyses intelligence on international terrorism, sets UK threat levels.
- **National Protective Security Authority** — protective security advice; security clearance policy.
- **Joint State Terrorism Threats** — state-based threats.
- **Defence Intelligence** — MOD's intelligence assessment function.
- **National Cyber Force** — offensive cyber operations alongside GCHQ, MOD, MI6, Dstl.

## Home Office Innovation

- **Accelerated Capability Environment (ACE)** — Home Office capability within Homeland Security Group. 260+ organisations and experts. Key entry point for SMEs with cyber, data, AI capabilities relevant to homeland security.

## Clearances, Regulations & Compliance

For SMEs working in the security space:

- **Security Clearance (SC)** — required for OFFICIAL-SENSITIVE and above.
- **Developed Vetting (DV)** — required for SECRET and above.
- **List X** — facility vetting for handling classified information on company premises.
- **Cyber Essentials / Cyber Essentials Plus** — baseline cyber security certification.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/JOSCAR|JOSCAR]] — Joint Supply Chain Accreditation Register.
