# MOD Structure

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/`. When you start engaging with an MOD body, add it to your CRM via `/add-organisation` and link from there.

The Ministry of Defence internal organisation, focused on bodies relevant to defence innovation and procurement.

## National Armaments Director Group (NAD Group)

Established April 2026, the NAD Group is the senior-level organisation within UK Defence responsible for delivering warfighting capability. It brings together a set of enabling organisations and named functional areas under the National Armaments Director. The structure is still settling as of mid-2026.

GOV.UK reference: [National Armaments Director (NAD) Group](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/national-armaments-director-nad-group)

### Enabling organisations (the operational arms)

- **Dstl — Defence Science and Technology Laboratory.** Science and technology arm.
- **Defence Support.** Support and logistics functions across defence.
- **National Armaments Materiel (NA-M).** Procurement and equipment support; the successor to DE&S (Defence Equipment & Support). Around 11,000 staff.
- **Defence Infrastructure Organisation (DIO).** Manages the MOD estate, training areas, and facilities.
- **Defence Digital (NA-D&D).** Delivers IT and digital services across defence.

### Functional pillars (the cross-cutting functions)

- **National Armaments International (NA-Intl).** International collaboration and exports. Includes UK Defence and Security Exports (UKDSE).
- **National Armaments Commercial & Industry (NA-C&I).** Commercial and industry functions. Includes the National Shipbuilding Office (NSO).
- **National Armaments Plans & Portfolios (NA-P&P).** Portfolio management and capability planning.
- **National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov).** Innovation pillar. Houses UK Defence Innovation (UKDI), which in turn houses DASA (Defence and Security Accelerator) and FCI (Future Capability Innovation).
- **National Armaments Corporate.** Corporate and shared services. Includes UK Hydrographic Office (UKHO), Oil and Pipelines Agency (OPA), and Single Source Regulations Office (SSRO).
- **National Armaments Logistics & Support (NA-L&S).** Materiel logistics and support.

## Other MOD organisations (outside the NAD Group)

- **Defence Office for Small Business Growth (DOSBG).** MOD's dedicated SME engagement function.
- **Defence Nuclear Organisation.** Nuclear deterrent programme. Includes AWE Nuclear Security Technologies and the Submarine Delivery Agency.
- **Defence Futures.** Strategic futures and foresight function.
- **Innovation and Research InSight Unit (IRIS).** Strategic advice on emerging science and technology.
- **Defence Electronics and Components Agency (Deca).** Defence electronics support.

## Service-specific innovation channels

- **Army.** Army Innovation, Army Futures, Army Warfighting Experiment (AWE).
- **Royal Navy.** Navy Develop, Royal Marines innovation programmes, UK Hydrographic Office.
- **RAF.** RAF Innovation, RAF X (rapid experimentation), RAF Projects and Programmes.
- **Joint / cross-service.** Strategic Command, Cyber and Specialist Operations Command, Defence Cyber Marvel (annual exercise), Military Intelligence Services, Integration Design Authority, Joint Counter-UAS Office.
