# MOD Structure

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/`. When you start engaging with an MOD body, add it to your CRM via `/add-organisation` and link from there.

The Ministry of Defence internal organisation, focused on bodies relevant to defence innovation and procurement. Each body is placed once here; the CRM pages carry the detail.

## National Armaments Director Group (NAD Group)

Established in March 2025, the NAD Group is the part of the MOD responsible for delivering warfighting capability: innovation, acquisition and support under the National Armaments Director. The Defence Investment Plan (June 2026) describes it as the single integrated delivery entity through which most of the plan's investment will flow. It has nine areas, listed below with the names gov.uk uses. The parent page is [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/NAD Group|NAD Group]].

GOV.UK reference: [National Armaments Director (NAD) Group](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/national-armaments-director-nad-group)

Notes:

- **Dstl sits inside NA-Innov.** Earlier versions of this page listed it as a separate "enabling organisation"; gov.uk now places it in NA-Innov.
- **DE&S.** Its delivery functions now sit in NA-M. The executive agency is covered on [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Delivery Executive|National Armaments Delivery Executive]]. Many framework and programme pages still name DE&S as the customer, because the contracts do.
- **A Defence Investment Unit** inside the NAD Group is the MOD's centre of expertise for finance and investment (Defence Investment Plan, section 1.4).
- The NAD Group CRM pages carry more detail on each area, including sponsored public bodies such as the UK Hydrographic Office and the Single Source Regulations Office. Where they and gov.uk differ, gov.uk wins.

## Other MOD organisations (outside the NAD Group)

- [[CRM/organisations/government/Defence Office for Small Business Growth|Defence Office for Small Business Growth (DOSBG)]]. MOD's SME team, launched 27 January 2026. gov.uk does not say which part of the MOD it sits in, and the gov.uk NAD Group page does not list it.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Nuclear Organisation|Defence Nuclear Organisation]]. Nuclear deterrent programme. Includes [[CRM/organisations/mod/AWE Nuclear Security Technologies|AWE Nuclear Security Technologies]] and the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Submarine Delivery Agency|Submarine Delivery Agency]].
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Futures|Defence Futures]]. Strategic futures and foresight function.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Innovation and Research InSight Unit|Innovation and Research InSight Unit (IRIS)]]. Strategic advice on emerging science and technology.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Electronics and Components Agency|Defence Electronics and Components Agency (Deca)]]. Defence electronics support.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Industrial Joint Council|Defence Industrial Joint Council (DIJC)]]. MOD's main strategic mechanism for defence-sector engagement under the Defence Industrial Strategy 2025. Replaces the former Defence Suppliers Forum.

## Military commands and service innovation channels

- **Army.** Army Innovation, Army Futures, Army Warfighting Experiment (AWE).
- **Royal Navy.** Navy Develop, Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office (DCTO), Royal Marines innovation programmes.
- **RAF.** RAF Innovation, RAF X (rapid experimentation), Rapid Capability Office.
- **[[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command|Cyber & Specialist Operations Command (CSOC)]].** The fourth military command, formerly Strategic Command (renamed 1 September 2025). Hosts jHub, the Defence AI Centre, Military Intelligence Services and the Integration Design Authority. Source: gov.uk [Cyber & Specialist Operations Command established](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/cyber-specialist-operations-command-established-to-tackle-the-threats-of-today-and-tomorrow).
- **Joint / cross-service.** Joint Counter-UAS Office; Defence Cyber Marvel (annual exercise); Taskforce RAID (Rapid AI Delivery), which reports to the Chief of the Defence Staff.
