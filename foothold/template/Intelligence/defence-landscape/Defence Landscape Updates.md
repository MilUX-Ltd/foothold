# What's New in the Defence Landscape

What changed in Foothold's defence landscape pages, and when, newest first. Run `/foothold-update` to bring each change into your vault. Every change is checked against a public source, linked from the page it changed.

## 15 October 2026 (Foothold 2.2.0)

A full review of the landscape against public sources from May to October 2026.

### Government and the MOD

- **DSIT has been broken up.** Science and innovation moved to the renamed Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST), which now sponsors UKRI, ARIA, the UK Space Agency and the Export Control Joint Unit. Digital moved to the Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport, and AI to the Cabinet Office. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Government Departments|Government Departments]], [[CRM/organisations/government/BIST|BIST]] and [[CRM/organisations/government/DSIT|DSIT]].
- **Raising money?** The Investment Security Unit, which screens investments under the National Security and Investment Act, now sits in BIST. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Government Departments|Government Departments]].
- **DASA is now UK Defence Innovation (UKDI)**, the single front door for MOD innovation, with five themes and Innovation Partners who reply within 15 working days. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI: How to Engage]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Innovation Organisations|Innovation Organisations]].
- **Crown Commercial Service is now the Government Commercial Agency (GCA)**, from 1 April 2026. Framework pages use the new name. See [[CRM/organisations/government/Crown Commercial Service|Government Commercial Agency]].
- **Strategic Command is now Cyber & Specialist Operations Command.**
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/MOD Structure|MOD Structure]] is brought into line with the gov.uk NAD Group page.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Export Controls Orientation|Export Controls Orientation]]: F680 applications now use LITE, there is a new low-value open licence under the Agreement on Defence Export Controls, and the control list classification service is paused.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Security Clearance Primer|Security Clearance Primer]]: notes on the government vetting review and UK Security Vetting's planned move.

### Money and plans

- New page: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]]. The June 2026 plan read as a demand signal for SMEs: £298bn over four years, where it goes, and what it cancelled.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Funding Routes|Funding Routes]] adds the 2026 money: UK Export Finance's £50bn Defence Export Fund, the National Wealth Fund, the Sovereign AI Fund, Innovate UK's defence package and NATO DIANA's 2027 cycle.
- **The Defence Open Call has not reopened** and **Defence Innovation Loans are paused**; the Security Open Call is open. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI: How to Engage]].
- New page: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Commercial X and Defence Unicorns|Commercial X and Defence Unicorns]]. Fast contracts up to £50m for firms new to the MOD, from the 2026 SME Action Plan.
- New page: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Single-Source Contract Rules|Single-Source Contract Rules]]. The 2026 profit-incentive change and what is still to come.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Founder's Procurement Playbook|Founder's Procurement Playbook]] is rewritten for 2026.

### Programmes

- New pages: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Taskforce RAID|Taskforce RAID]] (the MOD's £100m AI delivery taskforce), [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Hybrid Navy|Hybrid Navy]] (the uncrewed fleet replacing the Type 83 and Type 32 plans), [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Project CORVUS|Project CORVUS]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Project NYX|Project NYX]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Counter-drone - PRESAGIUM and PANOPTES|Counter-drone: PRESAGIUM and PANOPTES]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Digital Targeting Web|Digital Targeting Web]].
- **The Multi-Role Strike Ship is cancelled**, in favour of a UK–Netherlands amphibious ship. **SKYNET 6 narrowband is not going ahead**; SKYNET 5 is extended. NavyX has merged into the Royal Navy's DCTO.
- GCAP, AUKUS Pillar 2, ASGARD, Typhoon, Fleet Solid Support, Type 31 and the New Medium Helicopter gain their Defence Investment Plan figures. The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Programmes Index|Programmes Index]] has no broken links.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Centres of Excellence|Centres of Excellence]] adds the Uncrewed Systems Centre in Swindon.

### Frameworks and events

- G-Cloud 15, DOS7 and Technology Services 4, under the Government Commercial Agency. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Frameworks Index|Frameworks Index]].
- [[Intelligence/events/Defence Events Calendar|Defence Events Calendar]] has next-edition dates: DPRTE has moved to Farnborough, and DSEI UK runs 7 to 10 September 2027.

### The pack itself

- Note templates in a new `Templates/` folder, `Inbox/` and `Attachments/` folders, and one shape for every folder Guide. The Templates Guide shows a one-minute Obsidian set-up.
- The `process-interviewer` and `prompt-master` skills are no longer shipped. If you already have them, they stay in your vault; `/process-map` covers getting a process out of your head.
