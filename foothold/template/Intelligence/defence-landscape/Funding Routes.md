# Funding Routes

The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Founder's Procurement Playbook|Procurement Playbook]] answers "how do I sell". This page answers "how do I stay alive long enough to sell". Award sizes, timelines and terms move; check the scheme's own gov.uk page before planning around a number here. For MOD innovation money, the single place to check is UK Defence Innovation's [live funding page](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/live-funding-opportunities); this page deliberately does not list closing dates that will go stale.

**What changed in 2026.** DASA is now part of **UK Defence Innovation (UKDI)**. The Defence Open Call has not reopened (a new "Innovating for Defence" route is being developed, no date), Defence Innovation Loans are **paused**, and the **Security Open Call** is open. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI: How to Engage]].

## The routes compared

| Route | Typical size | Time to money | Equity cost | What it is really for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| UKDI competitions (formerly DASA) | £100k to £1m+ per phase; Security Open Call up to £350k | 3 to 9 months | None | Funding a development phase against a named defence or security problem |
| Defence Innovation Loans (**paused**) | £100k to £1m when running | Months | None (repayable, below-market rate) | Bridging commercialisation between grant phases; paused while UKDI builds a new service |
| Commercial X and Defence Unicorns | Contracts up to £50m and under two years; first Unicorns round up to £4m each | Varies | None | Revenue, not a grant: accelerated contracts, with Defence Unicorns aimed at firms new to the MOD |
| Innovate UK / SBRI contracts | £50k to £2m | 3 to 9 months | None | Dual-use R&D contracts; keeps you civil-market credible |
| DTEP (launch to be confirmed) | Match-funded grants | Months | None | Collaborative projects with a higher-tier supplier; buys a prime relationship too |
| NATO DIANA | €100k, then up to €300k more for top performers | Annual cycle | None | A six-month NATO accelerator for dual-use technology (typically TRL 4 to 7) |
| UK Sovereign AI Fund | £1m to £10m equity | Rolling | Equity | AI start-ups in its priority sectors; comes with compute and visa help |
| NSSIF | Investment rounds | 6 to 12 months | Equity | Patient state-aligned capital for national-security-relevant tech |
| ARIA | Programme-dependent | Programme-dependent | None | High-risk research inside an ARIA programme thesis, not general funding |
| Venture capital | £250k to £5m+ (seed/A) | 3 to 12 months | 15 to 30% per round | Scaling a proven wedge; defence-literate funds only |

## New money in 2026

Public money for defence and dual-use firms widened in 2026. Most of it is not a grant you apply for; know which door each sits behind.

- **UK Export Finance: £50bn Defence Export Fund** (30 June 2026). Loans and guarantees for British defence companies of all sizes, whether already exporting or looking to expand internationally: UKEF guarantees bank loans to exporters fulfilling contracts and lends to overseas buyers of British defence products. Raises UKEF's capacity to £130bn. Talk to UKEF before you price an export deal. [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/ukef-launches-50-billion-defence-export-fund-to-back-british-defence-industry)
- **National Wealth Fund: first defence investment** (13 May 2026). £25m into Rowden, a Bristol engineering firm building sensing and data systems; the fund describes it as its first investment directly supporting defence, national security and resilience. Worth knowing if you are a scale-up with large capital needs. [National Wealth Fund](https://nationalwealthfund.org.uk/news-and-publications/news/national-wealth-fund-invests-25-million-in-uk-engineering-business-rowden/)
- **British Business Bank: defence commitment, no named scheme yet.** The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] says the government is working with the British Business Bank to improve access to capital for innovative defence and dual-use SMEs "through the investment of hundreds of millions". No scheme name, size or application route has been published; watch the British Business Bank. [GOV.UK: The Defence Investment Plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-defence-investment-plan), paragraph 13.
- **UK Sovereign AI Fund.** A £500m government-funded venture fund for British AI start-ups, pre-seed to growth: equity of £1m to £10m on market terms, fully funded access to national AI supercomputers, fast-tracked visas and access to national datasets. Priority sectors: compute and infrastructure, foundation models, AI in health and life sciences, scientific discovery, and trust, safety and assurance. No deadlines. The MOD lists it among its routes to market. [sovereignai.gov.uk](https://www.sovereignai.gov.uk/)
- **Innovate UK defence package** (17 July 2026). The first £20m package in Innovate UK's defence innovation portfolio, including £15m for dual-use innovation, and the **STRONG** programme (with the High Value Manufacturing Catapult and Make UK Defence) expanding to 30 SMEs developing dual-use aviation and counter-drone technology. No application route was published with the announcement; watch Innovate UK Business Connect. [Innovate UK Business Connect](https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/news/defence-innovation-turning-strategic-ambition-into-economic-growth/)
- **NATO DIANA 2027 cycle.** The 2027 call ran from 1 June to 3 July 2026 with six challenges; selected companies get €100k, with up to €300k more for top performers, in a six-month accelerator starting January 2027. Companies must be incorporated, headquartered and majority-owned in NATO nations. No 2028 call date had been published by October 2026. [Innovate UK Business Connect](https://iuk-business-connect.org.uk/opportunities/nato-defence-innovation-accelerator-diana-2027-cohort/)
- **Defence Unicorns and Commercial X.** Up to £100m over four years in accelerated contracts for British tech firms with little or no MOD business. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Commercial X and Defence Unicorns|Commercial X and Defence Unicorns]].

## Choosing between them

Three questions cut the table down fast.

1. **How many months of runway remain?** Under six: competitions and grants will not save you; the fastest cash is revenue (subcontract, services). Defence Innovation Loans are paused, so do not count on one. Six to eighteen: competitions, SBRI and DTEP are live options. Eighteen plus: you can afford the VC dance or an NSSIF conversation.
2. **What does equity cost you later?** Non-dilutive first is the usual discipline: a UKDI competition phase, and an Innovation Loan when loans restart, can carry a company to TRL 6 without giving anything up. But non-dilutive money buys phases, not scale; if the plan needs a team of twenty, someone has to buy shares eventually.
3. **Does the funder's clock match yours?** Grant assessment, contracting and payment cycles routinely add months. Whatever the scheme page says, plan cash assuming award-to-invoice-paid takes a quarter longer than advertised.

## Mixing routes

The strong pattern in UK defence tech is sequenced, not simultaneous: competition win (proof and a defence customer relationship), then DTEP or BattleLab (prime relationship and operational evidence), then either revenue or a priced round with the evidence pack those phases built. The weak pattern is grant-chaining: a company that strings competition wins for years without an exploitation route is a research charity with a cap table. The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Founder's Procurement Playbook|Playbook]]'s failure modes section applies in full.
