# Government Departments

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/`. When you start engaging with a department or agency, add it to your CRM via `/add-organisation` and link from there.

UK Government departments with roles in defence, security, and innovation procurement. The defence-sector ecosystem reaches well beyond MOD; this page is the orienting map.

> **July 2026 machinery of government changes.** DSIT is being dissolved. Science and innovation moved to the renamed Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST, formerly the Department for Business and Trade). Digital moved to the Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport. AI strategy and the AI Security Institute moved to the Cabinet Office. Source: gov.uk [Machinery of government changes fact sheet](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/machinery-of-government-changes-fact-sheet) (22 July 2026).

## Departments

- **Ministry of Defence (MOD).** Central department for defence. Most internal MOD bodies sit under the NAD Group structure. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/MOD Structure|MOD Structure]] for the detail.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Cabinet Office|Cabinet Office]]. Coordinates cross-government security policy. Houses [[CRM/organisations/government/National Security Secretariat|National Security Secretariat (NSS)]], [[CRM/organisations/government/Resilience Directorate|Resilience Directorate]], [[CRM/organisations/government/UK Integrated Security Fund|UK Integrated Security Fund (UKISF)]], and the Joint Intelligence Organisation. Since July 2026 it also holds AI strategy, public-sector AI adoption and the AI Security Institute. UK Security Vetting is part of the Cabinet Office; see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Security Clearance Primer|Security Clearance Primer]].
- [[CRM/organisations/government/BIST|BIST (Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade)]], formerly the Department for Business and Trade. Business growth, trade, and now science and innovation. Sponsors [[CRM/organisations/government/UKRI|UKRI]] and [[CRM/organisations/government/ARIA|ARIA]]; the [[CRM/organisations/government/UK Space Agency|UK Space Agency]] and the Export Control Joint Unit are part of it; it works with the British Business Bank. The Investment Security Unit is moving to it (see below).
- **Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport (DCMS).** Expanded in July 2026 to take digital: the Government Digital Service, telecoms, online safety and digital identity policy.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/DSIT|DSIT (Department for Science, Innovation and Technology)]]. Being dissolved; functions moved to BIST, DCMS and the Cabinet Office. The page is kept so older links resolve.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Home Office|Home Office]]. Immigration, drugs policy, crime, counter-terrorism, policing. Houses [[CRM/organisations/government/Homeland Security Group|Homeland Security Group (HSG)]] which holds [[CRM/organisations/government/Accelerated Capability Environment (ACE)|ACE]], [[CRM/organisations/government/Joint Security and Resilience Centre|JSaRC]], [[CRM/organisations/government/National Protective Security Authority|NPSA]], and [[CRM/organisations/government/Joint Terrorism Analysis Centre|JTAC]]. Operational delivery via [[CRM/organisations/government/Counter Terrorism Policing|Counter Terrorism Policing]] / [[CRM/organisations/government/ProtectUK|ProtectUK]] and the [[CRM/organisations/government/National Crime Agency|National Crime Agency]].
- [[CRM/organisations/government/FCDO|FCDO — Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office]]. UK foreign policy and international development. Houses [[CRM/organisations/government/UK NACE|UK NACE]] (via FCDO Services). Relevant to defence exports and Five Eyes / NATO partnerships.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Crown Commercial Service|Government Commercial Agency (GCA, formerly Crown Commercial Service)]]. The central government procurement agency, formed on 1 April 2026 from Crown Commercial Service and Cabinet Office commercial teams. Runs G-Cloud, DOS, Technology Services, and other public-sector frameworks; see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Frameworks Index|Frameworks Index]].
- [[CRM/organisations/government/National Security Online Information Team|National Security Online Information Team (NSOIT)]]. Previously in DSIT; parent under review following the July 2026 machinery of government changes.

## For founders raising money: the Investment Security Unit

If you take investment, the **National Security and Investment Act 2021** may apply. Acquisitions of control in 17 sensitive areas of the economy, defence among them, must be notified to government before they complete, and government can review other deals. The **Investment Security Unit** runs the regime. The July 2026 changes move it from the Cabinet Office to BIST.

What it means in practice: a funding round, a strategic investor or an acquisition offer can trigger a mandatory notification, and completing a notifiable deal without approval can make it void. Ask your lawyers early, and build the review time into your round. Guidance: gov.uk [National Security and Investment Act 2021](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/national-security-and-investment-act) and [notifiable acquisitions in the 17 areas](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-security-and-investment-act-guidance-on-notifiable-acquisitions).

## Where the cross-government work happens

Many defence-relevant programmes are joint between MOD and another department. Common patterns:

- **MOD + BIST.** Research and innovation funding (UKRI and Innovate UK, ARIA), export licensing through ECJU, and investment screening. The MOD's own innovation route is UK Defence Innovation (UKDI, formerly DASA).
- **MOD + Home Office.** Border, counter-terror, and resilience-adjacent technology.
- **MOD + Cabinet Office.** National-security strategy, resilience, integrated security planning, vetting, and government AI.
- **MOD exports.** Defence export promotion moved from DBT to the MOD in July 2025. The Defence Investment Plan (30 June 2026) describes UK Defence and Security Exports (UKDSE) as now inside the NAD Group, in NA-Intl; gov.uk's UKDSE page still says it is part of BIST, so check gov.uk if in doubt.
