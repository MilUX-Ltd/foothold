# Government Departments

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/`. When you start engaging with a department or agency, add it to your CRM via `/add-organisation` and link from there.

UK Government departments with roles in defence, security, and innovation procurement. The defence-sector ecosystem reaches well beyond MOD; this page is the orienting map.

## Departments

- **Ministry of Defence (MOD).** Central department for defence. Most internal MOD bodies sit under the NAD Group structure. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/MOD Structure|MOD Structure]] for the detail.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Cabinet Office|Cabinet Office]]. Coordinates cross-government security policy. Houses [[CRM/organisations/government/National Security Secretariat|National Security Secretariat (NSS)]], [[CRM/organisations/government/Resilience Directorate|Resilience Directorate]], [[CRM/organisations/government/UK Integrated Security Fund|UK Integrated Security Fund (UKISF)]], and the Joint Intelligence Organisation.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/DSIT|DSIT — Department for Science, Innovation and Technology]]. Science, research, and technology policy. Houses [[CRM/organisations/government/UKRI|UKRI]], [[CRM/organisations/government/ARIA|ARIA]], [[CRM/organisations/government/UK Space Agency|UK Space Agency]], and [[CRM/organisations/government/National Security Online Information Team|NSOIT]].
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Home Office|Home Office]]. Immigration, drugs policy, crime, counter-terrorism, policing. Houses [[CRM/organisations/government/Homeland Security Group|Homeland Security Group (HSG)]] which holds [[CRM/organisations/government/Accelerated Capability Environment (ACE)|ACE]], [[CRM/organisations/government/Joint Security and Resilience Centre|JSaRC]], [[CRM/organisations/government/National Protective Security Authority|NPSA]], and [[CRM/organisations/government/Joint Terrorism Analysis Centre|JTAC]]. Operational delivery via [[CRM/organisations/government/Counter Terrorism Policing|Counter Terrorism Policing]] / [[CRM/organisations/government/ProtectUK|ProtectUK]] and the [[CRM/organisations/government/National Crime Agency|National Crime Agency]].
- [[CRM/organisations/government/FCDO|FCDO — Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office]]. UK foreign policy and international development. Houses [[CRM/organisations/government/UK NACE|UK NACE]] (via FCDO Services). Relevant to defence exports and Five Eyes / NATO partnerships.
- **Department for Business and Trade (DBT).** Supports UK business growth and international trade. Includes defence exports and SME support.
- **Crown Commercial Service (CCS).** Government procurement body. Runs G-Cloud, DOS, TCS, and other public-sector frameworks — see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Frameworks Index|Frameworks Index]].

## Where the cross-government work happens

Many defence-relevant programmes are joint between MOD and another department. Common patterns:

- **MOD + DSIT.** Most R&D and innovation programmes (DASA themed competitions, ARIA breakthrough programmes).
- **MOD + Home Office.** Border, counter-terror, and resilience-adjacent technology.
- **MOD + Cabinet Office.** National-security strategy, resilience, integrated security planning.
- **MOD + DBT.** Defence exports, SME growth support.
