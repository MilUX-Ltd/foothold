# NATO and International

> **This is a map, not an entity home.** Canonical entity content lives in `CRM/organisations/`. When you start engaging with an international body, add it to your CRM via `/add-organisation` and link from there.

International defence-innovation and procurement organisations relevant to UK defence-technology companies. Useful when scaling beyond the UK market or accessing allied procurement.

## NATO

- **NATO DIANA — Defence Innovation Accelerator for the North Atlantic.** Alliance-level innovation accelerator. A primary entry point for UK SMEs into NATO innovation. Runs annual challenge cycles with funding and acceleration support.
- **NATO Innovation Hub.** Challenges, hackathons, and partnership programmes.
- **NATO DIANA Challenge Portal.** Online platform for DIANA challenge submissions.
- **NATO ACO — Allied Command Operations.** Operational command for NATO military operations.
- **NATO ACT — Allied Command Transformation.** Strategic command for transformation, capability development, and doctrine. Includes the NATO OPEX experimentation programme.
- **NATO Support and Procurement Agency (NSPA).** Logistics and procurement support across the alliance.
- **NATO Centres of Excellence.** Accredited centres across domains: Cyber Defence (CCDCOE, Tallinn); Strategic Communications (Riga); Energy Security (Vilnius); Crisis Management; many others.
- **NATO Rapid Adoption Action Plan.** Framework for accelerating adoption of innovative technologies across the alliance.

## European Commission

- **DG for Defence Industry and Space.** Directorate-General for EU defence-industrial policy and the European Defence Fund.
- **European Defence Fund (EDF).** EU funding mechanism for collaborative defence research and capability development. UK participation post-Brexit is limited but possible through specific agreements and consortia involving EU partners.

## International collaboration points for UK SMEs

UK defence SMEs can access international markets through several established routes:

1. **NATO DIANA challenges.** Direct alliance-level engagement.
2. **UK Defence and Security Exports (UKDSE).** UK government support for defence exports. Part of NA-Intl in the NAD Group structure.
3. **London Tech Bridge.** US Navy / Royal Navy collaboration in the London tech ecosystem; one model for bilateral innovation engagement.
4. **Five Eyes partnerships.** Intelligence and technology sharing across the US, UK, Canada, Australia, and New Zealand.
5. **GCAP / TEMPEST.** Trilateral combat air programme with Italy and Japan. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/TEMPEST (GCAP)|TEMPEST (GCAP)]].
6. **Bilateral and trilateral working groups.** Many UK defence programmes have parallel arrangements with named partner nations; engagement is typically via the relevant programme office.
