# Western Regional Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** WRDSC
- **Region:** Bristol, Gloucestershire, Bath & North East Somerset, Wiltshire, Swindon
- **Website:** [wrdsc.co.uk](https://www.wrdsc.co.uk/)
- **Email:** admin@wrdsc.co.uk

## Description

The Western RDSC facilitates industry, academia, research and technology organisations (RTOs), and government in sharing ideas and opportunities, encouraging collaboration, and identifying routes to market.

The region has the potential to become one of the largest and most influential defence clusters, given a strong academic base, capable catapult presence bridging research and industry, leading OEMs including Babcock, QinetiQ, Airbus, and Rolls-Royce, and an innovative SME community. Key regional assets include the £225M Isambard AI supercomputer, Temple Quarter Enterprise Campus (TQEC), Dstl Porton Down, MoD Boscombe Down, and Tidworth Super Garrison.

## Strategic objectives

- Identify and pursue research, technology, and opportunities aligned with defence needs.
- Build industry and academic networks, supporting knowledge sharing and collaboration.
- Help new solutions move from concept to commercialisation.
- Attract further investment by showcasing regional strengths in defence and security.
