# Greater Lincolnshire Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** GLRDSC
- **Region:** Greater Lincolnshire and Rutland
- **Website:** [greaterlincolnshirerdsc.com](https://www.greaterlincolnshirerdsc.com/)
- **Membership application:** [Lincolnshire County Council form](https://www.lincolnshire.gov.uk/xfp/form/781)

## Description

The Greater Lincolnshire Defence and Security Cluster (GLRDSC) is an industry-led forum bringing together national and international defence organisations alongside manufacturing, research, development, and innovation expertise. The cluster has a particular focus on intelligence, surveillance, target acquisition, and reconnaissance (ISTAR).

The ambition is to establish Greater Lincolnshire as a national defence and security innovation, production, and service hub and to promote the area's ability to develop and support vital defence and security programmes.

## Key capabilities

- Multi-domain ISTAR
- Defence communications
- Mission support
- Data analytics and AI
- Cyber-security
- Unmanned aerial systems
- Virtual and augmented reality
- Training, simulation, and emulation services
