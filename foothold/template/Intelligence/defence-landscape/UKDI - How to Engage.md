# UKDI: How to Engage

**UK Defence Innovation (UKDI)** is the MOD's single front door for companies and individuals who want to innovate with defence and security. It replaces the routes that used to sit under DASA. This page says what is open now, what is paused, and how to start a conversation. Funding windows move: always check the [UKDI live funding page](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/live-funding-opportunities) before planning around a date.

## What UKDI is

- **Formerly DASA.** The Defence and Security Accelerator became part of UKDI in **February 2026**.
- **Launched 1 July 2025**, following the Strategic Defence Review. It sits within the NAD Group; a Director General Innovation manages both UKDI and Dstl.
- **Money.** A ringfenced budget of **at least £400m a year**, totalling **£1.6bn** to 2030 in the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]].
- **Five themes** that set what it buys:
  1. **Autonomy**: trusted, independently operating systems across land, sea, air and space.
  2. **Decision Advantage**: AI, secure communications, intelligence, cyber defence and encryption.
  3. **Logistics and Support**: medical support, energy, maintenance and logistics in harsh conditions.
  4. **Effects**: precise specialist systems combined with simple, low-cost technologies at scale.
  5. **Protection**: concealment, detection, layered defences and counter-drone.
- UKDI also runs a **Rapid Innovation Team** for urgent operational problems using commercially available dual-use technology, and **Regional Engagement Teams** across the UK.

## How to start

1. **Register on the UKDI submission service** and sign in to your supplier dashboard.
2. **Ask for an Innovation Partner.** Choose "Contact UKDI Innovation Partner" under guidance. Innovation Partners are civil servants who discuss competitions, signpost opportunities and guide you through a submission. **One will contact you within 15 working days**, and UKDI says your intellectual property is protected when you speak to one.
3. **Join your Regional Defence and Security Cluster.** UKDI runs 13 regional clusters that help companies into supply chains and give them a voice in shaping UKDI work. See [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/Regional Defence and Security Clusters|Regional Defence and Security Clusters]].
4. **Sign up to the UKDI mailing list** for competitions, events and news.

Help centre: accelerator@dstl.gov.uk, or 01980 950000 (option 3, 9am to midday, Monday to Friday), as published on gov.uk on 6 October 2026.

**Overseas companies.** Most competitions expect project work in the UK and "genuine UK operational capability". Overseas organisations can usually take part as subcontractors, consortium members or technology partners if the work is done in the UK.
