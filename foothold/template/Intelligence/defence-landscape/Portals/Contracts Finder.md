# Contracts Finder

## Summary

Central UK government portal for publishing and finding public-sector contract opportunities. All contracts above £12,000 (central government) or £30,000 (sub-central) must be published here. Primary source for finding live UK public-sector opportunities.

## Operating model

Buyer-side: publishing tool for procurement notices. Supplier-side: searchable index with alerts. Operated by the UK government (Cabinet Office Government Commercial Function).
