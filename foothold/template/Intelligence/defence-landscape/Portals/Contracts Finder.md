# Contracts Finder

## Summary

The central UK government portal for finding public-sector contract opportunities and viewing contracts already awarded. Operated by the Cabinet Office Government Commercial Function. All UK non-devolved public sector contracting authorities are required to publish contract notices on Contracts Finder above the relevant thresholds (current gov.uk guidance: c.£12,000 in central government and c.£25,000 in the wider public sector and NHS).

GOV.UK: [Contracts Finder](https://www.gov.uk/contracts-finder). Search service: [contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/Search](https://www.contractsfinder.service.gov.uk/Search). Data collection: [data.gov.uk: Contracts Finder](https://www.data.gov.uk/collections/government/contracts-finder).

## Operating Model

- **Buyer-side.** Contracting authorities (central government departments, local authorities, NHS, agencies) publish notices for contract opportunities and award decisions.
- **Supplier-side.** Searchable index with the ability to **create a free account** to save searches and receive email alerts on relevant CPV codes, keywords, and authorities.
- **Open access.** No paid tier; free to all businesses, including SMEs.

## Relationship to Find a Tender Service

Contracts Finder publishes **lower-value** public sector contracts within England and across the UK. Higher-value contract notices that previously appeared on the EU's TED (Tenders Electronic Daily) now publish on the sister service [Find a Tender (FTS)](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/Search). Both are part of the Cabinet Office procurement transparency stack, alongside the post-Procurement-Act-2023 [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Central Digital Platform|Central Digital Platform]].

## What appears on Contracts Finder

- Live opportunities and tenders above the thresholds.
- Awarded contracts (post-decision transparency).
- Contract notices from central government, agencies, local authorities, NHS, and other non-devolved public sector bodies.
- Across all categories: defence procurement is a subset, surfaced alongside non-defence opportunities.

## How Contracts Finder fits into the MOD procurement stack

For MOD-specific procurement, the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal (DSP)]] is the dedicated route for tendering and competitions. Contracts Finder is the wider central government index where MOD opportunities also appear, alongside everything else in HMG procurement.
