# Central Digital Platform

## Summary

UK government central platform for digital procurement activities. Part of the Procurement Act 2023 reform — a central record of supplier information and procurement notices.

## Operating model

Suppliers register and maintain their supplier information centrally. Buyers use the platform to publish notices and access supplier records under the Procurement Act 2023 supplier-information regime.
