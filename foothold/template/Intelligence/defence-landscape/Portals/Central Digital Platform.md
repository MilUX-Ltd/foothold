# Central Digital Platform (CDP)

## Summary

The UK government's central online procurement platform under the **Procurement Act 2023**. The platform allows contracting authorities to publish notices (and other Act-required information) for both covered and below-threshold procurements, and enables suppliers to **submit and store their core organisational information once**, then reuse it across covered procurements.

Launched on **24 February 2025**, on time and in full alignment with the requirements of the Act. Operated by the Cabinet Office.

GOV.UK: [Guidance: Central Digital Platform and Publication of Information](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents-procure-phase/guidance-central-digital-platform-and-publication-of-information-html); [Procurement Act 2023: A short guide for suppliers](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/procurement-act-2023-short-guides/the-procurement-act-2023-a-short-guide-for-suppliers-html); [Central Digital Platform Factsheet (PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/674586ca1034a5f4a5856972/Central_Digital_Platform_Factsheet.pdf).

## Operating Model

The Cabinet Office is delivering a fully integrated digital platform combining three functions:

- **Registration.** Supplier identity and core organisational data.
- **Supplier Information.** Standard data suppliers would historically have submitted on each PQQ or SQ, including financials, qualifications, accreditations, exclusion-grounds information, and details of any convictions, conduct issues, or contract breaches.
- **Find a Tender** (noticing). Publishes contract notices and award decisions across covered procurements.

These three together make up "the Central Digital Platform".

### Practical implications for suppliers

- Suppliers create one CDP profile, populate the supplier information once, and reuse it across covered procurements rather than re-submitting at every PQQ.
- The Act's **exclusion grounds** information is captured at the CDP layer.
- Accessible at [gov.uk/find-a-tender](https://www.gov.uk/find-a-tender).

### Delivery

The Cabinet Office signed **Ernst & Young** as the major build partner for the CDP. See [UKAuthority: Cabinet Office signs Ernst & Young for Central Digital Platform](https://www.ukauthority.com/articles/cabinet-office-signs-ernst-young-for-central-digital-platform) and [Goaco: Transforming Public Procurement with a New Central Digital Platform for the Cabinet Office](https://goaco.com/our-work/transforming-public-procurement-with-a-new-central-digital-platform-for-the-cabinet-office/).
