# Defence Sourcing Portal (DSP)

## Summary

The Ministry of Defence's online procurement platform for MOD contract opportunities, tenders, and information on doing business with the MOD. The single front door through which **all MOD contract opportunities are advertised and tendered**. Free for suppliers to use; registration is open.

GOV.UK: [Further information on the Defence Sourcing Portal (DSP)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mod-contracting-purchasing-and-finance-e-procurement-system/further-information-on-the-defence-sourcing-portal-dsp); [Contracting, Purchasing and Finance (CP&F) and DSP tools](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mod-contracting-purchasing-and-finance-e-procurement-system/contracting-purchasing-and-finance-cpf-tool); [DSP Supplier Registration Guide (PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/government/uploads/system/uploads/attachment_data/file/956927/Defence_Sourcing_Portal_-_Supplier_Registration_Guide.pdf). DE&S: [New Defence Sourcing Portal and Supplier Registration](https://des.mod.uk/new-defence-sourcing-portal/). User guide: [contracts.mod.uk: DSP](https://contracts.mod.uk/web/user-guide.html).

## Operating Model

- **Single front door.** All MOD opportunities and tenders are advertised and run via the DSP.
- **Supplier registration.** Suppliers register using the "Register now" button on the DSP homepage. The online form is described as quick and easy.
- **Categorisation.** Suppliers associate categories against their profile to receive alerts when the MOD publishes opportunities in those categories.
- **Opportunity threshold.** Tender and contract opportunities valued at over £10,000 are advertised on the DSP.
- **Audit trail.** The system supports secure online document exchange and a clear audit trail, removing inefficient email and paper-based practices.
- **Helpdesk.** Users are supported by a 24-hour Helpdesk.
- **Cost.** Access and registration to bid for MOD opportunities is free.

## Technical implementation

- **eSourcing system.** Delivered to the MOD by **Jaggaer**, which provides the underlying e-sourcing platform.
- **AWARD®.** A specialist solution for complex, strategic procurements, supplied by **Commerce Decisions** in partnership with Jaggaer. See [Commerce Decisions: MOD Defence Sourcing Portal and AWARD®](https://help.commercedecisions.com/support/solutions/articles/80001028784-mod-defence-sourcing-portal-and-award-).

## Sub-contracting opportunities

Suppliers are also able to publish their own sub-contracting opportunities on the portal, which makes the DSP useful both as a buyer route (find MOD opportunities) and as a Tier-2 supplier route (advertise to the wider defence supply chain).
