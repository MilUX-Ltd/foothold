# Defence Sourcing Portal

## Summary

MOD-specific procurement portal for suppliers. The supplier-facing front door for MOD competitions that don't run via Crown Commercial Service or other named frameworks.

## Operating model

Suppliers register, declare interest in opportunities, and respond to MOD-issued competitions. Many MOD-specific framework activities (including the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/eSourcing Tool|eSourcing Tool]]) are surfaced here.
