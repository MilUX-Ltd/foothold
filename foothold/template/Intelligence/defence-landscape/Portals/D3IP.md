# D3IP

## Summary

D3IP is a **brand of DII Ltd** that runs an innovation-as-a-service community out of **Dorset Innovation Park**, the same site that hosts the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence BattleLab|Defence BattleLab]]. D3IP leverages a diverse collaboration community and a range of capabilities to work with Defence, government, and industry partners on innovation challenges, typically posed by entities such as London Tech Bridge, NavyX (now consolidated into [[CRM/organisations/mod/Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office|DCTO]]), and the Defence BattleLab itself.

D3IP runs a member directory, a portal with active challenges, a forum, a calendar of events (masterclasses, webinars, in-person sessions at the BattleLab), and an Initiatives stream covering things like Digital Engineering Standards and Commercial X. It is distinct from but co-located with, and operationally entwined with, the Defence BattleLab. **Dorset Council** owns the underlying Dorset Innovation Park site.

## Directory scale

The D3IP directory had **193 listed organisations** at a May 2026 snapshot, with **100 of them naming a single point-of-contact**. Many members use `@partners.d3ip.io` or `@partners.d3ip.net` aliases for D3IP-routed enquiries rather than their corporate email.

## Services and streams

- **Directory** — searchable index of members, with per-member "Make an enquiry" routing.
- **Forum** — discussion space, used for webinar follow-up.
- **Initiatives** — themed streams (e.g. Digital Engineering Standards).
- **Events** — masterclasses, webinars, symposia, and in-person sessions at the BattleLab.
- **Challenges** — posed by external organisations (NavyX / DCTO, London Tech Bridge, etc.) and routed through the portal for members to respond.
- **News, Rules, Acronyms, FAQ, Helpdesk** — supporting reference content inside the portal.

## How to engage

D3IP membership is the gateway. Members can browse the directory, respond to challenges, attend events, and use the portal as the connective tissue between organisations at Dorset Innovation Park. Membership is the main route through which defence-tech SMEs engage with the Dorset defence ecosystem alongside the Defence BattleLab and the South West Regional Defence and Security Cluster.
