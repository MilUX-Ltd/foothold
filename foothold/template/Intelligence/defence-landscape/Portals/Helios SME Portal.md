# Helios SME Portal

## Summary

A **free** SME-facing portal created by **Hellios Information Limited** in collaboration with the **JOSCAR Governance Group**, launched in response to the MOD's SME Action Plan. Allows UK SMEs to register their capabilities so that the MOD and Prime contractors (notably BAE Systems) can discover them as potential suppliers in the defence, aerospace, and security sectors.

External: [SME Portal](https://hellios.com/sme-portal); sign-in at [sme-portal.hellios.com](https://sme-portal.hellios.com/). Launch via [BAE Systems story: Hellios, the Ministry of Defence and BAE Systems launch a free SME portal](https://www.baesystems.com/en-uk/story/hellios-the-ministry-of-defence-and-bae-systems-launch-a-free-sme-portal); [aerospace variant: Hellios Launches New Portal for SMEs in Aerospace Sector](https://memuknews.com/inmotion/aerospace/hellios-launches-new-portal-for-smes-in-aerospace-sector/).

## Operating Model

- **Self-registration.** SMEs upload company information to the portal free of charge. The process is described as quick and easy and requires only minimal company information for a listing to be submitted.
- **Discovery side.** The MOD and Prime contractors use the portal as a market-intelligence tool to discover SMEs by capability area.
- **Eligibility.** UK SMEs only. Hellios provides a check tool.
- **Launch venue.** The initial unveiling was at the Farnborough International Airshow.
- **Operator.** Hellios Information Limited (a Supplier Information and Risk Management organisation; same operator as [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/JOSCAR|JOSCAR]]).
- **Governance.** Created in collaboration with the JOSCAR Governance Group, which is the buyer-community body behind the JOSCAR accreditation.

## How the Helios SME Portal differs from JOSCAR

| | Helios SME Portal | JOSCAR |
|---|---|---|
| **Type** | Capability-discovery portal | Pre-qualification and compliance accreditation |
| **Purpose** | Make SMEs visible to MOD and primes | Assure buyer community that suppliers meet PQQ criteria |
| **Cost** | Free | Paid by the supplier |
| **Eligibility** | UK SMEs only | Any supplier in defence, aerospace, security |
| **Required for** | Nothing on its own; visibility tool | A pass/fail criterion on many defence framework PQQs |

Both are operated by Hellios. The Portal is the lighter-touch visibility surface; JOSCAR is the substantive accreditation. SMEs aiming to bid into defence supply chains typically need both eventually.

## Wider supplier-visibility landscape

The Helios SME Portal is one of three principal supplier-visibility surfaces the MOD currently steers SMEs through. Per public guidance, SMEs are also expected to register on:

- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal (DSP)]]: the MOD's e-sourcing platform.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Central Digital Platform|Central Digital Platform (CDP)]]: the Procurement Act 2023 platform.
