# Helios SME Portal

## Summary

SME-facing portal created by Hellios in partnership with the JOSCAR Governance Group in response to the MOD's SME Action Plan. Allows SMEs to demonstrate capabilities to MOD buyers, and lets MOD procurement teams discover SMEs by capability area. Essential registration for any SME wanting to be discoverable to MOD buyers.

## Operating model

Suppliers register, declare capabilities, and become discoverable to MOD buyers via the portal's search and matching functions.
