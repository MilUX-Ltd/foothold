# RAINCLOUD DEFENSE

## Summary

RAINCLOUD DEFENSE is an invitation-only global digital ecosystem for the defence-technology sector, operated by RAINCLOUD GROUP (a Washington, D.C.-headquartered platform company founded in 2021). Membership is free but vetted. The platform combines a member directory, a virtual product catalogue, an opportunities board, a curated news feed, an events directory, and paid private workspaces ("Pavilions") for member organisations.

Unlike the UK government portals on this index (Helios, Contracts Finder, Defence Sourcing Portal, MOD Acquisition Pipeline, Central Digital Platform) and unlike the paid opportunity aggregator [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/DefTechLink|DefTechLink]], RAINCLOUD DEFENSE is primarily a vetted **community** layer rather than an opportunity-feed or supplier-registration surface. Founders join because of who is on it, not because it indexes contracts.

External: marketing site at [raindefense.ai](https://raindefense.ai/); platform at [raincloud.network](https://raincloud.network/); group at [raincloud.group](https://raincloud.group/). Invitation request at [raincloud.network/signup](https://raincloud.network/signup).

## Who it's for

Per the public site copy, RAINCLOUD DEFENSE positions itself for "defence industry stakeholders" globally — companies (founders, executives, business-development leads), investors, advisors, and operators. Public membership figures: 6,600+ to 7,000+ members across 100+ countries and 5,000+ companies (figures cited on the LinkedIn About page and in recent founder posts vary; treat as approximate).

Most plausibly useful to:

- Defence-tech founders wanting curated international exposure without grinding LinkedIn.
- Companies scaling beyond their home market and needing introductions to vetted international counterparts.
- Advisors and investors looking for vetted deal flow in the defence and dual-use space.
- Organisations considering a paid "Pavilion" as a 24/7 alternative to one-off conference presence.
- Operators (serving and retired military) who want a defence-tech community channel that isn't open social media.

Less obviously useful to companies whose growth is wholly within a single national procurement system. The network's value is its international footprint and editorial layer; if both of those are noise to you, the time investment will be too.

## What it offers

Public-source description from the [raindefense.ai homepage](https://raindefense.ai/) and the [RAINCLOUD GROUP LinkedIn About page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/raincloud-group/):

- **Vetted member directory.** Search by name, location, skills, or industry. Encrypted member-to-member chat.
- **Companies directory.** Search by organisation.
- **Digital Defense Show.** A 24/7 virtual catalogue of defence and dual-use products and solutions. Public-source figure: 2,000+ capabilities listed (April 2026).
- **Private Pavilions.** Paid tailored member spaces for organisations and themes. Public-source descriptions frame these as 24/7 alternatives to conference presence, with controlled membership and dedicated features. Specific Pavilions are typically visible only to logged-in members; the public marketing site advertises the model rather than the roster.
- **Opportunities Board.** Posts for funding, jobs, short-term projects and missions, partners, speakers, and advisors.
- **Industry Thought Leadership.** Members publish documents, articles, and links; content is tagged by category for discovery.
- **Industry Events directory.** Defence-tech events globally.
- **Industry News.** Editorially curated defence and defence+AI news feed with extensive category taxonomy.
- **RAINCLOUD DEFENSE Daily Drop and Weekly Forecast.** Proprietary industry intelligence outputs published by the RAINCLOUD team, surfaced through the platform and through founder communications.

## How RAINCLOUD DEFENSE differs from the UK government portals and from DefTechLink

|  | RAINCLOUD DEFENSE | DefTechLink | UK gov portals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Operator** | RAINCLOUD GROUP (private, US) | DefTechLink (private, US) | UK Government / MOD / Hellios |
| **Cost** | Free, invitation-only | Paid (~$35/month) | Free |
| **Geography** | 100+ countries | ~29 allied countries | UK only |
| **Primary value** | Vetted community + editorial layer | Aggregated opportunity feed + explainer content | Direct UK procurement access |
| **Eligibility** | Application, vetted | Open registration | UK SMEs / public-sector suppliers |
| **Working language** | Defence and dual-use | Defence and dual-use | UK MOD and wider public sector |

The three are largely complementary rather than substitutable. UK government portals are the source of truth for UK MOD procurement; DefTechLink layers opportunity discovery across allied markets; RAINCLOUD DEFENSE provides the international community and editorial layer.

## How to evaluate joining

- **Time investment.** The platform rewards a complete profile (industry, skills, citizenship, headline, summary, last experience) and a steady presence in the directory and news feeds. Treat as a six-month commitment to assess.
- **Pavilion route.** If you want a curated 24/7 surface to present your capability to a vetted international audience, the Pavilion model is the paid product to investigate. Contact route is `raincloud@raindefense.ai`.
- **Thought-leadership route.** Cross-posting articles or papers into Industry Thought Leadership is a low-cost way to test whether the audience overlap with your customers is real.
- **Adjacent verticals.** Members may also gain access or visibility to RAINCLOUD UKRAINA when that launches at the Ukraine Recovery Conference in Gdansk in July 2026.
