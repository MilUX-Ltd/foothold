# MOD Acquisition Pipeline

## Summary

MOD's forward-look publication of planned procurement activity. Helps industry prepare bids, partner up, and plan investment. Published periodically; content varies by classification level and audience.

## Operating model

Periodic publication (annual or refreshed in-year) listing upcoming procurement intentions, value bands, and target dates. Used by industry to plan capacity and by primes to identify partnering opportunities.

## Source material

- Published via MOD and gov.uk channels. Specific URLs vary by edition; check the latest at [gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/) under MOD acquisition publications.
