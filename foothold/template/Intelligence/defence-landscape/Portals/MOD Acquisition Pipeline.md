# MOD Acquisition Pipeline

## Summary

The MOD's published **forward look of potential procurement activity and Framework Agreements**. Provides a 12-to-18-month view of future MOD procurement valued at **£2 million or above**, with the explicit aim of giving suppliers (and SMEs in particular) more time to plan, partner, and prepare high-quality bids. Refreshed and published every 6 months.

GOV.UK: [MOD Acquisition Pipeline](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mod-acquisition-pipeline). Context: [GOV.UK: Procurement at MOD](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ministry-of-defence/about/procurement). Industry coverage: [techUK: Ministry of Defence publishes Acquisition Pipeline](https://www.techuk.org/resource/ministry-of-defence-publishes-acquisition-pipeline.html); [techUK: Ministry of Defence publishes updated Acquisition Pipeline](https://www.techuk.org/resource/ministry-of-defence-publishes-updated-acquisition-pipeline.html).

## Operating Model

- **Publication cadence.** Refreshed and published every 6 months.
- **Horizon.** 12 to 18 months of upcoming procurement intentions.
- **Inclusion threshold.** Procurements valued at £2 million or above.
- **Content.** Procurement intentions, value bands, target dates, and the indicative sourcing route (competitive vs single source) where known.
- **Status.** The pipeline is for information only; sourcing routes are indicative at publication time and may change.

### Exclusions

The pipeline explicitly excludes:

- **Foreign Military Sales (FMS).**
- **Government-to-Government (G2G) procurements.**

So the pipeline is a partial view of MOD acquisition activity, not exhaustive. Suppliers using the pipeline as a planning tool should be aware that big-ticket items routed via FMS or G2G will not appear.

## Why it matters

Defence Online and techUK both frame the Acquisition Pipeline as one of the most valuable business development tools available to defence suppliers: it surfaces opportunities well before they reach the formal tender stage. The combination of forward look + bi-annual refresh allows suppliers to:

- Plan capacity and partnering.
- Position thought-leadership and warm-up engagements.
- Build the necessary clearances, JOSCAR accreditation, and framework registrations before a competition lands.

The Pipeline aligns with the [Defence Industrial Strategy 2025: Making Defence an Engine for Growth (PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/68bea3fc223d92d088f01d69/Defence_Industrial_Strategy_2025_-_Making_Defence_an_Engine_for_Growth.pdf), which positions defence procurement transparency as a key lever for UK industrial growth.
