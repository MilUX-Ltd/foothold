# Find a Tender Service (FTS)

The UK's central publication service for **high-value public procurement notices** above the published thresholds (currently c.£139,688 including VAT). Launched on **1 January 2021** as the post-Brexit replacement for the EU's Official Journal of the European Union (OJEU) / Tenders Electronic Daily (TED). Now operates inside the wider [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Central Digital Platform|Central Digital Platform (CDP)]] settled by the Procurement Act 2023.

GOV.UK: [Find a Tender](https://www.gov.uk/find-tender). Service: [find-tender.service.gov.uk](https://www.find-tender.service.gov.uk/).

## Role in the landscape

Every UK contracting authority publishing a "covered" procurement (above the high-value threshold) is required to post the notice to FTS. For defence-tech SMEs this is the single most important high-value notice surface alongside the Defence Sourcing Portal: a substantial share of MOD framework call-offs and major contracts will be visible here before they appear anywhere else.

### What it carries

- **Tender notices** — open procurements and pre-market engagement.
- **Award notices** — published contract awards.
- **Modification and termination notices** — Procurement Act 2023 transparency obligations.
- **Below-threshold notices** — voluntary publication is permitted; many MoD-adjacent contracting bodies do this to widen reach.

### Difference vs. Contracts Finder

- **Find a Tender (FTS):** high-value (above-threshold) notices, mandatory under Procurement Act 2023.
- **[[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Contracts Finder|Contracts Finder]]:** UK-wide contracts above £12,000 including VAT; broader coverage at the lower end of the market.
- **[[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal (DSP)]]:** MOD's own bidder-facing platform; notices on FTS are typically mirrored here for defence-specific procurements.

### Relationship to the Central Digital Platform

FTS is the notice-publication slice of the Central Digital Platform. CDP also handles supplier registration (the "submit once, reuse" model) and below-threshold publication. FTS-published notices are typically deep-linked from a supplier's CDP profile to enable single-click bid initiation.
