# Farnborough Aerospace Consortium (FAC)

## Company details

- **Full name:** Farnborough Aerospace Consortium (FAC).
- **Website:** [fac.org.uk](https://www.fac.org.uk).
- **Sector:** Aerospace and defence cluster / membership body.
- **Region:** Farnborough and the wider South Central aerospace corridor.

## What FAC does

The Farnborough Aerospace Consortium is the regional cluster representing aerospace and defence companies based around Farnborough, Hampshire. It functions as a membership-led trade body, running networking events, supplier development activity, advocacy with government and primes, and an annual presence around the Farnborough International Airshow. The consortium operates alongside the wider South Central defence and security ecosystem and is represented on the South Central Regional Defence and Security Cluster (SCRDSC) Steering Group.
