# ADS Group

## Company details

- **Website:** [adsgroup.org.uk](https://www.adsgroup.org.uk)
- **LinkedIn:** [ADS Group](https://www.linkedin.com/company/ads-group-ltd/)
- **Sector:** Trade association — aerospace, defence, security, and space
- **HQ:** Salamanca Square, London SE1 7SP
- **Other location:** ETPS Road, Farnborough GU14 6FD (Show Control, for the Farnborough International Airshow)
- **Founded:** 2009 (following the merger of the Society of British Aerospace Companies, Defence Manufacturers Association, and Association of Police and Public Security Suppliers)
- **Members:** 1,700+ member companies across aerospace, defence, security and space, ranging from primes (BAE Systems, Rolls-Royce, Airbus, GKN, Meggitt) down to hundreds of UK SMEs

## What ADS does

ADS is the UK trade body for the aerospace, defence, security, and space industries. It speaks for the sectors with government, runs major events (the Farnborough International Airshow being the marquee one), and provides member-facing services:

- Meet-the-buyer programmes.
- Business opportunity feeds and bulletins.
- Events bulletins and regional Engage events.
- Supply-chain excellence support.
- R&D funding signposting.
- Procurement guidance.
- The ADS-administered route into UK Security Vetting (UKSV) for SMEs that cannot sponsor their own clearances.
- International trade-association collaboration agreements supporting overseas market access for UK members.

The membership-services side runs an active outbound rhythm: weekly news round-ups, business-opportunity bulletins, events bulletins, regional Engage events, and ADS Rewards member perks.

## ADS Members Directory

ADS publishes a public member directory at [members.adsgroup.org.uk/profile-directory](https://members.adsgroup.org.uk/profile-directory). Useful for SMEs scoping potential primes, sub-contractors, and partners.

## Membership

ADS membership pricing depends on UK turnover and headcount. The most useful starting points for SMEs considering membership:

- The UKSV route for SMEs that cannot sponsor their own security clearances.
- Supply-chain visibility through the members directory.
- The defence-relevant bulletins and Meet the Buyer events.
