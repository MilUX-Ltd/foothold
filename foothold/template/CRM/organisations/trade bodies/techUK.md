# techUK

## Overview

- **Full name:** techUK (UK technology trade association)
- **Website:** [techuk.org](https://www.techuk.org)
- **Registered office:** 10 St Bride Street, London EC4A 4AD
- **Membership:** 1,000+ companies including 660+ SMEs and start-ups
- **Drop-in workspace:** Ludgate Circus, London (members)

techUK is the trade association representing the UK technology industry. It runs policy, innovation, and market-focused programmes across defence, cyber security, national security, and broader tech sectors. Membership provides access to private MOD briefings, early market engagement, events, policy input, and meeting space in central London.

## Defence and security programme

The core programme for defence-sector technology companies. Activities include:

- **Private briefings** with MOD, Defence Digital, and Front-Line Commands.
- **Early market engagement** for MOD programmes.
- **Policy engagement** on the Strategic Defence Review and other major policy moments.
- **Vulcan** — technology scouting platform for defence.
- **Defence Spring Dinner** — annual flagship event.
- **DPRTE** — Defence Procurement, Research, Technology and Exportability conference; techUK attends and runs panels.

Programme link: [techUK Defence and Security](https://www.techuk.org/developing-markets/defence-and-security.html).

## Defence and security SME forum

Sub-group representing SME members to wider industry and government, including ministers. Particularly relevant for SMEs looking to engage with MOD policy and procurement.
