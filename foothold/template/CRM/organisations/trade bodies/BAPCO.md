# BAPCO (British APCO)

UK trade association for public-safety communications. Brings together the UK members of the **Association of Public-Safety Communications Officials (APCO)** community: police, fire, ambulance, search and rescue, and the technology suppliers that serve them.

For defence-tech SMEs the BAPCO surface is most relevant where capability has overlap into public safety and emergency services communications, including tactical comms, resilient networks, situational awareness, and dispatch systems. Adjacent to the wider Home Office security innovation surfaces, including [[CRM/organisations/government/Accelerated Capability Environment (ACE)|ACE]].
