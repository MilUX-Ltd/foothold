# Make UK

## Company details

- **Website:** [makeuk.org](https://www.makeuk.org)
- **Sector:** UK manufacturers' trade association and representative body
- **HQ:** London, with regional offices across the UK
- **Subsidiary of note:** Make UK Defence (specialist defence-manufacturing division)

## What Make UK does

Make UK (formerly EEF — the Engineering Employers' Federation) is the trade body representing UK manufacturing. It provides advocacy, policy, training, HR and legal services, and industry events for member manufacturers across sectors including aerospace, defence, automotive, and general engineering.

Make UK Defence runs the Make UK Defence Summit and supports SME engagement in defence-manufacturing supply chains. It also operates as a route for self-sponsored SC clearance for SMEs that need their staff cleared but cannot get sponsorship directly through MOD or a prime.
