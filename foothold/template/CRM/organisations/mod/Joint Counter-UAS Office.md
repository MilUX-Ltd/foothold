# Joint Counter-UAS Office

The MOD's joint office coordinating counter-uncrewed-aerial-systems efforts across the three Services. Sits inside the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command|Cyber and Specialist Operations Command]] cluster.

> **Documentation caveat.** Public gov.uk documentation directly badged "Joint Counter-UAS Office" is sparse at time of capture. The substantive UK counter-UAS activity it coordinates is visible across Project Synergia (RAF-led), ORCUS, and recent US-UK joint counter-drone agreements.

## Role in the landscape

The Office sits at the cross-Service joint level, brokering between Service-specific counter-UAS requirements and the central programmes that deliver counter-drone capability into MOD operational use. Counter-UAS is increasingly central post-SDR following Ukraine-derived lessons on the prevalence and tactical impact of small uncrewed systems.

### Named UK counter-UAS programmes

- **Project Synergia.** Royal Air Force-led counter-drone R&D programme, managed by [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|DE&S]]'s Future Capability Group. Achieved initial operating capability for integrated detect, track, identify, and defeat technologies. See [DE&S: Counter-drone research and development programme reaches significant milestone](https://des.mod.uk/counter-drone-programme-milestone/) and [Unmanned Airspace: UK moves to next stage in delivering Project Synergia C-UAS systems to military bases](https://www.unmannedairspace.info/counter-uas-systems-and-policies/uk-moves-to-next-stage-in-delivering-project-synergia-c-uas-systems-to-military-bases/).
- **ORCUS.** RAF-operated counter-UAS system supporting emergency operations responding to drone-based crises in the UK and overseas. See [Airforce Technology: ORCUS Counter-UAS](https://www.airforce-technology.com/projects/orcus-counter-unmanned-aerial-system-united-kingdom/).
- **Army vehicle-mounted counter-drone.** Adjacent Army programme. See [UK Defence Journal: Army explores new vehicle-mounted counter-drone system](https://ukdefencejournal.org.uk/army-explores-new-vehicle-mounted-counter-drone-system/).

### International collaboration

The **US Department of War and the UK Ministry of Defence signed a Joint Declaration of Intent** to establish common data standards for counter-UAS technologies. See [US Army: US, UK set joint counter-drone technology standards](https://www.army.mil/article/290973/us_uk_set_joint_counter_drone_technology_standards). Project Flytrap 5.0 is the multinational exercise that brings together US, UK, Australian, and industry partners to assess emerging counter-UAS technology.

### Evaluation pipelines

The [National Protective Security Authority (NPSA)](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/national-protective-security-authority) runs counter-UAS equipment evaluations open to UK manufacturers. See [DroneLife: NPSA Launches 2026 Counter-UAS Equipment Evaluations](https://dronelife.com/2026/01/15/npsa-launches-2026-counter-uas-equipment-evaluations-for-uk-manufacturers/).
