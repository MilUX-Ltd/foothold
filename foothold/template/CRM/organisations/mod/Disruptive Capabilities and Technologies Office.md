# Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office (DCTO)

The Royal Navy's consolidated innovation unit, declared at full operational capability on 29 April 2025. DCTO unites three previously separate Navy innovation functions into a single office: NavyX (the Royal Navy's experimentation arm), the Office of the Chief Technology Officer, and the Navy AI Cell. Mission: rapidly prototype, test, and deploy advanced technologies to support naval operations and address the Navy's most pressing operational challenges.

## Role in the landscape

DCTO is the Royal Navy's answer to the wider MOD pattern of consolidating innovation activity (the same pattern that produced [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|UKDI]] at the centre on 1 July 2025). Where UKDI brings DASA, DIU, Command Innovation Hubs, and FCI under one MOD-level roof, DCTO performs the equivalent consolidation inside the Royal Navy by pulling NavyX, the OCTO, and the Navy AI Cell together.

### Stated mission and approach

Per the Royal Navy announcement and the [DSEI Gateway DCTO Commander interview](https://dsei-gateway.com/en/news/features/the-dcto-roadmap-the-commander-outlines-his-plans-for-innovation/): DCTO will "drive groundbreaking innovation forward" with an entrepreneurial mindset, supporting people to be successful while building on previous work around autonomy, AI, and other emerging technologies. Focus technology areas explicitly include:

- Artificial intelligence.
- Autonomy and uncrewed systems (surface, sub-surface, aerial).
- Directed-energy weapons.
- Quantum technology (notably PNT, position, navigation and timing).
- Next-generation nuclear.

### Fleet Experimental Squadron

DCTO has a sea-going experimentation arm: the **Fleet Experimental Squadron**, nested within the Surface Flotilla but reporting through the Develop Directorate. Stated framing is a deliberate echo of the Victorian-era experimental squadrons: a dedicated unit to experiment, iterate, and scale future autonomous and uncrewed vessels at sea.

### Recent demonstrated work (2025 to early 2026)

A useful sense of pace and posture from open-source reporting:

- **Rattler autonomous boats.** Seven 7.2m uncrewed boats trialled in 2025; multiple demonstration sorties, including a flotilla of uncrewed boats shadowing warships in a milestone trial.
- **XV Patrick Blackett.** Used as both technology demonstrator and trials platform for uncrewed and AI-enabled systems.
- **XV Excalibur quantum navigation trial.** Quantum position-navigation-timing trial on the uncrewed submarine, October 2025.
- **Arctic GPS-free navigation trial with Imperial College London.** December 2025, quantum nav in conditions hostile to satellite PNT.
- **Royal Navy P2000 quantum nav trial.** June 2025.
- **NATO autonomy exercise in Portugal.** September 2025, multinational uncrewed-systems testing.

This pace is unusual for a Royal Navy capability office and is a strong indicator of how DCTO is operating in practice.
