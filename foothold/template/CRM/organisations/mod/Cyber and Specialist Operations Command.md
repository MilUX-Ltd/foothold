# Cyber and Specialist Operations Command (CSOC)

The UK's fourth Military Command, alongside the Royal Navy, the British Army, and the Royal Air Force. Established 1 September 2025 by renaming UK Strategic Command (UKStratCom), with an expanded leadership remit for the cyber domain following the [Strategic Defence Review](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-strategic-defence-review-2025-making-britain-safer-secure-at-home-strong-abroad). Generates and operates the specialist capabilities that defence relies on across all domains.

## Role in the landscape

CSOC unifies the cyber, intelligence, medical, special operations, defence education, and overseas attaché capabilities under a single military command. It frames itself as "always on": across every UK operation, delivering the capabilities the other Services either do not see or cannot see. The Command sits inside the UK Ministry of Defence structure and reports to the Defence Secretary as a peer of the three Service Commands.

The CSOC name follows the SDR's call for greater leadership of the cyber domain across defence and government, and the MOD's stated need to defend UK military networks against more than 90,000 "sub-threshold" cyber attacks in the two years to mid-2025 ([MOD press release, 1 July 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/launch-of-new-body-to-harness-innovative-tech-for-the-uks-armed-forces)). Hockenhull described the change as "far more than a change in name, it is a clear statement of purpose."

### Five stated responsibilities

Per [gov.uk About](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/cyber-and-specialist-operations-command/about):

1. Delivering specialist operations, including medical support: integrated operations to protect the UK and in support of NATO.
2. Combatting cyber and electromagnetic threats: command and delivery of advanced cyber and electromagnetic capabilities, defending MOD networks and disrupting adversaries.
3. Arming defence with data, information, and intelligence: leading intelligence and ISR, with a data-centric approach and AI.
4. Advancing UK influence through defence diplomacy: the Integrated Global Defence Network.
5. Educating and innovating to prepare defence for future challenges.

### Sub-organisations commanded by CSOC

Drawn from the gov.uk About page:

- [Permanent Joint Headquarters (PJHQ)](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/the-permanent-joint-headquarters), including the deployable Standing Joint Force HQ. Commands and executes joint military operations and provides policy-aware military advice to the MOD.
- [Directorate of Special Forces](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/directorate-of-special-forces). Short-notice, high-risk operations.
- **Military Intelligence Services** including **Defence Intelligence**. Strategic defence intelligence for the MOD and the armed forces.
- [Defence Medical Services](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/defence-medical-services), including the Joint Medical Group.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Academy of the United Kingdom|Defence Academy of the United Kingdom]]. Professional defence and security education.
- **National Cyber Force**. Operating in and through cyberspace to counter, disrupt, degrade, and contest those who would harm the UK or its allies.
- [Integrated Global Defence Network](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/permanent-joint-operating-bases-pjobs/permanent-joint-operating-bases-pjobs). UK Overseas Bases Support Units, Global Support Organisation, and the UK's contribution to Arms Control and Verification.
- [Integrated Warfare Centre](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/integrated-warfare-centre). Joint Conceptual Development (CONDEV) and Warfare Development (WARDEV).

### CSOC Innovation cluster

Four innovation bodies sit under CSOC:

- [[CRM/organisations/mod/jHub|jHub Defence Innovation]]. CSOC innovation accelerator focused on high-TRL repurposing of dual-purpose technology.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence AI Centre (DAIC)|Defence AI Centre (DAIC)]]. AI policy and governance.
- **Defence Modelling and Simulation Office**.
- **Centre for Intelligence Innovation**.

### Locations

HQ at Northwood (North-West London). Forward sites include the Falklands and the Indian Ocean. The Integrated Warfare Centre at Shrivenham, and MOD Corsham as a major node (Global Operations Security Control Centre, Joint Cyber Unit, Joint Security Co-ordination Centre, Land Information Assurance Group, 10th Signal Regiment, 600 Signal Troop).
