# HMGCC Co-Creation

The open-innovation arm of His Majesty's Government Communications Centre (HMGCC). Created so the secretive engineering organisation can work openly with industry and universities on technology projects that contribute to national security, run at **OFFICIAL** classification so engineers and end-users can collaborate with anyone who can bring a brilliant concept.

HMGCC: [hmgcc.gov.uk](https://www.hmgcc.gov.uk/); [About Co-Creation](https://www.hmgcc.gov.uk/co-creation); [Challenge Portal](https://co-creation.hmgcc.gov.uk/). GOV.UK: [Government body behind secret tech opens doors to expand engineering excellence](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/government-body-behind-secret-tech-opens-doors-to-expand-engineering-excellence). Parent: [GOV.UK: HM Government Communications Centre](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/hm-government-communications-centre).

## Role in the landscape

HMGCC is a joint FCDO/MOD national-security engineering centre based at **Hanslope Park, near Milton Keynes**, established in 1938 and historically associated with computing pioneer **Alan Turing**. HMGCC Co-Creation is the deliberate opening-up of HMGCC's engineering capability to a wider community of UK industry, SMEs, and universities.

### Stated purpose

- Address rapid technological advances that pose challenges and opportunities to national security.
- Work with companies and universities more openly than before on national security technology projects.
- Accelerate the rate of innovation and create more real-world impact.

### Operating model

All Co-Creation projects are run at **OFFICIAL** level: the lowest security classification. This is deliberate, allowing engineers and end-users to work hand-in-hand with anyone able to bring a brilliant concept, regardless of existing clearances.

### Founding partner: Dstl

[[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|Dstl]] is a founding partner of HMGCC Co-Creation, working with HMGCC to deliver a new, bold, and innovative way of working with the wider UK science and technology community. Dstl provides the MOD science and technology backbone of the partnership.

### Engagement route: the Challenge Portal

The [HMGCC Co-Creation Challenge Portal](https://co-creation.hmgcc.gov.uk/) is the public-facing route into HMGCC for industry and academic partners. Open challenges are posted there.
