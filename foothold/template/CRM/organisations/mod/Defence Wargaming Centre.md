# Defence Wargaming Centre (DWC)

The UK's analytical wargaming centre of excellence and part of [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|Dstl]]. Helps meet the increasing demand for wargaming as a tool to support decisions and develop insights into the complex issues facing UK defence and security.

GOV.UK: [Defence Wargaming Centre](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/defence-wargaming-centre).

## Role in the landscape

DWC runs wargames for customers across UK defence and security, from frontline armed forces to the Cabinet Office. Situated at **Dstl's Portsdown West campus**. Sits inside the Centres of Excellence cluster alongside the Defence Materials CoE, the CoE in Defence Risk and Resilience, the Defence Availability Centre, the Defence AI Centre, and others.

### Sister facilities

DWC operates inside a wider UK wargaming ecosystem that includes:

- **Defence Experimentation and Wargaming Hub (DEWH).** Established at MoD Southwick Park in September 2023 and formally opened in March 2024. Complements DWC. See [GOV.UK: Defence Experimentation and Wargaming Hub](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/defence-experimentation-and-wargaming-hub) and [Defence Experimentation and Wargaming Hub brochure (PDF)](https://www.professionalwargaming.co.uk/24DEWHBrochure-O.pdf).
- **Defence College for Military Capability Integration (Shrivenham).** Hosts wargaming training.
- **Royal Navy Wargaming Hub.** A dedicated wargaming facility unveiled in 2024 to boost operations and lived experience inside the RN. See [Royal Navy: Dedicated wargaming facility to boost operations and lived experience](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/news/2024/march/22/240322-wargaming-hub).

### Training and tradecraft

The [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Academy of the United Kingdom|Defence Academy]] at Shrivenham runs the wargaming training pipeline:

- [Defence Wargaming Foundation Course](https://www.da.mod.uk/courses/defence-wargaming-foundation-course/).
- [Defence Wargaming Practitioner Course](https://www.da.mod.uk/courses/defence-wargaming-practitioner-course/).
- [Defence Wargaming Design Course](https://www.da.mod.uk/courses/defence-wargaming-design-course/).

The MOD doctrinal reference is the [Wargaming Handbook (DCDC, PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/5a82e90d40f0b6230269d575/doctrine_uk_wargaming_handbook.pdf) produced by the (then) Development, Concepts and Doctrine Centre, now Defence Futures and Force Design.

### Customer base

DWC supports customers across UK defence and security. Confirmed customer surfaces include:

- Front Line Commands (Army, Royal Navy, RAF, Strategic Command / CSOC).
- Cabinet Office.
- Wider government and security departments.
