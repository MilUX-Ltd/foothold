# Centre of Excellence in Defence, Risk and Resilience (CEDRR)

A [[CRM/organisations/academia/University of Portsmouth|University of Portsmouth]]-hosted Centre of Excellence bringing together expertise across global governance, geopolitical conflict, risk, security, deterrence, AI and autonomy, ethics, disaster response, and organisational and community resilience.

University of Portsmouth: [Centre of Excellence in Defence, Risk and Resilience](https://www.port.ac.uk/research/research-centres-and-institutes/centre-of-excellence-in-defence-risk-and-resilience-cedrr). Research portal: [CEDRR](https://researchportal.port.ac.uk/en/organisations/centre-of-excellence-in-defence-risk-resilience).

## Role in the landscape

CEDRR is one of the MOD-adjacent academic Centres of Excellence. Differs from the Dstl-funded [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Materials CoE|Defence Materials CoE (DMEx)]] in that CEDRR is university-hosted and education-oriented rather than a Dstl research partnership.

### Stated expertise areas

Per the University of Portsmouth page, the Centre brings world-class expertise across:

- Global governance.
- Geopolitical conflicts.
- Risk mapping.
- Peace, security, and deterrence.
- AI and autonomy.
- Ethics.
- Risk management.
- Disaster response.
- Organisational and community resilience.

### Stated mission

The Centre focuses on drivers of conflict and disaster while preparing professionals and organisations to better respond and promote resilience. Seeks solutions with governmental and non-governmental organisations, plus commercial and military collaborators.

### MOD recognition and education routes

The University of Portsmouth is an accredited MOD education provider and accepts **ELCAS credits** (Enhanced Learning Credits Administration Service), which significantly reduce the cost to individual service personnel students.

The Centre welcomes applications from military, first responders, and resilience practitioners; offers collaboration on research and innovation projects with access to world-leading experts.

### Other resilience networks

The Loughborough University-led [SCALE Centre](https://www.lboro.ac.uk/news-events/news/2026/april/scale-strategic-collaboration/) launched a strategic collaboration with the MOD in April 2026 to strengthen UK supply-chain resilience. Adjacent surface to CEDRR's resilience remit.
