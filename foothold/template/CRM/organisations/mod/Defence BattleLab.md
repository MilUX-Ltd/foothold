# Defence BattleLab

## Overview

Defence BattleLab (DBL) is a Ministry of Defence innovation facility located at Dorset Innovation Park. The site is owned by Dorset Council. Day-to-day innovation community activity on the site, the directory, events, challenges, masterclasses and forum, is run by **D3IP** (Dorset Innovation Park) as a brand of DII Ltd. The BattleLab itself hosts hackadays, innovation sprints, and collaborative events bringing together industry, academia, and defence.

## What it offers

- Hackadays and innovation sprints addressing live defence problems.
- A co-location space where defence-tech SMEs and primes can collaborate around shared challenges.
- Access to military end-users in a low-friction environment.
- A venue for the [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/clusters/South West Regional Defence and Security Cluster|South West Regional Defence and Security Cluster]] and similar regional events.

## How to engage

The D3IP directory and event calendar are the main entry points. Membership via D3IP gives access to the community and the events programme on the park.
