# Defence Scientific Advisory Council (DSAC)

## Summary

An **independent committee of scientists** that provides scientific and technological advice to the British Ministry of Defence. The DSAC is the **main source of independent advice on non-nuclear science, technology, engineering, analysis, and mathematics** to ministers and senior MOD officials. **Reclassified in October 2016** from an Advisory Non-Departmental Public Body (NDPB) to an **Expert Committee**; the successor body operates as the **Defence Science Expert Committee (DSEC)**. The DSAC name and gov.uk page persist; both names appear in current government documentation.

GOV.UK: [Defence Scientific Advisory Council](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-scientific-advisory-council); [About us](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-scientific-advisory-council/about); [Our governance](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-scientific-advisory-council/about/our-governance); [DSAC public appointments](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/public-appointments/defence-scientific-advisory-council-dsac). Review: [DSAC Triennial Review 2014](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defence-scientific-advisory-council-dsac-triennial-review-2014).

## What the DSAC does

The DSAC provides **independent, objective, timely advice based on its own knowledge and experience**. Per gov.uk About:

- Main source of independent advice on **non-nuclear** science, technology, engineering, analysis, and mathematics issues.
- Advice routed to ministers and senior MOD officials.
- Independent members are recognised specialists and experts in science, engineering, technology, or medicine.

## Independence

Independence is structural: **none of the independent members work in MOD or its agencies**. Their main employer is usually:

- Academia.
- Industry.
- The NHS.
- The Department of Health and Social Care (and other government departments).

The composition (chairman, vice chairman, plus 8 independent members) is small by design, supporting tight, expert-led deliberation rather than committee size.

## Sister and related bodies

- **Nuclear Research Advisory Council** (not yet a vault page): the **nuclear** equivalent of the DSAC's non-nuclear remit.
- US sister body: [Defense Science Board (DSB)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/GOVPUB-D-PURL-LPS111058/pdf/GOVPUB-D-PURL-LPS111058.pdf). The DSAC has a documented joint working relationship with the US DSB.
