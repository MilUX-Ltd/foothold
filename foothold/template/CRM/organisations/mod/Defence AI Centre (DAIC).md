# Defence AI Centre (DAIC)

The central hub for artificial intelligence across UK Defence. Champions, enables, and innovates AI in collaboration with government, industry, academia, and allies, working to strategic advantage of UK Armed Forces.

GOV.UK: [Defence Artificial Intelligence Centre](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/defence-artificial-intelligence-centre).

## Summary

DAIC sits inside the CSOC Innovation cluster alongside [[CRM/organisations/mod/jHub|jHub Defence Innovation]], the Defence Modelling and Simulation Office, and the Centre for Intelligence Innovation.

DAIC's stated vision is for UK Defence to become "the world's most effective, efficient, trusted, and influential Defence organisation for its size" in AI terms.

## Operating model: important distinction

DAIC focuses on AI **policy and governance**, that is, thinking about what could be done with AI and how to control it. The actual **building and application** of AI solutions to solve specific problems sits with [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|DE&S / NA-M]], not DAIC.

This distinction is not widely understood, and many have chased DAIC for build opportunities before discovering this. **Implication for defence SMEs:** AI solution opportunities should be targeted at NA-M (former DE&S), not DAIC.

The federated structure on gov.uk corroborates this: DAIC is supported by a core team drawn from [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Digital & Data (NA-D&D)|Defence Digital (Digital Foundry)]], [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|DE&S (Future Capability Group)]], and [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|Dstl (Cyber Information System Division)]]. The build muscle lives in those host bodies; DAIC coordinates.

## Three stated functions

Per [defencedigital.blog.gov.uk: The Defence AI Centre](https://defencedigital.blog.gov.uk/2022/07/31/the-defence-ai-centre/):

1. **Champion**: visionary hub accelerating coherent understanding, development, and use of AI capabilities.
2. **Enable**: provide common AI services, good practice, and a critical mass of expertise to support local adoption.
3. **Innovate**: rapidly develop, deliver, and scale AI projects that generate breakthroughs in strategic advantage in support of Defence priorities.

## Recent activity

- **AI Model Arena.** Launched to help redefine how Defence evaluates and procures AI technologies. Developed in partnership with UK AI company **Advai** and the **National Security Strategic Investment Fund**. See [GOV.UK: Launching the AI Model Arena](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/launching-the-ai-model-arena).
- **Defence and National Security AI Network.** DAIC establishing a cross-sector network to promote talent exchange and co-creation, encouraging civil sector investment in defence-relevant AI R&D.
- **Defence AI Playbook (January 2024).** Published guidance on how Defence approaches AI. [Defence AI Playbook PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/65bb75fa21f73f0014e0ba51/Defence_AI_Playbook.pdf).
- **Defence Artificial Intelligence Strategy (June 2022).** The underpinning strategy document. [PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/62a7543ee90e070396c9f7d2/Defence_Artificial_Intelligence_Strategy.pdf).
- **Commons Defence Committee.** Inquiry into [Developing AI capacity and expertise in UK Defence](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5901/cmselect/cmdfence/590/report.html), and the [Government response](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5901/cmselect/cmdfence/812/report.html).
