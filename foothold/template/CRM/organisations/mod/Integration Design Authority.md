# Integration Design Authority (IDA)

The cornerstone of Cyber and Specialist Operations Command's operating model. The focal point to drive the interventions, from concepts to capability, required to achieve Defence's ambition for an **Integrated Force**.

GOV.UK launch: [The launch of the Integration Design Authority](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/the-launch-of-the-integration-design-authority); [Driving Integration](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/multi-domain-integration). Procurement context: [Defence Procurement Minister oral statement on the Integrated Procurement Model (28 February 2024)](https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/defence-procurement-minister-oral-statement-on-the-integrated-procurement-model-28-february-2024).

## Role in the landscape

IDA was launched at **DSEI 2023** and became operational on **1 July 2024**. Its operationalisation triggered the evolution of the Development, Concepts and Doctrine Centre (DCDC) into Defence Futures, which itself has since become Defence Futures and Force Design (F&FD).

### Stated purpose

IDA exists to enable integration for Defence, across government, with allies, and international partners. Three named functions:

- **Lead Strategic Command's industry engagement**: helping develop cutting-edge integrated capabilities for Defence.
- **Provide "Integration as a Service"**: developing a high-level view in support of the Defence Force Development Board's role as the Defence Force Design Authority, advising and assuring project-level requirements to deliver integrated effects.
- **Drive Multi-Domain Integration (MDI)** across the Services and beyond.

### Role in the Integrated Procurement Model

From the second week of April 2024, the IDA formally took on its new oversight function in support of the **Integrated Procurement Model**. The mechanism is direct:

- If a capability proposal lacks a plan for data integration or accompanying enablers, IDA can send it back.
- IDA monitors programmes for opportunities where AI or novel technologies could be better harnessed.

This is a structurally significant role: IDA can shape the procurement decision before it is taken, not just review it afterwards.

### Sister evolution: DCDC to Defence Futures

The IDA launch on 1 July 2024 was paired with the evolution of DCDC into Defence Futures. The two now sit alongside each other inside CSOC / MSHQ, with IDA owning the integration design role and Defence Futures (now F&FD) owning concepts and force design.
