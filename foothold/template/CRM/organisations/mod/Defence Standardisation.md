# Defence Standardisation (DStan)

The MOD's centre of excellence for through-life standardisation management. Sits inside the Engineering Group under the Director Engineering and Safety. Responsible for developing MOD Standardisation Policy and managing the portfolio of UK Defence Standards (Def Stans).

GOV.UK: [UK Defence Standardization](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/uk-defence-standardization). External portals: [dstan.mod.uk](https://www.dstan.mod.uk/) and [StanMIS](https://www.dstan.mod.uk/StanMIS/).

## Role in the landscape

DStan develops and pursues MOD's standardisation policy, both nationally and internationally, with civil and military partners. The work supports increased interoperability and more effective acquisition. Policy framework: **Joint Service Publication (JSP) 920**.

### Standards portfolio

- **c.900 extant Defence Standards (Def Stans).** Reviewed within a four-year cycle to deliver and support current and future MOD capabilities.
- Managed by a team of **Standardisation Programme Managers** (qualified engineers).
- Supported by expert technical committees including representatives from academia, the Science and Technology Gateway, UK industry, and the MOD.

### Key responsibilities

- Maintenance and development of the relevant and required portfolio of UK Defence Standards.
- Negotiating, influencing, and agreeing **NATO standardisation policy** and procedures (STANAGs) to support UK military operations in achieving NATO Capability Targets.
- Provision of access for MOD employees to national and international civil standards: British Standards (BSs), European Standards (ENs), and International Standards (ISOs) via the Standards online service.

### Published Defence Standards

The Def Stan portfolio is published via gov.uk and via the [British Defence Standards portal at IHS Markit](https://global.ihs.com/standards.cfm?publisher=MODUK). One notable example: [Defence Standards (Def Stan) 00-970 amendments](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defence-standards-def-stan-970-amendments).

### Disambiguation

DStan, the standardisation function, is distinct from [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|Dstl]] (the Defence Science and Technology Laboratory). Both are MOD bodies but operate in different spaces: DStan handles standards; Dstl handles science and research.
