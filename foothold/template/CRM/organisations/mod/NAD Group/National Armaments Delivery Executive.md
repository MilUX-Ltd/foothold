# National Armaments Delivery Executive

> **New executive agency created 2 April 2026** as part of the NAD Group reorganisation. Holds the freedoms and delegations transferred from the retired DE&S.

## Summary

The executive agency to which the [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|DE&S]] freedoms and delegations have been transferred. All staff previously employed by DE&S now belong to this agency, working day-to-day to their respective Director General within the NAD Group structure. Terms and conditions of employment are unchanged.

## Role in the landscape

The Delivery Executive is the **legal vehicle** that lets the NAD Group act with executive-agency authority across its constituent pillar areas, particularly [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|NA-Materiel]], which is the bulk of the former DE&S core delivery function. Without an executive agency at this level, the NAD Group's nine pillars would not have the contractual and people-management freedoms they need to operate at pace.

### Why the agency exists

DE&S was a single executive agency with its own brand and CEO. The 2 April 2026 reorganisation:

- Retired the DE&S brand.
- Retired the DE&S CEO role.
- Distributed DE&S's functions across multiple new NAD Group pillars (NA-M, NA-P&P, NA-C, and NA-D&D, plus elements into NA-L&S).
- Created the National Armaments Delivery Executive as the single legal entity through which the freedoms and delegations operate across all those pillars.

### People

All former DE&S employees became staff of the Delivery Executive at the same point. Day-to-day each staff member reports through the NAD Group DG structure (DG Materiel, DG Plans & Portfolios, DG Corporate, etc.), while remaining contractually employed by the Delivery Executive. The DE&S "nine professions" framework continues inside the Delivery Executive structure.

### What it does

The Delivery Executive's executive-agency status gives it:

- Contracting authority for major defence acquisition contracts.
- People-management freedoms that the wider Civil Service does not routinely allow.
- The ability to operate the post-March-2025 [DE&S Operating Model](https://des.mod.uk/tag/operating-model/) across the new NAD Group structure.

### Relationship to the wider operating model

DE&S transitioned to its new Operating Model on 28 March 2025. The Delivery Executive carries that Operating Model forward inside the new NAD Group structure, with the four areas (Gateway, Corporate, Core Delivery, System Integration) mapping into the NAD pillars rather than sitting inside DE&S as a single agency.
