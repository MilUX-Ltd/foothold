# National Armaments Delivery Executive (NADEx)

> **Renamed 13 April 2026** from *Defence Equipment & Support (DE&S)*. The DE&S executive agency was renamed the National Armaments Delivery Executive (NADEx) as part of the NAD Group reorganisation. Old name retained for search continuity.

## Summary

The National Armaments Delivery Executive (NADEx) is the executive agency formerly known as Defence Equipment & Support (DE&S), renamed in April 2026 as part of the NAD Group reorganisation. It carries forward the executive-agency freedoms and delegations that DE&S held. Its staff now work day-to-day to their respective Director General within the NAD Group structure, while the agency continues as the employing body. Terms and conditions of employment are unchanged.

## Role in the landscape

As an executive agency, NADEx gives the NAD Group the contractual and people-management freedoms it needs to operate at pace across its constituent pillars. The former DE&S core-delivery function now sits as [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)]], with other former DE&S functions mapping into the wider NAD Group structure.

### People

Former DE&S employees continue as staff of the agency under its new name. Day-to-day each staff member reports through the NAD Group DG structure (DG Materiel, DG Plans & Portfolios, DG Corporate, and so on). The DE&S "nine professions" framework continues inside the agency.

### Operating model

DE&S transitioned to its new Operating Model on 28 March 2025. NADEx carries that Operating Model forward inside the NAD Group structure, with the four areas (Gateway, Corporate, Core Delivery, System Integration) mapping into the NAD pillars.
