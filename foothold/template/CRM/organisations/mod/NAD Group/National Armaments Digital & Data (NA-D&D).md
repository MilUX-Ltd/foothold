# National Armaments Digital & Data (NA-D&D)

> **Renamed 2 April 2026** from *Defence Digital*. Old name retained for search continuity.

## Summary

The MOD's central capability for digital, data, technology and ICT services. Now sits within NAD Group as the Digital & Data pillar, led by a Director General who also retains the cross-MOD Chief Digital Information Officer role.

Headquartered at MOD Corsham with a Whitehall presence at MoD Main Building. Per TechMarketView's *UK Defence SITS Suppliers, Trends and Forecasts 2025*, NA-D&D (as Defence Digital) accounts for an estimated **c.62% of total UK Defence SITS spend**, making it the single largest customer in the UK Defence systems-integration market.

Led by **DG Digital & Data (Chief Digital Information Officer for cross-MOD working)**.

## What NA-D&D Does

The remit spans the full lifecycle from policy and architecture through to delivery, operations and assurance across the Front Line Commands (Royal Navy, British Army, RAF, Strategic Command / CSOC) and the wider Defence enterprise. It runs the major Defence SITS contracts, operates the core MOD networks (DFTS / Modnet / Modnet 2), and is the customer-side authority for cross-Defence digital programmes including data, identity, cloud, end-user computing and information assurance.

Operationally, NA-D&D is structured around capability portfolios, with the **Foundry** as a delivery-and-experimentation function inside it. Across 2024-2026 the organisation has been moving from a portfolio of long-running contracts to a portfolio-and-services operating model, with implications for how suppliers contract and how UCD, product and service-design disciplines plug into MOD delivery.

## Strategic position

NA-D&D sits at the top of the MOD digital procurement food chain. Industry-analyst (notably TechMarketView) reporting through 2025-2026 frames it as the customer that suppliers must cultivate to be credible in UK Defence SITS:

- **NTT DATA UK&I** secured the Zodiac Independent Assurance Service contract in May 2026, its first direct UK defence contract win, with scope including "user-centred and requirements-focused evaluations." Independent assurance was the chosen route in because NTT DATA has no existing Armed Forces delivery contracts and could therefore remain objective.
- **Capgemini's** UK defence SITS revenues grew 27% in 2024 (TechMarketView, fastest of the leading suppliers in the segment), with NA-D&D among the principal clients.
- **Kainos** is the most visible "challenger" platform supplier across NA-D&D, the Foundry, and other public-sector customers.

## Constituent functions

NA-D&D operates the commercial-routes portfolio covered on [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Defence Digital Commercial|Defence Digital Commercial]]:

- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/DDAD|DDAD (Digital Decision Accelerators for Defence)]]: £180m Open Framework, ASGARD delivery vehicle.
- **TacCIS**: Tactical Communications and Information Systems.
- **Defence Tech Scaler**: scaling route for SMEs into MOD digital.
- **MODCloud (ACE/ICE)**: public cloud hosting platform on Azure and AWS up to OFFICIAL.
- **Foundry**: NA-D&D's digital delivery and experimentation function.
- **D2S (Defence Digital Service)**: software and applications acceleration capability inside the Foundry.
