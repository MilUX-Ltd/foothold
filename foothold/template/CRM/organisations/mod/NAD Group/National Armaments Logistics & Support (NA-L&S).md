# National Armaments Logistics & Support (NA-L&S)

> **Renamed 2 April 2026** from *Defence Support*. Old name retained for search continuity.

## Summary

The support and logistics pillar of NAD Group. Coheres MOD's inventory management, sustainment, and through-life logistics across the Defence enterprise.

Led by **DG Logistics & Support**, who also holds the cross-MOD post of **Chief Defence Logistics & Support (CDLS)** as the MOD's 3-star Functional Owner for Support.

## Role in the landscape

NA-L&S is the new name for what was previously called the **Defence Support** function, established on 1 April 2020 originally under UK Strategic Command (now CSOC). The 2 April 2026 NAD Group reorganisation moved the function under NAD Group leadership while retaining the cross-MOD CDLS post for joint working.

### Leadership

- **Current Chief Defence Logistics & Support (CDLS):** Vice Admiral **Andy Kyte**.
- Per techUK reporting, CDLS is the MOD's 3* Functional Owner for Support (FOfS) and Head of the Defence Support organisation.

### Mission

The Defence Support function exists to:

- Cohere MOD inventory management.
- Implement strategy, policies, and standards across the MOD and its defence organisations.
- Provide sustainment and through-life logistics across Defence.

### Functional split with DE&S

[[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|DE&S / NA-M]] is responsible for purchasing and delivering equipment and support services to the Front Line Commands (Army, RN, RAF, CSOC). NA-L&S sits at the policy and coherence level above this, with the Front Line Commands themselves responsible for the storage and distribution of inventory within their bases and at deployed locations.
