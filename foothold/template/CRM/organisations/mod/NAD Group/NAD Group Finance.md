# NAD Group Finance

> **Group-level function** established as part of the 2 April 2026 NAD Group reorganisation. Finance now sits as the tenth NAD Group area alongside the nine Director-General-led pillars, led by the **Group Finance Director**.

## Summary

NAD Group Finance is responsible for an overall budget of c.**£28 billion per annum** on the Equipment / Infrastructure plans (EP/IP), c.**£5.6 billion per annum** on Readiness / Run, and a Balance Sheet in excess of **£100 billion**, including **£15bn Net Book Value of Inventory**.

The purpose of NAD Group Finance is to deliver the right numbers at the right time, putting finance central to informed decision-making and maximising value from the available budget. The function frames its work three ways:

- **Driving Value** — being an active and influential partner to the business; expertise and judgement to support good decision-making.
- **Adding Value** — delivering accurate, insightful, timely, and meaningful financial information to stakeholders.
- **Protecting Value** — ensuring a robust control environment and accounting for all financial transactions accurately and efficiently.

Led by **Group Finance Director — David Johnson**. The mix of role titles below the Director level ('Deputy Director', 'Head of', 'Lead') reflects current local nuances across the Group and will be standardised as the new operating conditions are confirmed.

## Key roles of the Function

- **Finance Business Partnering** — advice and guidance to budget holders in projects and portfolios who commit resources and spend money to achieve Defence aims, ensuring they remain within the scope of expenditure approved by Parliament.
- **Informing decision-making** — financial implications considered as part of business planning.
- **The Strategic Planning Cycle (SPC)** — setting the Departmental Budget and internal allocation to deliver Defence capability.
- **In-Year Management (IYM)** — managing and reporting financial performance through the budgetary chain and to Parliament.
- **NAD Group inputs into the Annual Report and Accounts (ARAc)** — multiple stakeholder communities; financial accounting; governance, assurance, and controls.
- **Customs & Tax advice (including VAT)** — tax policy direction on VAT, employment-related Benefits in Kind, Customs Duty, and IR35 implications associated with Intermediaries legislation.

## New categories of spend under Defence Reform

Under Defence Reform, Finance is a **Group A function**: functional activities and services are mainly provided to the wider organisation through an end-to-end (E2E) service delivery approach. Some Finance staff remain deployed into Delivery and other parts of NAD Group, but with clear reporting lines back to the Function. The MOD finance function in the Department of State has authority to prioritise resource.

Two new categories of spend are set by Defence Reform: **INVEST** and **READINESS**.

- **INVEST budget** — for new capabilities. NAD Group is the budget owner and is delegated this funding directly. Includes the Equipment and Infrastructure Plans (EP/IP).
- **READINESS budget** — two aspects:
  - Normal running costs (pay, allowances, pensions, etc.) — NAD Group holds this budget directly.
  - Capability maintenance — MSHQ is the budget holder but delegates day-to-day budget management to NAD Group.

NAD Group also acts as **budget manager** for some funding delegated from the Defence Nuclear Enterprise (DNE) and the Department of State (DoS).

Terminology:

- **NAD "Owned"** — funding for which NAD is the direct budget holder (e.g. the INVEST budget).
- **NAD "Delegated"** — funding delegated to NAD but for which NAD is not the budget holder (e.g. budget delegated from MSHQ).
- For running costs the terms **NAD "Owned" Readiness** or **NAD "Owned" Run** are used.

## How the budgets flow

**INVEST.** The INVEST budget — including both the Equipment Plan and Infrastructure Plan — flows directly into NAD Group from the Strategic Finance team in the Department of State. For commissioned work, budgets flow to Delivery areas, which deliver against agreed priorities and are accountable for budgetary control within their pillars. **Unprogrammed funds are held in [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Plans & Portfolios (NA-P&P)|Plans & Portfolios (NA-P&P)]] until commissioned**.

**READINESS.** The READINESS budget is held by MSHQ (as Readiness Budget Holder) and delegated to NAD Group for delivery of in-service capability sustainment. The budget flows through Military Commands into NAD Group delivery areas once a **Balance of Investment (BOI)** assessment has been made by NA-P&P. The NAD Group READINESS or RUN budget will flow directly into NAD Group from the Strategic Finance team within DoS. Some aspects are held at Group level — most of the workforce budget, some professional services, and Other Programme Cost (OPC) budgets. Where possible, budgets for Professional Services are allocated in line with EP/IP programmes being delivered.
