# NAD Group Finance

> **Group-level function** established as part of the 13 April 2026 NAD Group reorganisation. Finance is a group-level function alongside the nine Director-General-led pillars, led by the Group Finance Director.

## Summary

NAD Group Finance is the group-level finance function for the NAD Group. It provides financial management, planning and control across the Group's pillars, supporting decision-making and accounting for the Group's spend under the wider Defence Reform programme.

Led by the **Group Finance Director**. The mix of role titles below the Director level ('Deputy Director', 'Head of', 'Lead') reflects current local nuances across the Group and will be standardised as the new operating conditions are confirmed.
