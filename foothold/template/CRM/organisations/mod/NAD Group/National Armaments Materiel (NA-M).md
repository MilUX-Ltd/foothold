# National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)

> **Renamed 13 April 2026** from *DE&S Core Delivery*. The former DE&S core-delivery function is now NA-M, a Director-General-led area within NAD Group. The DE&S executive agency itself was renamed the National Armaments Delivery Executive (NADEx). Staff Terms and Conditions unchanged.

## Summary

The materiel-acquisition arm of NAD Group: the former DE&S Core Delivery function, now operating under a Director General within the new structure. Around 11,000 staff inside core delivery, with the wider DE&S workforce cited at c.12,500 across the full operating model. Manages an annual programme of work worth approximately **£13 billion**, ensuring the UK Armed Forces have the equipment and support needed for their operations.

NA-M procures, equips, and supports the Armed Forces across the land, maritime and air domains. It also houses the **Engineering and Safety** function, which appears in the DE&S operating model as a System Integration Authority.

Led by **DG Materiel**. Staff belong to the [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Delivery Executive|National Armaments Delivery Executive]] (NADEx), the renamed DE&S executive agency, which carries the executive-agency freedoms and delegations. The mix of role titles below DG level ('Deputy Director', 'Head of', 'Lead') reflects current local nuances across the Group and will be standardised as the new operating conditions are confirmed.

DE&S corporate site: [des.mod.uk](https://des.mod.uk/); [About DE&S](https://des.mod.uk/who-we-are/).

## New operating model (post-March 2025)

DE&S fully transitioned to a **new Operating Model on 28 March 2025**: the biggest transformation at DE&S in a decade, designed around "operational excellence" as the guiding principle. The Operating Model has four interlocking areas, which now map onto the wider NAD Group structure:

| DE&S area | Now operates as |
|---|---|
| Gateway | [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Plans & Portfolios (NA-P&P)\|NA-P&P]] (renamed from Options & Commissioning) |
| Corporate | [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Corporate (NA-C)\|NA-C]] (renamed from Corporate & Shared Services) |
| Core Delivery | **NA-M (this page)** |
| System Integration | Cross-cutting through the wider NAD Group |

DE&S sources: [DE&S transition milestone puts operational excellence at its core](https://des.mod.uk/des-transition-milestone-puts-operational-excellence-at-its-core/); [DE&S transformation reaches operational milestone](https://des.mod.uk/des-transformation-reaches-operational-milestone/); [Standing up the Gateway](https://des.mod.uk/standing-up-the-gateway/).

DE&S is structured around **nine professions**, each supporting its own vital strand of the organisation.

## Key Areas (inherited from DE&S Core Delivery)

- Defence Digital Commercial.
- Engineering.
- Corporate Finance.
- Defence Intelligence (within NA-M's customer base).
- Missile Defence Centre.

## AI build vs governance distinction

AI solution **building and application** sits with NA-M (formerly DE&S), not with the Defence AI Centre (DAIC) (which owns AI **policy and governance**). For defence-AI delivery work, NA-M is the operating customer.

NA-M is also the operating customer for the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Engineering Delivery Partner (EDP)|EDP]] framework (delivered via the Aurora Provider Network).
