# National Armaments Director Group (NAD Group)

## Overview

The National Armaments Director Group (NAD Group) is a senior-level organisation within UK Defence responsible for delivering warfighting capability. Established March 2025; restructured 13 April 2026 with new identity, branding, and consolidated leadership under nine Director-General-led areas plus the Defence Infrastructure Organisation. NAD Group oversees the acquisition, integration, fielding, and in-service support of military capability across multiple value streams and mission threads.

NAD Group is led by the **National Armaments Director**.

**GOV.UK:** [National Armaments Director (NAD) Group](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/national-armaments-director-nad-group)

## Strategic context

NAD Group operates in a strategic environment defined by accelerating technological change, contested supply chains, and adversaries that adapt faster than traditional acquisition cycles. The structural challenge it sets out to solve: fragmented delivery, excessive work in progress, long and variable lead times, weak feedback loops, and governance optimised for compliance rather than operational effect.
