# National Armaments Director Group (NAD Group)

## Overview

The National Armaments Director Group (NAD Group) is a senior-level organisation within UK Defence responsible for delivering warfighting capability. Established March 2025; restructured 13 April 2026 with new identity, branding, and consolidated leadership under nine areas, one of which is National Armaments Infrastructure (the Defence Infrastructure Organisation). NAD Group oversees the acquisition, integration, fielding, and in-service support of military capability across multiple value streams and mission threads.

NAD Group is led by the **National Armaments Director**.

**GOV.UK:** [National Armaments Director (NAD) Group](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/national-armaments-director-nad-group)

> **How gov.uk lists it (checked 15 October 2026).** The gov.uk NAD Group page lists nine areas: NA-Intl, NA-P&P, NA-M (formerly part of DE&S), NA-Innov (Dstl and UKDI, formerly DASA), NA-L&S (formerly Defence Support), NA-D&D (formerly Defence Digital), NA-C&I (including the National Shipbuilding Office), NA-C (Corporate Services Modernisation and Defence Business Services) and National Armaments Infrastructure (the DIO). It counts the DIO as one of the nine and does not list Finance as an area. Where this page and gov.uk differ, gov.uk wins. [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/NAD Group Finance|NAD Group Finance]] is the Group's finance function, led by a Group Finance Director. gov.uk does not list Finance as an area, so it is a corporate function, not a tenth area.

The Defence Investment Plan (30 June 2026, paragraph 20) places a **Defence Investment Unit** within the NAD Group as the MOD's centre of expertise for finance and investment. It does not say which area it sits in.

The mix of role titles below DG level ('Deputy Director', 'Head of', 'Lead') reflects current local nuances across the Group and will be standardised as the new operating conditions are confirmed.

### Executive agency

- [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Delivery Executive\|National Armaments Delivery Executive]] (NADEx) — the DE&S executive agency renamed. Former DE&S staff work day-to-day to their respective DG.

### Former DE&S

- **Defence Equipment & Support (DE&S)** was renamed the **National Armaments Delivery Executive (NADEx)** in April 2026. Core-delivery functions now sit as NA-M. Staff Terms and Conditions unchanged.

## Strategic context

NAD Group operates in a strategic environment defined by accelerating technological change, contested supply chains, and adversaries that adapt faster than traditional acquisition cycles. The structural challenge it sets out to solve: fragmented delivery, excessive work in progress, long and variable lead times, weak feedback loops, and governance optimised for compliance rather than operational effect.
