# National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)

> **New pillar created 13 April 2026** as part of the NAD Group reorganisation. Brings the MOD's principal innovation organisations under a single Director General.
>
> **This page is the canonical home** for content previously held on separate pages for Dstl, UK Defence Innovation (UKDI), DASA, and the Defence Investment Unit.

## Summary

Defence's innovation pillar. Houses two named constituent bodies:

- **Defence Science and Technology Laboratory (Dstl)**, the MOD's executive-agency science and technology arm.
- **UK Defence Innovation (UKDI)**, the consolidated central MOD innovation function announced under the 2025 Strategic Defence Review; DASA (the Defence and Security Accelerator) became part of UKDI in February 2026; UKDI also takes in **FCI (Future Capability Innovation)**, the **Rapid Innovation Team**, and the **Command Innovation Hubs**. UKDI has a ringfenced annual budget of at least £400 million.
The **Defence Investment Unit** is covered below, but published sources place it only within the NAD Group, not in NA-Innov (see the Defence Investment Unit section).

Led by **DG Innovation** (3\* appointment pending). The mix of role titles below DG level ('Deputy Director', 'Head of', 'Lead') reflects current local nuances across the Group and will be standardised as the new operating conditions are confirmed.

## Dstl: Defence Science and Technology Laboratory

The MOD's science and technology arm. Executive agency, sponsored by the Ministry of Defence. Maximises the impact of S&T for the defence and security of the UK by supplying sensitive and specialist S&T services to MOD and wider government.

GOV.UK: [Defence Science and Technology Laboratory](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory).

### Sites

Three main sites:

- **Porton Down** (Salisbury, Wiltshire): primary campus.
- **Portsdown West** (near Portsmouth): naval and maritime focus.
- **Fort Halstead** (near Sevenoaks, Kent).

### Operating model

Dstl runs the **Defence Science and Technology Portfolio**, a series of programmes and standalone projects designed to meet MOD capability needs and ensure UK armed forces remain at the cutting edge of technology. Partners in industry and academia are crucial to delivery and there are routine opportunities to collaborate.

Key frameworks managed by or linked to Dstl:

- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/ASTRID|ASTRID]]: Analysis for Science and Technology Research in Defence.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Serapis|Serapis]]: scientific and technical R&D framework, explicitly aimed at broadening Dstl's supplier base (six capability lots spanning C4ISR, space, synthetic environments, ISTAR, C2, and data analytics with AI/ML).
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Weapons Sector Research Framework|Weapons Sector Research Framework]].
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Defence Technology Exploitation Programme (DTEP)|Defence Technology Exploitation Programme (DTEP)]]: route for transitioning successful innovation into capability.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Defence Supplier Capability Programme|Defence Supplier Capability Programme]].
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/R-Cloud|R-Cloud]]: Dstl's S&T research dynamic agreement.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/PROGENY|PROGENY]]: maritime research framework.

Key Dstl-led programmes:

- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/DragonFire|DragonFire]]: Laser Directed Energy Weapon (LDEW) capability demonstrator, Dstl-led consortium with MBDA UK, Leonardo UK, and QinetiQ. Royal Navy fielding from 2027.

### Commercialisation

**Ploughshare Innovations** is Dstl's commercialisation arm, transferring Dstl intellectual property to commercial markets.

### Dstl publication streams worth monitoring

Dstl publishes around 1,125 pages on GOV.UK including 68 policy papers, detailed guides, and document collections. The most useful ongoing publication streams for defence-SME intelligence:

- **[Dstl's work: delivering mission success](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/dstls-areas-of-work-programmes-and-project-portfolios)**: Dstl's published areas of work, programmes, and project portfolios. The most useful single starting point for finding which Dstl programme to engage on.
- **[Dstl annual reports and accounts](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/dstl-annual-reports-and-accounts)**: annual published view of Dstl spend, headcount, and major programme delivery.
- **[Apply For Funding](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/apply-for-funding)**: the funding-route collection covering ASTRID, Weapons Sector Research Framework, and ad-hoc competitions.
- **[Centre for Defence Enterprise case studies](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/centre-for-defence-enterprise-case-studies)**: legacy CDE programme outputs, from before DASA was set up.
- **[Dstl biscuit books](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/dstl-biscuit-books)**: short, accessible Dstl-authored primers on topics including [Assurance of AI and Autonomous Systems](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/assurance-of-ai-and-autonomous-systems-a-dstl-biscuit-book) and [Visualising Data](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/a-bite-sized-guide-to-visualising-data-a-dstl-biscuit-book). Useful explainers for non-defence audiences.
- **[Standards for Integrated C5ISR/EW Systems (STICS)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/standards-for-integrated-c5isrew-systems-stics)**: Dstl-led integration-standards work for C5ISR / EW.
- **Active competitions**: e.g. [Autonomy in Challenging Environments Phase 2](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/competition-autonomy-in-challenging-environments-phase-2) and [Countering Drones: Phase 2](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/countering-drones-finding-and-neutralising-small-uas-threats-phase-2).

## UK Defence Innovation (UKDI)

The MOD's single innovation front door. Launched on 1 July 2025 under the 2025 Strategic Defence Review, with a ringfenced annual budget of at least £400 million: £1.6bn over the four years of the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan]]. UKDI brought together previously separate innovation organisations:

- **DASA** (Defence and Security Accelerator). gov.uk records that DASA **became part of UKDI in February 2026**; the DASA name is no longer used as a front door, although some web addresses still carry it.
- **Defence Innovation Unit (DIU)**, distinct from the Defence Investment Unit below; note the acronym collision.
- **DE&S Future Capability Innovation (FCI)**.
- **Command Innovation Hubs** across the Services.

The Defence Investment Plan (30 June 2026) said UKDI would reach full operating capability on 1 July 2026, and that a new Director General Innovation manages both UKDI and Dstl. UKDI works to five themes: **Autonomy, Decision Advantage, Logistics and Support, Effects, and Protection** (gov.uk, 15 July 2026).

GOV.UK: [UKDI unifies innovation to accelerate frontline capabilities](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/ukdi-unifies-innovation-to-accelerate-frontline-capabilities-and-drive-national-growth) (15 July 2026); [How to work with UK Defence Innovation (UKDI)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-work-with-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi) (22 July 2026, updated 6 October 2026); [DASA organisation record](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-and-security-accelerator); [Launch of new body to harness innovative tech for the UK's Armed Forces](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/launch-of-new-body-to-harness-innovative-tech-for-the-uks-armed-forces) (1 July 2025).

The UKDI launch was paired with the announcement that UK Strategic Command would be renamed **Cyber & Specialist Operations Command (CSOC)** from 1 September 2025; the two reforms together form the core of the post-SDR MOD restructure. UKDI now sits inside NA-Innov, having originally been housed under Options and Commissioning.

### UKDI routes (formerly DASA)

- **Open Call for Innovation**: for disruptive ideas at any stage. At the 1 October 2026 update of the live funding page, the Defence Open Call had not reopened; a new "Innovating for Defence" route is in development, with no date.
- **Themed Competitions**: focused on a defined challenge. None open at 1 October 2026; Tactical Power, Rapid Detection of Toxic Gases and Generation After Next Electro-Optics are in the pipeline.
- **Security Open Call**: open; Cycle 2 closes 29 October 2026 (UK submissions only).
- **[[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Defence Innovation Loans|Defence Innovation Loans]]**: later-stage R&D and commercialisation funding with Innovate UK Loans Ltd. **Paused** "while we work on a new, improved service".
- **Ideas Marketplace**: a platform to share ideas and collaborate with innovators, government experts and end users. Collaboration, not funding.
- **Futures Lab**: run through the Engineering Delivery Partner; shapes innovation challenges with MOD clients.
- **UKDI Innovation Partners**: civil servants who advise on services and competitions and guide submissions. Ask through your UKDI submission service account; a partner contacts you within 15 working days.
- **Regional Defence and Security Clusters**: 13 regional networks that UKDI works with; see [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/Regional Defence and Security Clusters|Regional Defence and Security Clusters]].

- **[Defence Innovation Loans (past cycles)](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/defence-innovation-loans-past-cycles)**: archive of the loan programme.
- **[Submit a proposal](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/defence-and-security-accelerator-how-to-submit-a-proposal)** and **[Assessment process and criteria](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/assessment-process-and-criteria)**: the operational how-to.

- **[Ethical, legal and regulatory guidance](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/defence-and-security-accelerator-ethical-legal-and-regulatory-guidance)** and **[Applying for approval for MOD research involving humans](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/apply-for-ethical-approval-for-mod-research-involving-humans)**: relevant for any user-research-based proposal.

### FCI: Future Capability Innovation (within UKDI)

DE&S-rooted function for novel, disruptive, complex, and cross-cutting technology risks and opportunities that touch multiple defence projects. Current named priority: advanced manufacturing (3D printing) for defence logistics, ensuring equipment parts are available for Front Line Commands deployed globally.

#### Innovate UK adjacency (via UKDI / FCI / RIT)

UKDI's regional and dual-use orientation places several Innovate UK-funded programmes squarely adjacent to the NA-Innov funnel. Worth tracking even though these sit outside NAD Group:

- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/BridgeAI|BridgeAI]] (£100m Innovate UK programme to accelerate AI/ML adoption; consortium with Digital Catapult, Alan Turing Institute, STFC Hartree Centre, BSI).
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Aerospace Technology Institute (ATI) Programme|ATI Programme]] (£2.3bn to 2035, joint DBT (now BIST) / Innovate UK / Aerospace Technology Institute; principal civil aerospace R&D funding route with SME-tailored stream).
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Future Flight Challenge|Future Flight Challenge]] (UKRI / Innovate UK / DfT; AAM and drones dual-use trajectory).

### Rapid Innovation Team (within UKDI)

Wartime-pace function for urgent operational problems, using commercially available dual-use technology. Established on UKDI's launch day.

### Regional Engagement Teams (within UKDI)

UKDI established Regional Engagement Teams across the UK to identify and support dual-use innovation from SMEs and academic spin-outs, delivering targeted outreach and business development support.

## Defence Investment Unit

The MOD's defence-tech investment arm. The Defence Investment Plan (30 June 2026, paragraph 20) places it within the NAD Group as the MOD's centre of expertise for finance and investment, without naming an area; its notes are kept on this page so older links still resolve. **Acronym caution: "DIU" is also used for the Defence Innovation Unit inside UKDI.** The Defence Investment Unit (this entity) is the investment-facing function working with venture capital, banking, and strategic-finance partners. The Defence Innovation Unit (UKDI sub-component) is the consolidated innovation delivery function. Different bodies, same acronym.

### Recent activity

- **23 April 2026** ([gov.uk press release](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/unlocking-private-investment-to-drive-uk-defence-as-an-engine-for-growth)): "Unlocking private investment to drive UK defence as an engine for growth". Announcements:
  - **£20 million bespoke fund** for accelerated contracts to small, innovative British startups with limited or no previous business with the MOD.
  - **Defence Investors' Advisory Group (DIAG)** convening senior figures from UK banking, venture capital, and strategic finance to advise ministers on defence investment priorities.
  - Defence Secretary's first-of-its-kind meeting with venture capital firms.
  - A Dragon's Den-style event where pioneering defence firms pitched innovations directly to major investors.
