# National Armaments Corporate (NA-C)

> **Renamed 2 April 2026** from *Corporate & Shared Services*. Old name retained for search continuity.
>
> **This page is the canonical home** for content previously held on separate pages for the UK Hydrographic Office, the Oil and Pipelines Agency, and the Single Source Regulations Office.

## Summary

The corporate and shared services pillar of NAD Group. Houses the three executive bodies that are not core delivery, plus the cross-cutting corporate functions across NAD.

The three executive bodies are:

- **UK Hydrographic Office (UKHO)**: trading fund, world-leading hydrographic and marine geospatial data provider.
- **The Oil and Pipelines Agency (OPA)**: statutory public corporation managing MOD fuel infrastructure.
- **Single Source Regulations Office (SSRO)**: executive non-departmental public body, independent regulator of single source defence contracts.

Led by **DG Corporate**.

## UK Hydrographic Office (UKHO)

A trading fund of the Ministry of Defence and a world-leading centre for hydrography. UKHO supplies hydrographic, navigation, and marine geospatial data to mariners and maritime organisations across the world. Customers include the Royal Navy and other defence customers, plus the commercial shipping industry.

GOV.UK: [UK Hydrographic Office](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-hydrographic-office). External: [ADMIRALTY (UKHO commercial)](https://www.ukho.gov.uk/Pages/Home.aspx); blog [ukhodigital.blog.gov.uk](https://ukhodigital.blog.gov.uk/about/).

### Location and scale

- **Address.** Admiralty Way, Taunton, Somerset, TA1 2DN.
- **Workforce.** Approximately 900 staff.

### Purpose

UKHO delivers data and expertise to support safe, secure, and thriving oceans. Operational support to the Royal Navy and other defence customers; supply of charts and digital services to the commercial shipping industry; and contribution to Safety of Life at Sea (SOLAS), marine environment protection, and the efficiency of global trade.

Key outputs include **Admiralty charts** (SOLAS-compliant), publications, and digital services for ships trading internationally.

### History

The Admiralty's first Hydrographer was **Alexander Dalrymple**, appointed in 1795 on the order of King George III. UKHO traces its lineage to that appointment.

## The Oil and Pipelines Agency (OPA)

A statutory public corporation, sponsored by the Secretary of State for Defence. Formed in 1986 under the Oil and Pipelines Act 1985.

GOV.UK: [The Oil and Pipelines Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/oil-and-pipelines-agency); [About us](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/oil-and-pipelines-agency/about); [Governance](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/oil-and-pipelines-agency/about/our-governance).

### Current role

OPA manages, operates, and maintains **6 Naval Oil Fuel Depots and a Petroleum Storage Depot** on behalf of the Ministry of Defence. Two named sites in southern England support the adjacent HM Naval Bases:

- **Gosport OFD**: supports HM Naval Base Portsmouth.
- **Thanckes OFD**: supports HM Naval Base Devonport.

### Recent history

OPA was previously responsible for managing the **Government Pipelines and Storage System (GPSS)** until its sale in 2015. The government sold the GPSS to Compañía Logística de Hidrocarburos (CLH) for £82 million; CLH took over GPSS operations on 30 April 2015. OPA's remit is now narrower than pre-2015, focused on naval fuel infrastructure.

### Stated objectives

- Improve operational availability and regulatory adherence of the fuels infrastructure.
- Ensure value for money.
- Deliver and enable optimal energy outcomes, enabling resilience, operational capability, and advantage for UK Defence.

## Single Source Regulations Office (SSRO)

Executive non-departmental public body, sponsored by the Ministry of Defence. Established by the **Defence Reform Act 2014**, which created the regulatory framework for single source defence contracts.

GOV.UK: [Single Source Regulations Office](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/single-source-regulations-office); [About us](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/single-source-regulations-office/about); [I would like to find out about the SSRO and the regulatory framework](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/i-would-like-to-find-out-about-the-ssro-and-the-regulatory-framework). External: [ssro.gov.uk](https://www.ssro.gov.uk/).

### Purpose

Independent regulator on MOD single source procurement, and custodian of the single source procurement framework for contracts placed with defence suppliers without the usual competitive process. Mission: maintain a single source procurement framework that assures value for money for the UK taxpayer and allows a fair and reasonable price for single source suppliers.

### Scope of single source

Single source procurement is used when there is only one potential supplier (for example, building nuclear submarines) or when equipment or a service is needed urgently. SSRO oversees the regulatory framework for these non-competitive defence contracts.

### Key responsibilities

- Annual recommendations to the Secretary of State on the **Baseline Profit Rate** and other rates for determining profit in qualifying contracts.
- Opinions and determinations on questions referred by the MOD and defence contractors.
- Provision of a helpdesk to contractors and the MOD.
