# Defence Infrastructure Organisation (DIO)

> **Brand retained** through the 13 April 2026 NAD Group reorganisation. DIO sits as the ninth pillar of NAD Group alongside the eight National Armaments X pillars, keeping its existing name and identity.

## Summary

The MOD's estate expert. Plans, builds, maintains, and services the infrastructure that the armed forces depend on to operate. Manages around **1.8% of the UK's landmass**, including **over 200 Military Establishments** (Naval Bases, Air Stations, and Barracks), nearly **260,000 hectares of Defence Training Estate**, over **5,000 office buildings**, over **15,000 warehouses and storage facilities**, nearly **50,000 Service Family Properties**, **130 schools**, and nearly **1,000 sports facilities**. Also responsible for the MOD's overseas estate in Belize, Cyprus, the Falkland Islands, Kenya, and other locations.

Under the new NAD Group operating model, Defence Infrastructure runs as a dedicated **Infrastructure Function**. Work splits between new capital projects and maintenance and sustainment of the existing estate.

Led by the **DG Infrastructure**, who is also the DIO Chief Executive (see [GOV.UK profile](https://www.gov.uk/government/people/mike-green)). The mix of role titles below DG level ('Deputy Director', 'Head of', 'Lead') reflects current local nuances across the Group and will be standardised as the new operating conditions are confirmed.

GOV.UK: [Defence Infrastructure Organisation](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-infrastructure-organisation); [About DIO](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-infrastructure-organisation/about). Blog: [Inside DIO](https://insidedio.blog.gov.uk/).

## What DIO does

DIO is the customer-side authority and delivery partner for the entire MOD estate. The remit covers:

- **Built estate.** Barracks, headquarters, training facilities, technical buildings, fuel depots, ranges, ammunition storage, naval bases, RAF stations, Army garrisons.
- **Family and single living accommodation.** Service Family Accommodation (SFA) and Single Living Accommodation (SLA) for service personnel and their families.
- **Defence Training Estate (DTE).** The training estate covers around **190,000 hectares** in the UK and accounts for an estimated **78% of the defence estate**. DIO manages the DTE in partnership with **Landmarc**.
- **Overseas estate.** Permanent overseas bases and supporting infrastructure in Belize, Cyprus, the Falkland Islands, Kenya, and other locations.

DIO produces the long-running [Sanctuary magazine](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/sanctuary) covering conservation and stewardship of the defence estate, runs the [MOD Sanctuary Awards](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/ministry-of-defence-sanctuary-awards), and publishes the major firing-times notices for training areas (Lulworth, Dartmoor, Castlemartin, South East Training Estate, and others).

## Scale and usage

- **Daily users of the training estate:** an average of **9,000 service personnel**, rising to as many as **30,000 a day** during busy periods.
- **Training activities range** from urban warfare practice to live artillery, amphibious landings and assaults, armoured vehicle driving, and firing from naval vessels and aircraft.
- **Public-access constraints** are a recurring DIO theme, with regular safety campaigns ("Respect the Range") around access to training areas for ramblers, cyclists, and dog-walkers.

## Recent activity (2025 to early 2026)

DIO is publication-rich and the news flow is a useful sense of pace and direction:

- **2 November 2025: [Defence Housing Strategy](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/defence-housing-strategy-a-9bn-generational-renewal-for-forces-family-housing-and-100000-new-homes-on-mod-land-to-drive-growth).** £9bn "generational renewal" for forces family housing, plus 100,000 new homes on MOD land to drive growth. Improves over 40,000 military homes.
- **23 January 2026: [State-of-the-art training facilities and accommodation completed at MOD Lyneham](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/state-of-the-art-training-facilities-and-accommodation-completed-at-mod-lyneham).** New training and accommodation at the Wiltshire site.
- **30 January 2026: [New review of single living accommodation](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-review-of-single-living-accommodation-to-transform-military-housing-standards).** Independent review launched to fix substandard accommodation for around 80,000 single and unaccompanied service personnel.
- **12 February 2026: [Upgrade works under way on airfield at RAF Coningsby](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/upgrade-works-under-way-on-airfield-at-raf-coningsby).** £28m project to upgrade the Lincolnshire airfield.
- **26 February 2026: [New gym opens to boost soldier wellbeing at Kendrew Barracks](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-gym-opens-to-boost-soldier-wellbeing-at-kendrew-barracks).** Cottesmore facility for around 1,800 soldiers.
- **4 March 2026: [Respect the Range campaign](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/respect-the-range-how-following-access-rules-strengthens-uk-defence).** Public safety on training areas.

## Key publication streams

DIO publishes around 707 pages on GOV.UK, including 22 policy papers, detailed guides, and document collections. The most useful published streams for ongoing monitoring:

- **[DIO technical guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/dio-technical-guidance)**: the DIO Specifications, Standards, and Design Guides used across the MOD estate. The substantive technical-knowledge base for anyone working on MOD buildings, sites, or training areas.
- **[DIO practitioner guides](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/dio-practitioner-guides)**: operational how-to guides for DIO and partner teams.
- **[Airfield specifications and design guides](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/airfield-specifications-and-design-guides)**: airfield-specific specifications relevant to any aerospace-adjacent work.
- **[Military ranges firing notices](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/firing-notice)**: published firing-times by training area (Lulworth, Dartmoor, Castlemartin, South East Training Estate, etc.).
- **[Respect the Range: staying safe on MOD land](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/safe-access)**: the public-facing safety guidance behind the 2026 access campaign.
- **[Public access to the Defence estate and section 5 of the National Security Act 2023](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/public-access-to-the-defence-estate-and-section-5-of-the-national-security-act-2023)**: post-NSA-2023 guidance.
- **[DIO Information Notes](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/dio-information-note)**: ad-hoc DIO information notices.
- **[DIO accommodation customer satisfaction tracker survey: index](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/dio-accommodation-customer-satisfaction-tracker-survey-index)**: long-running tracker survey behind the Defence Housing Strategy direction.
- **[MOD heritage reports: index](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/mod-heritage-reports-index)**: published heritage reports across the estate.
- **[Dartmoor pages](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/dartmoor-pages)**: the Dartmoor training-area-specific publication stream.
- **[MOD byelaws: Scotland](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/byelaws-scotland)**: the Scotland-specific byelaws collection.
- **[Section 28 Directions](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/section-28-directions)**: published Section 28 Directions affecting MOD land and operations.

DIO also runs the [Sanctuary magazine](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/sanctuary) (conservation and stewardship) and the [Inside DIO blog](https://insidedio.blog.gov.uk/).
