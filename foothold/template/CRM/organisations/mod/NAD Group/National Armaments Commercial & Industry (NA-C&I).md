# National Armaments Commercial & Industry (NA-C&I)

> **Renamed 2 April 2026** from *Commercial & Industry*. Old name retained for search continuity.
>
> **This page is the canonical home** for content previously held on separate pages for the National Shipbuilding Office.

## Summary

The commercial and industry pillar of NAD Group. Covers the National Shipbuilding Office (NSO) and the broader defence-industry commercial relationships across the NAD portfolio.

Led by **DG Commercial & Industry**.

---

## National Shipbuilding Office (NSO)

The MOD-hosted, cross-government body responsible for driving transformative change in the UK shipbuilding enterprise. Established by the Secretary of State for Defence in September 2021. Coordinates vessel procurement across all government departments and implements the National Shipbuilding Strategy.

GOV.UK: [National Shipbuilding Office](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/national-shipbuilding-office); [Defence Secretary announces launch of National Shipbuilding Office](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/defence-secretary-announces-launch-of-national-shipbuilding-office); [Refresh to the National Shipbuilding Strategy](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/refresh-to-the-national-shipbuilding-strategy).

### Leadership

Chief Executive: **Rear Admiral Rex Cox** (appointed at launch in 2021).

### Operating model

The NSO is hosted by the MOD with an outward-facing remit across other government departments, including:

- HM Treasury.
- Home Office.
- Cabinet Office.
- BEIS (now part of DSIT and DBT).
- DEFRA.
- FCDO.
- Department for Transport.
- Department for International Trade (now DBT).
- Department for Education.
- The Devolved Administrations.

The team is based in London and works in a hybrid model nationwide.

### Recent activity

- **Refresh to the National Shipbuilding Strategy** published as the current operating mandate.
- **NSO Centres of Excellence Playbook** published in partnership with Maritime UK / the wider UK maritime sector, intended to drive productivity and competitiveness across UK shipbuilding.
