# Military Intelligence Services (MIS)

The UK Ministry of Defence's integrated military intelligence enterprise. Launched on 16 December 2025 in response to a surge in hostile intelligence activity against the MOD. MIS exists to integrate intelligence collection, assessment, targeting, and exploitation across Defence, ensuring coherence and operational effectiveness.

GOV.UK: [Military Intelligence Services](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/military-intelligence-services); [UK launches new Military Intelligence Services as hostile threats surge](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/uk-launches-new-military-intelligence-services-as-hostile-threats-surge).

## Role in the landscape

MIS brings every intelligence unit and organisation within Defence under one organisation for the first time. Sits inside [[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command|Cyber and Specialist Operations Command]] (CSOC).

### Constituent intelligence units

MIS brings together the previously-separate intelligence units from:

- Royal Navy.
- British Army.
- Royal Air Force.
- UK Space Command.
- Permanent Joint Headquarters (PJHQ).
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Intelligence|Defence Intelligence (DI)]]: the strategic-level all-source assessment function.

### Leadership

Led by the **Chief of Defence Intelligence (Adrian Bird)**, underpinned by the **Defence Intelligence Charter**.

### Disciplines covered

MIS comprises military and civilian experts across the full intelligence-discipline stack:

- **GEOINT** (geospatial intelligence).
- **HUMINT** (human intelligence).
- **SIGINT** (signals intelligence).
- Counter-intelligence.

### Defence Counter-Intelligence Unit (DCIU)

The **Defence Counter-Intelligence Unit (DCIU)** was launched alongside MIS to address the rise of more than 50% in hostile intelligence activity against the MOD in the year to 2025. DCIU is the dedicated counter-intelligence function within MIS.

### External partnerships

MIS works in close partnership with the other UK intelligence agencies:

- **GCHQ** (signals intelligence and cyber).
- **MI5** (the Security Service, domestic).
- **SIS / MI6** (the Secret Intelligence Service, foreign).

The partnership exists primarily to provide intelligence products for policymakers in the MOD and UK Government, and is set out in the published [Joint Doctrine Publication 2-00 (Intelligence, Counter-Intelligence and Security)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf).
