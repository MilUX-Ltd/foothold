# Defence Availability Centre

A Centre of Excellence focused on equipment availability and sustainment across UK Defence. Sits inside the Centres of Excellence cluster alongside the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Wargaming Centre|Defence Wargaming Centre]], the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Materials CoE|Defence Materials CoE]], the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence AI Centre (DAIC)|Defence AI Centre]], and the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Test and Evaluation Innovation Gateway|Test and Evaluation Innovation Gateway]].

> **Documentation caveat.** Public gov.uk documentation directly badged "Defence Availability Centre" is sparse at time of capture. The substantive activity it represents (equipment availability, sustainment, in-service support) sits across [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Logistics & Support (NA-L&S)|NA-L&S]] and [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|NA-M]] depending on platform and lifecycle stage.

## Role in the landscape

The Defence Availability Centre is the natural home for the disciplines that turn "we have ordered N platforms" into "N platforms are ready when the user needs them": availability modelling, reliability and maintainability engineering, supply-chain visibility, mission system status, and the integration of these into capability decisions. Equipment availability is increasingly central to the post-SDR readiness agenda.
