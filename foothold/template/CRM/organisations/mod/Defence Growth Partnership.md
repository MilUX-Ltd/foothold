# Defence Growth Partnership (DGP)

The long-running government-industry partnership for **growing the UK defence sector and increasing defence exports**. Co-led by the Department for Business and Trade (DBT) (lead department) and the MOD (UK customer), with **13+ defence primes plus ADS** as industry partners. Delivered operationally through the [[CRM/organisations/mod/UK Defence Solutions Centre|UK Defence Solutions Centre (UKDSC)]] in Farnborough.

UKDSC: [About DGP](https://www.ukdsc.org/about_dgp/).

## Role in the landscape

DGP's stated purpose is to **secure a thriving UK defence sector delivering security, growth and prosperity for the UK**. Two named aims:

- **Grow the UK's global market share** through increased defence exports.
- **Foster greater collaboration and innovation** across the sector.

### Funding and structure

- **50/50 funding model.** Government and industry align resources (mix of cash and resource contributions).
- **£30m initial commitment** for start-up plus three years.
- **17 members.** 13+ defence primes, plus DBT and ADS. Member primes provide secondees who staff UKDSC.

### Strategic positioning

DGP and the newer [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Industrial Joint Council|DIJC]] are different bodies with overlapping remits. DGP (2013-) focuses on UK defence exports and prime-led collaboration with narrow membership. DIJC (2025-) is the broader MoD-industry coordination forum replacing the Defence Suppliers Forum, with wider membership including SMEs, investors, and trade unions.
