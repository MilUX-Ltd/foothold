# Defence Science and Technology Laboratory (Dstl)

## Organisation Details

- **Website:** [gov.uk: Dstl](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory)
- **Sector:** Defence, Science and Technology, Government
- **Status:** an executive agency of the Ministry of Defence, and a Public Sector Research Establishment. Its operating costs are recovered mainly through charges to customers. Source: [Dstl Framework Document, March 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory-framework-document/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory-dstl-framework-document-july-2021).
- **Headquarters:** Porton Down, Salisbury, Wiltshire. gov.uk also lists Portsdown West (Fareham), Alverstoke (Gosport) and a Newcastle site ([gov.uk: About Dstl](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory/about)).
- **Formed:** 1 July 2001, from the split of the Defence Evaluation and Research Agency ([gov.uk: About Dstl](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory/about)).

## Overview

Dstl is the MOD's in-government science and technology organisation. It leads the MOD's science and technology programme, coordinates UK involvement in NATO science and technology activity, and works with more than 40 government departments and agencies, mainly the MOD ([gov.uk: About Dstl](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory/about)). The Framework Document describes its purpose as delivering high impact science and cutting-edge technology for the defence, security and prosperity of the UK.

The MOD is both Dstl's owner and its main customer. The MOD Chief Operating Officer is Dstl's sponsor, and Dstl's Chief Executive is its Accounting Officer ([Framework Document](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory-framework-document/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory-dstl-framework-document-july-2021)).

## Where it sits

gov.uk places Dstl, together with UK Defence Innovation (UKDI), in National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov), one of the nine areas of the National Armaments Director Group ([gov.uk: NAD Group](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/national-armaments-director-nad-group)). That page now carries a notice that the group is superseded by a [National Armaments](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/national-armaments) organisation page, with no date given. See [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/NAD Group|NAD Group]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/MOD Structure|MOD Structure]].

The Dstl "About" page on gov.uk still says the Defence and Security Accelerator is part of Dstl. Other gov.uk pages say it became part of UKDI. This pack follows the later pages: see [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)]].

## What it buys and funds

Dstl's guidance says the presumption is that work is done by external suppliers unless there is a clear reason for Dstl to do or lead it. It buys science and technology research, analysis and related services for the MOD and wider government, mainly through the routes below. Dstl follows the MOD procurement process but does not use the MOD's CP&F system, and it has no preferred supplier lists ([gov.uk: How to sell to or work with Dstl](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory/about/procurement), updated 12 November 2026).

## How a small supplier engages

1. **Join R-Cloud.** [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/R-Cloud|R-Cloud]] is Dstl's main place to advertise bid opportunities. Registration is free. Suppliers are matched to requirements and invited to compete inside the portal. Dstl's guidance says R-Cloud version 5 is open for applications and goes fully live in December 2026. Supplier guidance: [R-Cloud for suppliers](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/r-cloud-for-suppliers) (covers version 4 only).
2. **Watch the notice portals.** Dstl and MOD requirements appear on the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal]]. Contracts above £12,000 including VAT appear on [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Contracts Finder|Contracts Finder]], and high-value contracts, usually above £139,688 including VAT, on [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Find a Tender Service|Find a Tender Service]]. Purchases under £10,000 follow Dstl's low-value purchasing policy.
3. **Use the research frameworks.** Dstl's frameworks page (4 July 2025) lists three, each run by a lead contractor you approach to join: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/ASTRID|ASTRID]], available until 8 April 2027; the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Weapons Sector Research Framework|Weapons Sector Research Framework]], extended to 31 March 2027; and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Serapis|Serapis]], closed for new tasks from 9 July 2025. See [Dstl science and technology procurement frameworks](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/dstl-science-and-technology-procurement-frameworks). Dstl also names the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Engineering Delivery Partner (EDP)|Engineering Delivery Partner]] and the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Neutral Vendor Framework for Innovation|Neutral Vendor Framework for Innovation]] as occasional routes. The maritime research framework [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/PROGENY|PROGENY]] is covered in this pack.
4. **Use the SME programme.** [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/SME Searchlight|SME Searchlight]] supports small businesses working on defence science and technology research, with webinars and bidding help.
5. **Try UKDI funding.** UKDI sometimes runs funding competitions for Dstl, advertised on the same portals. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI - How to Engage]].
6. **Meet the supply chain.** Dstl lists [[Intelligence/events/DPRTE/About DPRTE|DPRTE]], [[Intelligence/events/DSEI/About DSEI|DSEI]], trade bodies and regional defence and security clusters as places to meet it.
7. **Check security early.** Everyone in Dstl's supply chain needs security screening to the Baseline Personnel Security Standard, and must meet [Dstl's security requirements](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/security-requirements-for-selling-to-or-working-with-dstl).

Intellectual property: Dstl says it protects supplier IP but reserves UK government use of research it funded ([Dstl suppliers guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-science-and-technology-laboratory/about/procurement)). The R-Cloud guide says contracts leave IP with the supplier unless the optional R-Cloud+ route is chosen. General enquiries: srm@dstl.gov.uk, as given on Dstl's suppliers guidance.

Programme pages in this pack that involve Dstl include [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/DragonFire|DragonFire]].
