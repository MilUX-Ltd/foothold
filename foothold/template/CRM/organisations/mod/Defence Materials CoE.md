# Defence Materials Centre of Excellence (DMEx)

The UK's flagship Centre of Excellence for advanced materials in defence, run under a £42.5 million research partnership set up by [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|Dstl]] in January 2024. Brings together world-leading UK experts in a national effort to accelerate advances in defence material technology for extreme physical environments.

GOV.UK: [New advanced materials centre of excellence](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-advanced-materials-centre-of-excellence).

## Role in the landscape

DMEx is the named home for the materials science work that underpins the next generation of UK defence capability. Sits inside the Centres of Excellence cluster alongside the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Wargaming Centre|Defence Wargaming Centre]], the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence AI Centre (DAIC)|Defence AI Centre]], and others.

### Stated purpose

Research, create, and prototype new materials for the armed forces that can survive in the harshest conditions, including:

- Temperatures of 1,000 °C.
- Polar to tropical operations.
- High-impact vibrations, shock, and blasts.
- Extreme water depth.

Application areas explicitly cited:

- Body armour for personnel.
- Protection of sensitive electronics in satellites from radiation damage.
- Corrosion-resistant submarine components.

### Leadership and partners

- **Lead partner.** The [Henry Royce Institute](https://www.royce.ac.uk/news/dstl-centre-of-excellence/) for advanced materials. Operates its hub at the [[CRM/organisations/academia/University of Manchester|University of Manchester]].
- **Other named partners.** 23 partners across academia, industry, and research organisations, including the Catapult Network.
- **University of Sheffield** is a named academic partner. See [Sheffield to partner in new £42.5m Dstl Materials Centre of Excellence](https://www.sheffield.ac.uk/engineering/news/sheffield-partner-new-ps425m-dstl-materials-centre-excellence).
- **Knowledge Centre for Materials Chemistry (KCMC)** is also engaged. See [KCMC: Dstl launch £42.5M advanced materials Centre of Excellence](https://www.materialschemistry.org.uk/news/2024/dstl-launch-42-5m-advanced-materials-centre-of-excellence).

### Adjacent: Centre of Excellence in Energetic Materials

Cranfield University hosts a separate but adjacent [Centre of Excellence in Energetic Materials](https://www.cranfield.ac.uk/press/news-2017/centre-of-excellence-in-energetic-materials-announcement), formalising the UK national requirement for energetic materials across government, industry, and academia.
