# National Cyber Force (NCF)

The UK's joint offensive cyber operations capability. A unified MOD-GCHQ partnership consolidating offensive cyber activity in the United Kingdom, enabling offensive cyber capability against security threats, hostile states, terror groups, extremism, hackers, disinformation, and election interference. Announced by the Prime Minister as part of the Integrated Review in November 2020.

GOV.UK: [National Cyber Force](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/national-cyber-force); [About us](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/national-cyber-force/about); [Working for NCF](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/national-cyber-force/about/recruitment).

## Role in the landscape

NCF is the offensive counterpart to the [National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC)](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/national-cyber-security-centre), which concentrates on defensive cyber activity to protect government departments, strategic infrastructure, and industry. The two work alongside each other:

- **NCF**: offensive cyber operations.
- **NCSC**: defensive cyber operations.

NCF sits inside [[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command|CSOC]] per the gov.uk About page for the Command, while being jointly owned with **GCHQ**.

### Constituent organisations

The NCF draws personnel from four bodies under a single unified command (a first for the UK):

- **GCHQ**: signals intelligence and cyber.
- **Ministry of Defence (MOD)**.
- **Secret Intelligence Service (MI6 / SIS)**.
- **Defence Science and Technology Laboratory (Dstl)**: now part of NA-Innov.

### Permanent location: Samlesbury

In 2022 the government announced the **permanent NCF headquarters at Samlesbury, Lancashire**, on land at the former Samlesbury Aerodrome alongside BAE Systems' aircraft components site. The build-out was scoped for **3,000+ people by 2023**, anchoring the wider "North West Cyber Corridor" alongside Manchester GCHQ presence.

### Leadership

A new Commander of the National Cyber Force was announced via GOV.UK: [New Commander of the National Cyber Force appointed](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-commander-of-the-national-cyber-force-appointed).

### Operations

Public information on NCF operations is deliberately limited. Open-source commentary frames NCF's role as countering hostile state cyber activity, disrupting terrorist networks, and supporting wider UK national security objectives. The pairing with [[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command|CSOC]] and the offensive-defensive split with NCSC is the structural pattern that matters.
