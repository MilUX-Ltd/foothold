# Submarine Delivery Agency (SDA)

An Executive Agency of the Ministry of Defence. Established April 2018. Manages the procurement, in-service support, and disposal of UK nuclear submarines: some of the most complex equipment in the world. Sits inside the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Nuclear Organisation|Defence Nuclear Organisation]] enterprise.

GOV.UK: [Submarine Delivery Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/submarine-delivery-agency); [About us](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/submarine-delivery-agency/about). Framework Document: [SDA Framework Document v1.0 (Feb 2025, PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/67bc6cf0ba253db298782cc2/Submarine_Delivery_Agency_Framework_Document_.pdf). Recent review: [SDA Public Body Review summary 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/submarine-delivery-agency-public-body-review-summary-2025/submarine-delivery-agency-public-body-review-summary-2025).

## Role in the landscape

SDA provides the assured capabilities the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Royal Navy/Royal Navy|Royal Navy]] needs to deliver and maintain the **Continuous at Sea Deterrent (CASD)**, plus the wider submarine fleet (Astute, Dreadnought). Sits alongside [[CRM/organisations/mod/AWE Nuclear Security Technologies|AWE]] (warheads) inside the DNO partnership.

### Key areas of delivery

Per [gov.uk About](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/submarine-delivery-agency/about):

- Maintaining CASD by safely delivering available, capable, and reliable in-service submarines and systems.
- Leading the design and construction of all new UK submarines.
- Managing the delivery of nuclear infrastructure needed to support the submarine fleet.
- Safely disposing of submarines no longer in service.
- Retaining and developing the submarine technology base.
- Supporting international partnerships.
- Developing a sustainable industrial base for the Submarine Enterprise.

### Locations and partners

- **HQ at Abbey Wood, Bristol.** Purpose-built MOD site shared with [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|DE&S (now NA-M)]]. Around three-quarters of SDA people are based there.
- **Embedded with BAE Systems at Barrow-in-Furness.** Part of the **Dreadnought Alliance**: SDA, BAE Systems, and Rolls-Royce people working together to drive delivery of the Dreadnought Class.

### International partnerships

SDA contributes to the international dimension of the UK submarine enterprise, notably AUKUS (Australia, UK, US) collaboration on nuclear-powered submarines.
