# Defence Nuclear Organisation (DNO)

One of the seven top-level budget holders within UK MOD. Formed in 2016 to oversee the Defence Nuclear Enterprise: the people, equipment, and infrastructure that together provide the UK's continuous-at-sea deterrent (CASD).

GOV.UK: [Defence Nuclear Organisation](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-nuclear-organisation); [About us](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-nuclear-organisation/about). Strategic context: [The UK's nuclear deterrent: the National Endeavour explained](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-nuclear-deterrence-factsheet/the-uks-nuclear-deterrent-the-national-endeavour-explained).

## Role in the landscape

DNO oversees the MOD's Defence Nuclear Enterprise (DNE), responsible for:

- The scientific research surrounding the nuclear programme.
- The delivery of nuclear warheads.
- Providing appropriate infrastructure for the programme.
- Disposing of nuclear assets.

### Leadership

Led by the **Chief of Defence Nuclear, Madelaine McTernan CB**.

### Headcount and locations

- **Headcount.** 750+ personnel.
- **HQ.** MOD Main Building, London.
- **Other sites.** Bristol, Reading, Scotland.

### Defence Nuclear Enterprise (DNE) partners

DNO leads a partnership of organisations that operate, maintain, renew, and sustain the UK's nuclear deterrent:

- **DNO** itself.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Royal Navy/Royal Navy|Royal Navy]]: operates the submarine-launched deterrent.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Submarine Delivery Agency|Submarine Delivery Agency (SDA)]]: delivers and supports the UK submarine fleet, including Dreadnought.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/AWE Nuclear Security Technologies|AWE Nuclear Security Technologies]]: designs and manufactures the warhead components.

### Costs and oversight

The Commons Library publishes regular research briefings on the cost of the UK's strategic nuclear deterrent: [CBP-8166](https://commonslibrary.parliament.uk/research-briefings/cbp-8166/). Annual cost figures are reported in the MOD's Major Projects portfolio.
