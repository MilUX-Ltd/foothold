# Defence Futures

The MOD's strategic foresight, concepts, and force design function. Currently operates as **Defence Futures and Force Design (F&FD)** within Military Strategic Headquarters (MSHQ), following Defence Reform. F&FD itself is the third iteration of this function: the original Development, Concepts and Doctrine Centre (DCDC) evolved into "Defence Futures" after the launch of Strategic Command's Integration Design Authority on 1 July 2024; F&FD then succeeded Defence Futures under Defence Reform. The Joint Doctrine Team has been spun out to its own [Joint Doctrine group](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/joint-doctrine).

## Role in the landscape

F&FD's stated mission is to design the forces the MOD needs to be prepared for the challenges and opportunities the country faces now, and to prepare for those expected in future. It informs, guides and supports strategy and force development through strategic foresight, concepts, capability strategy, and force design analysis. Sits inside MSHQ alongside the other strategic-level functions of the MOD.

### Three core teams

Per [gov.uk: Defence Futures and Force Design](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/defence-futures-and-force-design-ffd):

- **Strategic Foresight Team.** Long-term, threat-informed global strategic context for policy, strategy, force design, and capability development. Publishes through the Global Strategic Trends Programme and contributes to cross-government foresight work with international partners.
- **Insights, Analysis, Research, Challenge (IARC) Team.** Analysis and research to support continuous development of MOD policy and strategy. Reviews and disseminates external defence-sector publications to senior personnel via an internal blog, and facilitates external academic support through the Strategic Analysis Support Contract.
- **Strategic Concepts and Warfare Development Team.** Two branches: **Strategic Concepts** (15-year exploratory concepts shaping policy, force design, capability) and **Strategic Warfare Development** (assesses the current force, synthesises strategic lessons, codifies military strategic problems on a 0 to 5-year horizon).

### Internship Programme

F&FD runs a paid internship programme open to Masters-level postgraduate students, drawn from any academic discipline. A useful route for academic partners or student researchers.
