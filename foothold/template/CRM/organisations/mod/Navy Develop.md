# Navy Develop

The Royal Navy's Develop Directorate. The senior-level home for Navy innovation, experimentation, and capability development. Houses the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office|Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office (DCTO)]] consolidated in April 2025, the **Fleet Experimental Squadron**, and historically the standalone **NavyX** programme that DCTO has now absorbed.

## Role in the landscape

Navy Develop is the directorate-level function inside the Royal Navy through which the Service runs its innovation pipeline and brings new technology into the fleet faster than traditional procurement allows. Structurally, DCTO and the Fleet Experimental Squadron now report through Navy Develop.

### Origins: NavyX

NavyX was the Royal Navy's "Autonomy, Lethality and Innovation accelerator", inspired by the US Navy's NavalX and established in 2019. A small team of around 22 people drawn from the Civil Service, the Navy, and industry. NavyX sat within the Develop Directorate and was responsible for testing and trialling new technology with the aim of delivering new capabilities to the fleet faster than by traditional routes.

NavyX was consolidated into [[CRM/organisations/mod/Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office|DCTO]] on 29 April 2025 alongside the Office of the Chief Technology Officer and the Navy AI Cell. The NavyX brand has effectively been retired; its remit and people are inside DCTO.

### Key platforms

- **XV Patrick Blackett.** Bought for use by the original NavyX programme in 2022 as a platform for trials and experimentation with new technologies. Currently in service with DCTO.
- **XV Excalibur.** Uncrewed submarine used for quantum navigation trials in October 2025.
- **Autonomous Pacific 24 RIB (APAC24).** Long-running autonomy programme.

### Recent investment

The Royal Navy announced a [£75 million investment to revolutionise Royal Navy operations](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/75m-investment-set-to-revolutionise-royal-navy-operations) tied to the autonomy and lethality direction Navy Develop is pursuing. Drives much of the work now executed by DCTO and the Fleet Experimental Squadron.
