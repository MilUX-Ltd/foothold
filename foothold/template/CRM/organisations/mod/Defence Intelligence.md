# Defence Intelligence (DI)

The MOD's strategic intelligence and assessment function. Empowers decision-makers across the MOD and UK government by providing intelligence products and assessments. Part of [[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command|Cyber and Specialist Operations Command]], and a constituent of the Military Intelligence Services launched in December 2025.

GOV.UK: [Defence Intelligence](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/defence-intelligence). Doctrine: [Joint Doctrine Publication 2-00, Intelligence, Counter-Intelligence and Security (PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf).

## Role in the landscape

DI's stated role is **all-source intelligence analysis**: drawing information from a variety of overt and covert sources to provide the intelligence needed to support military operations, contingency planning, and to inform defence policy and procurement decisions.

DI differs from the UK's intelligence agencies (MI6, GCHQ, MI5) in that it is an integral part of the MOD rather than a stand-alone organisation. It works in close partnership with all three.

### Leadership and staffing

- **Chief of Defence Intelligence:** Adrian Bird.
- **Headcount:** approximately 4,500 personnel; two-thirds armed forces, one-third civilians.
- **Locations:** primarily London, Bedfordshire, Cambridgeshire, and Lincolnshire (with RAF Wyton being a key intelligence site).

### Place inside Military Intelligence Services

The Military Intelligence Services (MIS), launched December 2025, brings Defence's expert intelligence capabilities under a single enterprise: intelligence units from the Royal Navy, British Army, Royal Air Force, UK Space Command, and Permanent Joint Headquarters. DI is the strategic-level all-source analysis function inside MIS, led by the Chief of Defence Intelligence.

### Doctrine and counterintelligence

The published [Joint Doctrine Publication 2-00 (Intelligence, Counter-Intelligence and Security)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/653a4b0780884d0013f71bb0/JDP_2_00_Ed_4_web.pdf) is the joint doctrine that underpins DI's work and the wider MIS structure. The Defence Counter-Intelligence Unit (DCIU) was launched alongside MIS in response to a rise of more than 50% in hostile intelligence activity against the MOD in the year to 2025.
