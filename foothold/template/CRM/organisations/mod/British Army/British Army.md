# British Army

## Organisation Details

- **Website:** [army.mod.uk](https://www.army.mod.uk/)
- **Sector:** Defence, Military, Government
- **Location:** United Kingdom
- **Head of the Service:** the Chief of the General Staff, listed among the senior military posts on the [MOD organisation page](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ministry-of-defence).
- **Regular strength:** 73,660 at 1 July 2026. The Army Reserve total strength is given as 25,850 at the same date. Source: [Quarterly service personnel statistics: 1 July 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/quarterly-service-personnel-statistics-2026/quarterly-service-personnel-statistics-1-july-2026).

## Overview

The British Army is the land warfare force of the United Kingdom and one of the three single Services of HM Armed Forces. The MOD organisation page on gov.uk lists it, with the Royal Navy, the Royal Air Force and Cyber and Specialist Operations Command, as one of the military services and commands ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ministry-of-defence)).

For a founder, the Army is both a user and a sponsor of capability. It sets the need and tests ideas with soldiers. Most equipment is then bought through the MOD's central acquisition organisation, not by the Army itself.

## How it is organised

- **Command of the Service.** Under Defence Reform, the Chief of the Defence Staff now commands the Service Chiefs, and heads a new Military Strategic Headquarters responsible for force design and war planning across the integrated force. The Service Chiefs are held to account for their day-to-day spending through a readiness budget. Source: [Defence Secretary's speech on Defence Reform, 18 February 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/defence-secretarys-speech-on-defence-reform--2); [Major defence reforms launched, 25 October 2024](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/major-defence-reforms-launched-with-new-national-armaments-director-to-tackle-waste-and-boost-industry). See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/MOD Structure|MOD Structure]] for the wider picture.
- **Transformation.** The Army's modernisation programme is Future Soldier, announced in 2021 ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/british-army-unveils-most-radical-transformation-in-decades)).

## What it buys and how

- **Equipment and support** are bought through the National Armaments Director Group (NAD Group). Under Defence Reform the National Armaments Director runs a single investment budget that combines eight earlier procurement budgets and is responsible for the end-to-end acquisition system; the Military Strategic Headquarters holds the operations budget and the Service Chiefs hold readiness budgets. See [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/NAD Group|NAD Group]] and [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)]], which gov.uk describes as formerly part of Defence Equipment and Support. Sources: [Defence Reform speech](https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/defence-secretarys-speech-on-defence-reform--2); [NAD Group on gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/national-armaments-director-nad-group). Note that gov.uk now shows the NAD Group page as superseded by a [National Armaments](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/national-armaments) organisation page.
- **Army-specific programmes** in this pack include [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Land ISTAR|Land ISTAR]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/THEIA|THEIA]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Project VANAHEIM|Project VANAHEIM]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/ZODIAC|ZODIAC]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Project NYX|Project NYX]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Project CORVUS|Project CORVUS]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Project ANVIL|Project ANVIL]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Dismounted Situational Awareness (DSA)|Dismounted Situational Awareness]]. Funding is covered on [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]].
- **Industrial strategy for land.** The [Land Industrial Strategy](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/land-industrial-strategy) (18 May 2022) sets out how the Army, the MOD and industry are to work together on Army modernisation.

## How a small supplier engages

1. **Read the Army's Challenge Set.** The [British Army Challenge Set 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/british-army-challenge-set-2025) (21 July 2025, first version) lists the Army's rapid capability development challenges. gov.uk tells industry organisations that want to engage with it to use the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal]].
2. **Experiment with the Army.** [[CRM/organisations/mod/British Army/Army Innovation|Army Innovation]] is the Army's route for testing new ideas outside the normal procurement cycle. [[CRM/organisations/mod/British Army/Army Warfighting Experiment|Army Warfighting Experiment]] is its flagship experimentation programme. [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence BattleLab|Defence BattleLab]] is a collaborative prototyping space.
3. **Use the central innovation route.** UK Defence Innovation (UKDI) runs open calls, themed competitions and Innovation Partners who advise applicants. Start with [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI - How to Engage]] and [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)]]. Official guide: [How to work with UKDI](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-work-with-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi).
4. **Watch for published notices.** Opportunities are listed on the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Contracts Finder|Contracts Finder]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Find a Tender Service|Find a Tender Service]].
5. **Get SME help.** The [[CRM/organisations/government/Defence Office for Small Business Growth|Defence Office for Small Business Growth]] supports small suppliers. The MOD's [SME action plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mod-small-and-medium-sized-enterprise-sme-action-plan/ministry-of-defences-small-and-medium-sized-enterprise-sme-action-plan) aims to raise defence spend with SMEs by 50 per cent by summer 2028.
6. **Meet people.** [[Intelligence/events/DSEI/About DSEI|DSEI]] and [[Intelligence/events/DPRTE/About DPRTE|DPRTE]] are the main UK defence events. The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Founder's Procurement Playbook|Founder's Procurement Playbook]] explains the buying process.
