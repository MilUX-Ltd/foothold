# Army Warfighting Experiment (AWE)

The British Army's flagship experimentation programme. Runs the Army's exploration of new and emerging operational concepts and technologies at low to medium Technology Readiness Levels. Continuous since 2018 (previously known as URBan EXperimentation, URBEX). Disambiguation note: distinct from AWE Nuclear Security Technologies.

## Role in the landscape

AWE sits inside Army Innovation and is the principal vehicle through which the Army forges relationships with industry, academia, allies, and partners around future warfighting capability. Its stated purpose is to enhance access to new technologies and spur innovation at TRL levels the routine procurement pipeline does not reach.

Per [army.mod.uk: Army Warfighting Experiment](https://www.army.mod.uk/learn-and-explore/army-of-the-future/modernisation/army-warfighting-experiment/), AWE allows the Army to explore the possible and helps determine how the British Army wins wars in the future.

### Recent series

- **AWE: The Urban Series 2022 to 2024.** Collaboration with subject-matter experts to experiment with cutting and bleeding-edge technology, focused on how the Army can be optimised for future complex urban environments.
- **AWE Blunt and Dislocate.** Focus on manoeuvre supported by fires and information activities within an urban environment. Examines how a future Land Force can maximise advantage in the "first battle" by blunting an adversary's advance with organic and long-range firepower, while finding and striking to drive home an early culmination.
- **Warfighting Experimentation Force** ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/warfighting-experimentation-force-unveiled)): standing experimentation force unveiled as the persistent home for AWE delivery.

### Operating model

AWE acts as a brokerage between Army problem sets and outside solutions. Partners typically include:

- [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|Dstl]] for science and technology input. See [GOV.UK: Dstl tests new technology at Army Warfighting Experiment](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/dstl-tests-new-technology-at-army-warfighting-experiment).
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|DE&S / NA-M]] as the formal launch partner for AWE 20 (2019). See [GOV.UK: DE&S and British Army launch the Army Warfighting Experiment (AWE) 20](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/des-and-british-army-launch-the-army-warfighting-experiment-awe-20-experimentation-project).
- Industry, academia, allies, partners.

AWE is a strong candidate route for any defence-SME work touching user research, problem framing, or evidence-led capability assessment at low to medium TRL.
