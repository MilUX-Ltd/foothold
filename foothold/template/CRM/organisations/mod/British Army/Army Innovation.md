# Army Innovation

The British Army's solutions accelerator for delivering novel approaches and new technology into the hands of the user at pace. Sits inside Future Soldier, the most radical transformation of the Army in 20 years.

## Role in the landscape

Army Innovation is the branch that exists to give the Army an alternative pathway to test, develop, and adopt new technology that does not fit cleanly inside the normal procurement cycle. Two stated objectives, per [army.mod.uk: Army Innovation](https://www.army.mod.uk/learn-and-explore/army-of-the-future/modernisation/army-innovation/):

1. Enable the Army to test new and novel ideas, collecting evidence to allow capability sponsors to make informed decisions at the balance-of-investment stage.
2. Provide an alternative pathway for the Army to test and develop those new ideas that, due to the pace of technological change, have not been considered by the normal procurement process.

Innovation and experimentation form the bedrock of Future Soldier. The wider transformation is supported by a **Land Industrial Strategy (LIS)** designed to strengthen the relationship with the defence industry and unlock innovation and development. Inside the digital domain, **Programme THEIA** promotes new digital ways of working aligned to the Army Digital Data Plan.

### Recent funded activity

- **£66 million Army robotics boost** ([gov.uk, 2024](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/army-robotics-receive-66m-boost)): targeted investment to accelerate the adoption of robotic and autonomous systems across the Army.
- **British Army to increase lethality over the next decade** ([gov.uk press release](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/british-army-to-increase-lethality-over-the-next-decade-while-royal-navy-steps-up-innovation-in-nato)): paired announcement framing Army modernisation alongside Royal Navy NATO innovation.
