# MarWorks

The Royal Navy's information warfare technology accelerator. Specialists in delivering architectures that support data flow and tactical feeds over secure, agile communications. Works alongside the user community to run rapid experimentation that exploits leading technology and provides the naval service with cutting-edge solutions.

## Role in the landscape

MarWorks sits inside the Navy Innovation cluster alongside [[CRM/organisations/mod/Navy Develop|Navy Develop]], the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office|Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office (DCTO)]], and London Tech Bridge. It is the Navy's information warfare technology specialist, in contrast to the broader autonomy and AI focus of DCTO.

The unit's core relationship is with the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Royal Marines|Royal Marines]] and the **Future Commando Force** transformation, plugging tactical and communication technology into commando operations at the front of the user community.

### Stated focus

- Architectures that support data flow and tactical feeds over secure, agile communications.
- Rapid experimentation with leading information warfare technologies.
- Integration of commercial sensor and communications technology into Royal Marines and wider Navy use.

### Demonstrated work

- **Helmet Camera System for Royal Marines.** MarWorks procured both the network and the cameras, working with Visual Engineering to adapt general-purpose cameras to Royal Marines requirements. See [Royal Navy: Royal Marines to trial new helmet camera for enhanced tactical advantage](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/news/2020/june/05/200605-marines-helmet-camera).
- **Tactical IP network for Future Commando Force.** As part of Future Commando Force experimentation, MarWorks set up a tactical IP network, opening the way for new equipment and technology to be introduced to Royal Marines operating on the ground.
- **Messaging app rollout to 3 Commando Brigade.** During COVID lockdown, MarWorks led on behalf of Navy Digital the rollout of a secure messaging app to 3 Commando Brigade.
- **Commando Warrior 2019.** Early MarWorks-supported field experimentation: [Royal Marines take big step with new technology](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/news/2019/april/25/190425-commando-warrior).
