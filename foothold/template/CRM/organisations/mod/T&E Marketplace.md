# T&E Marketplace

The forthcoming MOD-led single portal for Test and Evaluation. A digital one-stop-shop allowing users to find, assess, and access T&E facilities and support services across the UK. Planned launch: **end of 2026**, with a **pilot earlier in 2026** running with select industry partners.

GOV.UK: [Defence Test & Evaluation (T&E) Transformation Programme](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-ministry-of-defence-test-and-evaluation-transformation-programme). Press: [DSEI UK 2025: UK to launch new T&E Marketplace](https://www.dsei.co.uk/news/dsei-uk-2025-exclusive-uk-launch-new-te-marketplace-next-year); [Defence Buyer: UK to launch T&E Marketplace](https://defencebuyer.com/uk-to-launch-te-marketplace/).

## Role in the landscape

The Marketplace sits inside the wider **T&E Transformation Programme**, started in 2022, which is the MOD's modernisation of how the UK tests and assures complex military systems. The Programme uses digital and mobile tools to combine real-world and virtual testing, aiming to speed up delivery of new capabilities to the frontline. The Marketplace is the consumer-facing portal of that Programme.

### Stated purpose

- Single online "one-stop shop" portal for T&E.
- Allow users to find, assess, and access T&E facilities and support services.
- Bring all UK T&E facilities into one single hub.
- Give industry a place to promote their capabilities and skills.
- Help the MOD find and make better use of the various testing ranges across the UK.

### Implementation timeline

- **2022**: T&E Transformation Programme started.
- **2025**: MOD announced (at DSEI UK 2025) intent to launch the T&E Marketplace.
- **Earlier 2026**: pilot launch with select industry partners to test and refine the portal.
- **End 2026**: full launch.
