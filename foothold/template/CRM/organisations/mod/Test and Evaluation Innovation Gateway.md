# Test and Evaluation Innovation Gateway

The T&E Innovation Gateway makes it easier and cheaper for smaller companies to access the UK's world-leading test and evaluation facilities. Established to reduce the access barriers that have historically made it difficult for SMEs to use the test ranges that QinetiQ operates on behalf of the MOD under the **Long Term Partnering Agreement (LTPA)**.

External: [T3E: Test and Evaluation Innovation Gateway](https://www.t3e.uk/en/services/test-and-evaluation-innovation-gateway). QinetiQ release: [New Test & Evaluation Gateway to unlock access for SMEs and boost defence innovation](https://www.qinetiq.com/en/news/test-and-evaluation-innovation-gateway).

## Role in the landscape

The Gateway is the customer-side route into QinetiQ's network of MOD test ranges, particularly for SMEs that would not normally navigate the LTPA-managed access process.

### Facilities and management

Through the LTPA, **QinetiQ manages 16 ranges on behalf of the MOD**, delivering Test, Trials, Training, and Evaluation (T3E) across land, air, and sea domains. The Gateway provides a dedicated SME-support team that helps companies:

- Access T&E expertise.
- Identify suitable ranges and facilities.
- Secure the right approvals.
- Facilitate rapid solution development and testing in realistic operational environments.

### SME access route

QinetiQ runs a dedicated support team for SMEs. Contact route: SMETandE@QinetiQ.com.

### Wider T&E Transformation Programme

The Gateway sits inside the MOD's broader **T&E Transformation Programme**, started in 2022, designed to modernise how the UK tests and assures complex military systems. The Programme uses digital and mobile tools to combine real-world and virtual testing, with the aim of speeding up delivery of new capabilities to the frontline. See [GOV.UK: Defence Test & Evaluation (T&E)](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-ministry-of-defence-test-and-evaluation-transformation-programme) and [Future Advantage Through Evaluation (FATE)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defence-test-and-evaluation-future-advantage-through-evaluation-fate/test-and-evaluation-te-future-advantage-through-evaluation-fate).

The forthcoming [[CRM/organisations/mod/T&E Marketplace|T&E Marketplace]] (launching end of 2026) is the next major piece of the Programme and will complement the Gateway with a digital one-stop-shop for finding and accessing T&E facilities.

### Related competition funding

The MOD has launched several themed competitions under the T&E banner, including the **£1 million themed competition** for [Delivering Future Advantage Through Testing and Evaluation (Phase 1)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/delivering-future-advantage-through-testing-and-evaluation-phase-1) and its [Competition Document](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/delivering-future-advantage-through-testing-and-evaluation-phase-1/delivering-future-advantage-through-testing-and-evaluation-competition-document).
