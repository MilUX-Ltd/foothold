# Centre for Intelligence Innovation

The innovation surface for [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Intelligence|Defence Intelligence (DI)]] and the wider UK intelligence community inside Defence. Sits inside the CSOC Innovation cluster alongside [[CRM/organisations/mod/jHub|jHub Defence Innovation]], the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence AI Centre (DAIC)|Defence AI Centre]], and the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Modelling and Simulation Office|Defence Modelling and Simulation Office]].

> **Documentation caveat.** Public gov.uk documentation directly badged "Centre for Intelligence Innovation" is sparse at time of capture. The substantive activity touches RAF Wyton (a key intelligence site) and DASA-mediated innovation pipelines into Defence Intelligence.

## Role in the landscape

The Centre is the named home for intelligence-domain innovation inside CSOC: the place where new tools and techniques are matured for adoption by [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Intelligence|DI]] and the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Military Intelligence Services|Military Intelligence Services]]. Operationally close to RAF Wyton, which has been a long-standing home of UK military intelligence functions.

### Demonstrated activity

The Centre has partnered with industry through DASA to mature intelligence-relevant innovation. A documented example: in DASA's first competition, Montvieux (an independent research-technology company) was awarded funding to develop a "predictive cognitive control system". In **Phase 2**, the system has been tested on the operational network at **RAF Wyton** and demonstrated to Defence Intelligence analysts on a daily basis.

Wider context: the **UK Defence Innovation and Cyber & Specialist Operations Command** Hansard debate ([Commons, 1 July 2025](https://hansard.parliament.uk/commons/2025-07-01/debates/25070145000013/UKDefenceInnovationAndCyberAndSpecialistOperationsCommand)) frames the structural connection between the post-SDR CSOC rename and the consolidation of innovation routes that the Centre for Intelligence Innovation sits inside.
