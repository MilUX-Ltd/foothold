# jHub Defence Innovation

## Organisation Details

- **Parent:** [[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command|Cyber and Specialist Operations Command (CSOC)]] — formerly UK Strategic Command (UKStratCom).
- **Team:** Military, civilian and contractor staff, Central London (White City office).
- **Sector:** Defence innovation, MoD-adjacent accelerator.
- **Website:** [gov.uk: jHub Defence Innovation](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/jhub-defence-innovation/about)

## Overview

jHub is the innovation accelerator for CSOC (formerly Strategic Command). It exists to pull defence-tech SMEs and startups into solving live military problem sets, and to push promising innovation toward adoption inside the CSOC portfolio. Sits alongside DASA, BattleLab, and the Rapid Capabilities Office as one of the major UK defence innovation routes — specifically focused on the cyber, special operations, intelligence, and joint enablers space.

jHub acts as a **brokerage between problem and solution** — engaging end users in CSOC and across Defence to understand their challenges, and running a network of innovation scouts to find relevant technology. A wider team of commercial, financial, security and integration experts moves successful pilots into sustained capability.

## Aim

> Source: [gov.uk — jHub: about](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/jhub-defence-innovation/about)

Deliver competitive advantage against the UK's adversaries by unlocking novel and disruptive capabilities, through an opportunity-led and user-centred approach — with a particular focus on harnessing leading **dual-purpose technology**. Aims to pioneer and champion methods that enable Defence to be "innovative by instinct".

**Mature solutions focus.** jHub targets solutions that can deliver impact at pace, with a particular interest in repurposing **high Technology Readiness Level (TRL)** products and services from areas that do not traditionally have a Defence focus — software, hardware, or solving a process or people issue.

This is the key distinction from DASA: DASA focuses on mid-TRL technology, jHub on high-TRL repurposing. The two work closely together to horizon-scan across the TRL spectrum.

## How jHub does business

A portfolio approach across three project types:

| Type | Description | Scale |
|------|-------------|-------|
| **Problem-led** | Start with a current end-user problem and scout for the best available solutions; also tackle wider obstacles to exploiting and scaling (accreditation, policy, training). | Majority focus |
| **Thematic priority-led** | The organisation is trying to change in a particular direction, or grapple with an anticipated future need. | Selective |
| **Technology opportunity-led** | Find a capability from industry that could be highly impactful if repurposed for Defence — then seek user traction to exploit. | Scout-driven |

**Routes to market:** existing frameworks (Crown Commercial Services), Open/Restricted Competition via Find a Tender Service, or single source where there is a robust justification. Bespoke route per project; ensures procurement compliance and value for money.

**No "front door" for industry.** jHub is **not resourced or mandated** to triage industry approaches at scale. They rely on DASA for that horizon-scanning role — DASA is the mid-TRL front door; jHub picks up at high-TRL repurposing.
