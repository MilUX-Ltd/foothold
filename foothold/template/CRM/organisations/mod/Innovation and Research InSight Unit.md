# Innovation and Research InSight Unit (IRIS)

Sometimes written IRISU. The MOD's horizon-scanning function for science and technology, set up in 2016 as part of the Defence Innovation Initiative. Sits within the Defence Science and Technology (DST) group at MOD Head Office.

## Role in the landscape

IRIS exists to inform the future direction of the MOD's science and technology programme, and to inform security thinking across government, by scanning the horizon for the latest trends in S&T. It does this from inside MOD Head Office rather than from a dedicated arm's-length body, sitting close to policy and capability decision-making. The unit was created off the back of the [Defence Innovation Initiative](https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/defence-innovation-initiative) announced by the then Defence Secretary.

### Functional positioning

IRIS is a sister capability to [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Futures|Defence Futures and Force Design (F&FD)]]: F&FD does long-range concept and force design work; IRIS focuses on S&T trends specifically and feeds those into the MOD's S&T programme. The two are complementary, both contributing strategic foresight inputs to MOD decision-making but from different angles (S&T scanning versus concept and force design).

IRIS sits under the wider [Defence Science and Technology](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/defence-science-and-technology) group, alongside [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|Dstl]] and other S&T entities under the umbrella.
