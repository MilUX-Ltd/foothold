# AWE Nuclear Security Technologies

The UK organisation that designs, manufactures, and maintains the components for all British nuclear warheads. AWE's stated mission: "deliver nuclear warheads for the UK's deterrent, and use our expertise to support national security." Disambiguation note: distinct from the [[CRM/organisations/mod/British Army/Army Warfighting Experiment|Army Warfighting Experiment (AWE)]].

GOV.UK: [Atomic Weapons Establishment](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/atomic-weapons-establishment). Corporate: [awe.co.uk](https://www.awe.co.uk/). Regulator: [Office for Nuclear Regulation: AWE Aldermaston and Burghfield](https://www.onr.org.uk/our-work/what-we-regulate/defence/defence-sites/awe-aldermaston).

## Role in the landscape

AWE sits inside the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Nuclear Organisation|Defence Nuclear Organisation (DNO)]] enterprise alongside the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Submarine Delivery Agency|Submarine Delivery Agency]] and the Royal Navy. AWE delivers the warhead end of the UK's continuous-at-sea deterrent; SDA delivers the submarine platforms; the Royal Navy operates them.

### Sites and history

- **AWE Aldermaston** (Berkshire). c.750 acres. Established on the former RAF Aldermaston airfield on 1 April 1950. Undertakes advanced research, design, and manufacturing.
- **AWE Burghfield**. Final assembly and maintenance of warheads.
- **AWE Cardiff** (historical) was combined with AWRE and Burghfield to form AWE in 1987.

### Ownership

AWE is an arm's-length non-departmental public body wholly owned by the Ministry of Defence. In **November 2020**, the MOD announced a contractual break point, and in **July 2021** took direct ownership of AWE Plc back from the previous management consortium.

### Scope of work

Covers the entire life cycle of nuclear warheads:

1. Initial concept and assessment.
2. Design.
3. Component manufacture.
4. Assembly.
5. In-service support.
6. Decommissioning and disposal.
