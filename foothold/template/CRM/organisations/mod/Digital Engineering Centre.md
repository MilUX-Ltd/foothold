# Digital Engineering Centre

A Centre of Excellence focused on model-based systems engineering, digital twins, and digital threads across UK Defence. Sits in the Centres of Excellence cluster alongside the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Wargaming Centre|Defence Wargaming Centre]], the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Materials CoE|Defence Materials CoE]], the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence AI Centre (DAIC)|Defence AI Centre]], and others.

> **Documentation caveat.** Public gov.uk documentation directly badged as "Digital Engineering Centre" is sparse at time of capture. Most public material on MOD digital engineering attaches to [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|DE&S (now NA-M)]] activity and to the wider [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Digital & Data (NA-D&D)|Defence Digital (NA-D&D)]] estate.

## Role in the landscape

The Digital Engineering Centre is the natural home for the techniques that the rest of MOD describes when talking about transforming the procurement and support of equipment through model-based methods. The MOD's published direction on digital engineering ([DE&S blog: DE&S embraces digital engineering to equip the frontlines "faster than ever"](https://des.mod.uk/digital-engineering-equip-frontlines/)) frames the headline use cases:

- **Digital twins.** Digitally creating equipment as a virtual model that can be tested thousands more times than would be possible physically, boosting efficiency and reducing cost without compromising safety.
- **AI-driven testing.** DE&S uses AI and machine learning to digitally test equipment thousands of times, generating data to shape decision-making and acceptance.
- **End-to-end acceleration.** From concept through production to fielded capability.

This is the substantive activity the Digital Engineering Centre is positioned to coordinate as a Centre of Excellence.
