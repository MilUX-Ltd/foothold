# Defence Support Group (DSG): legacy

> **Status: superseded.** The Defence Support Group was the MOD's trading fund providing equipment maintenance, repair, overhaul, and storage services to the armed forces. The land-vehicle and electronics-overhaul side was **sold to Babcock International in December 2014**, ending the trading-fund era. Residual functions inside the MOD have been absorbed over time and are now distributed across [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|NA-M (former DE&S Core Delivery)]], [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Logistics & Support (NA-L&S)|NA-L&S (former Defence Support)]], the [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Delivery Executive|National Armaments Delivery Executive]], and the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Electronics and Components Agency|Defence Electronics and Components Agency (Deca)]].
>
> **Current equivalents:**
> - For **land-vehicle and equipment overhaul** sold in 2014: Babcock (commercial).
> - For **MOD-retained electronics and avionics overhaul**: [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Electronics and Components Agency|Deca]] (formed 2015 from DSG's air division; merged into DE&S 2023; now part of [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|NA-M]]).
> - For **defence support policy and coherence**: [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Logistics & Support (NA-L&S)|NA-L&S]].

## Lineage at a glance

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 2008 | DSG established as an **MOD trading fund** providing maintenance, repair, overhaul, and storage services |
| December 2014 | Land-vehicle and electronics overhaul **sold to Babcock International**; trading fund dissolved |
| 1 April 2015 | DSG's **air division** spun off to form the **Defence Electronics and Components Agency (Deca)**, an MOD executive agency |
| April 2023 | Deca **merged into DE&S** |
| 1 April 2024 | Deca **full operating capability** achieved as DE&S Deca |
| 2 April 2026 | DE&S brand retired; DE&S Core Delivery (including Deca operating centre) became [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|NA-M]]; broader support policy now in [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Logistics & Support (NA-L&S)|NA-L&S]] |
