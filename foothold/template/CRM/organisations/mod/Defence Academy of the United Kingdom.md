# Defence Academy of the United Kingdom

## Overview

The Defence Academy of the United Kingdom is the Ministry of Defence's national institution for postgraduate-level professional military education, command and staff training, and joint, defence-wide learning. Its main site is at Shrivenham in Oxfordshire (postcode SN6 8ST), with elements at other locations.

The Defence Academy delivers training to UK and international officers, civil servants, and partner-nation personnel across all three services and the wider defence enterprise. It is also a hub for defence-related research, doctrine, and senior-level engagement.

## Sites and Constituent Parts

- **Shrivenham** — the principal site. Hosts JSCSC and a number of supporting buildings including the Cormorant Building and the Churchill Lecture Theatre at Watchfield.
- **Joint Services Command and Staff College (JSCSC)** — the staff college at Shrivenham. Runs the Advanced Command and Staff Course (ACSC) and other senior courses.
- **Royal College of Defence Studies (RCDS)** — based at Seaford House, Belgravia, London. Senior strategic course for those identified for the highest levels of command and leadership.

## Relevance to defence SMEs

Engagement with the Defence Academy provides:

- Access to a senior military and civil-service audience thinking about doctrine, capability, and the future operating environment.
- A route into research collaborations on defence concepts, command, and capability.
- Visibility into how the UK trains its operational and strategic leaders, which shapes the demand signal for defence technology.
