# Royal Marines

The amphibious commando force of the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Royal Navy/Royal Navy|Royal Navy]]. Currently undergoing the **Future Commando Force (FCF)** transformation, described by the Royal Navy as the most significant transformation and rebranding programme since the Second World War.

Royal Navy: [Royal Marines](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/organisation/royal-marines). GOV.UK context: [Armed Forces to be more active around the world to combat threats of the future](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/armed-forces-to-be-more-active-around-the-world-to-combat-threats-of-the-future).

## Role in the landscape

The Royal Marines are evolving from amphibious infantry held at readiness in the UK to a versatile **Special Operations-capable Commando force persistently forward deployed**. The FCF programme is the named vehicle for that transformation.

### Future Commando Force (FCF)

- **Investment.** Over £200 million of direct investment across the next decade.
- **Structure.** FCF retasks **40 Commando** and **45 Commando** to form two **Littoral Response Groups (LRGs)**, permanently deployed forward. Other units from UK Commando Force are also included in the composition of LRGs.
- **Mission scope.** Designed to conduct roles traditionally carried out by Special Forces, including: specialist capacity-building, maritime security operations, pre-emption and deterrence of sub-threshold activity, countering state threats, plus warfighting, combat missions, and humanitarian duties.
- **Deployability.** A force ready to deploy around the world immediately. See [Royal Navy: Royal Marines Commando unit created to shape the Future Commando Force](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/news/2020/july/20/200720-vanguard-strike-company).

### Technology partner: MarWorks

The [[CRM/organisations/mod/MarWorks|MarWorks]] team (Royal Navy's information warfare technology accelerator) is the primary technology partner for FCF, having delivered tactical IP networks, helmet camera systems with Visual Engineering, and Future Commando Force communications kit.
