# Defence Electronics and Components Agency (DE&S Deca)

The MOD's operating centre dedicated to maintenance, repair, overhaul, upgrade, procurement, and managed services across Defence electronics, components, and general equipment support. Now an operating centre within [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|DE&S (now NA-M)]] rather than a standalone executive agency.

GOV.UK: [Defence Electronics and Components Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-electronics-and-components-agency); [About us](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/defence-electronics-and-components-agency/about); [DE&S Deca group page](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/des-deca).

## Role in the landscape

Deca was formed as an executive agency of the MOD in **April 2015** from the air division of the Defence Support Group. In **April 2023** Deca ceased to be an executive agency and merged into DE&S, achieving **full operating capability on 1 April 2024**. The DE&S Deca brand has been retained for the operating centre under the post-merger structure.

### Sites

- **MOD Sealand** (Deeside, Flintshire): primary site.
- **MOD Stafford**: secondary presence.

### Stated remit

Deca provides maintenance, repair, overhaul, upgrade, and obsolescence management for a diverse range of avionic and electronic equipment.

### F-35 global repair hub

DE&S Deca is the **global repair hub** for maintenance, repair, overhaul, and upgrade services for the Lockheed Martin F-35 Lightning II aircraft avionic and aircraft components. This is the headline international role, anchoring UK industrial benefit from the F-35 programme.
