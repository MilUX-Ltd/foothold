# Reserve Forces' and Cadets' Associations (RFCA)

## Summary

The 13 Reserve Forces' and Cadets' Associations are Crown bodies in the United Kingdom that give advice and assistance to the Defence Council, and to the Army, Royal Navy, and Royal Air Force, on matters that concern reserves and cadets. Established in 1908 as Territorial Associations to provide local support to the Territorial Force in every county; renamed Reserve Forces' and Cadets' Associations in **2000** to reflect the new tri-service structure. The number of associations was reduced from 14 to 13 at the same point, with regional boundaries largely comparable to the regional government structure.

## What the RFCAs do

The RFCAs are responsible on behalf of the Ministry of Defence for:

- The **upkeep of approximately 460 tri-service Reserve Forces' sites** across the UK.
- The **upkeep of approximately 2,300 cadet centres**.
- Local advice and assistance to the Defence Council and the three Services on Reserves and cadets matters.
- Employer engagement on behalf of the Reserve Forces, including support for the Armed Forces Covenant.

## Structure: 13 regional associations plus CRFCA

The 13 individual associations together form the **Council of RFCAs (CRFCA)**, the central coordination body that focuses the Associations' delivery on behalf of their customers within resources. CRFCA also operates the national Employer Engagement function. The 13 regional associations are:

## Connection to the Regional Defence and Security Clusters

The RFCAs are the structural parents of the **Regional Defence and Security Clusters (RDSCs)**. The regional RFCA in each area provides the host or convenor function for the corresponding RDSC:

- [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/clusters/South Central Regional Defence and Security Cluster|SCRDSC (South Central)]]: convened through the Wessex / South East RFCA structure.
- [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/clusters/South West Regional Defence and Security Cluster|SWRDSC (South West)]]: convened through the South West RFCA.
- [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/clusters/London Regional Defence and Security Cluster|London RDSC]]: convened through the Greater London RFCA.
- [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/clusters/Western Regional Defence and Security Cluster|Western RDSC]] and other regional clusters: convened through their respective RFCAs.

Understanding the RFCA as the cluster parent matters because RFCA member organisations have automatic membership of the corresponding RDSC. Cluster membership is held at the organisation level, via the employing organisation, not the individual.
