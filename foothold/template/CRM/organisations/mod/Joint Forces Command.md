# Joint Forces Command (JFC): legacy

> **Status: superseded.** The name "Joint Forces Command" was retired in 2019 and replaced by **UK Strategic Command (UKStratCom)**. UKStratCom was itself renamed **Cyber & Specialist Operations Command (CSOC)** on 1 September 2025 following the 2025 Strategic Defence Review. The substantive functions and personnel migrated through each rename.
>
> **Current equivalent: [[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command|Cyber & Specialist Operations Command (CSOC)]].**

## Lineage at a glance

| Year | Name |
|---|---|
| 2012 | **Joint Forces Command (JFC)** established as a UK joint command |
| 2019 | Renamed **UK Strategic Command (UKStratCom)** |
| 1 September 2025 | Renamed **Cyber & Specialist Operations Command (CSOC)** |

Each rename was paired with a remit and operating-model expansion rather than a substantive break in continuity. The CSOC rename was announced 1 July 2025 (paired with the launch of UK Defence Innovation, UKDI) and took effect 1 September 2025.

## Why this page exists

GOV.UK still surfaces pages under the legacy `joint-forces-command` slug. Existing references using "JFC" or "Joint Forces Command" should resolve cleanly to this lineage page; substantive content lives on the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command|CSOC]] page.

## What JFC originally did

When it stood up in 2012, JFC consolidated four headline elements at the joint-command level:

- **Defence Intelligence.**
- **Permanent Joint Headquarters (PJHQ).**
- The **Defence Academy of the United Kingdom**.
- The **joint cyber and information capabilities** that would become the backbone of CSOC's cyber remit.

The current JFC successor functions are all on [[CRM/organisations/mod/Cyber and Specialist Operations Command|CSOC]]'s About page.
