# Royal Navy

## Organisation Details

- **Website:** [royal-navy.mod.uk](https://www.royalnavy.mod.uk/)
- **Sector:** Defence, Military, Government
- **Location:** United Kingdom

## Overview

The Royal Navy is the principal naval warfare force of the United Kingdom and one of the three branches of HM Armed Forces. Made up of five arms: the Surface Fleet, the Fleet Air Arm, the Submarine Service, the Royal Marines, and the Royal Fleet Auxiliary.

For defence SMEs, the most accessible Royal Navy innovation surfaces are:

- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office|Disruptive Capabilities and Technologies Office (DCTO)]] — the consolidated Navy innovation unit.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/Navy Develop|Navy Develop]] — the Develop Directorate (parent of DCTO).
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/MarWorks|MarWorks]] — the Navy's information-warfare technology accelerator.

The Royal Navy is also the operator of the UK's continuous-at-sea nuclear deterrent, supplied by the wider Defence Nuclear Enterprise.
