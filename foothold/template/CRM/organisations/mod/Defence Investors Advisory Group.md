# Defence Investors' Advisory Group (DIAG)

The MOD's senior advisory body on **private investment into UK defence**. Convenes senior figures from UK banking, venture capital, and strategic finance to advise ministers on defence investment priorities. Put on a **permanent footing on 23 April 2026** alongside the expansion announced under the *Defence as an engine for growth* programme.

GOV.UK: [Unlocking private investment to drive UK defence as an engine for growth (23 April 2026)](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/unlocking-private-investment-to-drive-uk-defence-as-an-engine-for-growth).

## Role in the landscape

DIAG sits between the MOD and the UK private-capital community as an advisory channel — not a funding body. The remit is to advise ministers on which capability areas, investment vehicles, and structural reforms would best unlock UK private capital into defence.

### Membership

Senior representation across the three main pillars of UK financial services:

- **Banking** — example member: Cathal Deasy, Global Co-Head of Investment Banking at Barclays.
- **Venture capital** — co-chair Kerry Baldwin, IQ Capital and the British Private Equity & Venture Capital Association (BVCA).
- **Strategic finance / sovereign-adjacent** — co-chair Baroness White, head of global affairs for the Canadian investment fund CDPQ (Caisse de dépôt et placement du Québec).

### Defence Finance "Zig-Zag" secondment programme

Announced alongside the permanent footing on 23 April 2026. The Zig-Zag programme brings private-sector financial professionals into the MOD on temporary secondments to embed market expertise inside defence decision-making and financial planning. Reciprocal MOD secondments into investment institutions are part of the model.

### Wider investment context

DIAG sits inside a wider 2025-26 MOD push on private finance: the [Defence Industrial Strategy 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defence-industrial-strategy-2025-making-defence-an-engine-for-growth) ("Making Defence an Engine for Growth"), a [£20m fund](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/search-for-britains-next-defence-unicorn-kicks-off-with-new-20-million-fund) for defence-tech startups (January 2026), and the Defence Investment Plan 2025.
