# RAF X

The Royal Air Force's innovation and experimentation function. Delivers experimentation by facilitating the ability to exploit technology, and supports the RAF technology roadmap by making sure there is a path to production from experimentation through to fielded capability.

## Role in the landscape

RAF X sits inside the RAF Innovation cluster alongside the Rapid Capabilities Office, ASTRA (grassroots innovation), Development & Testing, and the Joint Counter-UAS Office. It is the bridging unit between RAF capability owners and external technology suppliers, with a particular focus on experimentation, exploitation, and integration.

The principal delivery unit is **No. 4 Capability and Innovation Squadron (4 CIS)**, which provides technical support to interoperability, delivery of new concepts, and the integration and exploitation of Communication and Information Systems. Per [raf.mod.uk: 4 Capability and Innovation Squadron](https://www.raf.mod.uk/our-organisation/squadrons/4-capability-and-innovation-squadron/), 4 CIS consists of three Flights:

- **Application eXploitation (AX) Flight.**
- **Capability, Support and Exploit (CSE) Flight.**
- **Rapid Application Integration and Development (RAID) Flight.**

### Stated approach

- Facilitate the ability to exploit technology across RAF capability areas.
- Support the RAF technology roadmap by ensuring a path to production from experimentation.
- Plug 4 CIS Flights into specific capability problems where the RAF needs faster integration than the routine acquisition pipeline allows.

### Named programmes inside the RAF innovation cluster

- **RAF Advanced Manufacturing eXploitation (RAF AM-X).** Kicked off with innovation funding in 2022 to explore how 3D printing could produce aircraft and equipment parts quickly and securely; matured into RAF AM-X via testing and experimentation. See [raf.mod.uk: RAF AM-X: 3D Printing the Future of Air Power](https://www.raf.mod.uk/news/articles/raf-am-x-3d-printing-the-future-of-air-power/).
- **ASTRA.** RAF's grassroots innovation programme. Has equipped Innovation Hubs with 3D printers across the Service, enabling personnel to learn basic design and fabrication skills for local use.
- **Software-defined air power.** Software research accelerating Future Combat Aircraft capability per [raf.mod.uk: Software Research Accelerates Future Combat Aircraft Air Power Capability](https://www.raf.mod.uk/news/articles/software-research-accelerates-future-combat-aircraft-air-power-capability/).

### Adjacent capability strategy

The [Royal Air Force Autonomous Collaborative Platform Strategy](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/66019fa8f1d3a0666832acfc/RAF_Autonomous_Collaborative_Platform_Strategy.pdf) is the published RAF strategy for autonomous and collaborative platforms; the experimentation that underpins it routes through RAF X and the wider RAF innovation cluster.
