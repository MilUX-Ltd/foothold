# Royal Air Force

## Organisation Details

- **Website:** [raf.mod.uk](https://www.raf.mod.uk/)
- **Sector:** Defence, Military, Government
- **Location:** United Kingdom
- **Head of the Service:** the Chief of the Air Staff, listed among the senior military posts on the [MOD organisation page](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ministry-of-defence).
- **Regular strength:** 31,390 at 1 July 2026. The RAF Reserve total strength is given as 3,100 at the same date. Source: [Quarterly service personnel statistics: 1 July 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/statistics/quarterly-service-personnel-statistics-2026/quarterly-service-personnel-statistics-1-july-2026).

## Overview

The Royal Air Force is the air and space force of the United Kingdom and one of the three single Services of HM Armed Forces. The MOD organisation page on gov.uk lists it, with the Royal Navy, the British Army and Cyber and Specialist Operations Command, as one of the military services and commands ([gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/ministry-of-defence)).

For a founder, the RAF is a user and sponsor of capability. Its innovation units test and prove ideas with airmen and airwomen. Most major equipment is bought through the MOD's central acquisition organisation.

## How it is organised

- **Headquarters.** The RAF's headquarters was renamed from Air Command to Air & Space Command on 1 September 2026, and UK Space Command continues to operate under it. Source: [The Aviationist, 6 September 2026](https://theaviationist.com/2026/09/06/raf-air-command-officially-renamed-to-air-space-command/), reporting the [RAF's announcement](https://www.raf.mod.uk/news/articles/per-ardua-ad-astra-air-command-redesignated-to-air-space-command/). Older pages, including some in this pack, still say Air Command.
- **Command of the Service.** Under Defence Reform, the Chief of the Defence Staff now commands the Service Chiefs, and heads a new Military Strategic Headquarters responsible for force design and war planning across the integrated force. Source: [Defence Secretary's speech on Defence Reform, 18 February 2025](https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/defence-secretarys-speech-on-defence-reform--2). See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/MOD Structure|MOD Structure]].

## What it buys and how

- **Equipment and support** are bought through the National Armaments Director Group (NAD Group). Under Defence Reform the National Armaments Director runs a single investment budget that combines eight earlier procurement budgets and is responsible for the end-to-end acquisition system; the Military Strategic Headquarters holds the operations budget and the Service Chiefs hold readiness budgets. See [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/NAD Group|NAD Group]] and [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)|National Armaments Materiel (NA-M)]], which gov.uk describes as formerly part of Defence Equipment and Support. Sources: [Defence Reform speech](https://www.gov.uk/government/speeches/defence-secretarys-speech-on-defence-reform--2); [NAD Group on gov.uk](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/national-armaments-director-nad-group). gov.uk now shows the NAD Group page as superseded by a [National Armaments](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/national-armaments) organisation page.
- **Air programmes** covered in this pack include [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Eurofighter Typhoon|Eurofighter Typhoon]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/TEMPEST (GCAP)|TEMPEST (GCAP)]]. Funding is covered on [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]].
- **Autonomous platforms.** The RAF has a published [Autonomous Collaborative Platform Strategy](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/66019fa8f1d3a0666832acfc/RAF_Autonomous_Collaborative_Platform_Strategy.pdf).

## How a small supplier engages

1. **Start with the RAF's own innovation units.** [[CRM/organisations/mod/Royal Air Force/RAF X|RAF X]] runs experimentation and aims at a path from experiment to fielded capability. The [[CRM/organisations/mod/Royal Air Force/Rapid Capability Office|RAF Rapid Capability Office]] is the rapid-acquisition counterpart.
2. **Use the central innovation route.** UK Defence Innovation (UKDI) runs open calls, themed competitions and Innovation Partners who advise applicants. Start with [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI - How to Engage]] and [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)]]. Official guide: [How to work with UKDI](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-work-with-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi).
3. **Cross-Service routes.** The [[CRM/organisations/mod/Joint Counter-UAS Office|Joint Counter-UAS Office]] and [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence BattleLab|Defence BattleLab]] are shared across the Services.
4. **Watch for published notices.** Opportunities are listed on the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Defence Sourcing Portal|Defence Sourcing Portal]], [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Contracts Finder|Contracts Finder]] and [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Portals/Find a Tender Service|Find a Tender Service]].
5. **Get SME help.** The [[CRM/organisations/government/Defence Office for Small Business Growth|Defence Office for Small Business Growth]] supports small suppliers. The MOD's [SME action plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mod-small-and-medium-sized-enterprise-sme-action-plan/ministry-of-defences-small-and-medium-sized-enterprise-sme-action-plan) aims to raise defence spend with SMEs by 50 per cent by summer 2028.
6. **Meet people.** [[Intelligence/events/Farnborough International Airshow/About Farnborough International Airshow|Farnborough International Airshow]], [[Intelligence/events/DSEI/About DSEI|DSEI]] and [[Intelligence/events/DPRTE/About DPRTE|DPRTE]] are the main UK events for air and defence buyers. The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Founder's Procurement Playbook|Founder's Procurement Playbook]] explains the buying process.
