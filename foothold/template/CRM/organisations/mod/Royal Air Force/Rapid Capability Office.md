# RAF Rapid Capability Office (RAF RCO)

## Summary

- **Full name:** Royal Air Force Rapid Capability Office (RAF RCO).
- **Parent:** Royal Air Force / UK Ministry of Defence.
- **Location:** Cody Technology Park, Farnborough.
- **Mission focus:** Rapid acquisition and experimentation of capability for the Royal Air Force, bridging research and development with front-line service.

## Role in the landscape

The RAF Rapid Capability Office runs experimentation activity to bring novel capability into service quickly. Sits inside the RAF innovation cluster alongside [[CRM/organisations/mod/Royal Air Force/RAF X|RAF X]] and the wider ASTRA grassroots programme. The RCO is the rapid-acquisition counterpart to RAF X's experimentation function.

Recent open-source work includes experimentation with distributed low-attribution sensor systems to protect deployed aircraft on austere airfields — using sensor networks to stand up a virtual perimeter while a physical fence is being established. The user community is the **Royal Air Force Regiment**.

The RCO has been progressively improving usability of the products it is experimenting with through user feedback, an embryonic form of user-centred design operating downstream on already-developed products rather than at the upstream problem-definition stage.
