# Defence Modelling and Simulation Office (DMSO)

The MOD's central coordination function for modelling and simulation (M&S) capability, standards, and tools across the Defence enterprise. Sits within CSOC Joint Warfare with pan-Defence responsibilities (formerly UK Strategic Command Joint Warfare, prior to the 1 September 2025 CSOC rename).

GOV.UK: [Defence Modelling and Simulation Coherence](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/defence-modelling-and-simulation-coherence). Underlying policy: [Defence policy for modelling and simulation (JSP 939)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defence-policy-for-modelling-and-simulation-jsp-939). DMSO Blueprint: [The Defence Modelling & Simulation Office Blueprint (PDF)](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/67e57f5f6078e12e2c8c9bfd/The_Defence_Modelling___Simulation__M_S__Office__DMSO__-_Blueprint.pdf).

## Role in the landscape

DMSO provides Assessment and Assurance for all Defence M&S against the direction provided in JSP 939, and offers M&S Advice and Guidance to the rest of Defence. Mission: improve coordination and coherence of MOD M&S activity to promote value for money and stronger M&S capability across the Defence enterprise.

### Stated activities

Per the DMSO Blueprint and gov.uk:

- A **systems-of-systems approach** to promote coherent M&S capability across Defence.
- A **Defence Simulation Centre (DSC)** that provides a single point of contact for all Defence M&S matters.
- Engagement with industry and academia via the **DMSO Industry Advisory Group (DIAG)**.
- Custodianship of [Defence modelling and simulation standards profile](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/modelling-and-simulation-standards-profile).
- Co-delivery of new wargaming and analysis software for Defence (see also [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Wargaming Centre|Defence Wargaming Centre]]).

### Policy framework: JSP 939

JSP 939 is the UK MOD's policy for modelling and simulation, providing direction and guidance for the acquisition, development, and usage of M&S across UK Defence. DMSO is the custodian and assurance authority for this policy.
