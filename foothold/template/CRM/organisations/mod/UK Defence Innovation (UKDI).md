# UK Defence Innovation (UKDI)

UKDI is the Ministry of Defence's central innovation body, and the front door for companies that want to bring new technology to defence and security. DASA (the Defence and Security Accelerator) became part of UKDI in February 2026. Some gov.uk web addresses still carry the DASA name.

This page says what UKDI is. For what is open now and how to start a conversation, read [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI: How to Engage]].

## Summary

- **Created 1 July 2025**, after the Strategic Defence Review, as the MOD's focal point for innovation. The launch announcement said it would be fully operational by July 2026 ([GOV.UK launch announcement](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/launch-of-new-body-to-harness-innovative-tech-for-the-uks-armed-forces)).
- **What it brought together.** MOD guidance published on the launch day lists DASA, the Defence Innovation Unit, the Command Innovation Hubs and the Future Capability Innovation function from Defence Equipment and Support ([GOV.UK: A new era for Defence Innovation](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/a-new-era-for-defence-innovation-dasa-diu-and-des-fci-unite-under-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi)). The pack covers the last of these at [[CRM/organisations/mod/Future Capability Innovation|Future Capability Innovation]].
- **Money.** A ringfenced budget of at least £400m a year (same GOV.UK sources). For how this appears in the investment plan, see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]].
- **Five themes** set what it looks for: Autonomy, Decision Advantage, Logistics and Support, Effects, and Protection ([GOV.UK, 15 July 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/ukdi-unifies-innovation-to-accelerate-frontline-capabilities-and-drive-national-growth)).

## Where it sits

UKDI is part of the Ministry of Defence ([gov.uk organisation page](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-defence-innovation)). Within the MOD it sits in the National Armaments Director (NAD) Group, in the National Armaments Innovation area, which gov.uk lists as including Dstl and UKDI ([gov.uk: NAD Group](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/national-armaments-director-nad-group)).

- [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)]]: parent area, and the pack's fuller page on UKDI, Dstl and the Defence Investment Unit.
- [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/NAD Group|NAD Group]]: the wider group.
- The Rapid Innovation Team, for urgent operational problems using commercially available technology, sits inside UKDI: [[CRM/organisations/mod/Rapid Innovation Team|Rapid Innovation Team]].
- UKDI works with Dstl, the British Army and the Department for Transport on individual competitions, as its gov.uk news page shows. The UKDI Help Centre uses a Dstl email address.

UKDI is not the same as [[CRM/organisations/mod/jHub|jHub Defence Innovation]], which sits in a different MOD command and focuses on repurposing mature dual-purpose products.

## What it funds, and how

UKDI's own guidance describes three types of competition ([How to work with UKDI](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-work-with-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi)):

- **Open Call for Innovation**, for disruptive ideas at any stage. The Open Call needs delivery at technology readiness levels 6 to 7 ([UKDI advice for prospective applicants](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/advice-for-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi-prospective-applicants)).
- **Themed Competitions**, for a defined government challenge. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Counter-drone - PRESAGIUM and PANOPTES|Counter-drone: PRESAGIUM and PANOPTES]] for one example.
- **Defence Innovation Loans**, for later-stage R&D and commercialisation. Paused at the last check: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Defence Innovation Loans|Defence Innovation Loans]].

It also runs the Ideas Marketplace (collaboration, not funding) and the Futures Lab, delivered by the [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Engineering Delivery Partner (EDP)|Engineering Delivery Partner]]. A collaborative route with larger suppliers, [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Defence Technology Exploitation Programme (DTEP)|DTEP]], is listed as in the pipeline.

What is open changes often. The single place to check is the [UKDI live funding opportunities page](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/live-funding-opportunities), which at the 23 November 2026 update listed paused loans and a new Defence-focused route still in development.

UKRI lists the Defence and Security Accelerator among the public bodies that have used [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Innovate UK Contracts for Innovation|Innovate UK Contracts for Innovation]] ([UKRI](https://www.ukri.org/what-we-do/browse-our-areas-of-investment-and-support/innovate-uk-contracts-for-innovation/)). Each UKDI competition document says which terms apply to that competition.

## How a small supplier engages

- UKDI only communicates through its submission service, so you need an account. Organisations must be registered at Companies House ([UKDI advice](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/advice-for-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi-prospective-applicants)).
- Prior defence experience is not required. UKDI says an Innovation Partner, who is a civil servant, will contact you within 15 working days of a request, and that your intellectual property is protected when you speak to one ([How to work with UKDI](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-work-with-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi)). Innovators keep ownership of their IP ([UKDI advice](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/advice-for-uk-defence-innovation-ukdi-prospective-applicants)).
- Most competitions expect the funded work to happen in the UK. From 17 July 2026 UKDI stopped Innovation Partner support for innovators based outside the UK (UKDI advice, same page).
- A Regional Engagement Team gives advice to UK businesses, and UKDI works with 13 Regional Defence and Security Clusters: [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/Regional Defence and Security Clusters|Regional Defence and Security Clusters]].
- For civil and dual-use R&D funding from the civilian side of government, see [[CRM/organisations/government/Innovate UK|Innovate UK]].
- For SME help with bidding and contracting, see the [[CRM/organisations/government/Defence Office for Small Business Growth|Defence Office for Small Business Growth]].

The step-by-step is in [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/UKDI - How to Engage|UKDI: How to Engage]].
