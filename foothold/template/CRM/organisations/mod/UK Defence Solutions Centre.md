# UK Defence Solutions Centre (UKDSC)

The collaborative arm of the [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Growth Partnership|Defence Growth Partnership (DGP)]]. Brings government, industry and academia together to support **innovation, capability development, market intelligence and international engagement** for UK defence exports. Based in **Farnborough**. Led by **Samira Braund (CEO)**.

UKDSC website: [ukdsc.org](https://www.ukdsc.org/); [Contact](https://www.ukdsc.org/contact/); [About DGP](https://www.ukdsc.org/about_dgp/).

## Role in the landscape

UKDSC exists as the **delivery vehicle for the Defence Growth Partnership**: the long-running partnership between government and UK defence primes that aims to grow the UK defence sector and increase defence exports. UKDSC is staffed by a mix of permanent employees plus **23 secondees** from the DGP member companies, so it operates as a shared resource of the participating primes.

### Stated functions

- **Innovation and capability development.** Supports development of new defence capabilities by member primes and the wider UK supply chain.
- **Market intelligence.** Provides defence market analysis to inform UK export strategy.
- **International engagement.** Supports UK defence-export campaigns into target markets.

### DGP membership

UKDSC's 23 secondees are drawn from the 17 DGP member companies: Airbus, Atkins Global, Babcock International Group, BAE Systems, Boeing, Fujitsu, General Dynamics, GKN Aerospace, Leonardo, MBDA, Parker Meggitt, QinetiQ, Raytheon, Rolls-Royce, Spirit AeroSystems, Thales, Ultra Electronics Group, plus the Department for Business and Trade and ADS.

### Where it sits

- **Industrial-strategy peer of [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Industrial Joint Council|DIJC]].** DIJC (launched 2025) is the newer, wider-membership coordination forum that supersedes the legacy Defence Suppliers Forum.
- **Adjacent to [[CRM/organisations/government/Department for Business and Trade|DBT]]** as the lead government department on the DGP arrangement.
