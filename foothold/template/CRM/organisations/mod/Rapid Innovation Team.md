# Rapid Innovation Team

The wartime-pace function inside [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|UK Defence Innovation (UKDI)]] for urgent operational problems, using commercially available dual-use technology. Established on UKDI's launch day, 1 July 2025.

## Role in the landscape

The Rapid Innovation Team is the urgent-operational-need function within UKDI's wider innovation pipeline:

- **DASA**: themed competitions and the Open Call for Innovation, mid-TRL.
- **FCI (Future Capability Innovation)**: cross-cutting technology risks and opportunities, longer-term, deeply coupled to DE&S delivery.
- **Rapid Innovation Team (this page)**: wartime-pace, dual-use COTS technology applied to urgent operational problems.
- **jHub Defence Innovation** (in CSOC, not UKDI): high-TRL repurposing of dual-purpose technology.
