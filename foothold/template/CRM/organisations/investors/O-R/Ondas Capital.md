## About

Ondas Capital is the corporate venture and advisory business unit of Ondas Holdings, launched in September 2025 with $150M to scale unmanned and AI defence solutions globally. It combines advisory services with direct investment to accelerate the deployment of mature, combat-proven defence technologies to Allied markets in the US and Europe.

Initial geographic focus is Eastern Europe with a particular emphasis on Ukraine, and the unit is establishing a European headquarters with presence in Estonia, Ukraine and the UK. Investment themes include unmanned aerial and ground robotics, advanced simulation, and AI solutions for autonomous operations.

James Acuna leads the investment programme. Ondas Holdings is the listed parent (NASDAQ: ONDS).
