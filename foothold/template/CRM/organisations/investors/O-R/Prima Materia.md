## About

Prima Materia is a Stockholm-based venture firm founded by Daniel Ek (Spotify) and Shakil Khan, structured as a long-duration investment vehicle. It backs companies tackling foundational problems in deep tech, biotech, energy, materials and defence-relevant capability.

The firm took a high-profile defence position as lead investor in Helsing's growth rounds, anchoring it as one of Europe's most significant single-fund commitments to defence AI. Cheque sizes are concentrated and long horizon. Prima Materia operates as a family-office-style vehicle with the disciplines of a venture fund.
