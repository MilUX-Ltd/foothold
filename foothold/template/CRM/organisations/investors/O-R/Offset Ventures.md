## About

Offset Ventures is a London-based early-stage VC focused on European security and resilience technology. The fund backs dual-use and defence positions in space, autonomous systems, defence AI and advanced manufacturing.

Geography spans Europe, Canada and the US, with European deal flow concentrated in UK, France, Germany and the Nordics. Stages run seed and Series A, with selective later-stage participation. Portfolio includes Nu Quantum, Labrys and Solideon.
