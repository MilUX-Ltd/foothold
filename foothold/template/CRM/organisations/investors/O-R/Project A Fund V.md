## About

Project A is a Berlin-based pan-European venture capital firm founded in 2012. Fund V is the latest vintage at €325m. The firm runs an operational support model, with a dedicated in-house team of around 100 specialists supporting portfolio companies in engineering, marketing, sales, recruiting and finance.

Stage focus is Seed and Series A, with selective B participation. Sectors include B2B software, fintech, industrial tech, marketplaces, and a growing dual-use and defence-tech line led from the London office. Defence-tech is a stated thesis area for Fund V.
