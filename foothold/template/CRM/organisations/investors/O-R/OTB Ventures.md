## About

OTB Ventures is a deep-tech venture capital firm focused on Central and Eastern European founders alongside global deep-tech opportunities. Thesis areas are SpaceTech, enterprise AI, fintech infrastructure and cybersecurity, with explicit dual-use exposure.

Stages are Series A and B, with cheque sizes from €5m to €20m. Notable portfolio positions include ICEYE (synthetic aperture radar), Cosmic Engine, Aether Industries and Cervest. The fund is one of the more active Central European deep-tech investors operating at the dual-use intersection.
