## About

Polarion is a Nordic fund investing in DefenseTech and dual-use companies that strengthen the integrity, resilience and security of Nordic nations and the NATO alliance. Launched in 2025 by Thomas Falck and Oslo Family Office, the fund targets tech-enabled defence growth companies in the Nordics with a target fund size of €86m.

Thomas Falck is the former Managing Partner of Verdane Private Equity and former Chairman of Investinor (Norway's sovereign VC and fund-of-funds investor). Peter Due, an adviser to NATO DIANA and EUDIS, is closely involved with the fund and the wider Nordic dual-use community.
