## About

Paladin Capital Group is a global investor focused on cybersecurity, advanced technologies, dual-use and national security. The firm operates the Paladin Cyber Fund family alongside its broader strategies. Paladin Cyber Fund II is reported around €342m.

Paladin invests across early-growth to growth stages in cyber, AI security, secure communications, advanced sensors, autonomy and resilience-critical infrastructure. The firm maintains close working relationships with US and UK national-security communities and has co-invested alongside In-Q-Tel and the NATO Innovation Fund on relevant rounds.

UK and European presence sits within Paladin's London office. Portfolio companies frequently hold US government and Five Eyes contracts.
