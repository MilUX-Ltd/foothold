## About

Primo Climate is the climate-focused fund operated by Primo Capital, the Italian venture capital firm headquartered in Milan. The fund targets €60m and invests in climate-tech and energy-resilience companies with European focus.

Stages run early venture to Series B. Sectors of interest include energy transition, industrial decarbonisation, climate adaptation and resilience infrastructure. The defence overlap runs through energy and supply-chain resilience rather than primary defence tech.
