## About

OTB Ventures Fund II is the second vintage of OTB Ventures' deep-tech strategy, sized at €170m. The fund deploys into Central and Eastern European deep-tech founders and selected global opportunities in space, AI, cyber and fintech infrastructure.

Thesis emphasis remains dual-use deep tech and sovereign capability. Cheque sizes are €5m to €20m at Series A and B. Fund II co-invests alongside NATO Innovation Fund, EIF and select strategic corporates in the CEE region.
