## About

Radix is a Warsaw-based deep-tech venture capital firm founded in 2023, backing Central and Eastern European startups and spin-outs in energy, manufacturing, smart mobility, space tech and dual-use. The fund is reported at €41m and backed by the European Investment Fund (EIF) alongside PFR Ventures.

Radix is a member of the DeepTech Capital Consortium, a joint initiative of Poland's leading deep-tech investors (Rubicon Partners, iif.vc, Radix Ventures and individual investors) managing approximately PLN 572m. Recent 2025 investments include Fresh Inset, Fluence Technology and Protein Resources.
