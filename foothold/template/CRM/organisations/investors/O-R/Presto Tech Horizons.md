## About

Presto Tech Horizons is a €150m investment fund launched jointly by Presto Ventures and Czechoslovak Group (CSG). It invests in NATO and allied-nation founders building dual-use solutions in AI, cybersecurity, automation, robotics, logistics, defence, aerospace, mobility, energy, healthcare, advanced computing and critical infrastructure protection.

The Presto/CSG model is distinctive. CSG is one of Europe's fastest-growing primes, with over 100 companies across aerospace, defence and manufacturing. The joint vehicle gives portfolio startups a path to government and commercial defence customers via the CSG group and a credible route to industrial-scale exit.

Presto Ventures was founded in 2016 by Premysl Rubes; CSG is led by Michal Strnad.
