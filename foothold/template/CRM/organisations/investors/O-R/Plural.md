## About

Plural is a London-based early-stage VC founded by Ian Hogarth, Sten Tamkivi, Khaled Helioui and Taavet Hinrikus, with Carina Namih joining as Partner. The fund backs European founders building transformative companies, with explicit appetite for deep tech, defence and dual-use.

Plural was an early backer of Helsing and other European defence-tech category leaders. Co-founder Ian Hogarth chairs the UK AI Safety Institute, giving the fund a direct line into UK AI policy. The investing model is founder-partner-led, with concentrated cheque sizes typically $1m to $10m at seed and Series A.
