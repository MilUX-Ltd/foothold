## About

Primo Space is Primo Capital's space-tech fund, reported at €60m, with Milan headquarters and ESA / ASI (Italian Space Agency) co-investment exposure. The fund invests in upstream and downstream space technology including launch, satellite manufacturing, propulsion, earth observation and ground segment software.

Defence and resilience exposure is meaningful via ISR, satellite communications and PNT positions. Primo Space sits inside Italy's strategy to back national space-tech champions.
