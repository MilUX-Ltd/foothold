## About

Omnes Real Tech 2 is the second vintage of Omnes Capital's Real Tech strategy, targeting sovereign industrial and dual-use deep technology across Europe with a French weighting. The fund is reported at €112m and is in active deployment.

Thesis areas include space, advanced materials, energy transition, defence-adjacent industrial and semiconductors. The Real Tech strategy fits Omnes' wider positioning as a French sovereign-capability investor with Bpifrance and CDC co-investment exposure.
