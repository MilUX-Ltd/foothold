## About

Omnes Capital is a Paris-headquartered private equity and venture firm with strategies across LBO, infrastructure, renewable energy, life sciences, deep tech and dual-use. The firm spun out of Crédit Agricole in 2012.

Defence and resilience exposure runs primarily through the Real Tech strategy (Omnes Real Tech series), which targets sovereign and dual-use industrial technology. Omnes also operates the Industries and Services and Renewable Energy strategies, with the latter overlapping defence supply-chain resilience.
