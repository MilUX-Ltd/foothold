## About

Hyperion Fund is a Spanish €150M venture fund focused on defence, dual-use, and frontier deep-tech. The fund operates in the Spanish and Iberian defence-tech investor cluster alongside Adara Ventures and 201 Ventures.

Hyperion writes seed and Series A cheques into European founders building in autonomy, AI, ISR, and defence-relevant deep-tech. Public team and portfolio information is limited at this stage.
