## About

FORWARD.one is a Dutch deep-tech venture firm headquartered in Amsterdam. Fund III runs at €200M and continues the firm's thesis of investing in European deep-tech founders building hardware-heavy, capital-intensive technologies including semiconductors, photonics, robotics, and dual-use systems.

The fund writes Series A and B cheques, often as lead investor, and works with founders coming out of Dutch and broader European research institutions including TU Delft, ETH Zurich, and Imperial. Defence and dual-use exposure spans autonomy, materials, and industrial computing.
