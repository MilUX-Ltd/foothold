## About

Frst is a Paris-based pre-seed and seed venture fund founded by Pierre Entremont and Bertille Soriano (former Otium Capital team). The fund writes first cheques into French and broader European founders across SaaS, marketplaces, fintech, and increasingly deep-tech and dual-use categories.

Frst has supported a number of French defence-tech and dual-use founders and co-invests with the wider French defence-tech cluster including 360 Capital, Expansion VC, and Bpifrance.
