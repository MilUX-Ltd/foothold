## About

Form1 Partners is a UK-based venture firm operating a hybrid venture building and early-stage investment model. The firm works with technical founders building deep-tech and defence-relevant companies, providing operational support alongside capital.

The team draws from operating, engineering, and military backgrounds and writes pre-seed cheques into UK and broader European founders.
