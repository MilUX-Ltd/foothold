## About

HCVC (Hardware Club Ventures) is a transatlantic deep-tech venture firm focused on hardware founders. The fund grew out of the Hardware Club founder community founded by Alexis Houssou and writes pre-seed and seed cheques into hardware-driven founders in Europe and the US.

HCVC has supported a number of defence-relevant hardware companies in robotics, autonomy, sensing, and industrial systems. The fund co-invests with the broader European deep-tech and defence-tech community.
