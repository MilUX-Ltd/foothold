## About

High-Tech Gründerfonds (HTGF) is Germany's largest public-private seed fund, backed by the German federal economic affairs ministry alongside corporate and institutional investors. HTGF writes seed cheques into hundreds of German technology startups across software, hardware, life sciences, and increasingly defence and dual-use.

The fund has been a foundational backer of much of Germany's deep-tech and dual-use scene, often as a first institutional cheque. HTGF works with KfW, German federal programmes, and other regional public investors.
