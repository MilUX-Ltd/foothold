## About

First Momentum Ventures is a Karlsruhe and Berlin-based pre-seed deep-tech investor. Fund II runs at €35M and continues the firm's thesis of writing first-cheque tickets into German and broader European deep-tech founders, including defence and dual-use categories.

The fund focuses on technical founders building from university research environments (KIT, TU Munich, ETH Zurich) and writes pre-seed cheques of €100k to €1M with follow-on capacity.
