## About

Expansion VC is a Paris-based venture firm managing €142M in early-stage capital for European deep-tech, defence, and dual-use companies. The fund writes seed and Series A cheques and is one of the larger French defence-tech specialist managers.

Expansion sits in the same French and pan-European defence-tech cluster as 360 Capital, Bpifrance Definvest, and Tikehau. The fund co-invests across the European defence-tech network.
