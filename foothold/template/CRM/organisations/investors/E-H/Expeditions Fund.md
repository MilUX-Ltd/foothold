## About

Expeditions Fund is a Warsaw-based venture firm raising Fund II at €150M to invest in defence and dual-use companies across Central and Eastern Europe and broader Western-aligned markets. Co-founded by Mikolaj Firlej and Anthony Mensier, the firm has become one of the more visible CEE defence-tech specialists.

Expeditions writes seed and Series A cheques into founders building autonomy, AI, ISR, and resilience technologies. The fund is part of the cross-European defence-tech investor cluster and co-invests with NATO Innovation Fund and other regional partners.
