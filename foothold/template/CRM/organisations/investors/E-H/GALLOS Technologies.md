## About

GALLOS Technologies is a UK venture builder and early-stage investor focused on defence-tech and dual-use companies. The firm combines a thesis-driven investment approach with venture building, working closely with founders to develop and scale defence-relevant technologies.

GALLOS sits in the UK defence-tech investor community alongside firms with similar founder-operator models. The team draws from defence, technology, and operating backgrounds.
