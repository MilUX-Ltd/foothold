## Fund Details

- **Stage:** Seed (€500k to €3M)
- **Geography:** Europe
- **HQ:** Munich, Germany
- **Founded:** 2016

## About

42CAP is a Munich-based seed fund founded by Thomas Wilke and Alex Meyer, two software entrepreneurs who previously built and sold eCircle. The fund backs European founders building data-driven B2B software, with growing exposure to defence-relevant deep-tech and dual-use companies.

Tickets typically range from €500k to €3M at seed, with follow-on capacity. The fund operates as a generalist enterprise-software investor but has supported several dual-use companies in autonomy, cyber, and industrial AI.
