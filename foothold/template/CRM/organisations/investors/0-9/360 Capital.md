## About

360 Capital is a pan-European venture fund with offices in Paris and Milan, investing across deep-tech, dual-use, mobility, and climate. The firm runs both early-stage and growth vehicles, deploying tickets from seed through Series B.

In recent years 360 Capital has built out a defence and dual-use line under partner Alexis Houssou, backing companies in autonomy, sensing, and resilience. The fund is an active French defence-tech co-investor.
