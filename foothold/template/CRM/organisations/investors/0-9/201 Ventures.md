- **Website:** [201.vc](https://www.201.vc/)

- **Fund focus:** Defence-tech and dual-use; autonomy, cyber, space, energy security, supply-chain resilience
- **Stage:** Pre-seed and seed
- **Geography:** Europe
- **Fund size:** €20M (Fund I)
- **HQ:** Madrid, Spain

## About

201 Ventures is a Madrid-based early-stage venture fund investing in defence and resilience technologies across Europe. The first fund is €20M and targets pre-seed and seed cheques in dual-use companies, with a focus on autonomy, cyber, space, energy security, and supply-chain resilience.

The fund positions itself as one of the few Spain-headquartered managers writing dedicated defence-tech tickets, working alongside pan-European co-investors including NATO Innovation Fund and national defence-oriented funds.
