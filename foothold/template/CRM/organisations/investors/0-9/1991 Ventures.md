## About

1991 Ventures is an early-stage venture fund founded by Ukrainian entrepreneur Denis Gursky, named after the year of Ukrainian independence. The fund backs defence-tech and dual-use companies at pre-seed and seed, with a focus on founders building from Ukraine's combat-tested operating environment.

The fund's thesis sits at the intersection of frontline defence innovation and dual-use technology. Portfolio companies often emerge from Ukraine's wartime defence-tech surge, with applications in autonomy, EW, ISR, and battlefield software.
