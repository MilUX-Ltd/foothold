## About

7percent Ventures is a London-based pre-seed venture firm investing in transformational technology, including frontier and deep-tech areas with defence relevance. The fund has backed founders building in space, quantum, synthetic biology, AI infrastructure, and adjacent deep-tech categories.

Founder Andrew J Scott has positioned 7percent as a high-conviction pre-seed manager writing first cheques alongside angels and accelerators, with a global mandate but UK and US tilt.
