## About

Unconventional Ventures is a Copenhagen-based pre-seed and seed venture capital firm founded in 2019. Its thesis backs scalable tech startups led by underrepresented founders (women, LGBTQ founders, founders of colour, immigrants) across the Nordics and broader Europe.

The fund is not a defence specialist. Its presence on this list reflects the wider Nordic VC ecosystem rather than a stated dual-use mandate. Sector exposure is generalist tech with selective frontier-tech positions.
