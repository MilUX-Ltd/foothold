## About

VSquared Ventures (Vsquared) is a Munich-based deep-tech venture capital firm focused on European founders building category-defining hard technology. Sectors include space, defence, quantum, semiconductors, AI, advanced materials and energy. Funds I and II together are over €350m.

The firm is one of the most active European deep-tech investors with explicit defence and dual-use exposure. Notable portfolio positions include Isar Aerospace, Quantum Systems and ARX Robotics.
