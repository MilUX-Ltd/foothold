## About

Unruly Capital is a London-based early-stage VC founded in 2022. The fund backs founders working on ideas that feel too early, too weird, or too crazy in manufacturing, energy, climate, transportation and health. As of late 2025 the firm reported around 50 portfolio companies with seven new investments in the prior 12 months.

The fund is not a stated defence specialist but its frontier-tech mandate produces dual-use exposure across advanced manufacturing, energy resilience and emerging hardware categories. Co-investors include 8VC, Morpheus Ventures, Pale Blue Dot and Transition.
