## About

Vanagon Ventures is a Munich-based pre-seed venture firm launched in 2023, with Fund I closed at €20m in early 2026. The fund backs B2B founders building system-level deep-tech and AI categories, with cheque sizes up to €500k.

Sectors of focus include spatial AI, quantum computing, robotics and frontier software. Vanagon emphasises European sovereignty and sustainability. LPs include Allocator One alongside family offices, Apple and Google senior executives, and Munich unicorn founders. Fund administration is via ACE Alternatives; legal support from Orrick and Bird and Bird.
