## About

Verne Capital is a Germany and Luxembourg-based defence and security venture capital firm founded in 2025 by Benjamin Krahmer and Michael Singer. Fund I targets €100m and invests in early-stage European defence, security and cybersecurity startups.

Geographic allocation reserves around one third of the fund for the DACH region and 25 per cent specifically for Ukrainian defence-tech companies. Backers include the European Investment Fund and European Investment Bank under the InvestEU Defence Equity Facility, alongside Hessen federal land, Deutsche Bank, Deutsche Börse, Merck, Wisag and Dussmann (carried across from the founders' previous Futury fund). The industrial advisory board includes former Airbus, German Army Procurement Office (BAAINBw) and cybersecurity executives.
