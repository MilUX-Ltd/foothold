## About

ZAS Ventures is a Ukrainian-American early-stage venture firm founded in 2021 by Andrew Zinchuk. It invests $50k to $200k via SAFE into seed-stage startups from Ukraine and Central and Eastern Europe targeting the US market. Sectors span SaaS, logistics, deep tech, healthtech, climate tech and B2B marketplaces.

ZAS is not a defence specialist. Its position on this list reflects broader Ukrainian and CEE tech investor coverage rather than a stated dual-use mandate, though portfolio cross-over into resilience and security categories occurs post-2022.
