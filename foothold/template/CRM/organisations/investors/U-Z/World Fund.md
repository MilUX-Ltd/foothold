## About

World Fund is one of Europe's largest dedicated climate-tech venture capital funds, founded by Ecosia (the not-for-profit search engine) with a thesis built on Climate Performance Potential (CPP). Fund I is reported at €300m. The fund invests Series A and B in companies with measurable potential to reduce emissions at scale, across energy, food, industry, materials and mobility.

Defence and resilience exposure runs through energy resilience, sovereign supply-chain and critical-materials positions rather than primary defence technology. The fund is Berlin-headquartered with team across Munich, Amsterdam and London.
