## About

Vsquared Ventures Fund II is the second vintage of Vsquared Ventures' deep-tech strategy, sized at €214m. The fund backs European deep-tech founders across space, defence, quantum, AI, semiconductors, advanced materials and energy.

Vsquared is one of the most active deep-tech investors out of Munich, with explicit defence and dual-use positions. Portfolio companies include Isar Aerospace (small launch), Quantum Systems (uncrewed aerial systems) and ARX Robotics (uncrewed ground systems). Cheque sizes are seed to Series A with selective B participation.
