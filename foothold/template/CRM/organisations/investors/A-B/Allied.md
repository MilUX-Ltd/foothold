- **Website:** [allied.partners](https://www.allied.partners/)

- **Fund focus:** Autonomy, AI, secure communications, resilience

## About

Allied is a transatlantic defence and dual-use venture firm investing in companies aligned with allied nations' national security priorities. The fund focuses on autonomy, AI, secure communications, and resilience technologies.

Public information about the fund's portfolio and team is limited. The firm positions itself as a co-investor alongside other allied-aligned defence funds and operates across the UK, EU, and US.
