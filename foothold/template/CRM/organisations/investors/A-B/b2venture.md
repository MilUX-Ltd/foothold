## About

b2venture (formerly BTOV Partners) is a Swiss-German venture firm investing at seed and early stages across Europe. The firm operates a hybrid model combining institutional funds with a large network of private angel investors, giving founders unusual access to operator-investors.

The firm has multiple vehicles spanning industrial tech, digital, and crypto, with growing exposure to deep-tech and dual-use companies. b2venture has backed defence-adjacent companies in autonomy, cyber, and industrial software.
