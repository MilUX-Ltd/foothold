## About

Baryon Fund is a Luxembourg-domiciled €100M early-stage venture fund investing in defence, dual-use, and resilience companies across Europe. The fund operates in the pan-European defence-tech investor cluster that has expanded rapidly since 2022.

Baryon writes seed and Series A cheques and positions itself as a thesis-driven defence specialist rather than a generalist with defence exposure. Public information about the team and portfolio is limited.
