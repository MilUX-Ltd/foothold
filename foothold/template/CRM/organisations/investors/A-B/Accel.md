## About

Accel is a global venture firm with offices in Palo Alto, London, and Bangalore, investing from seed through growth across enterprise, consumer, infrastructure, and increasingly dual-use deep-tech. The London office leads European and Israeli investments and has backed several defence-adjacent companies in AI, cyber, and autonomy.

The firm runs multiple active vehicles spanning early-stage and growth funds. Within defence and dual-use, Accel has been a public backer of Tessera, Tessian, Snyk, and other security and infrastructure companies. UK partner Harry Nelis is the most visible point of contact in London.
