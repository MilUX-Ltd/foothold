## About

Balnord is a Luxembourg-domiciled early-stage venture fund with €70M under management, investing in Baltic and Nordic founders building defence, dual-use, and resilience technologies. The fund operates close to NATO's eastern flank and benefits from rapidly growing regional defence-tech activity.

Balnord writes seed and Series A cheques and is part of the Baltic-Nordic defence-tech investor cluster alongside Iron Wolf Capital, Archangel, Aneli Capital, and Coinvest Capital.
