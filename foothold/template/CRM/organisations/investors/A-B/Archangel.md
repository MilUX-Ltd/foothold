## About

Archangel is an Estonian early-stage venture fund investing in defence, dual-use, and resilience technologies. The fund has €50M under management and is part of the Baltic and Nordic defence-tech investor cluster that has grown rapidly since 2022.

Archangel writes seed and Series A cheques into Baltic, Nordic, and broader European founders building autonomy, cyber, ISR, and battlefield software. The fund often co-invests with NATO Innovation Fund, Iron Wolf Capital, and other regional defence managers.
