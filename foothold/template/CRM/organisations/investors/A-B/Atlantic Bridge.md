## About

Atlantic Bridge is a global deep-tech growth investor headquartered in Dublin, with offices in London, Silicon Valley, and Beijing. The firm manages several funds totalling over $1bn AUM and invests in companies commercialising university-spun and research-led deep-tech across the UK, Ireland, US, and Asia.

The firm manages the UK Innovation & Science Fund (UKI2S) on behalf of the UK government and is one of the UK's most active deep-tech growth investors. Portfolio companies span semiconductors, AI, quantum, photonics, and dual-use technologies.
