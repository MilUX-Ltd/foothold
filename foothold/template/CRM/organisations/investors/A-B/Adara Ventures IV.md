## About

Adara Ventures IV is the latest fund vehicle managed by Adara Ventures, a Madrid-based deep-tech firm founded in 2005. The fund targets €100M and continues the firm's focus on early-stage European companies across cyber, AI, infrastructure software, and dual-use technologies, with a sharper emphasis on resilience and defence-tech.

The fund vehicle sits within Adara Capital, the parent firm. The team writes Series A and late-seed cheques into European founders, often co-investing with pan-European deep-tech and defence-focused managers.
