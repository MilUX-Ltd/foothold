## About

Adara Ventures is a Madrid-based deep-tech venture firm investing in early-stage European companies across cyber, AI, infrastructure software, and dual-use technologies. The firm has been one of Spain's longest-standing deep-tech investors and has built a strong portfolio of cyber and defence-adjacent companies.

Adara Capital is the parent firm under which the Adara Ventures fund vehicles sit. The latest vehicle, Adara Ventures IV, targets €100M and continues the firm's thesis with a stronger emphasis on resilience and defence-tech.
