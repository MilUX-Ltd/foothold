## About

BlueYard Capital is a Berlin-based seed-stage venture fund investing in frontier deep-tech founders building decentralised, open, and resilient systems. Themes include cryptography, distributed computing, biotech, space, and energy.

The fund's thesis sits adjacent to defence and resilience through investments in autonomy, security, and sovereign infrastructure. BlueYard has backed companies including Status, Protocol Labs, and other widely cited deep-tech names.
