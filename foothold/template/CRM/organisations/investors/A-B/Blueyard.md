## About

BlueYard Capital is a Berlin-based seed-stage venture fund investing in frontier deep-tech founders building decentralised, open, and resilient systems. Themes include cryptography, distributed computing, biotech, space, and energy.

The fund's thesis sits adjacent to defence and resilience through investments in autonomy, security, and sovereign infrastructure. BlueYard has backed companies including Status, Protocol Labs, and other widely cited deep-tech names.

## Recent investments

Tracked from the last 120 days (last refresh: 2026-05-27). BlueYard Capital has materially shifted toward defence and AI infrastructure; 2025 was exceptionally active with portfolio companies raising $1B+ in follow-on capital and 13 new portfolio additions.

- **Castelion** — hypersonic missile systems, BlueYard's most recent unicorn (2025, one year after first investment). [Source: Newcomer / Tracxn](https://www.newcomer.co/p/exclusive-blueyard-capital-returns)
- **2025 cohort additions** — Aerska, Alterego, Boundless, CHOMP, Dost, Eigenblue, Foresight Data Machines, ManexAI, Miren, Number0, ZeroPhase. Several have defence or dual-use relevance. [Source: BlueYard 2025 Year in Review](https://blueyard.medium.com/blueyard-capital-2025-year-in-review-20a87bdf0a72)

BlueYard's stated thesis: defence will shift from large legacy primes to teams reinventing the supply chain from first principles.
