# Black Opal Ventures

## Fund Details

- **Website:** [blackopalventures.com](https://www.blackopalventures.com/)
- **Type:** Early-stage VC
- **Fund focus:** Women-led companies in digital health, AI, resilience
- **Stage:** Seed and Series A
- **Geography:** US and select international
- **HQ:** New York, United States

## About

Black Opal Ventures is a New York-based early-stage venture fund founded to back women-led companies across digital health, AI, and resilience-related categories. The fund writes seed and Series A cheques into US and selected international founders.

The fund's defence and dual-use exposure is limited but emerges through investments in resilience-relevant categories including biosecurity, mental health, and AI infrastructure.
