## About

Buildit is a Latvian hardware accelerator and early-stage investor based in Riga, focused on hardware, IoT, and industrial deep-tech startups. The programme combines a structured accelerator with seed-stage cheques and provides workshop and prototyping support.

Buildit operates in the Baltic deep-tech and dual-use cluster and has supported a number of hardware founders that have gone on to attract further regional defence-relevant investment.
