## About

Angel One Fund is a small Ukrainian early-stage fund with €2.8M deployed into Ukrainian defence-tech founders. The fund writes pre-seed and seed angel-style cheques and focuses on companies emerging from Ukraine's wartime defence innovation surge.

The fund is one of several small Ukrainian vehicles channelling local and diaspora capital into combat-relevant founders.
