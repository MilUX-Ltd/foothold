## About

Ananda Impact Ventures is a Munich-based impact VC investing in European founders building scalable solutions to social and environmental challenges. Fund V targets €73M and continues the firm's thesis across health, climate, education, and resilience.

The fund's defence and dual-use exposure comes through the resilience and sustainability lens, including supply-chain security, climate adaptation, and societal resilience. Tickets typically range from €1M to €5M at Series A.
