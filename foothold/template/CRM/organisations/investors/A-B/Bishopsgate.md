## About

Bishopsgate Corporate Finance is a London-based independent corporate finance advisory firm with a specialist focus on defence, security, aerospace, and technology sectors. The firm advises on sell-side and buy-side M&A, growth capital, and management buyouts for UK and European mid-market clients.

The firm is not a fund manager; it acts as advisor to founders, owners, and corporate clients. It is a well-recognised name in the UK defence sector advisory market, working with SMEs across the supply chain.
