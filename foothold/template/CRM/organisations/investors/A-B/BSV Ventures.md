## About

BSV Ventures is a Lithuanian early-stage venture fund with €15M under management, investing in Baltic and Central European founders building defence and dual-use technologies. The fund operates close to NATO's eastern flank and is one of several small Baltic defence-tech vehicles.

BSV writes pre-seed and seed cheques and is part of the Baltic defence-tech investor cluster alongside Iron Wolf Capital, Aneli Capital, Coinvest Capital, and Balnord.
