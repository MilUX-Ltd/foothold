## About

AM Ventures is a Germany-based venture firm focused on additive manufacturing and adjacent industrial technologies. Portfolio companies span industrial 3D printing, materials science, and process automation, with defence-relevance through dual-use applications in aerospace and manufacturing for hardened components.
