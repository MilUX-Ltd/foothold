## About

Air Street Capital is a London-based venture firm investing in AI-first companies at pre-seed and seed. Founded by Nathan Benaich, the firm is known for the annual State of AI Report, a widely read sector benchmark, and has a strong network across AI research, infrastructure, and applied AI.

The fund has supported companies operating in AI applied to defence, biology, autonomy, and industrial software. Air Street writes early cheques in European and US founders, often as a first institutional backer.
