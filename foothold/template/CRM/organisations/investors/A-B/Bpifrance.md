## About

Bpifrance is the French public investment bank, established in 2012 to consolidate state-backed financing and equity vehicles. It is France's primary state investor in startups, scale-ups, and industrial companies, with thousands of portfolio holdings spanning seed through growth.

Within defence, Bpifrance operates several vehicles including Definvest (a dedicated defence equity fund co-managed with the Direction Générale de l'Armement) and Tibi (sovereign tech). Bpifrance is the most active French co-investor in defence-tech and dual-use deals, often partnering with 360 Capital, Tikehau, and NATO Innovation Fund.
