## About

Alpine Space Ventures is a Munich-based space-tech fund investing across the European new-space sector. Fund I closed at €170M and targets early and growth-stage investments in launch, satellites, in-space services, and downstream applications including defence and intelligence.

The team was founded by former SpaceX operators including Bulent Altan, bringing operational experience from a leading global launch programme into European capital allocation. The fund backs companies serving both commercial and government customers, with dual-use applications across ISR, communications, and resilience.
