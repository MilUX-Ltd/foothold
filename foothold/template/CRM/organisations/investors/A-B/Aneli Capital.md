## About

Aneli Capital is a Lithuanian early-stage venture fund with €35M under management, investing in Baltic and Central European founders building defence, dual-use, and resilience technologies. The fund operates close to NATO's eastern flank and benefits from the region's accelerated defence-tech build-out since 2022.

Aneli is one of several Baltic and Eastern European defence funds writing seed and Series A cheques into local and regional founders.

## Frameworks and Co-investors

- Operates alongside Iron Wolf Capital, Coinvest Capital and other Baltic regional defence managers
