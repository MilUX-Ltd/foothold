- **Stage:** Pre-seed and seed
- **Geography:** Ukraine
- **HQ:** Kyiv, Ukraine

## About

D3 Ventures (Dare to Defend Democracy) is a Ukrainian early-stage venture fund focused on defence-tech founders building from Ukraine's wartime operating environment. The fund is one of several local vehicles channelling local and diaspora capital into combat-relevant founders.

D3 writes pre-seed and seed cheques into Ukrainian defence-tech and is part of the same Ukrainian investor cluster as 1991 Ventures and Angel One Fund.
