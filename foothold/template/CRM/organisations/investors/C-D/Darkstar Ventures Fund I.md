## About

Darkstar Ventures is an Estonian early-stage venture fund raising Fund I at €25M, focused on defence-tech and dual-use founders across the Baltics and broader Europe. The fund is one of several new Estonian and Baltic vehicles that have launched in the post-2022 defence-tech wave.

Darkstar writes seed cheques and is part of the Baltic defence-tech investor cluster alongside Archangel, Iron Wolf Capital, BSV Ventures, and Balnord.
