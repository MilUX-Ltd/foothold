## About

Cherry Ventures is a leading pan-European seed fund headquartered in Berlin, with offices across Europe. The fund writes first institutional cheques between €500k and €5M into ambitious European founders across enterprise software, consumer, climate, and deep-tech.

Cherry has been an active investor in defence-tech and dual-use, including high-profile backing of Stark, Aurora Defence, and other European defence-tech names. The London office gives the firm strong UK coverage.
