## About

DTCP (Deutsche Telekom Capital Partners) is a Hamburg-headquartered growth equity and infrastructure investor backed by Deutsche Telekom. Project Liberty is a €500M defence-tech and digital sovereignty fund being raised by DTCP to invest in European companies building sovereign digital infrastructure, dual-use, and resilience technologies.

The fund sits within DTCP's broader platform of growth, infrastructure, and venture vehicles and writes larger growth-stage cheques than most European defence-tech funds. Project Liberty is one of the largest dedicated European defence and sovereignty fund vehicles announced to date.
