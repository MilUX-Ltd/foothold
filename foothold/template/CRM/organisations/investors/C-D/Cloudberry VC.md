## About

Cloudberry VC is a Finnish early-stage venture fund with €30M under management, investing in Nordic and Baltic founders building defence, dual-use, and resilience technologies. The fund operates close to NATO's eastern flank and benefits from the rapid post-2022 growth in regional defence-tech.

Cloudberry writes seed and Series A cheques and is part of the Nordic-Baltic defence-tech investor cluster alongside Iron Wolf Capital, Balnord, Archangel, and Aneli Capital.
