## About

Double Tap Investments (DTI) is a Finnish early-stage venture fund focused on defence-tech and dual-use companies. The fund operates in the Nordic-Baltic defence-tech investor cluster, writing seed cheques into Finnish and broader Nordic founders.

Public information about the team and portfolio is limited. The fund sits alongside Cloudberry VC and other Nordic defence-relevant managers.
