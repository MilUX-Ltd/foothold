## About

DN Capital is a London-based multi-stage venture firm investing across European and US tech, with offices in Berlin and Menlo Park. The firm invests across B2B SaaS, consumer, fintech, marketplaces, and selectively in deep-tech and dual-use.

Notable historical exits include Shazam and Endeca. The firm writes cheques from Series A through growth and has a growing pipeline of dual-use exposure through fintech and infrastructure investments.
