## About

Coinvest Capital is a Lithuanian state-backed co-investment fund managed under INVEGA, the Lithuanian national development institution. The fund co-invests alongside private business angels and venture capital firms into Lithuanian SMEs and scale-ups, with growing exposure to defence-tech and dual-use companies.

The fund is part of Lithuania's strategy to channel public capital into private defence-tech alongside Aneli Capital, Iron Wolf Capital, and other regional managers.

## Recent investments

Tracked from the last 120 days (last refresh: 2026-05-27). Coinvest Capital is Lithuania's sovereign VC fund (100% subsidiary of ILTE), the first in the region fully authorised for defence investments and a pioneer in not requiring civilian use cases.

- **Unmanned Defense Systems (UDS)** — Coinvest's largest defence-domain investment to date, undisclosed amount. UDS is Vilnius-based, supplies Lithuanian and Ukrainian armed forces and other NATO allies. [Source: TechCrunch, Mar 2025](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/08/in-defense-tech-lithuanias-sovereign-vc-fund-is-one-step-ahead/)
- **51 portfolio companies, 6 exits** as of Oct 2025. €25.9M directly deployed by Coinvest; €12.1M of committed capital remaining. [Source: Coinvest portfolio page](https://www.coinvest.lt/portfolio-companies)

Approximately €6.8M of the total since 2023 is into aerospace, defence and dual-use specifically.
