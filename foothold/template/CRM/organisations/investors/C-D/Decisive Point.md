## About

Decisive Point is a US defence and national security venture firm investing in dual-use companies serving US government and allied defence customers. The firm writes seed and Series A cheques and is deeply embedded in the US defence venture community.

Decisive Point's team draws from US military, intelligence, and policy backgrounds and operates in the same investor cluster as Shield Capital, Lux Capital's defence line, and Founders Fund.
