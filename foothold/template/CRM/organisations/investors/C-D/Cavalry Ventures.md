- **Website:** [cavalry.vc](https://cavalry.vc/)

## About

Cavalry Ventures is a Berlin-based pre-seed and seed venture fund investing in early-stage European founders across B2B software, deep-tech, and emerging categories. The fund typically writes first cheques between €500k and €3M.

Cavalry has growing exposure to defence-relevant categories including cyber, autonomy, and industrial software. The team backs founders building category-defining companies rather than incrementally improving existing markets.
