## About

CyLon (also branded Cylon Lab) is a London-based cyber security accelerator and seed-stage investor, one of Europe's longest-running dedicated cyber programmes. The firm has supported over a hundred cyber and digital resilience startups since founding.

CyLon works closely with UK government cyber stakeholders, including the National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC), and runs programmes in partnership with industry sponsors. The firm has a strong UK defence-tech network and is a recognised pipeline for cyber founders entering the defence market.
