## About

IceNine Capital is an early-stage venture firm focused on the new industrial renaissance, backing founders in defence, energy, aerospace, and frontier technologies. Their thesis is built around reindustrialisation and technological sovereignty, investing in companies solving mission-critical problems in markets where industrial depth and government networks matter more than SaaS growth playbooks.

They differentiate through hands-on operational expertise, embedded government networks, and multi-cycle experience across hardware and industrial sectors. They work shoulder-to-shoulder with founders on technical, operational, and commercial decisions from day zero.
