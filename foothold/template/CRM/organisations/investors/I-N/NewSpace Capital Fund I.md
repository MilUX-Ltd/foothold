## About

NewSpace Capital is a Luxembourg-based growth equity fund focused on the space-tech value chain. Fund I closed at €105m and invests in established companies serving downstream space markets: connectivity, earth observation, navigation, launch, in-orbit services and ground segment hardware and software.

The fund's geographic focus is Europe, with selective allocations to the US and emerging space economies. Defence and resilience exposure runs through dual-use ground-segment, ISR and PNT (positioning, navigation, timing) positions. Typical tickets are growth equity in the €5m to €20m range.
