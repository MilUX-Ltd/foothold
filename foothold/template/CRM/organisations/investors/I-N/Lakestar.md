## About

Lakestar is a European venture capital firm founded by Klaus Hommels in 2012. It backs companies across the technology stack from early stage through to growth, with assets under management reported in the multi-billion euro range across consecutive funds.

Defence and deep tech sit within Lakestar's broader European tech focus. Recent activity in dual-use and security-relevant categories has put it on the radar of defence-tech founders. Portfolio names that have intersected the defence and resilience space include Isar Aerospace (small launch) and ARX Robotics (uncrewed ground systems), alongside its long-standing consumer and SaaS positions in Spotify, Revolut, Klarna and Airbnb.
