## About

Matterwave Ventures is a Munich-based early-stage venture capital firm backing European industrial technology, with a stated focus on resilient supply chains and sovereign dual-use industrial capability. The team spun out of btov's industrial-tech practice. Fund II had a first close at €75M and is targeting €130M overall.

Investments are Seed and Series A across industrial hardware and software. Portfolio companies span additive manufacturing (DyeMansion), waste-heat conversion (Orcan Energy), private 5G with defence-sector buyers (Firecell), and broader process and supply-chain technology.
