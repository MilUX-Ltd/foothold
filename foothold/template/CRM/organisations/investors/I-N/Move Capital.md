## About

Move Capital is a pan-European growth equity fund focused on the data value chain across IoT, AI, cyber security, data analytics, machine learning and Industry 4.0. Fund I is managed by KC Invest and targets large minority or control positions in fast-growing B2B digital services companies, with cheque sizes of €10m to €50m.

The fund is not a pure-play defence vehicle. Cyber and industrial-digital positions intersect dual-use territory but the central thesis is European B2B digital growth.
