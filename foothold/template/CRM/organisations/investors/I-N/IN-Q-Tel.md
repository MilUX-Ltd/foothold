## About

In-Q-Tel (IQT) is a non-profit strategic investor founded to identify and accelerate technology of interest to the US intelligence community and allied national security agencies. The firm was established by the CIA and has since expanded to serve other US and allied government partners.

IQT invests in commercial technology companies whose products can be adapted to national security missions. The firm runs a global office network including London and Sydney, supporting UK and Five Eyes engagement, and has backed hundreds of companies across cyber, AI, autonomy, biotechnology, sensing, and infrastructure.

Within the UK, IQT works closely with UK government partners and is part of the broader transatlantic defence and intelligence investor cluster.
