## About

Lightspeed Venture Partners is a US-headquartered global venture firm with a multi-stage strategy spanning seed to growth. It has been one of the more active US-origin investors in defence and dual-use, with positions in Anduril, Saronic Technologies, Rhombus Power, Castelion and Snorkel AI, among others.

The London office has grown over the past three years to cover European tech, with partners scouting defence-adjacent positions alongside enterprise software, fintech and consumer. Lightspeed's defence thesis emphasises commercial dual-use and software-defined hardware over pure-play primes.
