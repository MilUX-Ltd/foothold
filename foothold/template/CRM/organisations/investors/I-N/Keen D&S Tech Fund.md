## About

Keen D&S Tech Fund is a €150M defence and security technology fund being raised by Keen Venture Partners, an Amsterdam and London-based growth-stage VC. The fund vehicle sits within Keen Venture Partners and marks the firm's move into a dedicated defence-specialist position.

The fund will continue Keen's European-Atlantic thesis, writing growth-stage cheques into European defence, dual-use, and resilience companies. Keen co-invests with NATO Innovation Fund and other defence-tech partners.
