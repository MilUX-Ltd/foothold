## About

Mosaic Ventures is a London-based early-stage VC founded in 2014 by Mike Chalfen, Toby Coppel and Simon Levene. The fund backs founders building category-defining companies across consumer, enterprise, fintech, and emerging deep-tech areas.

Defence and dual-use is not the primary thesis but Mosaic has invested in companies whose underlying technology has resilience and security applications. The team is small and partner-led, with concentrated portfolio construction.
