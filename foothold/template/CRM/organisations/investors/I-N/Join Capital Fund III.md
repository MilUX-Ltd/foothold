## About

Join Capital is a Berlin-based venture firm investing in European deep-tech and industrial-tech founders. Fund III targets €235M and continues the firm's thesis of writing Series A cheques into founders building enterprise software, industrial AI, robotics, and dual-use deep-tech.

Join works closely with European industrial corporates and has a strong network across the German and European industrial base. The fund's defence and dual-use exposure includes autonomy, AI, and resilience-relevant industrial software.
