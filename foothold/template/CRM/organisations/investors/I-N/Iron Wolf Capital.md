## About

Iron Wolf Capital is a Vilnius-based early-stage venture fund with €30M under management, focused on defence-tech, dual-use, and frontier technology founders across the Baltics, Nordics, and broader Europe. The fund is one of the more visible Baltic defence-tech specialists and has been active in the region's accelerated post-2022 defence-tech build-out.

Iron Wolf writes seed and Series A cheques and co-invests with NATO Innovation Fund, Coinvest Capital, Aneli Capital, and other regional defence-tech managers. The team draws from technology, military, and operating backgrounds.
