## About

Kima Ventures is a Paris-based seed and pre-seed investor founded by Xavier Niel, the French entrepreneur behind Free (Iliad). Kima is one of the world's most active seed investors, making more than two investments per week across a wide range of sectors including B2B software, consumer, fintech, deep-tech, and dual-use.

The fund writes small first cheques (typically €150k) and follows on selectively. Kima's defence and dual-use exposure emerges through its high investment volume rather than a thesis-led specialism.
