## About

Lemonade Stand is an Estonian pre-seed and seed venture firm investing in Baltic and Central-Eastern European founders. Typical cheque size is €50k to €300k, with willingness to back pre-revenue and pre-product teams alongside seed and follow-on rounds.

The fund is sector-generalist across B2B, with portfolio touchpoints in fintech, enterprise software, healthtech and emerging dual-use categories. As of 2025 the fund had backed around 38 companies. Defence-tech is not the central thesis but Estonian-origin defence and dual-use names sit in adjacent investor networks.
