## About

IQ Capital is a UK deep-tech venture firm with offices in Cambridge and London, investing in early-stage British and European founders building algorithmic and data-driven technologies. The firm has multiple active vehicles including its main IQ Capital fund and the Growth Fund.

The portfolio spans AI, quantum, cyber, photonics, and dual-use deep-tech. IQ Capital is one of the most active UK deep-tech investors and has supported a number of defence-relevant founders.
