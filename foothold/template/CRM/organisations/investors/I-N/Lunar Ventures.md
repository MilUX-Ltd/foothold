## About

Lunar Ventures is a Berlin-based pre-seed deep-tech investor founded in 2017 by Dr Elad Verbin, Mick Halsband and Luis Shemtov. Fund I was €40M; Fund II closed in 2025 at €50M with backing from Isomer Capital, Lingotto, the Intesa San Paolo Endowment and the Grinnell College Endowment.

Cheque sizes run from €750k to €1m into 25 to 30 startups. The fund plans roughly 80 per cent into Europe with the remainder into underserved US locations outside the Bay Area. Thesis areas are AI infrastructure, techbio and physical-world systems infrastructure. The portfolio includes Deepset, Zama, Molecule, Hathora, and Lodestar Space, a London-based autonomous satellite-servicing and defence-relevant startup.
