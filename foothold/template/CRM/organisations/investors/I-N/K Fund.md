## About

K Fund is a Madrid-based venture firm investing across Spanish and southern European technology founders at seed and Series A. The firm runs multiple vehicles and writes cheques into B2B software, fintech, marketplaces, and selectively in deep-tech and dual-use.

K Fund is one of Spain's most active venture managers and co-invests with Adara Ventures, 201 Ventures, and other Iberian managers. Defence and dual-use exposure is emerging through investments in resilience-relevant categories.
