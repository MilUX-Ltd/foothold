## About

Magnetic is a Berlin-based venture firm founded by David Rosskamp and Mario Lebherz. It backs transformative technology in four domains: the compute and energy stack, security, financial technologies, and physical economy systems. Defence and resilience-adjacent investment lands inside the security and physical-economy categories rather than as a stand-alone defence thesis.

Stages run from pre-seed through seed-plus and Series A. The portfolio is small and selective at around 13 companies.
