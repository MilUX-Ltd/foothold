## About

Lazard is a global financial advisory and asset management firm with a long history in defence-sector M&A, capital markets advisory and sovereign restructuring. It is not a venture capital fund. Where Lazard appears on a defence-tech founder's investor list it is typically as a sell-side or buy-side adviser, capital markets arranger, or strategic counsel to corporates and governments.

Its Aerospace, Defence and Government Services practice advises primes, mid-tier suppliers and government bodies on transactions and strategic restructuring. The firm's UK operation is centred on London with senior practitioners in industrials and defence M&A.
