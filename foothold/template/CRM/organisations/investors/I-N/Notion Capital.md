## About

Notion Capital is a London-based VC focused on European B2B SaaS and cloud companies, founded by Stephen Chandler and Jos White in 2009. Stages typically run Series A and B with selective seed activity.

Defence is not the central thesis, but Notion has portfolio touchpoints in cyber, infrastructure software and AI-driven enterprise tooling that intersect with dual-use buyers. Founders typically have a London or wider European base.
