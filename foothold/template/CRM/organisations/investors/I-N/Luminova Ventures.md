## About

Luminova Ventures is a Czech deep-tech and dual-use venture capital firm founded in 2025. It invests in cybersecurity, defence technologies, autonomous systems, robotics, and quantum computing and encryption, with a thesis built on dual-use capability that serves civilian and military buyers.

Cheque sizes target pre-seed, seed and early Series A, with team members in Czech Republic and Slovakia. Luminova is featured in the Czech Security and Defence Technologies Catalogue 2025-2026, the official MFA publication distributed via diplomatic missions. The firm was a participant in European Defence Week 2025 in Paris.
