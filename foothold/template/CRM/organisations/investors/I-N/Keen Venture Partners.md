## About

Keen Venture Partners is an Amsterdam and London-based growth-stage venture firm investing in European deep-tech, dual-use, and defence-relevant companies. The firm writes Series A and B cheques and has built a strong portfolio across cyber, autonomy, and infrastructure software.

Keen is now raising the Keen D&S Tech Fund, a €150M defence and security technology vehicle, marking the firm's move into a dedicated defence-specialist position. The fund will continue Keen's European-Atlantic thesis and co-invest with NATO Innovation Fund and other defence-tech partners.
