## About

T|Y|R.vc is a Danish dual-use technology venture capital fund focused on resilience across the Baltic and Nordic region. Fund size is reported at €30m and the fund is in active deployment.

The fund's thesis covers dual-use deep tech, security and resilience-relevant capability across the Nordic and Baltic countries. It positions itself alongside Polarion, ETNA and other Nordic dual-use specialists in the post-2022 wave of regional defence-relevant funds.
