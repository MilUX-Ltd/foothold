## About

Starquest Capital is a Paris-based VC founded in 2008 with a PROTECT thesis: supporting companies that safeguard society against the negative externalities of progress (climate change, digital risk, resource overconsumption). Sectors include clean energy, energy efficiency, food security, digital security, circular economy and education.

Innovacom is a separate French venture capital firm with which Starquest has co-investment history alongside Bpifrance, Safran Corporate Ventures, Kima Ventures and Wilco. Starquest has space situational awareness exposure via Aldoria (formerly Share My Space). As of late 2025 the firm reported around 112 portfolio companies.
