## About

SFC Capital is one of the most active seed investors in the UK, specialising in SEIS and EIS-qualifying pre-seed and seed cheques. It backs companies across software, deep tech, fintech, healthtech and emerging dual-use categories.

The firm was selected by the British Business Bank as one of the delivery partners for the Regional Angels Programme, giving it formal status as a UK government-backed feeder of capital into the regional angel ecosystem. SFC writes typical first cheques of £50k to £500k.
