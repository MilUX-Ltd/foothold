## About

SMRK VC Fund is a Kyiv-based venture capital firm founded in 2013 by Oleksandr Kosovan and Andriy Dovzhenko. It invests in IT product companies at Seed and Series A, with a cheque range of $0.5m to $1.5m.

The fund is not a defence specialist but is one of the most active Ukrainian VC funds, and since 2022 has supported several Ukrainian companies operating in dual-use territory, including Osavul (information integrity and resilience) and Deus Robotics. Geographic exposure spans Ukraine, the US and Finland.
