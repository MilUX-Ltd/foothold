## About

Tiny Supercomputer Investment Company (TSIC) is a London-based pre-seed and seed venture firm operating across Western Europe. Founded in 2017 by Philipp Moehring, the firm has backed more than 450 companies through its successive funds. As of mid-2024 the firm was deploying its fourth vintage.

The legal vehicle is Tiny Supercomputer Management Company (UK) Ltd, an FCA-authorised AIFM also SFDR-compliant under EU sustainable finance disclosure rules. Defence is not the central thesis but the high-velocity early-stage model means TSIC has co-invested in a number of dual-use and frontier-tech companies alongside specialist defence investors.
