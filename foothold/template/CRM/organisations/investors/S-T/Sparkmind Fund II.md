## About

Sparkmind.vc is a Helsinki-based Nordic venture capital firm dedicated to the EdTech sector. Fund I closed at €55m; Fund II is targeting €150m and is in raise / target status. The fund invests across the education and learning value chain from K12 to corporate and lifelong learning.

LPs in prior vehicles include Tesi (Finnish Industrial Investment), KRR fund-of-funds, Jacobs Foundation, OP Banking Group, Elo Pension Insurance and the University of Jyväskylä. Defence relevance is limited. Sparkmind sits on this list as a Nordic venture investor in the wider category but is not a defence specialist; portfolio cross-over runs through workforce training, simulation and learning infrastructure.
