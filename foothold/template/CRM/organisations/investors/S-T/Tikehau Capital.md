## About

Tikehau Capital is a Paris-listed (Euronext) global alternative asset manager. Within its private equity platform, Tikehau operates a dedicated Aerospace and Defence strategy launched in 2023 in partnership with Bpifrance and Airbus, targeting French and European mid-cap supply chains in defence, aerospace, dual-use and resilience.

The Aerospace and Defence Fund (Fonds Aerospatial) reached €750m+ at first close and is one of Europe's largest dedicated defence-focused private equity vehicles. Tikehau also operates climate-transition, decarbonisation and regenerative agriculture strategies.
