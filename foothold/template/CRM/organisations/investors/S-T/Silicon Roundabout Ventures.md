## About

Silicon Roundabout Ventures is a London-based pre-seed deep-tech fund founded by Francesco Perticarari, born from the Silicon Roundabout founder community he runs in London. The fund backs UK and European deep-tech founders building hard technology with strong technical defensibility.

Thesis areas include AI infrastructure, quantum, robotics, biotech, energy and dual-use technology. The fund operates a high-volume scout-led origination model alongside concentrated cheque sizes. Defence-relevant portfolio positions include companies in quantum, autonomy and advanced computing.
