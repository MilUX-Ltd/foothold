## About

Sisyphus Ventures is a UK-based early-stage venture capital fund investing in European defence and autonomous technologies, with a stated mission to support dual-use, scalable, cost-effective technology and back the next generation of European neoprimes. The fund is reported at €20m and is in active deployment.

Sisyphus was founded by Louise Boucher, who began investing in defence in 2022. The fund operates a thesis around filling Europe's capability gap in defence autonomy and has been positioned in industry coverage as one of the smaller but increasingly visible UK defence-focused emerging managers.
