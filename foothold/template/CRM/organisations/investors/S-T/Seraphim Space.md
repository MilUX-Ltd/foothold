## About

Seraphim Space is the largest pure-play space-tech investor globally. Founded in 2016 and headquartered in London, it operates the Seraphim Space Investment Trust (LSE: SSIT) alongside the Seraphim Space Accelerator and the early-stage Seraphim Space Camp.

The portfolio spans space domain awareness, earth observation, satellite communications, launch and downstream analytics, with explicit defence and resilience exposure across most positions. Cheque sizes are seed to Series B. Seraphim has a deep network across UK Space Command, US Space Force and NATO programmes. Notable portfolio names include ICEYE, AST SpaceMobile, Hawkeye 360, D-Orbit and All.Space.
