## About

ScaleWolf is a Vilnius-based dual-use technology accelerator and venture capital fund founded in 2023. The ScaleWolf VC Fund is approved by the Bank of Lithuania as a closed-end investment company and runs alongside an allied technology accelerator. The fund is reported around €13.5m and is being scaled up.

ScaleWolf operates in collaboration with the Lithuanian Ministry of Economy and Innovation and aligns its thesis with NATO emerging and disruptive technologies (EDTs). Sectors include energy, data, defence and AI. The model is hybrid: grants, acceleration and venture capital.
