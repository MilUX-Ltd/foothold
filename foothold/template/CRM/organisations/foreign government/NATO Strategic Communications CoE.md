# NATO Strategic Communications CoE

NATO-accredited Centre of Excellence for strategic communications. Based in **Riga, Latvia**. Develops doctrine, research, and capability for strategic communications, counter-disinformation, information operations, and the wider cognitive dimension of NATO operations.

Particularly active in researching state-sponsored disinformation, social-media-based information operations, and the information dimension of contemporary conflict (including Russian, Chinese, and other adversary activity).

For UK defence-tech SMEs working on information integrity, counter-disinformation, narrative analysis, or psychological operations support, the StratCom COE is one of the most active research surfaces inside the NATO COE network.
