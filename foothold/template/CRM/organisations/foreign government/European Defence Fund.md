# European Defence Fund (EDF)

## Summary

EU funding mechanism for collaborative defence research and capability development. Run by the European Commission Directorate-General for Defence Industry and Space (DG DEFIS).

The EDF supports collaborative defence research and development projects across multiple EU member states. UK participation post-Brexit is limited but possible through specific agreements and consortia involving EU partners.

## Routes for UK engagement

- Participation as a third-country partner via EU-based consortium lead.
- Bilateral or trilateral collaboration with EU partner nations on EDF-funded projects.
- Watch for UK-EU defence cooperation announcements that may broaden access.
