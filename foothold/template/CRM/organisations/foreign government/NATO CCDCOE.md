# NATO CCDCOE — Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence

## Summary

NATO-accredited Centre of Excellence for cyber defence expertise, training, and doctrine. Headquartered in Tallinn, Estonia. Provides multinational and interdisciplinary expertise across the cyber domain — technology, strategy, operations, law.

Notable for hosting the **Locked Shields** annual live-fire cyber defence exercise, one of the largest international cyber defence exercises in the world.
