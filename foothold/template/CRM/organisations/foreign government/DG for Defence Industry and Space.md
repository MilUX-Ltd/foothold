# DG for Defence Industry and Space (DG DEFIS)

The European Commission's **Directorate-General for Defence Industry and Space**. The Commission body responsible for EU defence industrial policy, the **European Defence Fund (EDF)**, the EU space programme (Galileo, Copernicus, GOVSATCOM, IRIS²), and wider defence-industrial coordination across EU member states.

For UK defence-tech SMEs post-Brexit, DG DEFIS programmes are no longer directly accessible the same way as for EU-based firms, but the EDF remains a useful reference point for understanding European defence-industrial direction and for any UK SME working with EU-based partners on dual-use or defence-industrial topics.
