# NATO Allied Command Transformation (ACT)

NATO's strategic command responsible for **transformation**: capability development, experimentation, doctrine, and education. Headquartered in Norfolk, Virginia, USA. The counterpart to [[CRM/organisations/foreign government/NATO ACO|NATO Allied Command Operations (ACO)]], which runs current operations.

Website: [act.nato.int](https://www.act.nato.int/).

## Role in the landscape

ACT defines future NATO requirements, develops capability concepts, and pressure-tests them through experimentation before they reach the operational forces. The remit spans from long-range concept development through to operational experimentation and capability delivery support. Key sub-functions include:

- [[CRM/organisations/foreign government/NATO OPEX|NATO Operational Experimentation (OPEX)]] — controlled experimentation function.
- **Joint Air Power Competence Centre (JAPCC)**, **Joint Force Training Centre (JFTC)**, and other centres of excellence supported by ACT.
- The **Defence Innovation Accelerator for the North Atlantic (DIANA)** network and the **NATO Innovation Fund (NIF)**, both anchored on the ACT mission.

### Operating posture

ACT works with NATO member nations to harmonise capability development, accelerate technology adoption, and ensure interoperability across the Alliance. The Common Funded Capability Delivery model (six stages, persistent collaboration with ACO) is the operating framework ACT uses to take capability from concept to acceptance.
