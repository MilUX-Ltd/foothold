# Defense Procurement Agency (DPA) MoD of Ukraine

## Company details

- **Full name:** Defense Procurement Agency (DPA) of the Ministry of Defence of Ukraine.
- **Website:** [dpa.gov.ua](https://dpa.gov.ua).
- **LinkedIn:** [Defense Procurement Agency (DPA) MoD of Ukraine](https://www.linkedin.com/company/defense-procurement-agency-dpa-mod-of-ukraine).
- **Sector:** Government / national defence procurement.
- **HQ:** Kyiv, Ukraine.
- **Established:** 2023, as part of Ukrainian MoD reform to separate procurement from the operational ministry.

## What the DPA does

The DPA is the centralised procurement authority for the Ukrainian Armed Forces under the Ministry of Defence of Ukraine, established in 2023 as part of the broader procurement reform programme. It runs the sourcing of weapons, equipment, ammunition and supplies for the Ukrainian Armed Forces during the active wartime context with Russia, with a remit covering land systems, air defence, unmanned systems, firearms, ammunition, R&D and integration.

The agency operates internationally to source supply from allied defence industrial bases, including UK SMEs and primes, and runs an **International Programs** function that engages directly with foreign defence vendors. The agency's establishment was part of a broader reform push to professionalise and de-politicise Ukrainian defence procurement, separating it institutionally from the MoD's operational chain.

For UK defence-tech SMEs, the DPA is a notable customer surface for Ukraine-support work, particularly via the International Programs function.
