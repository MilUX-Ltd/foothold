# Army Futures Command (US)

> **Status: superseded.** In 2025 the US Army brought Army Futures Command and Training and Doctrine Command (TRADOC) together into a new **US Army Transformation and Training Command (T2COM)**, headquartered in Austin, Texas. TRADOC was inactivated on 26 September 2025 and T2COM was activated on 2 October 2025. The Army's futures work now sits in the **Futures and Concepts Command**, activated under T2COM on 12 February 2026. This page keeps the Army Futures Command name so older links and searches still land.

## Company details

- **Website now:** [army.mil/t2com](https://www.army.mil/t2com).
- **Sector:** Defence, military modernisation, capability development.
- **HQ:** Austin, Texas, USA (T2COM).
- **Established:** Army Futures Command, August 2018; T2COM, October 2025.
- **Parent:** United States Army.

## What it does now

T2COM is a single Army command for force generation, force design and force development: training, doctrine, leader development and modernisation. Its major subordinate commands include US Army Recruiting Command, Combined Arms Command and the Futures and Concepts Command.

The **Futures and Concepts Command** (Joint Base Langley-Eustis, Virginia) is the Army's main organisation for developing concepts, experiments and requirements. It grew out of the Army Capabilities Integration Center and the Futures and Concepts Center.

## Background: Army Futures Command (2018 to 2025)

Army Futures Command was set up in 2018 to consolidate the Army's capability development, owning its six modernisation priorities (Long Range Precision Fires, Next Generation Combat Vehicle, Future Vertical Lift, Air and Missile Defence, the Network, Soldier Lethality) and the Cross-Functional Teams behind them. It was deliberately located in Austin, close to US tech and academia.

## Why this matters

T2COM and its Futures and Concepts Command are the US Army's interface with the wider defence-innovation ecosystem (DIU, AFWERX, NavalX, xTech, SBIR). For UK defence-tech SMEs, they are a reference point for how the US Army organises modernisation, and a route to US-side innovation pipelines through UK-US bilateral channels.
