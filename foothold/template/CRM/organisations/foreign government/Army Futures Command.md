# Army Futures Command (US)

## Company details

- **Website:** [armyfuturescommand.com](https://armyfuturescommand.com).
- **Sector:** Defence, military modernisation, capability development.
- **HQ:** Austin, Texas, USA.
- **Established:** August 2018.
- **Parent:** United States Army.
- **Position:** Four-star Army Command, peer to FORSCOM, TRADOC and AMC.

## What Army Futures Command does

Army Futures Command (AFC) is the United States Army's modernisation headquarters, set up in 2018 to consolidate capability development and lead the Army's transition from an Iraq/Afghanistan-era force to a multi-domain force optimised for large-scale combat operations. AFC owns the Army's six modernisation priorities (Long Range Precision Fires, Next Generation Combat Vehicle, Future Vertical Lift, Air and Missile Defence, the Network, Soldier Lethality), the Cross-Functional Teams that drive them, and the requirements process behind major Army acquisition programmes.

Headquartered in Austin, Texas, deliberately co-located with US tech and academia rather than the traditional Pentagon and Aberdeen orbits. AFC subsumes the Combat Capabilities Development Command (DEVCOM), the Army Applications Lab, xTechSearch, and the Army's relationships with Carnegie Mellon, MIT, the University of Texas system and the broader defence-innovation ecosystem.

In 2024 the US Army announced a major restructure that re-aligns AFC's role; as of 2026 the command continues to operate as the Army's modernisation lead pending the full implementation of those changes.

## Why this matters

AFC is the US Army's interface with the wider defence-innovation ecosystem (DIU, AFWERX, NavalX, xTech, SBIR). For UK defence-tech SMEs, it is a useful reference point for how the US Army has structured a single modernisation command, and for accessing US-side innovation pipelines via UK-US bilateral channels.
