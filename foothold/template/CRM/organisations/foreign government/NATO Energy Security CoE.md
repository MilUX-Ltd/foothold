# NATO Energy Security CoE (ENSEC COE)

NATO-accredited Centre of Excellence for energy security. Based in **Vilnius, Lithuania**. Develops and shares expertise on the energy dimension of NATO operations, including resilience of military energy supplies, critical energy infrastructure protection, and the operational implications of the energy transition.

For UK defence-tech SMEs working on resilient power, micro-grids, fuel logistics, or energy-as-a-weapon-of-war analysis, the ENSEC COE is a useful collaborative surface inside the NATO COE network alongside [[CRM/organisations/foreign government/NATO CCDCOE|CCDCOE]] (cyber) and [[CRM/organisations/foreign government/NATO Strategic Communications CoE|StratCom COE]].
