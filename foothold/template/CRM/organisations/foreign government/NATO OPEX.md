# NATO Operational Experimentation (OPEX)

NATO's controlled-experimentation function. Sits inside **Allied Command Transformation (ACT)** in Norfolk, Virginia, and exists to test new concepts, ideas, and capabilities under structured conditions before NATO commits resources to them.

NATO ACT: [Experimentation and Demonstration](https://www.act.nato.int/activities/allied-command-transformations-innovation/experimentation-and-demonstration); [OPEX 2020 Fact Sheet (PDF)](https://www.act.nato.int/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/2020_opex.pdf); [ACT begins OPEX at Dynamic Messenger 2023](https://www.act.nato.int/article/act-begins-opex-at-dynmsg23/).

## Role in the landscape

OPEX delivers transformation to the Alliance through controlled investigations that discover information, test hypotheses, and validate concepts. It is the bridge between NATO concept development and operational adoption — the place where new ideas get pressure-tested with field operators before being committed to as full programmes.

### Operating posture

- **Operational bias.** Works with deployed NATO forces alongside civilians, academics, industry, other international organisations, and NGOs.
- **Three venues.** Experiments run in NATO theatres of operations, in live and computer-assisted exercises, and in collegial workshop settings.
- **Multi-Domain Operations focus.** Recent OPEX cycles have explicitly explored MDO concepts: undersea infrastructure protection, naval mine warfare, maritime security operations, force protection, underwater warfare, amphibious operations, and medical / logistical operations.

### Industry engagement route

OPEX experiments are not open competitions but deliberately invite industry, academic, and NGO participation. The most-visible recurring exercise is **Dynamic Messenger**, which has anchored the MDO-experimentation pipeline. For SMEs with NATO-relevant capability, the engagement route is usually via NATO DIANA or the NATO Innovation Hub, with selected participants then joining OPEX exercises.
