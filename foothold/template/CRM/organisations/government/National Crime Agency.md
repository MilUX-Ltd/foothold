# National Crime Agency (NCA)

The UK's lead agency on **serious and organised crime**. A non-ministerial department, accountable to the Home Secretary, established under the **Crime and Courts Act 2013** and operational from **7 October 2013**.

NCA website: [nationalcrimeagency.gov.uk](https://www.nationalcrimeagency.gov.uk/). GOV.UK: [National Crime Agency](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/national-crime-agency).

## Role in the landscape

NCA leads the UK response to serious and organised crime across a deliberately wide remit:

- **Serious and organised crime** (SOC) — drugs, weapons, organised immigration crime, fraud and economic crime.
- **Cybercrime** — through the National Cyber Crime Unit (NCCU), close operational partner to NCSC.
- **Child sexual abuse and exploitation** — through CEOP.
- **Modern slavery and human trafficking**.
- **Firearms** — illicit firearms supply.
- **Financial crime** — through the National Economic Crime Centre.
- **Fugitives**.
- **Border-related serious crime** (in partnership with Border Force).

### Where it sits

A **non-ministerial department**: not part of any single ministerial portfolio, although accountable to the Home Secretary. This is the same structural status as HMRC and Ofsted, giving NCA operational independence from day-to-day political direction.
