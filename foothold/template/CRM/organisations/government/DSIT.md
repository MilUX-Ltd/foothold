# DSIT (Department for Science, Innovation and Technology)

> **Status: being dissolved.** The machinery of government changes announced on 22 July 2026 redistribute DSIT's functions. Its gov.uk page now says it is being replaced by the Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade. This page is kept so older links still resolve.

## Where DSIT's work went

- **Science and innovation** to the [[CRM/organisations/government/BIST|Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST)]], the renamed Department for Business and Trade. BIST now sponsors [[CRM/organisations/government/UKRI|UKRI]] and [[CRM/organisations/government/ARIA|ARIA]], and the [[CRM/organisations/government/UK Space Agency|UK Space Agency]] is part of BIST.
- **Digital** (the Government Digital Service, telecoms, online safety, digital identity policy) to the expanded **Department for Digital, Culture, Media and Sport (DCMS)**.
- **AI strategy, public-sector AI adoption and the AI Security Institute** to the **Cabinet Office**.
- **[[CRM/organisations/government/National Security Online Information Team|NSOIT]]**: parent under review following the July 2026 machinery of government changes.

gov.uk gives no completion date for the transfers, and still shows a DSIT record while they go through.
