# Innovate UK Business Growth

Innovate UK Business Growth empowers innovation-driven businesses to grow at pace and achieve their industry and society-transforming ambitions. Sits inside [[CRM/organisations/government/UKRI|UKRI]] alongside the wider Innovate UK funding and competition pipeline.

For defence-tech SMEs, Innovate UK Business Growth is the business-development counterpart to Innovate UK's grant-funding programmes. It pairs supported companies with experienced Innovation and Growth Specialists who help shape scaling strategy, investor readiness, and route-to-market.
