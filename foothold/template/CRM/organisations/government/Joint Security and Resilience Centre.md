# Joint Security and Resilience Centre (JSaRC)

The Home Office's industry-engagement hub for security and resilience technology. Founded 2016. Sits within the Homeland Security Group and is **jointly funded by the UK security industry and the Home Office** to act as the interface between the security sector (industry and academia) and the complex security machinery of government.

JSaRC website: [jsarc.org.uk](https://www.jsarc.org.uk/); [About JSaRC](https://www.jsarc.org.uk/about); [Contact JSaRC](https://www.jsarc.org.uk/contact). GOV.UK: [JSaRC group page](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/joint-security-and-resilience-centre-jsarc).

## Role in the landscape

JSaRC brings together government, industry, and academia to tackle some of the UK's most complex national security challenges. Two named focus areas:

- **Accelerate innovation** by identifying, testing and promoting cutting-edge security technologies and capabilities.
- **Support UK prosperity** by enabling security exports, stimulating growth, and strengthening the domestic security sector.

JSaRC is structurally similar to [[CRM/organisations/government/Accelerated Capability Environment (ACE)|ACE]] (also under Homeland Security Group) but with a wider remit covering industry coordination, security exports, and the [Security and Policing](https://www.securityandpolicing.co.uk/about/jsarc/) annual event.

### Joint funding model

The joint-funding arrangement with the security industry is the defining feature: JSaRC is not a pure-government body, and its agenda is shaped by industry as much as by Home Office priorities. This makes it an unusually direct route into Home Office policy-making and capability requirements for security-focused SMEs.

### Engagement routes

- **JSaRC Vision** monthly newsletter (sign-up via the JSaRC website).
- **Security and Policing** annual event where JSaRC is the central convening body.
- **Direct contact** via Jsarc@homeoffice.gov.uk for specific challenge engagements.
