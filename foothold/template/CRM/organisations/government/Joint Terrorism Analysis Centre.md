# Joint Terrorism Analysis Centre (JTAC)

JTAC is the UK's cross-government body analysing intelligence on international terrorism and setting UK threat levels. Reports to the Director General of the Security Service (MI5) and works alongside the wider intelligence community.

For defence-tech SMEs, JTAC is a closed customer surface, but the published UK threat levels and JTAC-informed CT priorities are useful background context for any work touching counter-terrorism, homeland security, or critical national infrastructure protection.
