# Innovate UK

Innovate UK describes itself as the UK's innovation agency. It backs deep technology businesses in the UK's priority sectors with funding, expert support and connections ([UKRI: Innovate UK](https://www.ukri.org/councils/innovate-uk/)). It is civilian, and it matters to a defence founder because it funds dual-use work and runs the public-sector R&D contract route used by several defence bodies.

## Summary

- **Where it sits.** Innovate UK is one of the councils inside [[CRM/organisations/government/UKRI|UKRI]], alongside the seven Research Councils and Research England. UKRI is an executive non-departmental public body with a combined budget of more than £6bn ([gov.uk: UKRI](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-research-and-innovation)).
- **Sponsor.** gov.uk lists UKRI as sponsored by the [[CRM/organisations/government/BIST|Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST)]]. This follows the July 2026 machinery of government changes, which dissolve [[CRM/organisations/government/DSIT|DSIT]] and rename the Department for Business and Trade ([gov.uk fact sheet](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/machinery-of-government-changes-fact-sheet)).
- **Strategy, 19 March 2026.** Innovate UK named defence as one of six priority sectors. It announced Velocity, an account management service for high-potential businesses that is not a fixed programme, and said a new operating model would begin from April 2026. Funding is described as grants and loans matched to a business's stage and risk ([UKRI strategy page](https://www.ukri.org/publications/turning-breakthrough-ideas-into-the-uks-next-industry-giants/innovate-uk-turning-breakthrough-ideas-into-industry-giants/); [UKRI news](https://www.ukri.org/news/new-plan-to-help-the-next-generation-of-tech-businesses-thrive/)).

## What it funds, and through which routes

- **Grants.** Competitions, published on the Innovation Funding Service. In May 2026 Innovate UK ran two dual-use grant competitions: [Dual-use aviation systems and autonomy](https://www.ukri.org/opportunity/dual-use-aviation-systems-and-autonomy/) (£10m) and [Counter UAS Technologies](https://www.ukri.org/opportunity/counter-uas-technologies/) (£5m). Both were collaborative: the lead had to be a UK-registered business or a UK public sector organisation, with at least one UK SME claiming grant. Both have closed.
- **Contracts for Innovation** (formerly the Small Business Research Initiative). A public body sets a challenge and pays suppliers to do R&D under a contract. Phases can run from feasibility to demonstrator, and each phase must involve at least two funded suppliers ([UKRI](https://www.ukri.org/what-we-do/browse-our-areas-of-investment-and-support/innovate-uk-contracts-for-innovation/)). A defence example is "Battery innovation for dual use and defence", Phase 1, which ran from 28 September to 4 November 2026: up to £2m in contracts of £20,000 to £50,000, funded by BIST through Innovate UK with the MOD as a partner. It was open to organisations of any size, and applicants kept their IP ([Innovation Funding Service](https://apply-for-innovation-funding.service.gov.uk/competition/2587/overview/aafc825c-421e-47ea-bd56-d5d1cd50e513)). Detail on the route: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Innovate UK Contracts for Innovation|Innovate UK Contracts for Innovation]].
- **Innovation loans.** Awarded through competitions, with the loan agreement held by Innovate UK Loans Ltd, a wholly owned subsidiary of UKRI. You have to show you can pay interest and repay, and why public funding is needed ([UKRI: Innovation loans](https://www.ukri.org/councils/innovate-uk/guidance-for-applicants/guidance-for-specific-funds/innovation-loans/); [application process](https://www.ukri.org/councils/innovate-uk/guidance-for-applicants/guidance-for-specific-funds/innovation-loans/the-application-process/)). The MOD's own loan scheme is separate and paused: [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Programmes/Defence Innovation Loans|Defence Innovation Loans]].
- **Support.** [[CRM/organisations/government/Innovate UK Business Growth|Innovate UK Business Growth]] gives one-to-one help to ambitious small and medium-sized innovation-focused businesses. Innovate UK Business Connect, formerly Innovate UK KTN, focuses on networking and connections ([UKRI: connect with experts](https://www.ukri.org/what-we-offer/supporting-collaboration-in-the-uk/supporting-collaboration-innovate-uk/connect-with-experts-specialist-support-and-facilities/)).

## How it differs from UKDI

Innovate UK funds civil and dual-use technology across all priority sectors. [[CRM/organisations/mod/UK Defence Innovation (UKDI)|UK Defence Innovation]] is the MOD's own innovation body and funds against defence and security challenges. A product with both civil and defence uses can look at both. They are separate organisations with separate competitions and separate rules, so check each competition's own page.

## How a small supplier engages

1. Create an account on the [Innovation Funding Service](https://apply-for-innovation-funding.service.gov.uk/). Innovate UK does not accept applications made any other way, and advises using a professional email address, which cannot be changed later ([UKRI: your application](https://www.ukri.org/councils/innovate-uk/guidance-for-applicants/general-guidance/your-application/)).
2. Search the funding finder on the [UKRI opportunities pages](https://www.ukri.org/councils/innovate-uk/) for open competitions, and read each competition's eligibility section. Rules differ by competition, including who can lead and whether overseas organisations can apply.
3. For collaborative grants, UKRI's general guidance says no single organisation can claim more than 70% of total project costs. The competition text sets the actual funding rates.
4. Ask Innovate UK Business Growth whether it is taking new requests, and watch for Velocity, which UKRI says businesses can apply to directly or be referred to.
