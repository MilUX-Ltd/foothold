# UKRI Innovation Accelerator

UK Research and Innovation programme accelerating research-based innovation in target regions of the UK. Channels co-investment between UKRI, local partners, and private capital into named regional innovation clusters.

For defence-tech SMEs, the Innovation Accelerator is the regional counterpart to UKRI's national funding streams. Relevant where a defence-relevant SME sits in one of the targeted regions and the innovation focus areas align.
