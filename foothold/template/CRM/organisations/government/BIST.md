# Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST)

Formerly the **Department for Business and Trade (DBT)**. The July 2026 machinery of government changes renamed DBT and gave it science, innovation and research from the dissolving [[CRM/organisations/government/DSIT|DSIT]]. The acronym BIST was fixed on 27 July 2026.

## Company details

- **Short name:** BIST (formerly DBT).
- **Website:** [gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-business-innovation-science-and-trade](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-business-innovation-science-and-trade).
- **Sector:** UK central government department.

## What BIST does

BIST supports businesses to invest, innovate, export and grow, and now holds science and innovation policy as well as trade and business. DBT was formed in February 2023 from the Department for International Trade and parts of the Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy (BEIS). Its overseas trade network works from British embassies and consulates.

## Bodies a defence founder meets

- [[CRM/organisations/government/UKRI|UKRI]], including Innovate UK: sponsored by BIST.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/ARIA|ARIA]] (Advanced Research and Invention Agency): sponsored by BIST.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/UK Space Agency|UK Space Agency]]: part of BIST.
- **Export Control Joint Unit (ECJU)**: part of BIST; runs UK export licensing. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Export Controls Orientation|Export Controls Orientation]].
- **British Business Bank**: works with BIST.
- **Investment Security Unit**: moving to BIST from the Cabinet Office. It screens acquisitions under the National Security and Investment Act, which matters when you raise money. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Government Departments|Government Departments]].
- **Office for Investment** and the **Regulatory Innovation Office**: listed as groups within BIST.
- [[CRM/organisations/government/OSBC|Office of the Small Business Commissioner]]: sponsored by the department since its DBT days.

## Defence exports

Defence export promotion moved from DBT to the MOD in July 2025. The Defence Investment Plan (30 June 2026) describes UK Defence and Security Exports (UKDSE) as now inside the NAD Group, in [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments International (NA-Intl)|NA-Intl]]. BIST's gov.uk page still lists a UK Defence and Security Exports group, so treat the MOD as the home for defence export support and check gov.uk if in doubt. SMEs can use the MOD's [SME Defence Export Faculty](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/sme-defence-export-faculty) (27 May 2026).
