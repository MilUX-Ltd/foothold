# Office of the Small Business Commissioner (OSBC)

## Organisation details

- **Website:** [smallbusinesscommissioner.gov.uk](https://www.smallbusinesscommissioner.gov.uk).
- **Sector:** Government — small business support, late payment regulation.
- **Established:** 2016 under the Enterprise Act 2016.
- **Type:** Independent public body sponsored by the Department for Business and Trade.
- **LinkedIn:** [Office of the Small Business Commissioner](https://uk.linkedin.com/company/office-of-the-small-business-commissioner).

## What it does

The OSBC is the UK government's independent body set up to tackle late payments and unfair payment practices that affect small businesses. Its core responsibilities are:

- Reviewing complaints from small businesses about late payments by larger companies.
- Advising and supporting small businesses on payment rights and practices.
- Influencing larger businesses to improve their payment behaviour.
- Running the **Fair Payment Code** — a voluntary standard where businesses commit to paying suppliers promptly (550+ awardees as of March 2026).
- Publishing guidance for small businesses on payment rights, business structures, and related topics.

The OSBC is gaining new legislative powers to levy financial penalties against large businesses that are persistent late payers.

## Key initiatives

- **Fair Payment Code** — Voluntary code for businesses committing to fair payment practices. 550+ awardees. [More info](https://www.smallbusinesscommissioner.gov.uk/fair-payment-code/).
- **Get the Money Moving** — Webinar series launched March 2026.
- **SME Safari** — Commissioner visits small businesses across the UK to hear directly about challenges.
- **FUEL Roadshow** — National finance and funding roadshow for business leaders, delivered by UMi. [Register](https://www.fuelroadshow.co.uk/).
- **Guidance Library** — Published resources including [payment rights](https://www.smallbusinesscommissioner.gov.uk/help-and-guidance/all-advice/a-guide-to-payment-rights-for-small-businesses/), [late payments and mental health](https://www.smallbusinesscommissioner.gov.uk/help-and-guidance/all-advice/late-payments-and-mental-health-support-for-small-businesses/), and [types of businesses](https://www.smallbusinesscommissioner.gov.uk/help-and-guidance/all-advice/guide-to-types-of-businesses/).

## Why it matters for defence SMEs

- **Late payments legislation** — directly affects SMEs working with larger defence primes and MOD.
- **Fair Payment Code** — potential to apply for an award, demonstrating commitment to fair payment practices in the supply chain.
- **SME policy and support** — OSBC is a useful source of intelligence on government policy affecting small businesses.
