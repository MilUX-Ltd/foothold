# Defence Office for Small Business Growth (DOSBG)

## Overview

- **Full name:** Defence Office for Small Business Growth
- **Parent:** Ministry of Defence (MOD). gov.uk does not say which part of the MOD it sits in; this pack files it once, under the MOD, in [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/MOD Structure|MOD Structure]].
- **Launched:** 27 January 2026. Open now.
- **Website:** [digital.mod.uk/sme-dosbg](https://www.digital.mod.uk/sme-dosbg/about-us)
- **Sector:** Defence: SME engagement, procurement support, supplier growth

DOSBG is the MOD's team of policy and commercial specialists for small and medium-sized enterprises. It is meant to be a central hub for advice on working with defence: tendering and contracting guidance, mentoring, introductions to primes, signposting to regional clusters and trade bodies, and a confidential channel for SMEs to raise concerns. It started its portal and contact centre with 30 pathfinder SMEs before widening out.

## Targets it leads on

- Increase MOD spending with SMEs by 50% by 2028: an additional £2.5bn through to May 2028, taking total SME spend to about £7.5bn.
- DOSBG leads the [MOD SME Action Plan](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/mod-small-and-medium-sized-enterprise-sme-action-plan) (21 July 2026), which sets out how the MOD will reduce barriers for SMEs.
- The [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]] repeats the £2.5bn commitment.

## Relevance to defence SMEs

DOSBG sits at the heart of the Valley of Death challenge for defence SMEs. It offers:

- A route to understanding MOD's SME engagement priorities.
- Intelligence on how MOD procurement processes are evolving for smaller suppliers.
- A place to raise problems with a buyer or prime in confidence.
