# National Protective Security Authority (NPSA)

NPSA. Provides protective security advice across UK government and critical national infrastructure. Also responsible for elements of security clearance policy and runs published equipment evaluations open to UK manufacturers (notably in counter-UAS, where NPSA evaluations are a recognised industry route).

## Where NPSA matters for defence SMEs

- **Counter-UAS equipment evaluations** open to UK manufacturers, refreshed annually.
- **Protective security advice** to critical national infrastructure operators, including identity, physical, personnel, and cyber dimensions.
- **Clearance policy** input that affects how SMEs obtain SC and DV-cleared personnel.
