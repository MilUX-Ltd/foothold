# NSSIF — National Security Strategic Investment Fund

UK government investment fund for national-security-relevant technologies. Provides capital to startups and SMEs developing capabilities the national-security community needs. Frequently co-invests with UK and allied venture capital partners on dual-use technology.

For defence-tech SMEs, NSSIF is one of the principal national-security-specific capital sources alongside the MOD's [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|Defence Investment Unit]] (within the NAD Group) and Innovate UK.
