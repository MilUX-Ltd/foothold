# Department for Business and Trade (DBT)

## Company details

- **Short name:** DBT.
- **Website:** [gov.uk/dbt](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/department-for-business-and-trade).
- **Sector:** UK central government department.
- **HQ:** Old Admiralty Building, London, SW1A 2EG.

## What DBT does

DBT is the UK government department responsible for supporting international investment into the UK and helping UK businesses to grow and export. It was formed in February 2023 from the merger of the Department for International Trade and elements of the Department for Business, Energy and Industrial Strategy (BEIS). Activities span trade, foreign direct investment, export control, export finance, business support, and the wider UK business environment. Brand positioning includes the `#SoldToTheWorld` campaign and Trade Week.

DBT operates a substantial overseas footprint through Department for International Trade posts in British embassies and consulates around the world (Paris, Dubai, Sydney, Brasilia, The Hague, Bilbao and many others).

Note: defence export promotion was transferred from DBT to the MOD in July 2025 via a Machinery of Government change. The UKDSE function now sits inside [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments International (NA-Intl)|NA-Intl]] as part of the NAD Group structure.

## DBT-adjacent bodies of interest

- **UK Defence Solutions Centre (UKDSE) Export Faculty**, runs a regular bulletin (`UKDSEExportFaculty@email.businessandtrade.gov.uk`).
- **Small Business Commissioner**, separate but DBT-sponsored body running an SME-focused newsletter.
