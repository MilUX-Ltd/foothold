# Department for Business and Trade (DBT)

> **Renamed.** In July 2026 the Department for Business and Trade became the **Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST)**, taking on science and innovation from the dissolving DSIT. The current page is [[CRM/organisations/government/BIST|BIST]]. This page is kept so older links still resolve.
