# ProtectUK

The UK's **public-facing counter-terrorism protective security platform**. Operated by the **National Counter Terrorism Security Office (NaCTSO)** and delivered by the **Counter Terrorism Alliance** (NaCTSO + Home Office + Pool Reinsurance). Provides free guidance, learning, and tools to organisations responsible for venues, public spaces, and the wider Critical National Infrastructure footprint.

Website: [protectuk.police.uk](https://www.protectuk.police.uk/); [About](https://www.protectuk.police.uk/about). GOV.UK: [Join ProtectUK today](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/join-protectuk-today).

## Role in the landscape

ProtectUK is the operational delivery layer for CT advice and guidance to UK businesses, venue operators, and members of the public. It is the "single front door" the Counter Terrorism Alliance wants people to use rather than navigating a fragmented CT-advice estate.

### Named content

- **ACT (Action Counters Terrorism)** training products, including the award-winning ACT Awareness e-Learning package.
- **NaCTSO guidance videos** and protective-security advice.
- **Campaign toolkits** for protective-security awareness campaigns.
- **Bulletins** with current-state threat and guidance updates.
- **ACT online reporting form** and confidential hotline access.
- **Emergency response and post-incident guidance** for organisations facing a CT incident.

### Who runs it

- **Operator:** NaCTSO, the national policing CT unit.
- **Delivery partners:** Home Office; Pool Reinsurance.
- **Wider ecosystem:** Counter Terrorism Policing.
