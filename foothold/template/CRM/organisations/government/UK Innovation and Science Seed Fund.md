# UK Innovation and Science Seed Fund

Early-stage government investment fund focused on commercialising public-sector research. Co-invests alongside private capital in spin-outs from public research organisations including Dstl, the National Physical Laboratory, the British Geological Survey, and similar bodies.

For defence-tech SMEs the Seed Fund matters where a defence-relevant capability has originated in a public-sector research environment and now needs scale-up capital.
