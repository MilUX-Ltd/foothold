# Cabinet Office

## Summary

Coordinates cross-government security policy. Houses the National Security Secretariat (coordinates security and intelligence issues of strategic importance and supports the National Security Adviser and National Security Council) and the Joint Intelligence Organisation (produces independent all-source assessments on national security and foreign policy).

## Key Units

- [[CRM/organisations/government/National Security Secretariat|National Security Secretariat]]
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Resilience Directorate|Resilience Directorate]]
- **Joint Intelligence Organisation**
- **UK Security Vetting (UKSV)**: part of the Cabinet Office; runs national security vetting. See [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Security Clearance Primer|Security Clearance Primer]].
- [[CRM/organisations/government/Crown Commercial Service|Government Commercial Agency (formerly Crown Commercial Service)]]: executive agency sponsored by the Cabinet Office.

## July 2026 machinery of government changes

- **AI strategy, public-sector AI adoption and the AI Security Institute** move to the Cabinet Office from the dissolving DSIT. A new AI Taskforce sits in the Office for the Prime Minister and the Cabinet.
- **A new OneGov Delivery Agency** will be set up in the Cabinet Office family to run operational services at arm's length, including Security Vetting.
- **The Investment Security Unit** (National Security and Investment Act screening) moves from the Cabinet Office to [[CRM/organisations/government/BIST|BIST]].
- The [[CRM/organisations/government/National Security Online Information Team|National Security Online Information Team (NSOIT)]] was part of DSIT, not the Cabinet Office; its parent is under review following the July 2026 changes.

Source: gov.uk [Machinery of government changes fact sheet](https://www.gov.uk/government/news/machinery-of-government-changes-fact-sheet) (22 July 2026).
