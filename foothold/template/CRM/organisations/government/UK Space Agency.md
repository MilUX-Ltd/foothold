# UK Space Agency

## Summary

The UK Space Agency nurtures the UK space sector: investors, scientists, engineers, academia, and industry. gov.uk describes it as part of the [[CRM/organisations/government/BIST|Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST)]], which took over from DSIT in the July 2026 machinery of government changes.

The UK space sector generates around £18.9bn in annual income and employs approximately 52,000 people.

## Remit

- Civil space policy and strategy for the UK.
- Funding for UK space research and capability development.
- Co-ordination with international partners (ESA, NASA, allied space agencies).
- Industrial-base support for the UK space sector.

The Agency leads on civil space; military space is led through the MOD's space command and capability bodies (with the National Space Operations Centre as the joint civil-military coordination point).

## Current strategy

The [UK Space Strategy](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/uk-space-strategy) (8 September 2026; UK Space Agency, BIST and MOD) commits £7.8bn of investment to 2030. Read it for civil and defence space priorities in one place. The Defence Investment Plan sets defence space spending separately; see [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Defence Investment Plan 2026|Defence Investment Plan 2026]].

## Routes for engagement

- Watch UK Space Agency competitions and grant calls.
- Engage via the Space Technology Solutions framework on the Government Commercial Agency (formerly Crown Commercial Service).
- Attend UK Space Conference and other named UK Space Agency events.
