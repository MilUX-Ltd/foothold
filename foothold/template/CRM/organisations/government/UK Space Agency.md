# UK Space Agency

## Summary

The UK Space Agency nurtures the UK space ecosystem — a network of investors, scientists, engineers, academia, and industry. Arms-length body of DSIT (Department for Science, Innovation and Technology).

The UK space sector generates around £18.9bn in annual income and employs approximately 52,000 people.

## Remit

- Civil space policy and strategy for the UK.
- Funding for UK space research and capability development.
- Co-ordination with international partners (ESA, NASA, allied space agencies).
- Industrial-base support for the UK space sector.

The Agency leads on civil space; military space is led through the MOD's space command and capability bodies (with the National Space Operations Centre as the joint civil-military coordination point).

## Routes for engagement

- Watch UK Space Agency competitions and grant calls.
- Engage via the Space Technology Solutions framework on Crown Commercial Service.
- Attend UK Space Conference and other named UK Space Agency events.
