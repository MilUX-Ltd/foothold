# Crown Commercial Service (CCS)

## Summary

Government procurement body. Manages the central UK government procurement framework portfolio used by departments and arm's-length bodies. Many MOD frameworks are routed through CCS.

## Frameworks managed (Foothold coverage)

- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/G-Cloud|G-Cloud]] — Cloud services.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Digital Outcomes and Specialists (DOS)|Digital Outcomes and Specialists (DOS)]] — Digital outcomes, specialists, user research.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Technology Services (TCS)|Technology Services (TCS)]] — Technology services umbrella (TCS3).
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Digital and IT Professional Services (DIPS)|Digital and IT Professional Services (DIPS)]] — TCS3 sub-lot, c.£1.2bn ceiling.
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Neutral Vendor Framework for Innovation|Neutral Vendor Framework for Innovation]].
- [[Intelligence/defence-landscape/Frameworks/Space Technology Solutions|Space Technology Solutions]].
