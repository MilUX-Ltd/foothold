# Government Commercial Agency (GCA, formerly Crown Commercial Service)

> **Renamed.** On 1 April 2026 Crown Commercial Service (CCS) was combined with several Cabinet Office central commercial teams to form the **Government Commercial Agency (GCA)**, an executive agency sponsored by the Cabinet Office. This page keeps its old file name so existing links still resolve.

## Summary

The UK's largest public procurement agency. Manages the central government framework portfolio used by departments and arm's-length bodies. Many MOD buys are routed through its frameworks. GCA says existing CCS contracts and call-offs remain valid, and the legality of CCS frameworks and agreements does not change.
