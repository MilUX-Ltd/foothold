# UKRI — UK Research and Innovation

## Summary

UK's major research-funding body, sponsored by [[CRM/organisations/government/DSIT|DSIT]]. Brings together the seven Research Councils, Innovate UK, and Research England under a single funding strategy. Runs the UKRI Innovation Accelerator alongside its core research-council funding streams.

For defence-tech SMEs, UKRI is the principal civilian counterpart to MOD research funding. Many defence-relevant academic partnerships are routed through UKRI grants and Innovate UK programmes rather than directly through MOD.
