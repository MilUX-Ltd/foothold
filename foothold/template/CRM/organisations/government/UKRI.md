# UKRI (UK Research and Innovation)

## Summary

UK's major research-funding body, sponsored by the [[CRM/organisations/government/BIST|Department for Business, Innovation, Science and Trade (BIST)]] since the July 2026 machinery of government changes (previously DSIT). Brings together the seven Research Councils, Innovate UK, and Research England under a single funding strategy. Runs the UKRI Innovation Accelerator alongside its core research-council funding streams.

For defence-tech SMEs, UKRI is the principal civilian counterpart to MOD research funding. Many defence-relevant academic partnerships are routed through UKRI grants and Innovate UK programmes rather than directly through MOD.
