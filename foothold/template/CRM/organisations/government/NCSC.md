# NCSC — National Cyber Security Centre

## Summary

Part of GCHQ. Provides cyber security guidance, incident response, and threat intelligence to UK government, industry, and the public. Manages the Active Cyber Defence (ACD) programme and the Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus accreditation schemes (operated via certifying partners).

## What NCSC does

- Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus (the entry-level UK government-backed cyber accreditation schemes, required for many MOD-adjacent contracts).
- Active Cyber Defence (ACD) services protecting UK government and CNI.
- Threat intelligence and incident-response support.
- Guidance and standards for government, industry, and the public.
- Sector-specific advice for defence, finance, health, and other critical sectors.

## Relevance for defence SMEs

Most MOD and defence-adjacent contracts require Cyber Essentials at minimum, with Cyber Essentials Plus or higher (ISO 27001, IL2/IL3, JSP 440) required for more sensitive work. NCSC guidance is the entry point for understanding what's required.
