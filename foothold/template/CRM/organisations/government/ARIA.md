# ARIA — Advanced Research and Invention Agency

## Summary

UK R&D funding agency built to unlock scientific and technological breakthroughs. Empowers scientists and engineers with the resources and freedom to pursue breakthroughs at the edge of the possible. Many projects will fail by design; those that succeed are intended to generate substantial social and economic returns.

ARIA sits under DSIT (Department for Science, Innovation and Technology). Modelled in part on the US DARPA pattern but with its own UK-specific structure and governance.

## Programme model

ARIA funds Programme Directors who lead bold, hypothesis-driven research programmes. Each Programme Director runs their own portfolio of projects with significant autonomy.

## Routes for engagement

- Watch for open ARIA programme calls and Programme Director announcements.
- Engage directly with Programme Directors working in your technology domain.
- Apply to ARIA opportunities via the published application channels.
