# Homeland Security Group (HSG)

The Home Office executive directorate that owns counter-terrorism, critical-national-infrastructure protection, and the wider homeland-security policy portfolio. Established **2007** as the **Office for Security and Counter-Terrorism (OSCT)**; renamed **Homeland Security Group in 2021** to reflect a broader remit. Spent **c.£1.5bn** in 2022-23.

GOV.UK: [Home Office](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/home-office). NAO: [Home Office Departmental Overview (PDF, 2023)](https://www.nao.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/014017-Home-Office-Departmental-Overview-FINAL.pdf).

## Role in the landscape

HSG leads the UK's response to terrorism and homeland-security threats, working hand-in-glove with the security services (MI5, MI6) and Counter Terrorism Policing. The group reports up through the Home Secretary and the Minister of State for Security.

### Named responsibilities

- Exercise the UK's response to a terrorist incident.
- Develop counter-terrorism legislation (UK and overseas-effect).
- Provide security measures and protection packages for public figures.
- Ensure the UK's Critical National Infrastructure (CNI) is protected from attack (physical and cyber).
- Ensure the UK is prepared to deal with a CBRN release.
- Liaise with government and emergency services during terrorist incidents or CT operations.
