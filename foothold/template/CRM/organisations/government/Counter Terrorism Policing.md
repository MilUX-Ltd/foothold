# Counter Terrorism Policing (CTP)

The UK's **national policing network for counter-terrorism**. Not a single legal entity but a coordinated network of regional Counter Terrorism Units (CTUs) and a national CTP headquarters, working alongside MI5, the National Crime Agency, and the wider Homeland Security Group. The Metropolitan Police's Specialist Operations Command historically anchors the national structure.

Website: [counterterrorism.police.uk](https://www.counterterrorism.police.uk/). NaCTSO / ProtectUK (CTP's protective-security arm): [protectuk.police.uk](https://www.protectuk.police.uk/about).

## Role in the landscape

CTP delivers the operational CT response across the UK: investigations, arrests, public order at CT incidents, protective security advice via NaCTSO, and the ACT (Action Counters Terrorism) public-engagement programme. It operates inside the MI5-led [[CRM/organisations/government/Joint Terrorism Analysis Centre|JTAC]] assessment framework but is the policing-side delivery.

### National network

- **CTP HQ** — national coordination function.
- **Regional CTUs** — Counter Terrorism Units anchored on major forces (e.g. CTU North West, CTU South East, CTU West Midlands).
- **NaCTSO** — National Counter Terrorism Security Office; runs [[CRM/organisations/government/ProtectUK|ProtectUK]] and the Counter Terrorism Security Advisor (CTSA) network.

### Industry-facing surfaces

- **NaCTSO / ProtectUK** for public-realm protective security guidance.
- **ACT (Action Counters Terrorism)** training products including ACT Awareness e-Learning.
- **Counter Terrorism Security Advisor (CTSA)** field deployments to venues and CNI sites.
- **Security and Policing** annual event (delivered jointly with JSaRC).
