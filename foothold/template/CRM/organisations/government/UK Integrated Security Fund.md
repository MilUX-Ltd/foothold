# UK Integrated Security Fund (UKISF)

The UK government's cross-departmental security funding pot. Replaced the **Conflict, Stability and Security Fund (CSSF)** on **1 April 2024** and absorbed the National Cyber Programme and the Economic Deterrence Initiative (EDI), giving it a wider remit than the CSSF had.

GOV.UK: [UK Integrated Security Fund](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-integrated-security-fund); [About us](https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/uk-integrated-security-fund/about). DevTracker: [UKISF programme summary](https://devtracker.fcdo.gov.uk/programme/GB-GOV-53-UKISF/summary).

## Role in the landscape

UKISF uses a **whole-of-government approach** to fund creative responses to the most complex national security challenges set out in the **Integrated Review Refresh 2023 (IRR)**. Operating in over 90 countries and territories, it blends Official Development Assistance (ODA) with non-ODA funding to deliver both domestic and international programmes.

### Governance

- Managed by the **Integrated Security Fund Unit** inside the Cabinet Office.
- Ministerial governing board reporting to the **National Security Council**.
- Accountable to Parliament via the Cabinet Office minister.

### What it funds

The fund deliberately spans the soft-power / hard-security spectrum: conflict prevention and stability programming (the CSSF legacy), cyber capability building (the National Cyber Programme legacy), and economic deterrence and sanctions effectiveness (the EDI legacy). Programmes are categorised under a small set of cross-cutting national-security priorities derived from the IRR.

### Why it matters to defence-tech founders

UKISF is not a direct SME funding route in the way DASA or DTEP are. It is, however, the largest cross-government national-security funding pot and the upstream commissioner for a meaningful chunk of programmes that defence-tech and security-tech SMEs ultimately deliver under. Worth tracking via DevTracker for upcoming priority areas.

### Scale

UKISF combines what was previously a ~£1.3bn-a-year CSSF programme with the National Cyber Programme and EDI. Annual departmental allocations are published in HM Treasury supplementary estimates.
