## What K39 Consulting does

K39 Consulting (K39C) is an Australian defence consultancy. The company combines engineering, governance, procurement and commercial advisory work to help Commonwealth agencies and prime contractors deliver complex Defence programmes. K39C's client base spans the Guided Weapons and Explosive Ordnance (GWEO) enterprise, maritime sustainment, explosive ordnance handling, and other high-risk acquisition settings where decisions carry long-term operational, safety and sovereign consequences.

The company positions itself at the governance and decision-making layer of programme delivery rather than as a traditional engineering or project-delivery shop. Its services bring together engineering assurance, ASDEFCON contracting, supplier risk management, systems safety, S3, and governance aligned to the Australian Defence Industry Security Program (DISP), and apply them across the full acquisition and sustainment lifecycle.

K39 Consulting's team is drawn from Defence, the Capability Acquisition and Sustainment Group (CASG), the Australian Public Service, academia and industry. The company is certified under the Australian Veteran Owned Business (AVOB) scheme.

In September 2025 K39 Consulting incorporated a UK arm, K39 Consulting Ltd, headed by Managing Director [[Kevin Watkins|Kevin Watkins]], to build out a UK Defence Professional Advisory business.

## Services

Defence capability advisory, complex procurement, acquisition and sustainment, logistics and supply chain, project and programme management, systems engineering, integrated logistics support, engineering assurance, governance and assurance, commercial advisory, supplier risk management, systems safety, S3, ASDEFCON contracting, DISP-aligned governance.

## Sector positioning

K39 Consulting holds Level 1 accreditation under the Australian Government Defence Industry Security Program (DISP) and is a certified Australian Veteran Owned Business (AVOB). The company's Australian work focuses on the Guided Weapons and Explosive Ordnance (GWEO) enterprise and the Maritime domain, with presence across Canberra, Sydney and Melbourne. Its newly established UK arm extends the same defence advisory model into the UK market.
