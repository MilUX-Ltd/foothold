# inink Ltd

> **Foothold contributor.** inink is the defence engagement and communications specialists who informed much of Foothold's defence-sector context and shaped its positioning. Public-facing reference profile.

- **Website:** [inink.io](https://www.inink.io)

- **Region:** South East
- **Business type:** Management Consultancy Services
- **Co-founders:** [[James Pryor|James Pryor]] (Director), [[Gabi Brooks|Gabrielle Brooks]] (Director)

## What inink does

inink positions itself as defence engagement specialists "operating wherever deep tech meets defence and national security". They slot in alongside their clients' existing commercial functions, working on:

- Brand development and product marketing strategy.
- BD narratives and user briefs.
- Web copy and long-form content (sales demos, film scripts, press releases, thinkpieces).
- Campaign ideation, tone of voice definition, and core messaging.

Their differentiator is SC-cleared support for BD, sales, and MarComms functions that defence companies can hire rather than buy. They have worked with nearly 40 defence-focused start-ups, SMEs, and primes.

## Notable work

- **The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Valley of Death.** An interactive guide for SMEs navigating UK defence procurement, mapping 200+ organisations, programmes, and frameworks across the UK defence innovation landscape. Updated with input from Defence Procurement, MoD, and the National Armaments Director Group. Created by Gabi Brooks.

## Foothold context

inink were early conversation partners during Foothold's design phase. Their feedback on positioning, value proposition articulation, and the "bring your own ingredients" framing shaped how Foothold presents itself to defence-sector founders. Gabi's *Valley of Death* infographic was a direct input into Foothold's open-source content layer.
