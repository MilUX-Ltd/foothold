## What Defence IQ does

Defence iQ is one of the world's largest defence events and media organisations. The business runs around 40-50 international conferences per year and an active digital publishing operation alongside them: the Defence Digest weekly newsletter, articles, interviews, market reports, whitepapers, infographics, post-show reports, webinars, and podcasts.

Conferences are commercially run (paid delegate model with sponsor tiers, typically 2/3/4 Star Partners, and exhibitions). The publishing operation sits alongside and feeds into the events. Membership is free and provides full access to the content library plus discounted delegate pricing on events.

## For founders considering engagement

Defence iQ's audience overlaps strongly with the kind of senior military leadership, programme owners, and prime / non-traditional industry buyers most defence SMEs want to be in front of. Four routes worth assessing:

- **Free membership.** The lowest-cost option. Subscribe to Defence Digest and the content library; lift relevant pieces into your own reading and reference loop.
- **Conference attendance as a delegate.** Higher cost, but the most direct exposure to a single domain's leadership for two to three days. IAV, International Fighter, and Modern Warfare are the largest.
- **Speaking or contributing.** Defence iQ run a "Become a Contributor" path at [defenceiq.com/become-a-contributor](https://www.defenceiq.com/become-a-contributor) for whitepapers, articles and interviews. Useful as a thought-leadership lane.
- **Sponsorship and exhibition.** The Star Partner model (typically 2/3/4 Star tiers on each event) gives speaking slots, stand presence, and delegate access. Discuss with the Defence iQ partnerships team via [defenceiq.com/partners](https://www.defenceiq.com/partners).
