## What Policy Exchange does

Policy Exchange describes itself as the UK's leading think tank and an educational charity, with a mission to develop and promote new policy ideas for better public services, a stronger society, and a more dynamic economy.

Policy areas the think tank covers include crime and justice, demography and social integration, economics and social policy, education, environment and energy, foreign policy, gender and equality, government and politics, housing and urban regeneration, security and extremism, technology policy, and transport.

Policy ideas Policy Exchange has had adopted as UK government policy include free schools, police and crime commissioners, garden villages, and protections for the Armed Forces from prosecution under human rights laws.

## Defence and security positioning

Policy Exchange's defence and security work has been influential. The 2013 publication *The Fog of Law* argued that the increasing application of civilian norms to military conflict, and the resulting rise in legal claims against the MOD, risked undermining the effectiveness of the armed forces. The line of argument fed into subsequent government policy on legal protections for service personnel.

In April 2019, Policy Exchange launched its [Space Policy Unit](https://policyexchange.org.uk/space/), described as the first of its kind at any UK think tank.
