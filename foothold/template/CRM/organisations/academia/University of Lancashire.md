# University of Lancashire (UCLan)

## Company Details

- **Website:** [lancashire.ac.uk](https://lancashire.ac.uk).
- **Main campus:** Preston, Lancashire.
- **Sector:** Higher education and research.
- **Defence role:** **Core academic partner with the National Cyber Force**, anchored at Samlesbury, Lancashire.

## What the University of Lancashire Does

UCLan sits at the heart of one of the UK's most important defence, aerospace, and cyber regions. The University works with SMEs, government, and the major Lancashire defence employers, with research and education focused on cyber security, applied digital, and engineering disciplines that map directly onto the regional defence cluster.

## National Cyber Force partnership

UCLan was [announced as a Government partner for the new National Cyber Force](https://www.lancashire.ac.uk/news/uclan-announced-as-government-partner-for-new-national-cyber-force), based at **Samlesbury, Lancashire**, alongside Lancaster University. The NCF brings approximately **£5 billion of investment** into the region. UCLan's role is described as central to "establishing Lancashire as an undisputed national leader for cyber and security-related capabilities".

The wider partnership behind securing Samlesbury as the permanent NCF home is:

- **Universities of Central Lancashire (UCLan) and Lancaster.**
- **BAE Systems** (around 10,000 staff in Lancashire; lead on Typhoon, F-35, Tempest, and digital defence solutions via the Digital Intelligence business).
- **Lancashire County Council.**
- **Lancashire Enterprise Partnership.**

See [Lancashire announces new strategic partnership to maximise County's 'once in a generation' cyber opportunity](https://news.lancashire.gov.uk/news/lancashire-announces-new-strategic-partnership-to-maximise-countys-once-in-a-generation-cyber-opportunity).

## Cyber Solutions Centre and Preston Cyber Zone

UCLan operates a [Cyber Solutions Centre](https://www.lancashire.ac.uk/research/institutes/fdti/centre-for-digital-life/cyber-solutions-centre) within the Centre for Digital Life. The Centre works closely with the University's **Preston Cyber Zone** to support the developing regional cyber-skills pipeline into the NCF and the BAE Systems site.

## Aerospace adjacency

BAE Systems' Lancashire footprint (Warton, Samlesbury, and around 10,000 staff overall) anchors the aerospace side of the regional cluster, with the Typhoon, F-35, and Tempest programmes all running through Lancashire sites. UCLan is one of the academic feeds into that skilled-engineer pipeline.
