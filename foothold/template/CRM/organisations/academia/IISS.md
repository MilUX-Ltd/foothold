# International Institute for Strategic Studies (IISS)

## Company details

- **Website:** [iiss.org](https://www.iiss.org/); [Meet our team](https://www.iiss.org/people).
- **Sector:** Strategic studies think tank.
- **HQ:** Arundel House, London (since 1997).
- **Founded:** 1958, as the Institute for Strategic Studies (ISS). Renamed IISS in 1971.
- **Director-General and CEO:** Bastian Giegerich.
- **Executive Chairman:** Sir John Chipman.
- **Self-described:** "The world's leading authority on political-military conflict."

## What IISS does

IISS conducts and publishes research on political-military conflict, defence economics, deterrence, arms control, and the structural and capability balance between states' armed forces. The Institute hosts major dialogues and conferences (Shangri-La Dialogue, Manama Dialogue) and is known for the depth of its data-driven defence analysis.

## The Military Balance

The Institute's flagship publication. Published annually since 1959. Provides an open-source assessment of the armed forces and equipment inventories of over 170 countries, with accompanying defence economics data. Widely treated as the open-source reference for state-level armed forces data; routinely cited by MOD, FCDO, allied governments, and global media.

[Military Balance+](https://www.iiss.org/the-military-balance-plus/) is the online product version with deeper data access for subscribers.

## Annual dialogues

- **Shangri-La Dialogue** (Singapore) — Asia-Pacific security.
- **Manama Dialogue** (Bahrain) — Middle East security.
- **Geo-economics Strategy Group** — and other named programmes.
