## What Solent University does

Solent is the university formerly known as Southampton Solent University, a vocationally-oriented institution with notable strengths in maritime, sport, media, and digital subjects. The **Business and Civic Development Unit** is the institutional engagement function, owning the Business and Civic Development Strategy, alumni and civic engagement, business development, fundraising and philanthropy. Solent holds the NCCPE Engage Silver Watermark and University of Sanctuary status, and has launched a Civic University agreement and Industry Impact boards.

## Defence and security context

Solent sits inside the SCRDSC (South Central Regional Defence and Security Cluster) catchment alongside the wider Solent maritime and aerospace ecosystem. The April 2026 SCRDSC Steering Group discussed a Solent Freeport opportunity that the university's Business and Civic Development function is adjacent to.
