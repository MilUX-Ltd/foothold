## What Southampton does

The University of Southampton is a Russell Group research university based in southern England. Strong activity bases in research, education, medicine, engineering, computer science, law, and marine science. The Enterprise and Knowledge Exchange function is the principal route by which the university engages with industry partners.

## Defence-and-security context

The university sits on the [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/clusters/South Central Regional Defence and Security Cluster|SCRDSC]] Steering Group as the academic representative for the South Central regional defence and security cluster. Research strengths in marine, electronics, photonics, and AI are directly relevant to MOD and broader defence-tech ecosystems.
