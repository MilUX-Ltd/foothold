## What the University of Portsmouth does

The University of Portsmouth is a public research university on the south coast of England with a student population in the region of 28,000 and a strong applied-research orientation. Research strengths span cosmology and gravitation (Institute of Cosmology and Gravitation), forensic and security sciences (Centre for Cybercrime and Economic Crime), maritime and naval architecture (consonant with the city's naval heritage), creative technologies, and a growing defence-and-security research footprint built around the city's Royal Navy and BAE Systems Maritime presence.

The Faculty of Technology and Faculty of Science and Health host most of the engineering, computing, cyber and applied-physics work that intersects with the regional defence cluster. **Research and Innovation Services (RIS)** is the central function brokering academic-industry collaboration, KTPs (Knowledge Transfer Partnerships), Innovate UK and UKRI bids, and consultancy-style engagements with industry partners.

## Defence research surface

Portsmouth hosts the **Centre of Excellence in Defence, Risk and Resilience (CEDRR)** — see [[CRM/organisations/mod/CoE in Defence Risk and Resilience|CEDRR]]. The Centre is the most visible MOD-recognised academic Centre of Excellence at Portsmouth and the substantive defence-research interface for the university.

## Defence-and-security context

The university sits inside the SCRDSC (South Central Regional Defence and Security Cluster) catchment alongside MOD/Dstl Hampshire-and-Surrey activity, BAE Systems Portsmouth Naval Base, the wider Aurora Provider Network, and the cluster of Solent-based defence engineering firms (Roke at Romsey, BAE Maritime at Portsmouth, QinetiQ at Cody Technology Park).
