# University of Liverpool

## Company Details

- **Website:** [liverpool.ac.uk](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/); [Digital Innovation Facility](https://digitalinnovationfacility.com/); [DIF on liverpool.ac.uk](https://www.liverpool.ac.uk/institute-of-digital-engineering-autonomous-systems/facilities/digital-innovation-facility/).
- **Sector:** Higher education and research; Russell Group university.

## What the University of Liverpool Does

A Russell Group, research-intensive UK university with a substantial digital, robotics, autonomous-systems, and engineering research footprint. The university hosts the **Institute of Digital Engineering and Autonomous Systems (IDEAS)**, the umbrella research institute for digital twin, autonomy, robotics, and data-science work, which directly intersects with the wider UK defence-tech R&D space.

## Digital Innovation Facility (DIF)

The university operates the **Digital Innovation Facility (DIF)**, a £12.7 million Centre of Excellence in emerging digital technologies. Opened in May 2022 by the Mayor of the Liverpool City Region, Steve Rotheram, alongside tech entrepreneur Sir Robin Saxby. The DIF is located on the University's city-centre campus and provides a purpose-built environment to support collaborations and partnerships between academics, industry, and other organisations in **computer and data science, robotics, and engineering**.

DIF research activities are supported by a number of funding agencies, including the European Commission, EPSRC, and **the Ministry of Defence via [[CRM/organisations/mod/NAD Group/National Armaments Innovation (NA-Innov)|Dstl]]**. This is the substantive defence-research touchpoint at Liverpool.
