# University of Manchester

## Company details

- **Website:** [manchester.ac.uk](https://www.manchester.ac.uk).
- **LinkedIn:** [The University of Manchester](https://www.linkedin.com/school/the-university-of-manchester/).
- **Sector:** Higher education / research-intensive university.
- **HQ:** Oxford Road, Manchester, England.
- **Founded:** 2004 in its current form, through the merger of Victoria University of Manchester (1851) and UMIST (1824).
- **Size:** approximately 12,000 staff; approximately 46,000 students; Russell Group member.

## What the University of Manchester does

A large, research-intensive UK Russell Group university with a strong engineering and physical sciences base, including major activity in embedded systems, sensors, radar, signal processing, and AI/data. Notable for splitting the atom and isolating graphene; 25 Nobel laureates across its constituent institutions.

The Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (within the School of Engineering) hosts sensors and image signal processing groups doing work adjacent to defence research, including UAV-borne 3D sub-surface radar imaging research.

## Defence research surface

Manchester hosts the **Henry Royce Institute**, the UK's national centre for advanced materials research and lead partner of the Dstl-funded [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Materials CoE|Defence Materials CoE (DMEx)]]. That partnership is the substantive defence-research interface for the university.
