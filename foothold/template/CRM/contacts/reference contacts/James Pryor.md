## About

James styles himself a "DefTech sherpa". He authored *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Valley of Death*, an interactive guide for SMEs navigating UK defence procurement, updated with input from Defence Procurement, MoD, and the National Armaments Director Group. Alongside Gabi Brooks, he has worked with nearly 40 defence-focused start-ups, SMEs, and primes, supporting BD, sales, and marketing teams.

inink's positioning is SC-cleared support for BD, sales, and marketing functions that defence companies can hire rather than buy. Their wheelhouse is articulating complex technologies in ways that land with defence decision-makers, investors, and industry partners.
