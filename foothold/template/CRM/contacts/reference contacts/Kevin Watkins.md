## Background

Kevin Watkins is a Royal Navy Marine Engineer veteran with deep capability acquisition and sustainment experience, now leading the UK arm of an Australian defence consultancy. He served almost 31 years in the Royal Navy as a Marine Engineer (1985 to 2016), with roles including Marine Engineering Officer on HMS Liverpool, Head of Customer Liaison Cell at DES Ships in Bristol, Delegated Design Acceptance Authority on HMS Illustrious, and Delegated Acceptance Authority on the Queen Elizabeth Aircraft Carrier Project.

After leaving the Royal Navy, Kevin joined Nova Systems International, first as Principal P3M Consultant (June 2021 to June 2023) and then as Capability Services Programme Manager (May 2023 to April 2026). In April 2026 he moved full-time into K39 Consulting as Managing Director of the UK arm.

In January 2026 Kevin took on the concurrent role of Deputy Chair of the [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/clusters/South Central Regional Defence and Security Cluster|SCRDSC]] steering group.

Between 2020 and 2023, Kevin also undertook an Atlantic rowing challenge in support of Action Medical Research, documented at raringtorow.com.
