## About

Gabi works alongside James Pryor at inink, where together they have supported nearly 40 defence-focused start-ups, SMEs, and primes with BD, sales, and marketing communications. Her flagship work is *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Valley of Death*, a comprehensive interactive infographic mapping 200+ organisations, programmes, and frameworks across the UK defence innovation procurement landscape.

Her background spans brand development, product marketing strategy, BD narrative, web copy, long-form content (sales demos, film scripts, press releases), and campaign ideation. She is particularly strong on tone of voice definition and core messaging for defence-sector communications.
