# European Defence Tech Hub

> Note on spelling. The brand uses the American "Defense" throughout — domain `eurodefense.tech`, LinkedIn `European Defense Tech`, all event names. The British "Defence" is used in this page for narrative; quoted titles and URLs retain the brand's own spelling.

## Summary

The European Defence Tech Hub (EDTH) is a pan-European network operating Europe's largest defence-technology hackathon series. The stated mission is "to bring more talent into Europe's defense technology sector, accelerate defense innovation, and drive deployment at scale." EDTH connects builders, operators, defence agencies, primes, and investors through a recurring programme of intense weekend hackathons, online demo days, webinars, regional Defence Tech Weeks, and the planned New Defense Summit.

The operating cadence is aggressive. EDTH ran six hackathons in February 2026 alone (Brussels, London, Athens, Tallinn, and two co-organised editions) and has signalled 50+ events across 2026. By mid-2026 it had also delivered Naval Defense Tech in Karlskrona, Counter-Drone in Rzeszów, Stockholm, BuildArena with TECHARENA / Project Europe / Lovable, and Kyiv Defense Tech Week with 1,200+ attendees from 30+ countries.

External: [eurodefense.tech](https://eurodefense.tech); [event calendar on Luma](https://lu.ma/eurodefensetech); [LinkedIn company page](https://www.linkedin.com/company/european-defense-tech/); newsletter at [new-defense.com](https://www.new-defense.com).

## Who it's for

The visible audience splits into four populations curated together by the EDTH programme:

- **Builders and engineering teams.** University students, recent graduates, working engineers, and indie defence-tech founders. They form the bulk of the hackathon attendance.
- **Defence-tech founders and CEOs.** Often hacker-graduates returning to the network as company leads. The Hub deliberately optimises for the founder loop: hacker today, founder next year, partner the year after.
- **Operators and military end-users.** Active service members, veterans, and recently-serving personnel from European and Ukrainian forces. They bring real battlefield feedback into the room.
- **Investors, primes, and government users.** Sponsors, jury, partner organisations. The Hub uses hackathons as a curated discovery surface for this group.

The hackathon weekends are open by application. Mentor and jury seats are invitation-only. Year-round community access is via paid subscription.

## What it offers

Visible from the public site, Luma calendar, and the network's own posts:

- **The European Defence Tech Hackathon series.** Three-day weekend hackathons across major European cities, with parallel London / Berlin / Stockholm / Vilnius / Athens / Tallinn / Brussels / Warsaw / Munich / Hamburg / Milan / Paris / Kyiv editions plus specialist Counter-Drone, Naval, and Critical Infrastructure formats.
- **Defence Tech Weeks.** Multi-day, single-city events that stack a hackathon, conference, side events, and partner programming. Prague (Jun 2025), Kyiv (early 2026 with 1,200+ attendees), with Dutch Defense Week in November 2026 confirmed in partnership with the European Defence Innovation Forum (EDIF).
- **Virtual Demo Days.** Online showcases between live events, themed (e.g. front-line medicine and casualty evacuation in April 2026).
- **Webinars and member sessions.** Procurement, "escaping the project loop", country-specific routes to market (e.g. Dutch MoD), counter-drone workshops.
- **The New Defense Summit.** Planned EDTH-hosted summit announced for 2026.
- **The New Defense Post newsletter.** Weekly defence-tech news brief co-authored by Benjamin Wolba and Paolo Trecate.
- **A Discord community.** Channels covering announcements, resources, events, idea lab, team formation for hackathons, and regional hubs (London, Paris).

## How to evaluate engaging

- **Defence-tech founder fit.** If you are a defence-tech founder building anything from counter-UAS to naval autonomy to front-line medical evacuation, the EDTH hackathon weekends and the year-round community surface are designed for your population. The Hub explicitly recruits founders from its own hacker graduates.
- **Mentor / jury fit.** Mentor and jury seats are invitation-only and curated. Surfacing into the mentor pool tends to flow through an existing mentor, a Hub team member, or a partner organisation.
- **Sponsor / partner fit.** EDTH co-hosts with sponsoring partners across the European defence-tech ecosystem (Snake Island Institute, BRAVE1, TYTAN Technologies, Fire Point, STARK, Quantum Systems, Project Europe, EY, Lovable, TECHARENA, Marine Technology Center of Sweden, EDIF). Sponsor conversations sit alongside the hackathon programme rather than as a separate sales surface.
- **Investor fit.** Hackathon Demo Day juries pull in venture investors and primes alongside operators. Investor relationships flow from being in the room rather than from a separate investor track.
- **Cadence.** The London edition runs roughly three times a year. The wider calendar is updated rolling on Luma at [lu.ma/eurodefensetech](https://lu.ma/eurodefensetech).
