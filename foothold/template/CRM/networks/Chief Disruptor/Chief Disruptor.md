# Chief Disruptor

## Summary

Chief Disruptor is a UK membership community for business and technology leaders. The operating company is **Nimbus Ninety Ltd** (founded 2005, Companies House 06803745); the "Chief Disruptor" brand sits over the wider Nimbus Ninety community. The community brings together senior leaders from global blue-chip companies, fast-growing challenger brands, and the UK public sector around a "Connect, Learn, Disrupt" framing.

The Chief Disruptor brand has a clearly visible **defence sub-brand, Chief Disruptor Defence**, that is the most relevant entry point for defence-sector founders. The wider community is technology-leadership-and-transformation oriented, but the defence-side activity is sufficient on its own to justify paying attention.

External: [chiefdisruptor.com](https://www.chiefdisruptor.com/); [Membership page](https://www.chiefdisruptor.com/membership); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/chiefdisruptor/).

## Who it's for

Per the [Membership page](https://www.chiefdisruptor.com/membership), Chief Disruptor operates two tiers:

- **Associate Membership** — for those with "a professional or academic interest in business leadership and disruption, or the capability to support our Full Members through their business transformation". The accessible tier; broad eligibility.
- **Full Membership** — "C-level executives, directors, heads, or equivalent senior leadership positions who have the ability to bring about disruption or technological change within their organisations". Includes early notification of activities, pre-release of reports, and Full-Member-only sessions.

Membership pricing is not on the public surface. Both tiers are application-based.

Most plausibly useful to:

- Defence-sector founders who want to be in the room when senior UK military and MOD digital-transformation conversations happen.
- Companies whose customers include UK CIOs and CTOs across blue-chip and public sector.
- SMEs interested in the MOD Office for Small Business Growth EOI track (Chief Disruptor has been hosting the EOI surface for the initial cohort of up to 30).
- Operators who want a venue for thought-leadership content with senior reach.

## What it offers

Per the public [About](https://www.chiefdisruptor.com/about) and [Membership](https://www.chiefdisruptor.com/membership) pages:

- **Roundtable discussions.**
- **Expert speaker-led activities.**
- **Quarterly networking activities.**

Conference programme visible at 2026-05-23:

- **Defence Strategic Communications Conference**, 9 June 2026 — "New Narratives for a New World Order? The Challenge for Middle Powers".

## Defence credentials

Chief Disruptor is an [Armed Forces Covenant ERS Gold](https://www.armedforcescovenant.gov.uk/) employer (2022). The Members' Advisory Board, publicly listed on the About page, includes serving senior military leadership — notably **Lt Gen Sir Tom Copinger-Symes KCB CBE, Deputy Commander UK Strategic Command** — alongside C-suite from RS Group, NatWest, PepsiCo, Avon Cosmetics, Pier Health Group, Flo Health, Shift, and The Portfolio Collective. The mix is overwhelmingly senior civilian + one serving Lieutenant General. That mix is the network's calling card.

Recent and forthcoming defence-relevant activity visible on the public site:

- MOD Office for Small Business Growth — Expression of Interest hosted on Chief Disruptor's insights surface, calling SMEs into the initial cohort of up to 30.
- Public commentary on the 2025 Defence Industrial Strategy and its SME-impact question (the "UK-based" vs "UK-owned" / "UK-headquartered" redefinition).
- Army Reserve "Too Good To Leave" Senior Soldier Entry webinar series.
- Defence Strategic Communications Conference (9 June 2026).
- DAIC Connect 2027.

## How to evaluate joining

- **Defence-only filter.** If you're scoping defence work specifically, decide whether the defence vertical activity (one major conference a year plus the EOI-style SME hosting and the Army Reserve content) is worth the application time on its own. The wider Chief Disruptor activity is general business / tech and may dilute attention.
- **Application is the gating step.** Both tiers go through application, so the meaningful action is the application form on the [Membership page](https://www.chiefdisruptor.com/membership). Public pricing is not advertised.
- **Newsletter as a free entry.** The Chief Disruptor Insights newsletter is a no-cost way to get visibility on the defence-vertical output (conferences, EOI calls, MOD-relevant commentary) before committing to a full application.
- **Member-led activity model.** Reports and roundtables are member-led. Active participation is the value driver; passive lurking will be much less useful than for a news-feed-style platform.
