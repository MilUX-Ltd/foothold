# Wales Defence and Security Cluster

*Clwstwr Amddiffyn a Diogelwch Cymru*

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** Wales DSC
- **Region:** All of Wales
- **Website:** [walesdsc.co.uk](https://walesdsc.co.uk/)
- **Contact:** Via [website contact form](https://walesdsc.co.uk/contact/)
- **Launched:** 9 March 2026, at a Welsh Government event with Welsh Secretary Jo Stevens and Cabinet Secretary Rebecca Evans

## Description

A collaborative network of Welsh companies, academia and government organisations operating across defence, national security and critical national infrastructure. Strengthens Wales as a defence and security ecosystem by improving collaboration, visibility and access to opportunities.

Membership is free and open to SMEs, primes, universities, engineering, cyber and digital specialists, and government-aligned organisations. Focus areas include autonomous capabilities, opto-electronics, cyber, semiconductors and aerospace systems.

## Publicly named co-chairs

- **Leanne Connor** — Co-chair, Thales UK
- **Stephanie Eden** — Co-chair, Airbus Defence and Space

Industry-led Steering Group of SMEs, prime contractors, technology specialists, academic institutions and advisory organisations.

## Publicly visible founding partners

Airbus Defence and Space, Thales UK, Welsh Government, UK MoD, the Wales Office, and CSconnected (compound semiconductors). Connects to Wales's eight universities.

## Context — Wales Defence Growth Deal

The cluster launched in lockstep with the **£50 million Wales Defence Growth Deal** (announced 9 March 2026), part of £250M UK-wide regional defence growth investment. Combined Welsh defence and aerospace sector at launch: 16,000 jobs, £3.7B turnover, £1.5B GVA.
