# North West Regional Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** NWRDSC
- **Region:** North West England
- **Website:** *Public website yet to be confirmed; LinkedIn presence active.*

## Description

The North West has a strong heritage in defence operating across five operational domains (Land, Sea, Air, Space, and Cyber). The sector interlinks and underpins large elements of the aerospace, maritime, nuclear, and digital industries in the region.

The region is home to leading defence companies and academic institutions, as well as advanced research capabilities that are collaborative with the sector. The North West is also the planned new home for the National Cyber Force, expected to bring economic stimulus and related benefits to the region.

## Focus areas

- Improving collaboration and knowledge sharing across defence primes, SMEs, academia, and government science groups.
- Supporting innovation and research to develop and accelerate new technologies and capabilities.
- Establishing effective routes for implementation and commercialisation of new defence-relevant innovations.
- Encouraging cross-over applications of new technology between defence and adjacent sectors.
