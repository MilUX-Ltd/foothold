# West Midlands Regional Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** WM RDSC
- **Region:** West Midlands (delivered by West Midlands Growth Company; covers the WMCA footprint)
- **Website:** [westmidsrdsc.co.uk](https://www.westmidsrdsc.co.uk/)
- **Launched:** 14 May 2026 at the UK Telecoms Lab (UKTL) in Solihull

## Description

A region-wide partnership aligning local defence capabilities with UK demand. Brings together industry, academia, government and the Armed Forces to boost collaboration and help organisations of all sizes innovate and grow. Free to join.

Supports delivery of the **Midlands Defence and Security Blueprint** and connects to the **Midlands Defence and Security Industry Council**.
