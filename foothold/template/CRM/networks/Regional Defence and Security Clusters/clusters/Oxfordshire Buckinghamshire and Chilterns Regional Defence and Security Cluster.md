# Oxfordshire, Buckinghamshire and Chilterns Regional Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** OBC RDSC
- **Region:** Oxfordshire, Buckinghamshire and the Chilterns, including parts of historic Berkshire
- **Website:** [obcrdsc.co.uk](https://obcrdsc.co.uk/)
- **LinkedIn:** [linkedin.com/company/obc-rdsc](https://www.linkedin.com/company/obc-rdsc/)
- **Contact:** Via [website contact form](https://obcrdsc.co.uk/contact/)
- **Launched:** 16 April 2024 (chair appointed December 2023)

## Description

OBC RDSC develops and exploits regional innovation and technology to create capabilities that enhance UK defence and security while increasing regional prosperity. It brings together defence and security organisations, government agencies, universities, research organisations and businesses, with a particular focus on innovative startups and SMEs.

Capability priorities span space, AI, cyber, quantum, uncrewed systems, energy systems, nuclear, lasers and photonics, advanced materials, defence medicine and defence education.

## Publicly named chair

- **Maj Gen (Retd) Carew L Wilks CB CBE CEng FIET** — Chair (appointed December 2023, named on the cluster website)

## Publicly visible founding partners

Harwell Campus (founding partner), STFC-UKRI, MOD DASA. Technology growth hubs at Harwell, Culham and Westcott. Signatory to the Women in Defence Charter.
