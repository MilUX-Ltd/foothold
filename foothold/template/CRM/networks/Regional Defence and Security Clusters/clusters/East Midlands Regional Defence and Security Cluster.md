# East Midlands Regional Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** EMRDSC
- **Region:** East Midlands (Nottingham/Nottinghamshire, Derby/Derbyshire, Leicester/Leicestershire and the wider East Midlands)
- **Website:** [emrdsc.co.uk](https://emrdsc.co.uk/)
- **LinkedIn:** [linkedin.com/company/emrdsc](https://www.linkedin.com/company/emrdsc/)
- **Email:** info@emrdsc.co.uk
- **Status:** Active

## Description

EMRDSC brings together academic institutions, government bodies, public sector agencies and the defence and security industry across the East Midlands. It aims to enhance military capabilities and drive economic growth working with the UK MoD, facilitating innovation testing, partnerships and growth at scale using the region's R&D capabilities and defence assets.

Three pillars: defence capability and capacity; economic and business development; skills, education and employment.

## Publicly visible partnerships

- Formal partnership with the **East Midlands Cyber Security Cluster (EMCSC)**, announced 2025
- Linked to the East Midlands Combined County Authority's Defence Office for Small Business Growth
- Sits within the **Midlands Defence and Security Industry Council** and the wider **Midlands Defence and Security Blueprint** alongside the West Midlands Combined Authority
