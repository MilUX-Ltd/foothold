## Description

The South Central Regional Defence and Security Cluster (SCRDSC) is run by industry to ease and encourage collaboration between supplier tiers, academia, and the MoD across the Hampshire, Surrey, West Sussex, and Isle of Wight region. The cluster unites industry leaders and aims to grow a community dedicated to developing innovative, long-term advancements to the security and protection of communities.

## Regional assets

The region benefits from a significant Armed Forces presence, being home to the Royal Navy and the British Army, as well as the UK's National Air Traffic Control Centre. Major MoD strategic suppliers based in the region include Airbus, Babcock, BAE Systems, QinetiQ, and Serco. Innovation hubs in the region include Space South Central, Maritime UK Solent, and the Farnborough Aerospace Consortium.
