# Three Counties Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** 3CRDSC (cluster); 3CDSG (group, historical); SDSC-UK (surviving annual event, formerly 3CDSE)
- **Region:** Herefordshire, Worcestershire and Gloucestershire
- **Website (legacy):** [3crdsc.co.uk](https://www.3crdsc.co.uk/) (landing page currently empty)
- **LinkedIn (expo entity):** [linkedin.com/company/the-three-counties-defence-and-security-expo](https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-three-counties-defence-and-security-expo)
- **Status:** Ceased. Membership activity was folded into the annual SDSC-UK convention from 2022; the public landing page is currently blank.

## Description (historical)

An association of defence and security SMEs in the Three Counties (Herefordshire, Worcestershire, Gloucestershire). Supplied technology and innovation to the military Specialist User, later expanding to UK-wide defence and security suppliers.

## Historical naming

- **2014** — Initiated by **Harrison Clark Rickerbys (HCR Law)** as the **Herefordshire Security and Defence Group (HSDG)**
- **2018** — Renamed **Three Counties Defence and Security Group (3CDSG)**
- **November 2022** — Renamed **Three Counties Regional Defence and Security Cluster (3CRDSC)**; membership activity absorbed by SDSC-UK

## Publicly named historical leadership

- **Richard Morgan** — President, 3CRDSC; initiated SDSC-UK
- **Harrison Clark Rickerbys Ltd** — founder and chief sponsor

## Territorial successor

No public source documents a formal territorial handover. Likely successors based on current cluster geographies:

- **South West RDSC** — likely to cover Gloucestershire
- **West Midlands RDSC** (launched 14 May 2026) — likely to cover Herefordshire and Worcestershire as part of WMCA's wider Midlands remit

Note: **OBC RDSC did not absorb the Three Counties territory** — OBC covers Oxfordshire, Buckinghamshire and the Chilterns.

## Surviving event

**SDSC-UK** (Specialist Defence & Security Convention UK, formerly 3CDSE) continues to run. SDSC-UK 2026 took place 3–4 February 2026.
