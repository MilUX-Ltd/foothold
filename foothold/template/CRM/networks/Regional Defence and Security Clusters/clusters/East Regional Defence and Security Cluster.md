# East Regional Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** ERDSC
- **Region:** Essex, Suffolk, Norfolk, Cambridgeshire, Hertfordshire, Bedfordshire
- **Website:** [eastrdsc.co.uk](https://www.eastrdsc.co.uk/)
- **Launched:** 5 June 2025 (at Lockheed Martin UK, Bedfordshire)

## Description

The East Regional Defence and Security Cluster (ERDSC) connects innovators, SMEs, and government to drive defence and security innovation across the East of England. It is a collaboration between DASA, industry participants, academic institutions, local authorities, and SMEs.

The cluster aims to drive innovation, support collaboration, and stimulate economic growth within the defence and security sectors across the eastern regions of the UK.

## Publicly visible founding members

Organisations publicly associated with the cluster's launch and early activity include Lockheed Martin UK, Airbus, Blue Bear, Space East, the University of Hertfordshire, and Anglia Ruskin University.
