# Yorkshire Regional Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** YRDSC
- **Region:** Yorkshire (currently anchored on the York and North Yorkshire Combined Authority footprint; full regional scope expected to extend across the wider Yorkshire region)
- **Website:** [yorkshirerdsc.co.uk](https://www.yorkshirerdsc.co.uk/) (expression-of-interest page only at time of entry)
- **Launch Date:** 10 June 2026
- **Status:** Pre-launch, in development

## Description

Supported by the Ministry of Defence through UK Defence Innovation (UKDI). The cluster will foster collaboration, accelerate innovation and help businesses bring new ideas to market through practical support and commercialisation activity. It is being set up as a collaboration between industry, academia, local government and the MoD.
