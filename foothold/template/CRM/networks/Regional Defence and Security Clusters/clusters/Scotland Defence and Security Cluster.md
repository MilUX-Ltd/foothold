## Description

A Scottish cluster is being scoped to parallel the English and Welsh RDSC model. Public discussions reference lessons learned from existing English clusters being applied to Scotland. The scoping work sits alongside the **£50 million Scotland Defence Growth Deal**, announced 12 March 2026 by Defence Minister Luke Pollard MP and the Secretary of State for Scotland, which focuses on maritime, space, quantum and advanced engineering.
