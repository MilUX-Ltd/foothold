# North East Regional Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** NERDSC
- **Region:** North East England
- **Website:** [nerdsc.co.uk](https://nerdsc.co.uk/)
- **Email:** enquiries@nerdsc.co.uk

## Description

The North East Regional Defence and Security Cluster (NERDSC) drives opportunity and innovation for North East SMEs. The region is home to a fast-growing defence and security community, bringing together businesses, government agencies, and universities active in applied research, skills development, and technological innovation.

With strong links to digital, chemical, maritime, advanced manufacturing, and offshore industries, the North East is positioned to support the UK's defence and security priorities. The North East Combined Authority is championing this ambition through its Interim Local Growth Plan, focused on investment, innovation, and high-value job creation.

## Regional statistics

- Around 900 jobs in the North East supported by MoD expenditure.
- Approximately 1 in 20 adults in the North East have served in the armed forces.
- Above-average concentration of firms in the wider defence and security sector.
- Around half a billion pounds in Innovate UK grant funding received by North East defence and security firms since 2002.
