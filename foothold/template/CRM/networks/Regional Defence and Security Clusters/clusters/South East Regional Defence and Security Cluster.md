# South East Regional Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** SE RDSC (provisional)
- **Region:** South East England — likely Kent and East Sussex (with possible overlap into parts of South London not covered by the London RDSC). Hampshire, Surrey, Isle of Wight and West Sussex are already covered by the [[CRM/networks/Regional Defence and Security Clusters/clusters/South Central Regional Defence and Security Cluster|South Central RDSC]].
- **Website:** None
- **LinkedIn:** None
- **Status:** Under consideration — no formal entity, no published steering group, no launch date

## Description

A South East RDSC has been listed as "under consideration" in publicly distributed cluster status briefings as of May 2026, but no public-facing entity has been announced. The South Central RDSC already covers a substantial portion of what could be called the South East; any new cluster would likely be carved out of the remainder (Kent / East Sussex) rather than overlap.

## Adjacent existing capability

- **South Central RDSC (SCRDSC)** — covers Hampshire, Surrey, Isle of Wight, West Sussex
- **Surrey Cyber Security Cluster** ([surreycyber.com](https://surreycyber.com/)) — likely seed organisation
