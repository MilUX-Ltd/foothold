# Northern Ireland Defence and Security Cluster

## Cluster details

- **Abbreviation:** NIDSC (provisional)
- **Region:** All of Northern Ireland
- **Website:** None yet
- **LinkedIn:** None yet (adjacent cluster: **NI Cyber** at [nicyber.tech](https://nicyber.tech/))
- **Target Launch:** January 2027
- **Status:** In preliminary development

## Description

A new cluster being scoped under the **£50 million Northern Ireland Defence Growth Deal**, announced on 22 April 2026 at Belfast Met College by Defence Minister Luke Pollard MP and NIO Minister Matthew Patrick.

The cluster will form part of a targeted programme to help SMEs and start-ups enter the defence supply chain, anchored by a new **Secure Innovation Hub** providing secure R&D facilities for early-stage businesses. Sits within the Strategic Defence Review's commitment to grow the UK defence industrial base.

## Publicly named announcement leads

- **Luke Pollard MP** — Minister for Defence Readiness and Industry, UK MoD
- **Matthew Patrick** — Northern Ireland Office Minister

## Publicly visible founding partners

- **UK MoD**
- **Northern Ireland Office**
- **Invest NI**
- **Belfast Met College** (launch venue)
- Likely academic anchor: **Centre for Secure Information Technologies (CSIT), Queen's University Belfast** and Belfast's Cyber-AI Hub

Existing NI defence procurement at announcement: more than £271M/year supporting around 900 jobs.
