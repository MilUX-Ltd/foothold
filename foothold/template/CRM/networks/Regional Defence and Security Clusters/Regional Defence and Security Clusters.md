# Regional Defence and Security Clusters

The Regional Defence and Security Clusters (RDSCs) are industry-led networks across the UK that bring together defence and security companies, SMEs, academia, research institutions, and government to enable innovation, collaboration, and economic growth in the defence and security sectors.

Each cluster is supported by the Ministry of Defence through the Defence and Security Accelerator (DASA), and applies a "Team UK" approach to enable joined-up working between industry, academia, and government.

For a defence-sector founder, the cluster in your region is often the most useful single entry point. Membership is typically open and free, events are regular, and the people in the room are the ones building, buying, and shaping the sector locally.

## Common structure

All RDSCs share a common model:

- **Led by industry,** with support from DASA and the MoD.
- **Open membership** for businesses, academia, and research institutions.
- **Regular events** for networking, knowledge sharing, and collaboration.
- **Focus areas** include innovation, skills, funding access, and routes to market.

## How to engage

For each cluster, the public website is the canonical starting point. Membership is typically a sign-up form; some clusters have steering or working groups that may invite participation later.

Each cluster page in `clusters/` is the canonical entity home: public details, mission, contacts. When you join a cluster, add your engagement context to that page (events attended, conversations, follow-ups) so the relationship knowledge accumulates in one place.
