# Uncrewed Systems Network

## Summary

The Uncrewed Systems Network (USN) is a UK national initiative launched in May 2026 by SDO Associates. It convenes industry, government, academia and investors around uncrewed-systems policy and capability, with workstream activity hosted at the STEAM Museum in Swindon and a Q1 2027 white paper as the explicit forcing function. The white paper is targeted at UK Ministry of Defence, government departments and trade bodies.

USN is an initiative, not a separate legal entity. The lead organiser is **SDO Associates**, a defence and aerospace commercial consultancy. **STARK** is the title sponsor. Entry is via the organiser's public channels and the Regional Defence and Security Cluster distribution lists.

External: [sdoassociates.com](https://www.sdoassociates.com/) (lead organiser).

## Who it's for

USN is set up around six workstreams that, taken together, span the supply, technology, regulatory, workforce and capital sides of uncrewed systems. Per the May 2026 launch communication, the workstreams are:

| Workstream | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Logistics and Supply Chain** | UK industrial base for uncrewed-systems supply, including dual-use manufacturing. |
| **Dual Use and Export** | Commercial-to-defence pathways and UK export of uncrewed systems. |
| **Technology** | Capability roadmap, R&D priorities, technology insertion. |
| **Skills and People** | Workforce planning, training, human-factors dimension. |
| **Testing and Certification** | Airworthiness, maritime / land certification, regulatory route to deployment. |
| **Funding and Investment** | Public and private capital flow into UK uncrewed-systems SMEs. |

Most plausibly useful to:

- **SMEs and non-traditional suppliers** in uncrewed air, maritime or land systems looking for a structured route into UK policy development ahead of a likely 2027 refresh.
- **Dual-use commercial firms** considering defence pathways for uncrewed technology.
- **Research institutions and academic groups** with an uncrewed-systems track and evidence to feed into a near-term UK policy artefact.
- **Investors** evaluating UK uncrewed-systems opportunities and wanting visibility ahead of the white-paper publication.

## What it offers

Per the May 2026 launch communication and SDO Associates' public materials:

- **Six themed workstreams** running through 2026, each generating evidence and recommendations.
- **Workstream sessions hosted at the STEAM Museum, Swindon**.
- **A Q1 2027 white paper** consolidating workstream output, delivered to UK MoD, government and trade bodies.
- **A triple-helix framing** (industry, government, academia) explicitly invited into each workstream.

Membership is workstream-contributor based; the documented joining route is approach via SDO Associates.

## How to evaluate joining

- **Time-bounded window.** The Q1 2027 white paper sets the forcing function. Workstream contributions through the second half of 2026 will land in the published artefact; later joiners may get less leverage on the output.
- **Pick the right workstream.** USN spans six tracks. Contributors who join the workstream closest to their core capability get more value than generalist participation.
- **Direct approach is the route.** Per public communication, the documented entry point is via SDO Associates rather than a self-service signup. Treat as a curated convening rather than an open network.
- **STARK as title sponsor.** Worth understanding STARK's involvement and any participation implications for SMEs working with adjacent providers.
