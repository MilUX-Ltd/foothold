# MissionLink UK

## Summary

MissionLink UK is an invite-only non-profit network that connects the founders / CEOs of leading early-stage UK technology companies with senior government decision-makers, investors, and thought leaders from the defence and national-security community. The stated purpose is to strengthen UK national security and resilience by improving the connections between the country's best technology entrepreneurs and the government users they need to reach.

The operating entity is **MissionLink UK Ltd**, registered at Companies House under number 14762535. The network runs an online community platform (Higher Logic), a member directory, and a programme of curated events and "Primers" (a member-facing programme line referenced by current members).

MissionLink UK is the UK chapter of a wider MissionLink family. The US sister organisation pre-dates it and was cofounded by Jeremy King (Benchmark Executive Search), Andy Lustig and Katherine Ferguson (Cooley LLP), and Matt Devost (OODA Inc.).

External: [missionlink.co.uk](https://www.missionlink.co.uk/home); [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/missionlink); [Benchmark profile of the wider MissionLink network](https://www.benchmarkes.com/missionlink/).

## Who it's for

The publicly stated membership profile is the founders and CEOs of leading early-stage UK technology companies, plus government decision-makers, investors, and thought leaders from defence and national security. That is, the network is curated around two populations:

- **Defence and national-security technology founders / CEOs.** Early-stage UK technology companies whose work has relevance to UK national security and resilience.
- **Government users and adjacent senior figures.** Decision-makers, investors, and thought leaders who hold the demand side of the conversation.

Membership is invite-only. The public-facing surface does not advertise a self-service join route; access flows through introductions by existing members.

## What it offers

Visible from the public site and member references:

- **A curated invite-only directory** of members across the founder / CEO, government, investor, and thought-leader populations.
- **Communities** — themed sub-groups inside the platform.
- **A discussion and content surface** — discussion posts, library entries, blogs.
- **A programme of events**, including a member-facing "Primers" line.

The day-to-day platform is Higher Logic (the same community platform many invite-only US trade and policy networks use), which gives MissionLink UK a structured directory, threaded discussions, and a private library, rather than a Slack / WhatsApp-style chat surface.

## UK leadership

Publicly identified UK co-founder: **Major General (Retd) James Chiswell**, formerly Commander 16 Air Assault Brigade and 1st (UK) Armoured Division. Other team members are listed on the MissionLink Team page.

## How to evaluate engaging

- **Defence-tech founder fit.** If you are a UK defence / national-security technology founder, MissionLink UK is one of the small handful of networks explicitly designed for your population. It is invite-only, so the path is through people, not forms.
- **Application route is via existing members.** Get into the orbit of someone already inside — at the time of writing this can be done via the broader UK defence-startup ecosystem (Defence BattleLab tenants, MOD Office for Small Business Growth pilot participants, Chief Disruptor Defence members) where MissionLink members tend to surface.
- **Public surface is intentionally thin.** Do not expect to learn much from the website itself. The value lives behind the member login, in the directory, the discussion surface, and the events.
- **The US sister network is not the same.** MissionLink (US, sometimes "MissionLink.Next") has its own programme and cohort cycle. UK members may engage with US activity, but the two are operationally distinct.
