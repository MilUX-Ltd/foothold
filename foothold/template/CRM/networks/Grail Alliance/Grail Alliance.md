# Grail Alliance

> [!info] Coalition, not a standalone company
> The Grail Alliance is a coalition / network operated by Tiberius Aerospace Inc. It is not a separate legal entity. The Grail Alliance public site footer reads "© 2026 Tiberius Aerospace Inc". General contact: grail@tiberius.com.

## Overview

The Grail Alliance describes itself as a *Coalition Defense Innovation Network* — a vetted network of defence stakeholders connected through Tiberius Aerospace's GRAIL platform. It targets OEMs, primes, SMEs, allied governments, software / AI companies, research institutions, and capital.

**Tagline (per site):** "Mobilizing Allied Innovation at the Speed of Conflict."

**Stated scale (graildefense.com, May 2026):** 1,000+ members across 200+ organisations spanning manufacturing, academia and capital.

**Cost to join (per site FAQ):** No fee to submit a Letter of Intent. Formal engagement agreements set out participation benefits and obligations.

**Membership scope (per site FAQ):** Vetted defence contractors, subsystem suppliers, integrators, OEMs, U.S. DoD, MoD, NATO, Allied Governments, and select academic / R&D entities.

## Platform Modules

GRAIL is described on the public site as six interconnected modules:

| Module | Purpose (per graildefense.com) |
|---|---|
| **Agility** | Defence marketplace. AI-powered opportunity matching across SAM.gov, NATO and UK Defence sources. Component-level competition, "complete-once" compliance profiles, performance-based supplier visibility. Site claims procurement timelines reduced "from 12+ years to under 2 years". |
| **Effects** | Lethality / effects scoring. AI-assisted simulation of weapon configurations across environmental conditions; digital-twin "what-if" analysis. Produces CELS (Cost-Efficient Lethality Scores), TELS (Target Efficiency Lethality Scores), and HALO (Human and Logistics Overhead) metrics. |
| **Prize** | Multi-phase innovation challenges with tiered rewards from Discovery through Implementation. Objective AI-powered scoring, pre-set IP terms, procurement pathway for winners. |
| **Collaboration** | Secure cross-organisation / cross-national workspaces. Integrations with common productivity suites. Sovereign data protection and fine-grained access controls. |
| **Compliance** | Pre-vetted partner network. Export-control management with automatic data tagging for ITAR and classification levels. Site states "1–2 day supplier onboarding vs. weeks with legacy systems". |
| **Security** | Multi-tenant isolation with per-organisation encryption keys, zero-trust architecture, immutable audit logs. Stated standards: CMMC Level 2+, NIST SP 800-171, ITAR, ISO 27001; FedRAMP High, DoD Impact Level 2/4/5/6 and allied equivalents. |

## Process (per site)

A five-step submission-to-deployment flow: **Submit** → **Validate** → **Collaborate** → **Certify** → **Deploy**. Submissions go through the GRAIL Portal; validation is described as "secure, AI-assisted"; testing is both simulated and live before deployment.

## Audiences (per site nav)

The site addresses eight audience segments separately: US Government (DoW, Services, PAEs/PEOs); Allied Ministries of Defence; Prime Contractors; SME / Non-Traditional Suppliers; Coalition Industrial Base; Software / AI Companies; Research Institutions / Academia; Investment / Capital.

## Why a defence-sector founder might care

Relevant if you are:

- An **SME or non-traditional supplier** looking for a single-profile route into US, UK, NATO and allied opportunity feeds, with the headline pitch being merit-based discovery rather than relationship-based access.
- A **software or AI company** seeking dual-use pathways into defence procurement.
- A **research institution or academic group** that wants an objective validation route from research into deployable capability.
- An **investor** evaluating defence-tech opportunities and wanting consistent metrics across portfolio candidates.

Independent verification of throughput claims (e.g. the "12+ years to under 2 years" procurement-timeline figure) is not provided on the public site at time of writing. Treat as marketing claim until you can corroborate from a customer or member.

## Joining

Per the site FAQ, joining requires a non-binding Letter of Intent submitted via the Alliance form on graildefense.com. A different signatory can be designated. The site notes that the inaugural cohort is "limited and nearing capacity".
