# MOD Policy Library

A defence-sector founder runs into a wall of named MOD policy documents in the first year: JSPs, DEF STANs, Joint Concept Notes, framework documents. This page is the navigation surface — it tells you what the named publication series are and where to find them.

## Joint Service Publications (JSPs)

JSPs are the MOD's policy publications. The whole library is at [gov.uk: Joint Service Publications (JSPs)](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/joint-service-publications-jsps). The ones a defence-tech SME is most likely to encounter:

|-----|-------------|-------|
| **JSP 440** | Defence Manual of Security | MOD (Defence Security) |
| **JSP 441** | Defence policy for records management | MOD |
| **JSP 462** | Financial Management Policy Manual | MOD |
| **JSP 528** | Defence Procurement Policy | MOD Commercial |
| **JSP 800** | Defence Logistics and Movement | NA-L&S |

Full JSP collection: [gov.uk JSPs](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/joint-service-publications-jsps).

## Defence Standards (Def Stans)

Def Stans are MOD technical standards, distinct from JSPs (which are policy). There are around 900 extant Def Stans, reviewed on a four-year cycle by [[CRM/organisations/mod/Defence Standardisation|DStan]].

- Searchable catalogue: [British Defence Standards at IHS Markit](https://global.ihs.com/standards.cfm?publisher=MODUK).
- DStan portal: [dstan.mod.uk](https://www.dstan.mod.uk/) and [StanMIS](https://www.dstan.mod.uk/StanMIS/).
- Notable example: [Def Stan 00-970 amendments (military aircraft design)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defence-standards-def-stan-970-amendments).

Adjacent: NATO **STANAGs** (Standardization Agreements) — DStan represents the UK in the NATO standardisation process.

## How to use this library

Most of these are not bedtime reading. They are reference points — read the relevant section when:

- You hit an acronym in a tender document. Look it up in [[Resources/Reference/Defence Acronyms|Defence Acronyms]], then find the underlying policy here.
- You need to understand the rules for a specific procurement (JSP 528, MEAT, PA23).
- You're scoping work in a regulated domain (aviation, nuclear, ordnance) — check the relevant DSA regulator's policy.
- You're writing a proposal that needs to align with named MOD doctrine — JDPs and JCNs are the source.
