# Defence Acronyms

UK defence runs on acronyms. This glossary is the most useful ones for a defence-sector founder or SME operator. Not exhaustive; curated for first-year usefulness. Expand as you encounter new ones in your engagement.

## MOD structure and bodies

| Acronym | Full name | What it is |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| MOD | Ministry of Defence | UK government department for defence |
| NAD Group | National Armaments Director Group | Post-April-2026 reorganisation of DE&S and adjacent functions under the National Armaments Director |
| NA-Intl | National Armaments International | NAD pillar for international collaboration and exports |
| NA-M | National Armaments Materiel | Delivery arm (former DE&S core) |
| NA-Innov | National Armaments Innovation | Innovation pillar (covers Dstl, UKDI, Defence Investment Unit) |
| NA-C&I | National Armaments Commercial & Industry | Commercial and industry functions |
| NA-C | National Armaments Corporate | Corporate and shared services |
| NA-D&D | National Armaments Digital & Data | Defence Digital, formerly the Defence Digital function |
| NA-L&S | National Armaments Logistics & Support | Defence Support, supply chain and logistics |
| NA-P&P | National Armaments Plans & Portfolios | Portfolio management |
| DIO | Defence Infrastructure Organisation | Defence estate, training areas, accommodation |
| DASA | Defence and Security Accelerator | MOD innovation arm; funds early-stage capability work |
| Dstl | Defence Science and Technology Laboratory | MOD's in-house science and technology centre |
| UKDI | United Kingdom Defence Innovation | Innovation-focused capability investment |
| UKDSE | UK Defence and Security Exports | Defence export support |
| UKHO | UK Hydrographic Office | Maritime navigation, charts |
| NSO | National Shipbuilding Office | Shipbuilding strategy and coordination |
| OPA | Oil and Pipelines Agency | Fuel logistics |
| SSRO | Single Source Regulations Office | Regulator for single-source defence contracts |

## Service branches and commands

| Acronym | Full name | What it is |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| RN | Royal Navy | Maritime service |
| RAF | Royal Air Force | Air service |
| BA | British Army | Land service |
| FLC | Front Line Command | The operational commands (RN, BA, RAF, plus Strategic Command) |
| StratCom | Strategic Command | Joint command for cyber, space, special forces, defence digital |
| JFC | Joint Forces Command | Former name for Strategic Command |

## Procurement

| Acronym | Full name | What it is |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| CCS | Crown Commercial Service | Cross-government procurement function |
| DPS | Dynamic Purchasing System | Procurement vehicle with continuous supplier entry |
| EOI | Expression of Interest | Early-stage market engagement signal |
| ITT | Invitation to Tender | Formal procurement competition document |
| PQQ | Pre-Qualification Questionnaire | Standard supplier-qualification document |
| SQ | Supplier Questionnaire | Newer name for PQQ under Procurement Act 2023 |
| RFI | Request for Information | Pre-procurement information gathering |
| RFP | Request for Proposal | More open procurement document |
| RFQ | Request for Quotation | Price-led procurement document |
| OJEU | Official Journal of the European Union | Pre-Brexit procurement notice publisher; superseded by Find a Tender |
| FTS | Find a Tender Service | Current UK procurement notice platform |
| PA23 | Procurement Act 2023 | Current UK procurement legislation |
| MEAT | Most Economically Advantageous Tender | Standard award criterion combining price and quality |

## Capability and acquisition

| Acronym | Full name | What it is |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| TRL | Technology Readiness Level | 1–9 scale for technology maturity |
| MRL | Manufacturing Readiness Level | 1–10 scale for production maturity |
| IBC | Initial Business Case | First stage gate in the MOD acquisition lifecycle |
| OBC | Outline Business Case | Second stage gate |
| FBC | Full Business Case | Third stage gate |
| DLOD | Defence Lines of Development | The eight cross-cutting capability dimensions (Training, Equipment, Personnel, Information, Doctrine, Organisation, Infrastructure, Logistics) |
| TEPIDOIL | Training, Equipment, Personnel, Information, Doctrine, Organisation, Infrastructure, Logistics | Mnemonic for the DLODs |
| ILS | Integrated Logistics Support | Through-life sustainment planning |
| ConOps | Concept of Operations | High-level operating concept for a capability |
| OA | Operational Analysis | MOD discipline for capability and procurement modelling |
| URD | User Requirements Document | What the user needs the capability to do |
| SRD | System Requirements Document | What the system needs to do to meet the URD |

## Domains and capability

| Acronym | Full name | What it is |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| C2 | Command and Control | The systems through which commanders direct forces |
| C4 | Command, Control, Communications, Computers | Common shorthand |
| C4I | C4 plus Intelligence | More inclusive shorthand |
| C4ISR | C4 plus Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance | Most common digital-domain umbrella |
| C4ISTAR | C4ISR plus Target Acquisition | Land-domain umbrella |
| ISTAR | Intelligence, Surveillance, Target Acquisition, Reconnaissance | Sensing and analysis capability |
| ISR | Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance | Sub-set of ISTAR without targeting |
| GEOINT | Geospatial Intelligence | Mapping and imagery intelligence |
| SIGINT | Signals Intelligence | Electronic-emissions intelligence |
| HUMINT | Human Intelligence | People-sourced intelligence |
| OSINT | Open Source Intelligence | Publicly-available intelligence |
| EW | Electronic Warfare | Electromagnetic spectrum capability |
| UAV | Unmanned Aerial Vehicle | Drone |
| UAS | Unmanned Aerial System | Drone plus ground station, comms, etc. |
| BVLOS | Beyond Visual Line of Sight | Long-range drone operations |
| GVC | General Visual Line of Sight Certificate | UK drone operator qualification |

|---------|-----------|------------|

## How to use this glossary

You'll meet a new acronym every other week in defence. Add it here with a one-line definition the moment you find out what it means. Future-you and your team will thank you.

If an acronym refers to a specific MOD body, framework, programme, or portal that has its own page in this vault, link from the acronym entry to the page. The glossary is for quick lookup; the entity pages carry the depth.
