## What this is

The macro read in a market-scan set, complementary to SWOT's micro read. For a defence business it is the disciplined way to map the operating environment before committing to a strategy or a market.

## The six factors, read for defence

1. **Political**, defence policy and reviews, government spending priorities and programme funding, ministerial direction, alliance and coalition commitments, sovereign-capability and onshoring pressure.
2. **Economic**, the defence budget cycle and the Equipment Plan, programme funding stability, inflation against fixed-price contracts, exchange rates on imported kit.
3. **Social**, recruitment and skills in defence, public and parliamentary attitudes, the reserve and veteran workforce.
4. **Technological**, dual-use and emerging technology, the pace of adoption, the valley of death between R&D and procurement, cyber and data-handling demands.
5. **Legal**, export control and ITAR, security accreditation and JOSCAR, data protection, procurement law and the route to contract, IP and liability.
6. **Environmental**, sustainability requirements in defence, resource and supply-chain resilience, climate-driven demand.

## Method

Identify 3 to 5 factors per category; rate impact and likelihood (high, medium, low); prioritise by impact times likelihood; develop responses (leverage, mitigate, comply); name leading indicators to monitor and assumptions to research. Render the result.
