# Stakeholder Mapping and Engagement Plan

Forked from [phuryn/pm-skills](https://github.com/phuryn/pm-skills) v2.0.0 (`pm-execution/stakeholder-map`, MIT, copyright Pawel Huryn). ## What this is

A power/interest map and an engagement plan, for an engagement, a capture or bid, or campaign-audience planning.

## The defence move

Map the real defence cast, not a generic org chart: the **customer** who selects and pays (commercial officer, capability lead, requirements owner), the **user** who operates, the **primes** you sell through or compete with, the approving and accrediting authorities, and the budget holder. Keep the customer and user distinct, because they hold different power and different interest.

## Method

1. **Identify stakeholders:** individuals and groups inside and around the customer, plus primes, partners, authorities and end users.
2. **Classify each** on Power (ability to influence the decision or resources) and Interest (how much it affects them or how engaged they are).
3. **Place on the grid:**

   | | High interest | Low interest |
   |---|---|---|
   | **High power** | Manage closely: involve early, seek input | Keep satisfied: periodic updates, escalate only the critical |
   | **Low power** | Keep informed: regular updates, demos, feedback | Monitor: light touch, available on request |

4. **Per quadrant,** recommend frequency, format, key messages, and the risk if neglected.
5. **Engagement plan table:** stakeholder, role, power, interest, strategy, frequency, channel, key message.
6. **Flag conflicts:** stakeholders with competing interests, and how to align them.

Save as markdown.

## Notes

- **Map each stakeholder to visibility, advocacy or sponsorship.** Being known (visibility) or supported (advocacy) does not move a programme; progress comes when someone carries risk and ownership on your behalf (sponsorship). Note which each stakeholder actually offers, and plan to convert toward sponsorship.
- Pairs with `pre-mortem`.
