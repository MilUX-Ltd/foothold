## What this is

A go-to-market plan. The method holds; the defence reframing is the substance.

## The defence move

- **Route to contract, not consumer channels.** The "channels" that matter are the frameworks, the primes, direct-to-customer routes, and the relationships that lead to a contract. Paid search and viral loops are rarely the point.
- **Capture, not a launch event.** A defence "launch" is usually a capture campaign against a programme: positioning, references, trials and bid, over a long cycle, not a single go-live.
- **Message-market fit.** Messaging leads with what the offering delivers against what the segment selects on, and respects OPSEC and classification.

## Method

1. **Gather research:** the offering, the target segment, validation data, the competitive and incumbent picture.
2. **Choose the route to market:** the frameworks, primes, direct routes and partnerships that reach the customer; focus on a few done well.
3. **Develop messaging:** the core value proposition for the segment (use `value-proposition`), the differentiators, the proof points, by channel.
4. **Define success metrics:** awareness, engagement, qualified opportunities, and the contract metrics that actually matter; set a baseline first.
5. **Build a phased plan:** pre-launch, launch or first capture, post-launch momentum, with go/no-go decision points; a 90-day roadmap.

Render the plan.

## Two qualifying tests before you invest

- **Interest is not budget authority.** Before committing effort to a route, qualify whether it is actually funded, at what order of magnitude, and on what timeframe. A funded route to contract is the test, not a warm meeting or a hub's attention.
- **Chase sponsorship, not visibility.** Being known (visibility) or supported (advocacy) does not move a programme. Progress comes when someone carries risk and ownership on your behalf (sponsorship). Plan the engagement to convert visibility into sponsorship, and map it with `stakeholder-map`.

## Notes

- Start with the most confident segment; validate before scaling.
