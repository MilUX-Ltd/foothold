# A tender lands

**When to use it.** A tender or competition you might bid for has been published.

## The steps

1. **`eligibility-check`.** Checks the opportunity against four walls before you spend a week on it: cyber, clearance, export and commercial basics. Reads your context and the defence reference, and writes a go or no-go page into the engagement folder.
2. **`bid-response`.** Drafts the response against the published criteria, using your context, your services and the evidence in the engagement folder. Everything it produces stays in that folder.

## Notes

Run the check first. If the verdict is no-go, stop there: the page records why, which is useful the next time a similar tender appears.
