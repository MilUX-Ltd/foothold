# You meet someone at an event

**When to use it.** You have met someone from an organisation that matters to you and want to follow up well.

## The steps

1. **`add-organisation`.** Adds the organisation to your CRM from public information, under its parent where it has one, and asks you to confirm before it writes.
2. **`meeting-prep`.** Prepares a briefing for the follow-up meeting from your CRM, your initiatives, the events you have logged and your context. It writes nothing; the briefing is given to you in the conversation.

## Notes

If you want the person recorded as well as the organisation, run `add-contact` between the two steps. It asks for the lawful basis for holding their details.
