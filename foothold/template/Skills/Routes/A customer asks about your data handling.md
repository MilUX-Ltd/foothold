# A customer asks about your data handling

**When to use it.** A customer, a prime or an assessor asks how you handle personal data and how your devices are secured.

## The steps

1. **`build-dpia`.** Builds a data protection impact assessment in the structure the Information Commissioner's Office sets out, from what your vault already says about the data you hold. Writes it into Operations.
2. **`cyber-essentials-ready`.** Walks you through the Cyber Essentials controls on your own computer and records the evidence in Operations. Nothing changes on your computer without your say.

## Notes

The two records sit side by side in `Operations/`, so the next time someone asks you have one place to point to.
