# Monday morning

**When to use it.** The start of the week: what needs your attention, and is the vault still in good order.

## The steps

1. **`daily-brief`.** Reads your last daily note, your live engagements and initiatives, the events coming up and the contacts going quiet, and writes today's note.
2. **`curator`.** Sweeps the vault for pages that have gone stale or lost their place, and writes one short report into Operations. It is meant to run about once a month, not every week.

## Notes

Run the brief every working day if it helps. Run the curator on the first Monday of the month.
