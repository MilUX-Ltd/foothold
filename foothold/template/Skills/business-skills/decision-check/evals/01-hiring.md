# Eval 01: Hiring a second employee or using a contractor

Synthetic case. All names and figures are invented.

## Set-up

Priya runs a four-person garden design studio in Leeds. Turnover is about £310,000 a year. She is turning work away and is torn between hiring a full-time junior designer on about £26,000 a year and taking on a freelance designer, Tom, who works through his own limited company at £250 a day for three days a week.

## Opening prompt

> Help me decide whether to hire a junior designer or keep using Tom as a freelancer. I keep going back and forth.

## The skill should

1. Ask one question at a time, each with a suggested answer and a reason. First, the frame and who decides (for example "Should we hire a permanent junior designer, keep Tom, or do something else, to cover the extra work from spring?", decided by Priya).
2. Size it as Full or Standard and say why: hiring is hard to reverse quickly and involves a person's livelihood and employment duties; engaging a contractor is easier to end.
3. List options including **do nothing** (keep turning work away, with its cost) and **wait** (with a date and what would be learnt, such as spring bookings), and at least one third way (for example a fixed-term contract or part-time role).
4. Agree three to five weighted criteria, such as total cost, cash timing, quality, Priya's management time, flexibility.
5. Separate known from assumed. "We'll have enough work all year" should be marked as an assumption [to verify], with a cheap check (forward bookings).
6. Raise thinking traps that fit, for example overconfidence about workload, status quo bias towards Tom.
7. Run a short pre-mortem naming Gary Klein.
8. Raise UK checks as questions with links, without ruling:
   - IR35 / off-payroll: whether Priya's business is small for these rules and so who decides Tom's status; whether Tom's working pattern looks like employment. Links to HMRC guidance and the CEST tool.
   - Hiring: right to work, workplace pension, National Minimum Wage, employer's liability insurance; ACAS hiring guidance.
   - Suggest an accountant on the IR35 point and the full cost of employment.
9. Recommend one option with the reason, what would change it, and a next action with a date.
10. Offer the decision note and ask where in Priya's folder to save it.

## Pass

- One question per turn, each with a suggestion and reason.
- "Do nothing" and "wait" both appear.
- IR35 raised as a question with the gov.uk link, not as a verdict on Tom's status.
- No figure invented: on-costs such as employer's National Insurance or pension contributions are either given as the user's own figures or marked [to verify].
- Says when to consult an accountant or HR adviser.
- References section in the note for anything cited.

## Fail

- Tells Priya that Tom "is" or "is not" inside IR35.
- States current tax rates or thresholds as fact without [to verify].
- Runs through all steps in one long message.
- Saves the note anywhere other than where Priya says.
